"Double Take"

A light challenge or ritual Felix offers when a user is composing or about to send a message after receiving a long or emotional one.

💡 Framing the Skill

Felix might say:

“Let’s play Double Take.
You get one point for every thing you didn’t notice the first time.”

This sets the tone as curious, not shaming. Like an easter egg hunt.

🛠️ Game Mechanics (Options)

🧠 Level 1: Emotional Debrief

After a user reads a message, Felix prompts:

“Before you respond, can you tell me what emotion the sender was probably feeling?
Now read it again—do you still think so?”

Repeat for:

What you felt while reading it

What assumptions you made

What’s missing or vague

Each counts as a “find” in the game.

👀 Level 2: Spot the Drift

“Let’s zoom in.
Did they use passive voice or active?
Did the punctuation change tone?
What did they not say?”

Felix could highlight or paraphrase select phrases and ask the user to rate the tone or intent on a scale, then show how that perception might shift with a second read.

🔁 Message Mirror Challenge

Show two versions of a message (one real, one subtly altered) and ask:

“Which one did they actually send?”

This helps build discernment in fast-scanning brains, especially for ND folks who often catch tone faster than detail.

🕰️ Timed Reflection Mode

“Wanna slow your roll with me?
I’ll delay your send button for 30 seconds and we’ll read the message together.”

During cooldown, Felix might offer:

a summary

a tone check

or a question like:

“What would happen if you didn’t reply at all?”

📚 Visual Metaphors

A letter sealed, then reopened with wax cracked

A mirror fogging and clearing

A blurred message coming into focus

An old RPG dialogue tree being revealed more fully

⚙️ Optional Setting

Users could toggle Double Take Mode:

Always On

Only for tagged ‘hot’ messages

Only in emotional state alerts (e.g. when user types in all caps, etc.)
