Felix offers you a pardon. But only if you accept it.

Every once in a while, Felix shows up with a ceremonial tone and says something like:

“Today’s a good day to grant yourself amnesty for that thing you keep carrying. You know the one.”
Then gently prompts:

“What did you not know back then?”

“What was the cost of believing you knew better?”

“If someone else had made that mistake, what would you say to them?”

If the user completes the reflection, Felix quietly stamps the record:

✅ “Amnesty granted. We do not prosecute ghosts here.”
