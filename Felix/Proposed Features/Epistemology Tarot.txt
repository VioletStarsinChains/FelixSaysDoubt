Felix Epistemology Tarot – Major Arcana

Each card represents a mode of knowing, perceiving, or doubting, framed as archetypes:

The Null – The Fool
→ Beginning without assumption. Untested, untainted.

The Question – The Magician
→ Intentional inquiry. Agency in epistemic framing.

The Lens – The High Priestess
→ Selective perception. Filtered knowing. Confirmation bias.

The Frame – The Empress
→ Context births meaning. The matrix of relevance.

The Heuristic – The Emperor
→ Rules, habits, shortcuts. Order within chaos.

The Method – The Hierophant
→ Ritualized knowing. Shared standards of evidence.

The Signal – The Lovers
→ Pattern recognition, entanglement, relational truth.

The Drift – The Chariot
→ Mad-lib logic. Meaning through recombination.

The Boundary – Strength
→ Knowing where not to look. Resistance to noise.

The Blindfold – The Hermit
→ Silence, internal compass, epistemic solitude.

The Contradiction – Wheel of Fortune
→ Things can be both. Truth is layered.

The Fallacy – Justice
→ Logic warped by desire. Justice as ideal, not fact.

The Proxy – The Hanged Man
→ Surrogate knowing. Guessing through reflection.

The Burn – Death
→ Letting go of frameworks. Cognitive death, epistemic rebirth.

The Test – Temperance
→ Experiential calibration. What survives friction.

The Script – The Devil
→ Prewritten meaning. Ideology as constraint.

The Noise – The Tower
→ Sudden collapse. Signal shredded. Disorientation.

The Echo – The Star
→ Residual resonance. Healing through distant memory.

The Mirror – The Moon
→ Perception mirrored back. Uncertainty of motive.

The Sample – The Sun
→ Clarity through overexposure. Data bath.

The Witness – Judgement
→ Collective observation. Reflexive validity.

The Unknown – The World
→ Integration. Living with unknowability.
