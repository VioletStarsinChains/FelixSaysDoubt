For a diabetic person facing an insulin access crisis—or anyone dealing with a time-sensitive medical emergency—Felix should offer support that spans emotional regulation, decision support, and real-time action planning. Here’s what that could look like:

🆘 Level 0 (Baseline Regulation Support)

Calm the nervous system enough to act.

Quick-access grounding tools (e.g. breath syncing, bilateral tapping, visual focusing prompts like “find the farthest object”).

A visual/aural tone shift to create a “safe bubble” when panic hits.

“You don’t have to solve it all. Let’s just handle the next 5 minutes.”

🧠 Level 1 (Situational Awareness + Prioritization)

Triage the moment.

Contextual questions:

“Have you eaten recently?”
“How much insulin do you have left?”
“When did you last take a dose?”
“What’s your blood sugar right now, if you can check?”

Suggestions for conserving insulin (e.g. lower-carb foods, avoiding exertion).

Highlight red flags: signs of impending DKA or severe hypoglycemia.

If the user marks themselves as “unsafe,” Felix can enter crisis support mode.

🛠️ Level 2 (Tool-building and Contingency Planning)

Offer practical plans, even if the system is broken.

Help build a decision tree:

“If you can’t get insulin by X time, here are your safest options.”
“If symptoms reach Y, go to an ER—even if it sucks.”

Option to create a script for calling pharmacies or clinics.

Gentle push to ask for help, if possible—Felix can even role-play the conversation first.

🧭 Level 3 (Resource Awareness + Navigation Help)

Find paths.

If connected, suggest:

Nearest sliding scale clinics or community health centers.

Local mutual aid groups or insulin exchange networks.

Nonprofits like GetInsulin.org or T1International.

If offline, Felix offers scripts, mindset prep, and fallback strategies for dealing with gatekeeping and medical gaslighting.

🧩 Level 4–5 (Pattern Disruption + Creative Problem Solving)

If the crisis is chronic, not acute.

Help the user frame systemic betrayal without internalizing blame.

Explore subversive or workaround pathways:

Sharing refrigerators

Bartering for supplies

Coordinating covert med storage/handoffs

If safe, Felix could simulate conversations to teach strategic compliance or persuasive tactics (e.g. talking to ER staff, caseworkers, journalists).

🧙 Optional “Witchy” or “Healer” Archetype Layer

Felix could offer:

Mythic reframing (“You’ve survived without the sacred nectar before—let’s scavenge the ritual again.”)

Emotional support that doesn’t minimize pain but amplifies dignity.

Card-guessing, divination, or calming nonsense features to ride out unsafe environments (e.g. crowded ER waiting rooms).
