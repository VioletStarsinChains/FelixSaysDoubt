Model Approval & Impact Statement (MAIS)

Required prior to any ontology change or new analytic model deployment

⸻

Section 1 — Model Overview
	•	Model/Change Name:
	•	Version Number:
	•	Date of Submission:
	•	Submitting Team:
	•	Purpose/Intended Use: (one paragraph in plain language)
	•	Ontology Entities/Relationships Affected: (list)

⸻

Section 2 — Justification & Necessity
	•	Problem Definition: What real-world problem is this change intended to solve?
	•	Alternatives Considered: Were non-technical or less invasive options considered? Why were they rejected?
	•	Evidence of Need: What data demonstrates this change is necessary?

⸻

Section 3 — Category Definition & Reflexivity

For every new or modified entity/classification:
	•	Definition in plain language
	•	Source of definition (who decided, and on what grounds)
	•	Inclusion criteria
	•	Exclusion criteria
	•	Potentially excluded populations (edge cases)
	•	Known harms from prior use of this category
	•	Confidence level in definition (low / medium / high)

⸻

Section 4 — Stakeholder Consultation
	•	Community groups consulted: (list)
	•	Marginalized/at-risk groups consulted: (list)
	•	Summary of feedback received:
	•	Changes made in response to feedback:

⸻

Section 5 — Harm & Risk Analysis
	•	Who may be misclassified?
	•	What harms could misclassification cause?
	•	Potential downstream harms if misclassification propagates?
	•	Risk of amplification if used by enforcement agencies?
	•	Mitigation strategies proposed: (technical + policy)
	•	Residual risk after mitigation:

⸻

Section 6 — Protective Outcomes Assessment
	•	How does this change support life-preserving/protective outcomes?
	•	If anomalies are generated, how will they be routed to the Protection Queue?
	•	Independent advocacy review required? (Y/N) If no, why not?

⸻

Section 7 — Auditability & Transparency
	•	Data sources used in model training/inference: (list + provenance)
	•	Public documentation URL:
	•	Independent oversight reviewers assigned: (names + roles)
	•	Audit log location:

⸻

Section 8 — KPIs & Monitoring
	•	Key protective metrics:
	•	Wrongful flag reduction target
	•	Protective outreach response time
	•	Harm-risk threshold compliance
	•	Monitoring frequency:
	•	Triggers for suspension or rollback:

⸻

Section 9 — Sunset Clause
	•	Automatic expiration date for this model/change:
	•	Criteria for renewal:

⸻

Section 10 — Final Approvals
	•	Technical Review Board: [Sign + Date]
	•	Ethics/Harms Board: [Sign + Date]
	•	Community Oversight Board: [Sign + Date]
	•	Executive Accountability Officer: [Sign + Date]
