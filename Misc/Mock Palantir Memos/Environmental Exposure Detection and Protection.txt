Pilot: Environmental Exposure Detection and Protection

6-Month Implementation Plan

⸻

Goal

Use Palantir’s existing data-integration capabilities to detect early signs of harmful chemical or environmental exposures (e.g., lead, PFAS, industrial pollutants, air particulates) and route anomalies to protective public health interventions instead of enforcement.

⸻

Scope
	•	Geography: 1 metropolitan area + surrounding county (≈ 1.5M residents).
	•	Focus: Air, water, and soil contaminants with proven links to neurological/immune destabilization.
	•	Timeframe: 6 months (pilot, renewable).
	•	Output:
	1.	Early detection of exposure clusters.
	2.	Protective alerts routed to public health partners.
	3.	Public dashboard of anonymized signals + actions taken.

⸻

Data Sources
	•	Public health: ER visit logs (anonymized, daily refresh), poison control call summaries, school absenteeism rates.
	•	Environment: Air-quality sensors, water-utility contaminant test results, EPA/CDC datasets, satellite imagery (thermal + particulates).
	•	Consumer signals (aggregate only): OTC sales (antihistamines, asthma meds), anonymized wearable health data.
	•	Historical baseline: Prior exposure events and remediation efforts (EPA Superfund, state DEQ).

⸻

Technical Architecture
	1.	Ingestion Layer: Pull daily feeds from health, environment, and consumer sources.
	2.	Ontology Layer: Entities = Location, Exposure Type, Cluster, Health Signal. Relationships = “occurs_at,” “linked_to,” “suspected_exposure.”
	3.	Analytics Layer:
	•	Run anomaly detection across ER/OTC/absenteeism vs. baseline.
	•	Correlate with environmental sensor spikes.
	•	Assign confidence scores + uncertainty ranges.
	4.	Protection Queue: All flagged clusters route to public health officials, not enforcement.
	5.	Public Dashboard: Weekly anonymized map of flagged clusters, actions taken, outcome metrics.

⸻

Governance
	•	Independent Oversight Board: local public health reps, community orgs, environmental scientists, ethicists.
	•	Advocacy Gate: All escalations must be reviewed by a non-law-enforcement advocate (clinician or social worker) before any action.
	•	Audit Trail: Immutable log of data sources, confidence scores, actions, and outcomes — published quarterly.

⸻

Timeline

Month 1 — Planning & Setup
	•	Convene partners + oversight board.
	•	Finalize data-sharing agreements (HIPAA-compliant, DP safeguards).
	•	Build protective ontology schema.
	•	Hire 2 community liaison officers.

Month 2 — Integration
	•	Connect ingestion pipelines (ER data, utility data, sensors).
	•	Stand up cloud environment with privacy layers (differential privacy + federated queries).
	•	Draft protective response protocols with public health agencies.

Month 3 — Pilot Launch (Soft)
	•	Start daily ingestion + anomaly detection on 2–3 exposures (e.g., particulates, nitrates, lead).
	•	Route flagged anomalies into Protection Queue.
	•	Dry-run protective outreach (alerts to utility/public health only, no public alerts yet).

Month 4 — Expansion
	•	Add more exposure types.
	•	Launch anonymized public dashboard (heat maps, not person-level).
	•	Begin independent audit of first 6 weeks’ logs.

Month 5 — Optimization
	•	Refine anomaly thresholds using feedback from protective outreach outcomes.
	•	Expand advocacy network (NGOs, clinics).
	•	Issue first public “Protective Outcomes Report.”

Month 6 — Evaluation & Scale Decision
	•	Oversight board evaluates KPIs.
	•	Publish independent Harms Assessment.
	•	Decide: scale up to state level, replicate in second metro, or extend pilot 6 months.

⸻

Budget (approx., 6 months)
	•	Tech stack & licenses: $2.5M (Palantir Foundry/Gotham seats, secure cloud hosting).
	•	Data integration & privacy layer: $1M.
	•	Staffing: $1.2M (data engineers, analysts, 2 community liaisons, 2 public health officers, oversight admin).
	•	Audit & oversight board: $500k (stipends, independent auditors).
	•	Protective interventions fund: $800k (filters, bottled water, medical outreach, community alerts).
	•	Comms & public dashboard: $200k.

Total: ≈ $6.2M.

⸻

Key KPIs
	1.	Protective outreach rate: ≥90% of flagged clusters receive outreach within 48 hrs.
	2.	Wrongful-flag reduction: ≥75% reduction after 3 months of feedback-loop optimization.
	3.	Public trust metric: 70% of surveyed residents report increased trust in protective alerts.
	4.	Independent audit compliance: 100% quarterly audit pass.
