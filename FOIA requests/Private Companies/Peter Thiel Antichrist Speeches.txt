To DHS / DOJ (FBI) / ATF / DOD / GSA / CISA

Request:

Requesting any and all contracts, task orders, interagency agreements, memoranda of understanding, statements of work, vendor invoices, email communications, meeting notes, procurement records, data transfer agreements, or deliverables referencing the sharing, purchase, ingestion, or use of consumer firearms sales data, retail point-of-sale firearms/ammunition transaction data, or derived behavioral indicators tied to firearms purchases by commercial analytics providers (including but not limited to Palantir Technologies, Palantir Foundry/Gotham/Apollo, Booz Allen, Accenture, and similar firms) from January 1, 2010 to present.

This includes:
•	Contracts or task orders between any of the above agencies and Palantir Technologies Inc. or its subcontractors that reference firearms sales data, retailer point-of-sale (POS) data, ammunition distribution data, or dealer-level inventory data
•	Communications, meeting notes, or email threads discussing the ingestion, normalization, or use of firearms or ammunition retail data in analytic platforms used for modeling or predictive purposes
•	Data sharing agreements, data use agreements, or memoranda of understanding between the federal government and commercial analytics providers or private sector entities (including but not limited to major firearms retailers, payment processors, or data brokers) that include firearms or ammunition transaction datasets
•	Records of any datasets or feeds labeled (explicitly or implicitly) as “behavioral indicators,” “consumer risk signals,” “weaponization signals,” “firearms demand indices,” or similar vocabulary that were provided to analytics platforms used by federal agencies
•	Subcontractor deliverables, vendor invoices, or project reports showing the flow of firearms-related datasets into any analytics environment or data lake accessible to both government clients and private sector clients
•	Communications or contracts referencing analytics products described as “digital twin,” “public figure modeling,” “persona modeling,” “speech generation,” or “influence modeling” that were provided to, or developed in collaboration with, Thiel-affiliated entities, speakers’ bureaus, or private individuals associated with Peter Thiel
•	Any records of approval or legal review concerning the use of commercial behavioral datasets (including firearms purchasing behavior) in analytic products delivered to external private sector clients
•	Records of meetings or communications between agency procurement officials and representatives of firearms manufacturers or major firearms retailers about data sharing, analytics, or joint modeling projects
•	Any contract modifications or confidentiality agreements that obscure the identity of data providers for analytics projects involving the categories above
