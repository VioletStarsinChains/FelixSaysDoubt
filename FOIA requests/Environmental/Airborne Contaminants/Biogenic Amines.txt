To: U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) / Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / Minnesota Pollution Control Agency (MPCA) / Minnesota Department of Health (MDH)

Request:

Requesting any and all records, monitoring data, internal communications, or research programs referencing the presence of biogenic amines—including putrescine, cadaverine, spermidine, spermine, and related volatile amines—in municipal or regional wastewater systems within Minnesota, from 2000 to present.

This includes:
	•	Any routine or special-project wastewater sampling for biogenic amines in Minnesota
	•	Records of amines detected as part of broader Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD) or Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD) studies
	•	Communications between EPA, CDC, MPCA, or MDH regarding wastewater monitoring for amines or decomposition byproducts
	•	Reports, research proposals, or grant-funded studies examining biogenic amines as indicators of environmental contamination, industrial release, or public health risk
	•	Any overlap with SARS-CoV-2 wastewater monitoring projects that considered additional chemical/biological markers

Keywords: wastewater monitoring, biogenic amines, putrescine, cadaverine, spermidine, spermine, volatile amines, BOD, COD, Minnesota Pollution Control Agency, municipal sewage
