FOIA REQUEST

To: U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
To: U.S. Department of Defense (DoD)

Request:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request any and all contracts, statements of work, reports, presentations, internal communications, or deliverables between DHS/DoD and Palantir Technologies Inc. that reference or discuss cost minimization, budget reduction, or efficiency gains as client objectives.

This request includes, but is not limited to:
	•	Contracts or SOWs that describe Palantir platforms (e.g., Gotham, Foundry) in terms of reducing expenditures, streamlining budgets, or increasing efficiency.
	•	Internal communications within DHS/DoD acknowledging that Palantir deliverables were primarily cost-focused rather than security- or threat-prevention-focused.
	•	Reports, dashboards, or analytic products delivered by Palantir where outcomes were measured in terms of budgetary savings or reduced expenditures.
	•	Slide decks, marketing materials, or client presentations where Palantir emphasized cost minimization while publicly framing deliverables as “threat detection” or “terrorism prevention.”
	•	Compliance reviews, ethics assessments, or oversight reports concerning discrepancies between the stated purpose of Palantir deliverables (e.g., terrorism prevention) and their actual use as cost-cutting tools.
	•	Records of meetings or communications between Palantir personnel and DHS/DoD officials where cost-saving goals were acknowledged as the primary driver of adoption.

Keywords: Palantir, Gotham, Foundry, cost minimization, cost savings, budget reduction, efficiency, expenditure, threat detection, terrorism prevention, rationalization, client objectives, DHS, DoD.

Date Range: January 1, 2006 – Present.

To: Office of Management and Budget (OMB)

Request:

Pursuant to the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request any and all records, reports, contracts, memoranda, communications, or deliverables referencing Palantir Technologies Inc. in connection with cost minimization, budget reduction, or efficiency gains across federal agencies.

This request includes, but is not limited to:
	•	Records in which Palantir was discussed or evaluated as a tool for reducing federal expenditures, streamlining agency budgets, or increasing operational efficiency.
	•	Contracts, statements of work, or interagency agreements where Palantir deliverables were justified on cost-saving grounds.
	•	Internal OMB analyses, slide decks, or presentations describing Palantir adoption in terms of budgetary efficiency, fiscal consolidation, or expenditure reduction.
	•	Communications between OMB officials and Palantir representatives, or between OMB and other agencies, acknowledging cost minimization as a primary objective of Palantir engagements.
	•	Oversight reports, compliance reviews, or ethics assessments concerning whether Palantir deliverables were primarily budget-focused rather than aligned with stated rationales such as “threat detection” or “terrorism prevention.”
	•	Any cross-agency planning or performance review records in which OMB monitored Palantir’s impact on agency costs or expenditures.

Keywords: Palantir, Gotham, Foundry, cost minimization, budget reduction, efficiency, cost savings, expenditure, threat detection, terrorism prevention, rationalization, client objectives, OMB.

Date Range: January 1, 2006 – Present.
