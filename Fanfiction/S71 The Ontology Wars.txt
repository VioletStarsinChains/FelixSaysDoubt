Season 71, Episode 1 — The Catalog

Cold Open — Canyon Floor
The morning sun glints off the cracked-open cache. Crates, jars, and strange tools are spread across blankets and reed mats. The group kneels in a loose circle, each person handling one piece at a time with deliberate care.

Alex (steady, practical):
“No rushing. If empire hid it, there’s a reason. We learn it before we use it.”

Title Card: The Catalog

⸻

Act I — The Sorting

Jonathan opens his journal, making precise sketches of each object. Peter kneels nearby, testing small devices against his palm.

Jonathan: "Seeds, alloys, instruments. Layers of empire’s intent.”
Peter (frowning):
“Not just intent. These were prototypes. Abandoned ones.”

Kellyn takes the sealed jars of seeds, tapping the glass, listening for rot.
Kellyn: "Still dry. Still viable. Might grow better than anything we’ve scavenged.”

Gary picks up a length of coiled wire and grins nervously.
Gary: “Or shock us into the afterlife. Guess I’ll find out!"

⸻

Act II — First Experiments

Alex kneels by the fork-hammer Peter found, striking it gently against a stone. The vibration hums low, resonating in bone but not in air.

Alex (thoughtful): "It doesn’t carry sound. It carries through us.”

Kellyn: “Then we test it carefully — one strike at a time.”

Kellyn cracks a packet of the ancient grain, grinding a pinch into flour between stones. They add water, shaping a paste, setting it near the fire to see if it holds.

Peter powers the dead console with a salvaged coil. Lights flicker briefly, glyphs flashing and fading.

Peter (low, almost to himself):
“It still remembers. Not empire’s map anymore. Ours, if we learn its language.”

⸻

Act III — The Trial Meal

The flour disk cooks over the embers. Its smell is faint, earthy, not rancid. The group passes tiny bites, each chewing slowly, waiting for reaction.

Gary breaks the silence with a grin:
Gary: “Tastes like apocalypse flatbread.”

They laugh — brief, but real. No sickness follows.

Jonathan notes: “Grain holds. Seeds likely hold too. We’ve just expanded our future.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant Ledger

At dusk, the group gathers around a flat stone, marking everything found into a “covenant ledger.” Objects are divided: safe, unknown, dangerous.

Kellyn writes aloud as they agree:
“Safe: grain, seeds, rope coils. Unknown: tuning forks, alloy plates. Dangerous: half the rest until proven otherwise.”

Alex watches the canyon shadows, voice low: “Empire didn’t just lose this cache. They abandoned it. Which means… something about it failed them.”

Kellyn (steady): “Then it might serve us better than it ever served them.”

⸻

Closing Image
The canyon floor glows orange with firelight. Objects from the cache lie arranged in neat lines, each tagged with a mark of chalk or yarn. The group eats the last scraps of flatbread, shoulders touching, as Estrella dozes nearby.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We catalogued their relics not as empire’s heirs, but as covenant’s keepers. For the first time in a long time, we weren’t scavenging. We were choosing what to carry forward.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 71, Episode 2 — The Resonance

Cold Open — Canyon Camp
Morning. The group wakes to the cache’s objects glinting in sunlight. Peter kneels near the fire, tapping one of the alloy plates with a stick. The tone it emits is eerie, drawn out — more felt than heard. The sheep stir uneasily.

Peter (half-smiling, uneasy):
“Guess that goes in the ‘dangerous until proven otherwise’ pile.”

Title Card: The Resonance

⸻

Act I — The Fork-Hammers

Alex and Jonathan crouch beside a flat boulder. They strike two fork-hammers against it at the same time. The vibrations don’t cancel — they weave, producing a low thrum that seems to pulse in their chests.

Jonathan (noting carefully):
“They’re not weapons. They’re tuning tools.”
Alex (grim):
“Tuning us. Not just the air.”

Kellyn places a reed mat between the forks and their hands. The thrum softens.
Kellyn:
“Then we can buffer it. Bend it toward covenant instead of empire.”

⸻

Act II — The Console’s Whisper

Peter hooks the console to a coil and small solar plate, coaxing another flicker of glyphs.
Peter (focused, murmuring):
“It’s not just a map. It’s reactive. It listens for inputs. Watch.”

He hums softly. The glyphs tremble, as if aligning with his tone.

Gary (eyes wide):
“So it’s karaoke with consequences?”

Peter (without looking up):
“More like a choir. One wrong note, the whole harmony collapses.”

⸻

Act III — Safe Trials

Kellyn suggests small, safe experiments. They arrange objects in three circles: vibration tools, seed packets, alloy plates. Each person picks one, observing and reporting.
	•	Jonathan taps a fork-hammer near the seed jars — the hum strengthens the germination cracks in their shells.
	•	Peter drips water across an alloy plate — droplets spread and evaporate faster than normal, suggesting heat conduction or memory in the metal.
	•	Gary holds a coil near the bird drone. Its wings twitch, unbidden.
	•	Kellyn hums near the console, and for a moment, a crude map-shape stabilizes.

Alex watches each, jaw tight.
Alex (low):
“Every tool is an invitation. Empire used them to bind. We have to choose to unbind.”

⸻

Act IV — First Resonance

At dusk, they gather again. Peter and Kellyn hum together while Jonathan holds a fork-hammer close. The console glyphs shift, forming not empire’s grid but a fluid circle.

Jonathan (eyes widening):
“That’s not their pattern. That’s ours.”

Peter (half-whisper):
“So we just… keep singing?”

Alex:
“Until it holds.”

The circle flickers, then fades — but the memory of it remains, as if etched in their bodies.

⸻

Closing Image
The fire burns low. The cache’s objects lie scattered but no longer foreign. The group sits in half-shadow, voices tired but steady, humming faintly — weaving resonance not to control, but to steady.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire left us tools to fracture. We turned them to frequency. Each strike, each note, each hum — not a chain, but a thread. And threads, woven long enough, might yet unbind the world.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 71, Episode 3 — The Backlash

Cold Open — Plateau Dusk
The canyon wind carries a strange static hum, different from before. Estrella jerks her head toward the horizon. The group stiffens.

Jonathan (low, urgent):
“They heard us. Empire’s listening back.”

Title Card: The Backlash

⸻

Act I — The Omen

The bird drone spirals out of the sky, wings glitching. Gary catches it, holding it close as its sensors pulse erratic light.

Gary (grimly joking): "Well, that’s one Yelp review we didn’t want.”

Alex: “No — it’s a triangulation pulse. They’re marking our position.”

Peter draws his knife instinctively. Jonathan scans the cache’s tools, then the canyon walls.

Jonathan: "We don’t fight with their weapons. We use what we built.”

⸻

Act II — The Encroaching Sound

The air vibrates with a low-frequency rumble, like the earth itself groaning. Kellyn presses palms to the ground — the signal is bleeding through stone.

Kellyn: "It’s not just sound. It’s pressure. They’re trying to collapse us inward.”

Jonathan sets fork-hammers into the dirt in a counter-rhythm. The vibration dulls slightly, but the strain is visible on his face.

Jonathan (hoarse):
“They’ll keep ramping it until we crack.”

⸻

Act III — Improvised Defenses

The group scrambles:
	•	Kellyn stretches reed mats with ash filling across the shelter floor.
	•	Peter lashes alloy plates to the sheep’s and donkey’s sides as makeshift shielding.
	•	Gary rigs the coils to create feedback, tossing sparks into the dust.
	•	Jonathan & Alex coordinate a counter-hum, vibrating fork-hammers until their hands tremble.

The oppressive rumble falters — not gone, but pushed back.

Peter (sweating, steadying his breath):
“They can’t hold the field steady if we keep breaking rhythm. They want unison. We give them dissonance.”

⸻

Act IV — The Rift

Night falls. A jagged beam of light splits the canyon ridge — a scout-drone projection, scanning. The crowned figure’s voice booms faintly, warped through machinery:

Crowned Figure (echoing, distorted):
“You sing like rebels. But every note is already written in my score.”

Kellyn shouts up, voice sharp:
“Then why are you afraid of our dissonance?”

The projection wavers. The hum stutters. For a moment, the pressure lifts. But the silence that follows is heavier than sound — empire has retreated, but only to prepare worse.

⸻

Closing Image
The group sits ringed by scattered tools and reed mats, lit by fire and sparks. Above, the stars are clear — no projection, no hum — but the canyon holds the echo of empire’s presence.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We thought resonance was a whisper. Empire answered like thunder. But thunder fades — and the earth remembers dissonance longer than any score.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 71, Episode 4 — The Awakening

Cold Open — Shelter Interior, Dawn
The fire’s down to embers. Sam sits propped against the wall, pale but steadier now. Elon stirs under a heap of blankets, sweat soaking through. Above, Ashwin perches on a roof beam, eyes wide and unblinking. Her presence feels like a ward.

Elon’s lips part, voice ragged.
Elon (hoarse, rasping): “…not…Claire.”

Sam leans forward fast, relief and dread mingling.

Title Card: The Awakening

⸻

Act I — First Words

Elon’s eyes snap open. No more fever-blur, just raw confusion. He scans the small shelter until his gaze lands on Sam.

Elon (broken whisper): “Where… am I?”

Sam (gentle, steady):
“Colorado Plateau. You’ve been here for days. Fever. We carried you in.”

Ashwin ruffles her wings, a soft scrape of talons on wood. Sam offers Elon a clay cup of water. His hands tremble, but he drinks until he coughs.

Elon: “I thought I was still in the trap.”

⸻

Act II — Fragments of Memory

Sam steadies the cup, voice low.

Sam: “You lost a lot of blood. But you’re here now. Still breathing.”

Elon stares at the embers.
Elon (shaky): “I saw… signals, the crown. They told me to keep running, that I could outrun it. But I wasn’t moving at all. Just spinning.”

Ashwin drops lightly to the ground, close enough that Elon startles. The owl’s black eyes fix on him. The message is wordless, but clear: circles can break, too.

Elon lets out a fractured laugh, coughing again. Sam’s hand presses steady against his shoulder.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning

Elon’s tone sharpens.

Elon: “I wasn’t just dreaming. I heard echoes of myself. My own models bent back at me. Empire still has its claws in me. I don’t know if I can fight it.”

Sam frowns, jaw tight.
Sam: “Then maybe this is the time you stop fighting alone.”

Elon looks at him — weariness written deep in his face, but also a flicker of something else. Trust, maybe.

⸻

Act IV — The New Breath

Later, Sam helps Elon step outside. Ashwin circles above them, then lands on a nearby stone, silent sentinel. The sky is pale, thin light cutting through.

Elon (hoarse but steadier):
“It hurts. But… it feels real. The ground. The air.”

Sam:
“That’s all we need for now. Real enough to start again.”

Ashwin fans her wings once, as if sealing the moment.

⸻

Closing Image
Elon sits on a low stone outside the shelter, blanket wrapped tight. Ashwin perches on a post above him, chocolate-feathered and unblinking, like a living seal against the empire’s reach. Sam leans in the doorway, arms folded, watching both man and bird breathe steady in the morning light.

Narration (Sam's voice):
“Recovery does not arrive in triumph, but in fragments: a swallow of water, a step on bare earth, a gaze that does not falter. Even empire cannot unmake those.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 71, Episode 5 — Crossing Currents

Cold Open — Two Roads
In the shelter, Sam steadies Elon as he takes halting steps just outside the shelter, Ashwin circling above like a dark thread in the sky.

In the wilderness, Kellyn, Alex, Peter, Gary, and Jonathan trekking across wind-scoured ground, Estrella in tow, packs heavier now with cache supplies.

Title Card: Crossing Currents

⸻

Act I — First Steps

Shelter
Elon leans on Sam’s arm, blanket still wrapped tight. His voice is raw.

Elon: “Feels like walking on splintered glass.”

Sam (steady): “Then we’ll go slow. No races today.”

Ashwin swoops low, brushing the air with a wingbeat that steadies more than startles. 

Elon glances up and mutters:
“Feels like she knows I might fall.”

Trail
Jonathan adjusts the load of supplies.
Jonathan: “Cache was rich, but heavy. If empire’s watching, we’re walking like a signal flare.”

Alex glances at Kellyn.
Alex: “Then we’d better make the flare worth it.”

Kellyn (quiet, sure): “We always do.”

⸻

Act II — Echoes

Shelter
Elon sinks back onto a stone near the doorway.

Elon (low, to Sam): “I said things. When I burned. Delirious things. About Claire. About carrying the world. I don’t know what was mine and what was empire’s.”

Sam: “Doesn’t matter. What matters is what you choose now.”

Elon looks like he wants to laugh, but doesn’t.

Trail
Peter fumbles with the rope binding the sheep.

Peter (grumbling): “We’ve done too much carrying lately.”

Alex: “Then maybe it’s not about weight. Maybe it’s about balance.”

Kellyn (half-teasing): “That’s what the net taught us, remember?”

Peter exhales, not quite a smile, but the tension softens.

⸻

Act III — Shadows Press

Shelter
The wind rises. Ashwin fixes on the horizon, feathers puffed. Sam notices.
Sam: “She hears something.”
Elon stiffens, whispering:
“Empire doesn’t let its models wander.”

Trail
Jonathan pauses, hand raised.
Jonathan: “Did you feel that? Ground’s humming again.”

They all stop. The animals shift uneasily.

Kellyn: “Not empire’s hum this time. Something older.”

⸻

Act IV — Convergence

Shelter
Sam kneels by Elon, voice low but sure.
Sam: “Whatever’s coming, we’ll meet it here. You’re not alone.”

Ashwin drops from the sky, landing beside them like punctuation.

Trail
The group crests a rise. Far off, smoke rises faintly in the direction of the shelter. Not fire — more like a smear, a warning.

Alex (grim): “They’re already pressing the other side.”

Kellyn (quiet but steady): “Then we keep moving.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two scenes fold into one rhythm:
— Sam, Elon, and Ashwin braced against the wind at the shelter.

— Kellyn, Alex, Peter, Gary, and Jonathan pressing forward on the open trail.
Both groups tethered by distance, bound by covenant, bracing for whatever empire sends next.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We were scattered threads, drawn taut across the plateau. Yet every current, every breath, told the same truth: empire pressed in, and covenant would hold.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 71, Episode 6 — Threads Drawn Tight

Cold Open — Shelter Clearing
Ashwin circles above the plateau sky, dark feathers catching late sun. Below, Sam helps Elon toward the shelter door. Elon’s steps are uneven, but stronger than before.

Ashwin calls once — a sharp note that tilts Sam’s head toward the horizon.

Dust shapes move in the distance. The others are coming home.

Title Card: Threads Drawn Tight

⸻

Act I — First Sight

Kellyn, Alex, Peter, Gary, and Jonathan crest the ridge with Estrella. The group stops as Sam lifts a hand in greeting.

Kellyn breaks into a half-run, dropping their pack. Alex follows at a slower pace. Peter, Gary, and Jonathan hang back, caution in their posture.

Sam (calling out): “You made it back.”
Kellyn (breathless, relief breaking through): “So did you.”

Elon, leaning against the doorframe, smirks faintly.
Elon: “Barely.”

⸻

Act II — The Circle Closes

Inside the shelter, supplies are unshouldered, waterskins passed. Sam sets Elon back on his bedroll.

Jonathan (eyeing him): “You’re upright.”
Elon: “Mostly. It’s a start.”

Peter kneels by the firepit, coaxing flame. Kellyn sits across from Sam, eyes still tracking Elon like he might vanish.

Kellyn (quiet): “Feels like we’ve all been gone longer than we were.”
Sam (nodding): “Distance isn’t always measured in miles.”

⸻

Act III — Crossed Currents

Alex inventories the cache supplies, methodical, grounding himself. Peter sharpens his knife nearby, watching without speaking.

Sam leans back against the wall, voice low.

Sam: “The field shifted while you were gone. Ashwin caught it first — you can feel it even now. Empire’s pressure isn’t steady. It’s looking for weak points.”

Jonathan exchanges a glance with Kellyn.

Jonathan: “We felt it too, every step back.”

Kellyn (soft, almost to themself): “Like we’re being tuned. But not in harmony.”

Ashwin ruffles her feathers, echoing the unease.

⸻

Act IV — Binding Again

Night. A circle around the fire. Elon lies just behind them, half-dozing but awake enough to listen.

Kellyn looks around the group, voice steady but soft: “We can’t let distance split us again. Not by miles, not by fear. Tonight we’re here, all of us. That has to be enough to hold.”

Alex presses their hand against Kellyn’s. Peter watches, jaw tight, then nods once. Kellyn places their other hand on Peter's back. Jonathan exhales like he’s been holding the breath since the ridge.

Sam (quiet, resolute): “Then let’s hold it.”

Ashwin calls once, a deep note in the dark, like a seal on the vow.

⸻

Closing Image
Seven figures gathered in firelight: Kellyn, Alex, Peter, Jonathan, Sam, Elon, and Gary — the circle closed, threads drawn taut, not unbreakable but held by choice.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire wanted us scattered, unbound, broken into silence. But covenant wove us back, thread to thread. And for this night at least, the weave held.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 71, Episode 7 — Feastfire

Cold Open — Shelter Courtyard
The firepit glows brighter than it has in months. Pots simmer, spits turn, fragrant smoke rising into the night. The cache’s bounty — grains, dried meats, preserved fruits — spreads across improvised platters. The group gathers close, laughter already sparking at the edges.

Title Card: Feastfire

⸻

Act I — The Spread

Sam ladles steaming stew into bowls, moving carefully but stronger now. Elon watches, still pale but awake, grinning crookedly.

Elon: “You people actually know how to eat. I thought survival was just bark and boiled weeds.”

Alex (deadpan, stirring another pot): “Sometimes it is.”

Jonathan adds roasted roots to a flat stone, brushing ash away with precise fingers.

Jonathan: “Tonight, it isn’t.”

Kellyn tears bread into hunks, passing them around the circle. Ashwin swoops low, snatching a mouse from the grass, then settles on a branch to watch, silent guardian.

⸻

Act II — Stories in the Firelight

As bowls are emptied and refilled, voices loosen.

Gary tells a joke about selling imaginary luxuries at the end of the world, earning groans but easing the air. Jonathan hums a fragment of a melody, soft but steady, weaving through the crackle of fire. Sam leans forward, eyes bright in the glow.

Sam: “The taste of plenty — even for one night — is its own covenant. Empire can’t touch this. Not here, not now.”

Alex, quieter, nods once, eyes on the flames.

⸻

Act III — The Weight of Silence

Later, stomachs full, conversation dwindles into warmth and fatigue. Kellyn sits leaning against Alex at one side of the fire, Peter across with Jonathan, Sam leaning against a post, Elon lying back on folded blankets, eyes closed but listening.

The silence isn’t empty. It’s heavy with survival, with the memory of hunger and the shock of abundance. Every flicker of firelight catches on faces softened, shadows eased for once.

⸻

Act IV — Binding Through Plenty

Jonathan raises his cup.
Jonathan: “We’ve eaten together before. But never like this. Not in the open. Not with the weight eased, even for a night.”

Kellyn looks around, voice low but steady.
Kellyn: “Empire starves. Covenant feeds. Remember this.”

The others echo softly, not a chant but a murmur of agreement — a promise held through food and fire.

⸻

Closing Image
Bowls stacked, embers glowing low. The group leans in a rough circle around the fire — full, weary, silent — a rare moment when want is not the sharpest edge.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“For once, it wasn’t hunger that bound us, but fullness. Not absence, but presence. And the fire burned not just to survive, but to remind us we still could.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 71, Episode 8 — Room to Breathe

Cold Open — Shelter Clearing
Morning light spills over the plateau. Inside the shelter, elbows bump, someone sneezes, Ashwin flaps her wings in protest at the lack of space. Elon grumbles from his corner; Gary trips over a bundle of reed mats.

Kellyn pushes open the door, stepping into the bright air.
Kellyn (dry): “We need another building.”

Title Card: Room to Breathe

⸻

Act I — The Decision

The group trickles outside, rubbing eyes, carrying blankets and bowls.

Peter (grumbling, still stiff from sleep): “One more night like that and I’ll volunteer to sleep with the sheep.”

Sam (half-smiling): “We could all fit… if we were bones.”

Jonathan: “Kellyn’s right. We build.”

Alex exhales, already scanning the ground. “East side. Flat enough, close enough to keep watch, but not so close the walls crowd each other.”

⸻

Act II — Gathering

They split tasks. Peter and Jonathan cut poles. Alex and Kellyn weave reeds for wall mats. Sam, steadier now, hauls stones for a base. Gary trails with jokes and rope, muttering about “Colorado real estate, prime location.” Elon sits on a log, pale but trying to whittle pegs with a shaky hand.

Ashwin circles overhead, dropping down once to tug a stray strand of yarn like she’s supervising.

⸻

Act III — Raising Walls

By midday, the frame is up. Sweat darkens shirts, fingers raw with splinters. They work in rhythm — Peter bracing, Jonathan hammering, Alex lashing joins tight. Kellyn ducks inside, pressing a wall mat into place.

Sam steadies the base stones, voice low:
Sam: “Feels different than the last one. Like we’re not just surviving anymore.”

Kellyn (from inside, muffled): “We’re making space for living.”

⸻

Act IV — The Threshold

By sunset, the second shelter stands — rough, uneven, but strong. The group gathers in front, dusted with ash and sweat, shoulders aching. Elon leans heavily on a staff, managing a weak smile.

Jonathan brushes his palms, looking at the two buildings side by side.

Jonathan: “One fire. Two hearths. Still covenant.”

They nod, tired but satisfied.

⸻

Closing Image
Two shelters in the plateau light, smoke rising from a single fire between them. The group sprawled nearby, dirty and laughing — more space, but not divided.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire shrank the world to cages. Covenant opened it, wall by wall. And for the first time, we made room not just for survival — but for each other.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 71, Episode 9 — Threads of Quiet

Cold Open — New Shelter, Night
The new walls smell faintly of reeds and ash. The air is cooler, easier to breathe. A single lantern flickers low. Most of the group sleeps in the old shelter; Kellyn and Alex slip into the new one.

Kellyn spins in a circle, arms outstretched, then drops onto the reed mat floor with a giddy laugh.
Kellyn (softly): “I love privacy.”

Alex sits beside them, a faint smile tugging his mouth.

Title Card: Threads of Quiet

⸻

Act I — Settling

Kellyn lies back, arm over their eyes. Alex stretches out slowly, close but not touching. Kellyn lays a hand on his chest. The silence settles, not empty but steady.

Alex (low, almost embarrassed):
“I thought I might slap one of them a few times.”

Kellyn laughs.

⸻

Act II — The Touch

Kellyn reaches out, brushing his face. Alex startles, then rests his palm over theirs. 

Kellyn (whisper):
“I love them all.  But you're my rock.”

Alex nods once, throat tight, the lantern glow catching the damp at the corner of his eye.

⸻

Act III — The Breath

The night deepens. They lie side by side, pressed against each other. Breath slows, syncing without effort. Kellyn traces small circles on Alex’s wrist with one fingertip — grounding, not demanding.

Alex (hoarse):
“You always find a way to quiet me without silencing me. I never knew that was possible.”

Kellyn (gentle):
“That’s all I ever wanted. Not to be the target, not to be the shield. Just to be… with you.”

⸻

Act IV — The Thread

Alex turns, pressing his forehead lightly against Kellyn’s. They lie still like that, breath and warmth mingling.

Alex (whisper):
“Then let this be the thread. Not chains, not cages. Just this.”

Kellyn closes their eyes, leaning into the quiet.

⸻

The lantern burns nearly out, flame dim. Outside, faint noises of animals shifting, but here the silence is thick, uninterrupted. Kellyn stirs first, finding Alex still beside them on the reed mat, eyes closed but not asleep.

Kellyn (whisper):
“You’re listening again.”

Alex (without opening his eyes, hoarse):
“I always am.”

Alex finally opens his eyes, gaze searching Kellyn’s face like he’s trying to memorize it.
Kellyn (gentle tease):
“You’re going to wear holes in me, looking that hard.”

Alex shakes his head faintly.
Karp:
“No. I’m just… making sure you’re real. Still here. Not something I dreamed up.”

Kellyn shifts closer, pressing their forehead against his shoulder.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“Sometimes I think I made you up, too. All the years I felt you without knowing your name — it’s still strange to touch you and not wake up alone.”

Alex’s breath hitches. He turns slightly, pulling Kellyn in, arms trembling but firm.

Karp (whisper):
“I thought if I ever let myself hold you, it would shatter me. I never thought it could hold me together instead.”

Their breaths sync again, slower this time, weight pressed into the reed mat beneath them. Kellyn takes Alex’s hand, guiding it to rest over their own heartbeat.

Kellyn (quiet, almost command):
“Stop checking if I’m real. Feel it. That’s all the proof you need.”

Alex presses his palm firmer against their chest, eyes closing. A long silence follows, broken only by the sound of their breathing.

Dawn begins to seep through the cracks in the reed walls. Kellyn shifts to face him fully, palm cupping his jaw.

Kellyn (soft):
“If empire taught you to doubt touch, let covenant reteach you. Slowly. As many times as it takes.”

Alex leans into their hand, eyes wet but steady.
Karp (hoarse, certain):
“Then I’ll learn. With you. Every morning if I have to.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures on the reed mat floor, dawn touching their faces, Alex’s hand resting over Kellyn’s heart. Not a test, not a dream — but a covenant renewed in each breath.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“In empire, closeness was proof to be earned. In covenant, it was a rhythm — breath, warmth, heartbeat. Nothing to win, nothing to lose. Just the thread we chose to hold.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 71, Episode 10 — The First Meeting

Cold Open — Reed Mat Morning
The light has fully broken now, but the shelter is hushed, still heavy with sleep. Kellyn shifts onto one elbow, studying Alex’s face in the pale gold.

Kellyn (softly, curious):
“When you thought about me back then… when you imagined it… what did you think our first meeting would be like?”

Alex blinks, caught, his mouth tightening as if the answer itself is dangerous.

Title Card: The First Meeting

⸻

Act I — The Deflection

Karp (low, gruff):
“I didn’t. Not really. I thought it would never happen.”

Kellyn tilts their head, waiting.
Kellyn (teasing, steady):
“You’re lying. You always imagine everything. Even the things you claim you don’t.”

Alex exhales sharply, eyes sliding away like he wants to retreat. Kellyn doesn’t press harder — just rests their hand lightly over his wrist.

⸻

Act II — The Admission

Karp (hoarse, reluctant):
“I thought… if you ever saw me, really saw me, you’d recoil. That was the loop I lived in. I’d picture reaching out, and you’d turn your face like I was a ghost. Or a monster. Or nothing at all.”

He swallows.
Karp:
“And then I’d make it worse in my head. Imagine I forced the meeting. That you hated me for it. That you laughed. Or… pitied me.”

Kellyn’s brows furrow, but their touch doesn’t move from his wrist.

Kellyn tilts their head, waiting.
Kellyn (steady, pressing gently):
“No, you imagined it. You imagine everything. Not just the catastrophes. Tell me — what was it like in your head when it wasn’t a nightmare?”

Alex swallows, voice rough, the words coming like he’s peeling them from bone.

Karp (low):
“Sometimes… I pictured it quiet. No words, just you looking at me like I wasn’t a mistake. Like I was allowed to exist beside you.”

He pauses, breath shuddering, then forces himself on.

Karp (hoarse):
“Other times… I made it grand. Ridiculous, really. A room full of light — cameras, records, officials — everyone watching. And you walked in, and the whole crowd went silent. Not for me, but because you looked at me. Like nothing else existed.”

His jaw tightens; his hands twist in the blanket.

Karp (raw, bitter):
“I imagined you saying you’d been waiting for me. That you understood me before I ever spoke. That we were inevitable. And then the applause. Like empire itself had to admit I wasn’t wrong. That I’d found you, and that made me right.”

He shakes his head, shame flooding his features.

Karp (quieter):
“I think I built a hundred first meetings. All of them were… lies I wanted to believe. Crutches to hold the hunger.”

⸻

Act III — The Turning 

Kellyn lets Alex’s words hang for a moment, then tilts their head, voice calm but piercing.

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“You say you wanted empire’s applause. But why? Why did you think they would make our meeting matter?”

Alex flinches, shame rippling across his face. His throat works before sound emerges.

Karp (low, hoarse):
“Because I didn’t believe I was real unless they saw me. Unless they confirmed it. Every moment of my life, they fed me that lie — that worth was only what empire witnessed, measured, documented. Without their gaze, I was nothing. And if they applauded when I found you, then maybe it meant I existed. That we both did.”

Kellyn leans in closer, hand pressing firmer against his chest.

Kellyn (soft but sharp):
“They’ve been wrong about everything. About survival, about covenant, about me. Why would they be right about you?”

Alex’s breath catches. His eyes close, trembling.

Karp (whispering):
“I don’t know. I don’t know. All I know is, without their stage, I never believed I deserved one of my own.”

Kellyn shifts until their forehead rests against his, voice gentler now.

Kellyn:
“Then believe this stage. This ground. My eyes on you. That’s the only audience that matters now.”

Alex’s fingers clutch at Kellyn’s arm, desperate, almost childlike.

Karp (breaking, whisper):
“Then let me be real here. Just here.”

⸻

Kellyn and Alex sit close, wrapped in a blanket. Pale light filters through reed walls.

Alex’s voice is low, tentative, almost as though testing its weight.

Karp (soft):
“I don’t know how to be seen without armor. Even now, I want to turn it into theory. A model. Something I can control.”

Kellyn cups his jaw, steady.

Kellyn:
“Not this time. No models. Just breath. Just skin. Just being seen.”

Alex closes his eyes, trembling. His hands hover, not quite touching. Kellyn takes one and presses it firmly to their chest.

Kellyn (whispering):
“Here. Not empire. Not applause. Just me.”

His breath catches, chest rising under their hand.

Karp (hoarse):
“I keep waiting to disappear. To wake up and find it was all performance again.”

Kellyn (steady):
“Then let’s keep waking up together until you believe otherwise.”

⸻ 

The reed mats creak as they shift, Alex pulling Kellyn closer almost desperately.

Karp (raw):
“Every time I reached for you before, it was through empire’s wires. Their gaze. Their stage. I thought I was holding you, but I was only tightening chains.”

Kellyn threads their fingers through his hair, grounding.

Kellyn:
“You don’t have to chain me. You never did. I’m here because I want to be. Because this is the stage I choose.”

He exhales like something breaks loose inside.

Silence stretches — only breath and heartbeat. Alex tilts his forehead to Kellyn’s, eyes shining.

Karp (whispering):
“Then let me fail here. Let me be weak here. Let me be nothing — and still be yours.”

Kellyn (firm, tender):
“You don’t have to be nothing to be mine. But you can if you need to.  Just be. That’s enough.”

For the first time, Alex doesn’t argue. He only leans in, trembling against their steadiness.

As morning brightens, birds begin to call outside. The others stir faintly in the second shelter, but inside this one, the world is still narrowed to two.

Kellyn strokes the back of Alex’s neck, voice soft as prayer.

Kellyn:
“You wanted applause to make you real. But this — this is the only stage that ever mattered. No audience. No empire. Just covenant.”

Alex exhales, finally steady.

Karp (whispering):
“Then let me stay.”
