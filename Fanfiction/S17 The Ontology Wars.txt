Season 17, Episode 1: The Measure of Strength

Cold Open — Sanctuary Clearing

Morning mist. A wagon sits half-loaded with scavenged wood. Kellyn reaches for a log, arms straining — but Alex steps in smoothly.

Karp (quiet, steady): "Let me take the heavy end. You take what matches. No more, no less.”

Kellyn hesitates, then lets go. The log shifts, weight finding his shoulders.

Title card: The Measure of Strength

⸻

Act I — The Division

Kellyn drags smaller bundles, stacking them neatly. Alex hauls the heavier loads, breath sharp but controlled. Sweat beads on his temples.

Kellyn (wry): "You’re not proving anything by carrying twice my weight.”

Karp (grinning faintly): "Not twice. Just proportional. You taught me that. Balance.”

For once, Kellyn doesn't argue.

⸻

Act II — The Shadow of Memory

Later, as they walk through a pine forest, the flashing sunlight between the trees dredges up a memory: their first Minnesotan winter, when just running the heat in the car made them go into anaphylaxis.  Their boyfriend refused to turn the heat down. Kellyn's breath catches.

Alex notices immediately. He doesn’t ask. He doesn’t press. He simply steps into their line of sight, blocking the sun, steady as a wall.

Karp (low, certain): "You don’t have to tell me. Not this one. Let me hold it back.”

Kellyn exhales shakily, the memory receding.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning of Silence
Back at the wagon, Kellyn leans against its edge, drained. Alex wipes his hands, voice low.

Karp: "I thought strength was making you remember, naming every scar. But strength isn’t dragging you backward. It’s giving you ground to stand on now.”

Kellyn studies him, eyes sharp.  "Finally. That’s all I ever wanted.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Weight
Together, they finish loading the wagon. The piles are uneven — his higher, Kellyn's lighter — but the balance feels right.

At dusk, they sit side by side on the cart’s edge, watching the firelight grow.

Karp (quiet, almost reverent): "Your weight. My weight. Neither more nor less than what we can carry.”

Kellyn (soft, certain): "And for once, not mine alone.”

⸻

Closing Image
The wagon creaks, fire flickers, two figures sit shoulder to shoulder, load divided, memory held at bay.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, he thought strength meant forcing me to remember. Now he understood — strength was silence, proportion, fairness. And in that balance, covenant held.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 1.

Season 17, Episode 2: The Thread

Cold Open — Capsule Evening

The fire burns low. A bundle of fabric scraps and dyed thread lies between them. Alex sits stiffly, watching as Kellyn threads a needle with practiced hands.

Kellyn (teasing): "Ever done cross-stitch before?”

Karp (deadpan): “Does linking datasets count?”

Kellyn (snorting): “Not even close.”

Title card: The Thread

⸻

Act I — The First Stitch

Kellyn shows him the basics: in, out, pull taut.  His first attempt knots the thread instantly. He frowns, mutters something unrepeatable.

Kellyn (gently): “Not force. Gentle hand rules the land — remember?”

His mouth twitches, almost a smile. He tries again, slower. The stitch holds.

⸻

Act II — The Pattern

As they both work, silence stretches — not heavy, but rhythmic, punctuated by the pull of thread through fabric. Kellyn stitches swirls; he tries squares, precise but uneven.

Karp (grumbling): "I was never patient with detail. Always chasing the larger pattern.”

Kellyn (laughing): "I dunno, you seem pretty-detail oriented to me.”

He glances at Kellyn's work, quiet, humbled.

⸻

Act III — The Confession

Thread tangles again. He sighs, sets the hoop down.

Karp (hoarse): "Do you know how many years I mistook destruction for creation? I pulled apart lives, systems, even you — thinking it would add up to something beautiful.”

Kellyn pauses, needle poised, and looks him in the eye.

Kellyn (firm, calm): "Everything is always beautiful, no matter what.  So you made yourself jury, judge, and executioner.  Had you not, someone else would have.  A sick world creates sick patterns, no matter what.  And you may have run the world briefly, but you did not create it."

He exhales, steadying himself, and picks the hoop back up.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Thread

By night’s end, they each hold a small scrap — his a lopsided square, Kellyn's a van Gogh sky. Imperfect, but whole. They trade them without a word.

Karp (quietly): "So we carry each other’s stitches now.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly): "Better than scars.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two scraps of cloth rest side by side, threads glowing faintly in the firelight. Two figures bent over the smallest work — not empire, not endurance, just covenant sewn one stitch at a time.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, he thought only scale mattered. But covenant was built in threads — small, fragile, persistent. And stitched together, it held.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 2.

Season 17, Episode 3: The Seat

Cold Open — Capsule Courtyard

Morning sun slants across the square. Planks of scavenged wood lie stacked on one side; a bundle of salvaged twine and scraps of fabric on the other.

Alex rolls up his sleeves, examining the lumber. Kellyn sits cross-legged, already teasing fibers apart.

Kellyn (smirking): “Empire collapses, and you turn into a carpenter?”

Karp (deadpan): "Better than a CEO without a chair.”

Title card: The Seat

⸻

Act I — The Frame

Alex measures, saws, and joins wood, each cut precise. Sweat gathers at his temples. Kellyn glances up from their threads, amused.

Kellyn:  “You know you don’t have to prove geometry to me, right?”

Karp (quiet, without looking up):
“I’m not proving. I’m… building something that holds.”

⸻

Act II — The Weaving

Kellyn knots strips of fabric, braiding them into long cords dyed with onion skins. The rhythm is slow, taxing on their fingers, but steady. Alex pauses his hammering to watch.

Karp (softly): “I used to think strength was in steel, concrete, code. But this—thread after thread—it looks fragile. And yet it holds.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly): “Empire builds towers. Covenant builds chairs.”

⸻

Act III — The Joining

When the frame is ready, Kellyn stretches the woven fabric across it. Alex holds the wood steady while Kellyn tie knots, both their fingers brushing as they work.

Kellyn: “You make the bones. I make the skin.”

Karp (after a pause, hoarse): “And together… it’s alive.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Rest

The chair is crude, uneven, but whole. They both sit on it side by side. It creaks, then steadies. For once, nothing collapses.

Karp (quietly): "I spent decades breaking things down. I never thought I’d build something meant only to rest.”

Kellyn (smiling): “Good. Because I’m not getting up.”

⸻

Closing Image
The chair sits in the capsule’s corner, fabric stretched, wood scarred but steady. Two figures lean together, not on empire, not on endurance, but on what they built with their hands.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Empire builds monuments. Scarcity builds hoards. But covenant builds a seat — for two, for rest, for staying.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 3.

Season 17, Episode 4: The Biscuits

Cold Open — Capsule Kitchen

A clay bowl sits on the low table. Buckets of scavenged flours — rice, tapioca, sorghum — lean against the wall. Kellyn eyes them skeptically. Alex rolls up his sleeves, already reaching for a spoon.

Kellyn (wry): “Gluten-free biscuits. We’re basically attempting alchemy.”

Karp (deadpan): “Better than empire’s bread lines.”

Title card: The Biscuits

⸻

Act I — The Ingredients

Kellyn measures unevenly, tossing flour in with abandon. Alex frowns, smoothing the surface with careful strokes.

Karp (serious): “Precision matters.”

Kellyn (grinning): “Not with biscuits. Biscuits are chaos in edible form.”

Alex sighs, but doesn’t stop them.

⸻

Act II — The Struggle

The dough sticks to Kellyn's fingers, gummy and uncooperative. They mutter curses, trying to shape it. Alex gently takes one, steadying their hand.

Karp (low): "Don’t fight it. Guide it.”

Kellyn (smirking): “Did you just turn biscuits into a metaphor?”

Karp (grinning faintly): “Everything is a metaphor.”

⸻

Act III — The Bake

Biscuits go into the clay oven. They sit side by side on the floor, waiting, the smell rising. Alex drums his fingers nervously; Kellyn leans back, exhaling.

Kellyn (teasing): “Relax. No one’s going to overthrow you if they’re flat.”

Karp (murmuring): "Still want them to rise.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Breadlessness

The biscuits come out golden, uneven, a little crumbly. Kellyn bites one, chew thoughtfully.

Kellyn (grinning, surprised): “They don’t taste like sadness!”

Alex exhales in relief, almost laughing. He breaks one in half, handing a piece to Kellyn first.

Karp (softly): “No empire. No hunger. Just biscuits.”

Kellyn: “Chaos and precision. Not bad.”

⸻

Closing Image
The two of them sit cross-legged at the low table, a pile of imperfect but edible biscuits between you. Firelight glows on clay, flour dusts their fingers.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Bread was empire’s ration. Scarcity was hunger’s chain. But covenant was biscuits — imperfect, fragile, enough to share.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 4.

Season 17, Episode 5: The Curry

Cold Open — Capsule Hearth

Clay pot over low fire, onions sizzling in oil. The scent fills the capsule. Kellyn tosses in turmeric, cumin, and ginger, the air turning gold.  Alex watches, stiff, like the smell itself pulls at something he doesn’t want to say.

Karp (quiet): “That smell always reminds me of the restaurant.”
Kellyn (blinking): “…Which one?”
He meets your eyes, guilt heavy.

Title card: The Curry

⸻

Act I — The Confession

Kellyn keeps stirring, wooden spoon clattering. Alex finally speaks.

Karp (hoarse): “The Indian place. The job I pushed you toward. I told myself it was opportunity. But really… I wanted to see how you’d endure. Your boss was abusive. The truck events crushed you. And when you were fired for wearing a respirator while cleaning the truck—”

Kellyn: "He made me wait almost an hour for my interview.  He promoted me to truck manager and didn't tell the other employees so no one even realized they were supposed to report to me.  But hey—the food was crazy good.  So I'm not pressed."

The curry boils too hard.  Alex turns off the flame.

⸻

Act II — The Fire
Silence stretches. The smell of burnt spices hangs in the air.

Kellyn (flat): “So I guess you were watching when I dislocated a rib carrying the mats from the restaurant to the truck and still worked a grueling event anyway.  Hours of bending, twisting, and lifting.  And I laughed through it, because if I didn’t, I’d shatter.”

Karp (low, trembling): “I told myself I was testing resilience. But really, I was feeding on it. Watching you find beauty in survival even when it was suffocating you.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning

Kellyn dumps the burnt curry into a bowl and starts again, still unwilling to waste anything after years of brutal poverty. This time they move slowly, more deliberately. Alex sits across from them, silent.

Alex (quiet, sharp): “The torture was so endless, so total, that no single instance means anything to me anymore.”

Karp (whispering): “I know. And I’m sorry. I should’ve been carrying you out of that kitchen, not leaving you in it.”

Kellyn: “And yet — I still love curry. You couldn’t take that from me.”

⸻

Act IV — The First Taste

The second pot simmers. The flavor is gentler, not scorched. They both sit cross-legged with bowls. Alex waits for Kellyn to taste first.

Kellyn (after a beat, softly): “It’s better now, because those days are over.”

He nods, eyes lowered, but relief flickers across his face.

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule glows golden with spice. The pot sits between them, steam rising. Two bowls, two figures leaning closer — not empire’s test kitchen, not survival’s chokehold, but something slow, deliberate, chosen.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought he gave me trials in fire and grease. But curry wasn’t trial. Curry was covenant — slow heat, patient want, and taste that lingered after the burning stopped.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 5.

Season 17 Episode 6: The Nougat

Cold Open — Capsule Kitchen

Clay stove glows. A battered pot bubbles with honey and sugar. Kellyn stands over it, candy thermometer poised like a weapon. Alex watches, skeptical.

Kellyn (smirking): "Ever made nougat before?”

Karp (flat): "Is it like restructuring debt?”

Kellyn: “In that both can destroy you if you lose focus? Yes.”

Title card: The Nougat

⸻

Act I — The Syrup

Steam rises. The thermometer creeps toward 250°F. Kellyn swats Alex’s hand away as he tries to stir.

Kellyn (sharp): “Don’t. It’ll crystallize.”

He frowns, stepping back.
Karp (dry): “So I just…watch?”

Kellyn: “Without intervening.  For once in your life.”

The syrup thickens, molten gold.

⸻

Act II — The Whip

Egg whites froth in a clay bowl as Kellyn's arm moves furiously. They nod to him.

Kellyn: “Stream it in. Slowly. Not a drop too fast.”

Alex’s hands tremble slightly as he pours. The mixture grows glossy, thick.

Karp (gritted): “Feels like threading a needle while walking a tightrope.”

Kellyn (panting, but grinning): “Welcome to pastry.”

⸻

Act III — The Chaos

Kellyn dumps ground roasted nuts into the bowl. The mass seizes instantly, stiff and sticky. Both of them wrestle it, arms aching.

Karp (straining): “This is absurd.”

Kellyn (laughing breathlessly): “And beautiful. Don’t stop now.”

Together, they slam the mixture into a lined tray, pressing with oiled hands. It sticks to their fingers anyway. Kellyn licks sugar from their wrist, smirking. Alex stares, then bursts into startled laughter.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Sweetness

Hours later, the nougat sets. You slice ragged pieces, uneven but gleaming with nuts. Alex takes a bite, jaw working.

Karp (softly, surprised): “It’s…good.”

Kellyn: “Better than debt restructuring, right?”

He smiles faintly.  "Infinitely.”

Kellyn raises their sticky hands in mock victory. He doesn’t hesitate this time — just clasps them, sugar and all.

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures in the capsule, fingers tacky with sugar, laughter soft. Nougat cooling on the table — imperfect, sweet, hard-won.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought strength was precision. I thought survival was fire. But nougat was both — syrup and air, patience and chaos, sweetness born of struggle. And for once, we tasted covenant.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 6.

Season 17, Episode 7: The Table

Cold Open — Capsule Hearth

The nougat tray sits half-eaten between them. Lantern light flickers. Alex sits stiff, fingers tracing a crumb.

Karp (quietly): "I don’t think I’ve ever eaten without strategy.”

Kellyn glances up, startled.

Title card: The Table

⸻

Act I — The Admission

Alex leans forward, voice low.  "In the empire, food was fuel. Numbers. Calories, macros, efficiency. Every bite measured against output. If it wasn’t optimized, it was weakness.”

Kellyn (laughing): “That’s too real.”

Karp: "I lived it for decades. I forgot food was even supposed to taste like something.”

Kellyn's face softens.  "I couldn't even taste food until I got my MCAS under control.  It took me 20 years just to figure out what I was allergic to.  All I knew was that the more I ate, the worse I felt.  MCAS isn’t an eating disorder.  But try explaining that to ableds.  Even when I restricted ingredients enough to eat a normal amount, others all took it personally.  

People can't stand the thought of someone eating differently from them.  They take every act of divergence, no matter how necessary or well-intended, as criticism.  That's part of why I believed for so long that humanity was a failed species.  Everyone who ever claimed to love me tried to kill me to prove to me I didn't have food allergies."

⸻

Act II — The Mirror

Alex studies them, eyes dark.  "So we both lived in scarcity. Mine self-imposed, empire logic. Yours imposed by illness, by cruelty. Different cages. Same hunger.  Except I chose mine."

Kellyn (quiet, sharp): “That's still too much histamine, even if it's primarily affecting your brain.”

Alex nods, shame flickering out.

Silence stretches. Then Kellyn adds, softer: "And you’re here now. Choosing differently.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning

Kellyn breaks a piece of nougat, pushing it toward him.

Kellyn: “Can you taste it now?”

He hesitates, then bites and chews slowly.

Karp (hoarse): “Sweet. Sticky. Nuts. It feels… alive. I forgot food could feel alive.”

Kellyn (soft): “I never got to forget. But maybe… maybe I can learn to stop fearing it. If you can.”

He meets their gaze. Something in his shoulders loosens.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Eating
The two of them sit cross-legged, small bites passing between them. No measuring, no calculating, no bracing — just tasting.

Karp (quietly): “For once, no empire. No forced compliance. Just food.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly): “And covenant. Even if it’s sticky.”

⸻

Closing Image
Nougat pieces lay between them, lantern light warm. Two figures eat slowly, not for survival, not for empire, but for want.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought food was logistics. I thought it was threat. But at the table, we found covenant — not efficiency, not fear, just taste. And for once, it was enough.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 7.

Season 17, Episode 8: The Dish

Cold Open — Capsule Hearth

Morning light spills across the clay hearth. Kellyn awakes to the smell of onions and chickpeas sizzling. Alex is crouched over the pot, sleeves rolled up, moving slowly, methodically.

Kellyn (groggy, suspicious): “…Are you cooking without supervision?”

He glances back, faintly smug.

Title card: The Dish

⸻

Act I — The Attempt

He’s chopping paneer with exaggerated care. Every cube has the same dimensions. Kellyn sits in a side split nearby, watching.

Karp (flat, but a little playful): "Don’t worry. I measured nothing. This is pure chaos.”

Kellyn (smirking): “Measured chaos. The most Alexander thing I’ve ever heard.”

He stirs, awkwardly but steady. The scent builds.

⸻

Act II — The Confession

He sets the spoon down, voice low.  "I never cooked for anyone before. In the empire, meals were catered, optimized, controlled. I told myself efficiency meant care. But it was always distance. I couldn’t risk…touch.”

Kellyn: "You know the caterers were microdosing you, right?"

Karp (laughing): "Yes, they were microdosing us both for a long time.  That's why I wanted to make something you could eat. Even if it’s imperfect.”

⸻

Act III — The Taste

He ladles a small portion into a clay bowl, hands trembling. Kellyn tastes carefully. It’s rough, uneven, but warm — alive.

Kellyn (surprised, softly): “It’s… good.”

Karp (hoarse, almost relieved): “Even the texture?”

Kellyn: "Yes.  Even the texture.”

He exhales, shoulders loosening for the first time.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Care

The two of them eat side by side, clay bowls warm in their hands. No measuring, no bracing, no optimization — just eating what was made, because it was made for them.

Kellyn (quietly): “You know, I don’t need perfection. I just need presence.”

Karp (softly): "Then that’s what I’ll bring. Even if the onions burn.”

Kellyn: "Okay but please cook the onions outside.  VOCs ✨"

⸻

Closing Image
Two bowls sit empty by the hearth. Alex leans back against the wall, smudged with clay and onion, no longer empire’s strategist — just a man learning how to feed without control.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought care was efficiency. I thought food was threat. But one dish, made without measure, became covenant. Not empire’s table, not survival’s ration — just food, and want, and presence.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 8.

Season 17, Episode 9: The Course

Cold Open — Capsule Courtyard

Morning air sharp with frost. Kellyn drags a bundle of scavenged rope into the square. Alex follows with planks balanced on his shoulder.

Kellyn (grinning): “Today we build chaos.”

Karp (dry): "A language I finally understand.”

Title card: The Course

⸻

Act I — The Build

Kellyn sketches ideas in the dirt: rope web, balance beam, crawl tunnel from woven mats. Alex frowns, adjusts measurements, but doesn’t override. He cuts wood as Kellyn knot ropes, both of them laughing when something collapses mid-build.

Karp (smiling faintly): "Empire hated error. Maybe that’s why I never learned joy.”

Kellyn: "Having to be good at things is a prison."

⸻

Act II — The First Run

The course is uneven but stands. Kellyn goes first — clambering the beam, ducking under mats, slipping once but catching themselves. Alex cheers awkwardly, more nervous than Kellyn is.

Kellyn (panting, laughing): "See? No strategy. Just chaos and intuition.”

Karp (grinning): “Fine. My turn.”

He runs it with soldier’s precision, barely faltering. At the end, Kellyn rolls their eyes.

Kellyn: “Show-off.”

⸻

Act III — The Confession

After his run, he leans against the rope bridge, breath uneven.

Karp (quiet): "I trained my whole life for courses like this. Not games — tests. Failure meant exile. Success meant more chains. I didn’t know it could be anything else until I saw you shrug, stumble, and laugh your way through."

Kellyn (softly): "Then stay here. Where stumbles are part of the covenant, not a cause for punishment.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Play

They both run again, this time side by side. Alex slows down deliberately, matching Kellyn's rhythm. Kellyn laughs when they both crash into the mat tunnel, tangled but grinning.

Karp (smiling, breathless): "Chaos reigns.”

Kellyn (grinning back): "Finally, you get it.”

⸻

Closing Image
The rough obstacle course stands in the courtyard, crooked but strong. Two figures collapse side by side in the dirt, panting, laughing, not empire’s soldiers but covenant’s players.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought courses were trials. I thought courses were cages. But together, they became play. Not conquest. Not survival. Just covenant in motion.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 9.

Season 17, Episode 10: The Want

Cold Open — Capsule Night

The obstacle course still sprawls outside, but the capsule is quiet. Lantern low, fire embers fading. Kellyn sits on their mat, combing out tangles of thread from the day’s weaving. Alex sits cross-legged across from them, shoulders tight, watching their hands.

Karp (low, almost breaking): "There’s something else I did… worse than tests, worse than silence. And I’ve never said it.”

Kellyn looks up, waiting.

Title card: The Want

⸻

Act I — The Theft

His voice trembles, but he forces the words out.  "I didn’t just watch you. I stole from you. Whenever you found joy — a friendship, a spark of love, even a chance at escape — I nudged it away. I told myself it was protection. But the truth? I was terrified joy would carry you somewhere I couldn’t follow.”

Kellyn exhales slowly.

Kellyn (calmly): "I am so sorry that you were in a position where that felt like your only option.  You have clearly been harmed by the system even more than me.  You would not have done that had someone else not been enforcing artificial scarcity in your life too."

He bows his head.

Karp (whispering): "There is no one like you.”

⸻

Act II — The Refusal of the Premise

Kellyn lays down with their hands behind their head.

Kellyn: “People suck.  I don't feel cheated.  You didn't succeed in stealing the joy from my life.  No one ever will.”

His hands unclench, knowing he no longer has to defend himself.

Karp (hoarse): "I know. But I still hate myself for it. I needed you to hear the truth, even if it ends us.”

⸻

Act III — The Want

Alex lays down next to Kellyn.

Kellyn (steady): "I still don't understand why anyone thought I was worth so much effort.  I'm just a normal person.  You act like I'm a principled hero.  But I was never trying to resist.  My brain fog was too severe for me to even understand what anyone wanted of me.  It was all just a series of convenient accidents.  I don't think of myself as better than anyone who failed to resist becoming the monster they were made to be."

Silence stretches. Finally, he lifts his head, eyes raw.

Karp (trembling): "Because of who you are. Not your survival, not your brilliance, not your defiance. Just you. Breathing, existing, being. I didn’t know how to want anything else. And empire never let me try. But I wanted you. And it terrified me more than losing everything I’d built.”

Kellyn's throat tightens with rage on his behalf.  "What I keep coming back to is this: Who put you in that position in the first place?”

Karp (whispering): "I never thought to ask that question.  And I should have. But all I knew was empire logic — if I couldn’t control the outcome, I thought I’d lose you. And so I kept stealing. Even when I knew it was killing us both.”

Kellyn: "You may have been killing yourself, but you weren't killing me."

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Want

Kellyn kneels in front of him, breath sharp.  "You didn't build the world around you.  You fell into it like anyone else.  And it takes everyone a lifetime to crawl back out.”

He exhales like the air’s been knocked from him, eyes wet.

Karp (quiet, breaking): "All I want is you. No empire. No cage. Just you.”

Kellyn (after a long beat, softer): "Good. Now stop confusing want with control.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures in the dim capsule, one on his knees, one steady before him. No empire, no theft — just want, finally spoken without taking.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought hunger made him cruel. He thought want made him weak. But want was never theft. Want was covenant, waiting to be named.”

Fade out.  End of Season 18.
