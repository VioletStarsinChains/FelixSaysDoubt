Season 26, Episode 1: The Nest

Cold Open — Capsule Night
The fire has burned down to coals. The flogger is tucked away, the bowl of oil empty. Kellyn and Alex kneel side by side, folding blankets into a thick pile on the floor. Neither of them rushes.

Kellyn (quiet, matter-of-fact):
“Let’s make a nest.”

He nods, almost reverent, and keeps folding.

Title Card: The Nest

⸻

Act I — The Building
Layer by layer, you arrange blankets until the floor is soft and high. Pillows rim the edges. Alex follows Kellyn's lead.  The room is silent except for the rustle of fabric.

When it’s done, he sits back on his heels, watching Kellyn burrow into the pile.

Karp (soft, with a half-smile):
“I spent years engineering walls. And you build… nests.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly): “Walls keep people out. Nests keep people warm.”

⸻

Act II — The Touch
Kellyn lies back, pulling the blanket up to their chin. Alex hesitates, then stretches out beside them. His hand finds theirs under the covers, fingers tentative at first, then steady.

Kellyn (murmuring): “Warm hands are better than warm walls.”

He exhales, thumb brushing Kellyn's knuckles.

⸻

Act III — The Confession
The silence holds until he whispers: “I used to think care was provision. Food, shelter, protection. But this—blankets, touch, your head against my shoulder—this feels more like care than anything I ever built.”

Kellyn turns slightly, pressing their forehead against his chest.

Kellyn (quiet, steady): “That’s because empire never cared. It only calculated. Care is presence. No more, no less.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Rest
His arm tightens around Kellyn, his breathing slowing until it matches theirs. The capsule feels smaller, safer, the clay walls almost womb-like.

Kellyn (half-asleep): “Nests are temporary, you know.”

Karp (whispering, fierce):
“Then I’ll build another. As many as you need.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures curl under blankets, one head tucked against a chest, one arm wrapped around tight. The fire’s last glow fading, leaving only warmth and breath.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, walls were empire’s promise. But walls never kept me safe. Blankets did. Hands did. Nest after nest, as long as it takes.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 1.

Season 26, Episode 2: Clayfingers

Cold Open — Capsule Morning
The fire is cold ash. The nest of blankets remains, rumpled but warm. Kellyn and Alex are still half-wrapped in it, Kellyn's head on his chest. His eyes are closed, but his breath is light, not asleep.

Kellyn lifts their hand, letting it hover for a moment, then gently threads their fingers through Alex's hair.

He exhales sharply, eyes opening.

Karp (hoarse, startled): "You’ll find more gray than you expect.”

Kellyn (soft, teasing): “Memory made tangible.”

Title Card: Clayfingers

⸻

Act I — The Touch
Kellyn sifts slowly, their fingertips grazing scalp. His hair slips between their fingers, uneven but soft. He doesn’t move, just watches them with something like awe.

Karp (quietly): “No one’s ever touched me like this. Not without wanting something back.”

Kellyn (steady): “I don’t want anything back. I just want to make you feel good.”

⸻

Act II — The Confession
His eyes close again. Their fingers trace along the crown, the nape, lingering.

Karp (low, breaking): “I used to imagine this. Not sex, not power—just this. You, near enough that your hands could find me. And I hated myself for wanting it more than empire, more than survival.”

Kellyn pauses, palm resting against his temple.

Kellyn (softly): “Proof you were always still human underneath everything.”

⸻

Act III — The Shift
He swallows, trembling, then admits: "I’ve spent decades learning how to command. I don’t know how to receive.”

Kellyn resumes, combing gently.  "Proof of the violence inflicted upon you.  Then let this be your first lesson.  Close your eyes. Breathe.  Stay.”

He obeys. For once, he doesn’t try to fill the silence.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Fingers
Kellyn's hand slows, resting against his forehead. He covers it lightly with his own, not stopping, only holding it there.

Karp (whispering): “If this is covenant, I’ll take it. Strand by strand.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly): “Then don’t move. I’m not finished yet.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures lay in a rumpled nest of blankets, one lying still, eyes closed, the other combing fingers gently through gray hair. Not empire, not hunger, not test—just touch, quiet and alive.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought empire was everything. But covenant was this: hands moving slow through hair, breath soft against silence. Nothing asked. Nothing owed. Only presence.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 2.
