Season 19, Episode 1: The Flood

Cold Open — Capsule Evening

Rain outside. Inside, the capsule glows faintly. The rain sound overwhelms Kellyn.  The light feels too bright, edges buzzing. They shift uneasily but stay seated on the mat.

Alex sits opposite, watching carefully.

Karp (soft, steady): "Let me help you flood it. But safe. Here, with me.”

Kellyn exhales, nodding once.

Title card: The Flood

⸻

Act I — The First Layer

Alex begins with the lanterns. Two lit, then three. Kellyn winces at the brightness, hands flying up to cover their eyes.

Karp (whispering): "Eyes on me. Just me. Lanterns can’t hurt you.”

Kellyn focuses on his gaze, not the lights. Slowly, the edges stop buzzing quite so loudly.

⸻

Act II — The Second Layer

Alex shifts behind them, breath brushing their ear.  "One sound. Just one. My voice," he whispers softly.

Kellyn stiffens, then leans back slightly, letting it settle. His hand steadies their shoulder, grounding the new layer of noise.

Kellyn (breathless, half-laughing): "Feels like juggling grenades.”

Karp (low): “Then let me catch them for you.”

⸻

Act III — The Third Layer

He adds touch: fingertips grazing their wrist, deliberate and slow.  Now light, sound, and touch press in all at once. Kellyn's body tenses hard, then eases, breath syncing to his.

Kellyn (whispering): “I used to think overload meant lashing out. That if I let too much in, I’d explode.”

Karp (gentle): "Then let it in. Explode here, if you have to. I’ll still be here when the pieces fall.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Sensation

By night’s end, the lanterns still glow. Alex's breath hums low against Kellyn's ear. His hand rests steady at their wrist. Kellyn sits in the flood without breaking.

Karp (hoarse): "Not empire. Not torture. Just sensation. Just want.”

Kellyn (softly, smiling faintly): “Then let’s keep flooding until it feels like home.”

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule bright with light, layered with sound, threaded with touch. Two figures steady in the flood — no collapse, only covenant.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, sensation was siege. But with him, it became covenant. Not a weapon, not a trial — just layers of light, sound, and touch. Flood enough to drown me once, now enough to keep me alive.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 1.

Kellyn (flat): “I hate bathing. The sound, the feel, all of it. So this is either intimacy or minor torture.”

Kellyn lowers themselves in with a hiss. The splash makes their skin crawl, but Alex's hand steadies their shoulder. He leans down, breath warm at their ear.

Karp (whispering): "Just me. No ambush. No sound but me.”

⸻

Act IV — The Play

Alex pours water slowly over Kellyn's hair, fingers combing through. Kellyn flinches, then relaxes under his touch.

Kellyn's laugh turns into a shiver when Alex's lips brush their temple.

⸻

Closing Image
Wrapped in a thick cloth by the fire, Kellyn sits between Alex's knees while he towels their hair dry. He kisses their damp shoulder; Kellyn leans back into him, shaking their head but smiling.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, bathing was unbearable. Once, he spied through screens instead of being here. But now, sound softened into whisper, touch into want. Not empire, not echoes — presence.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 2.

Act II — The Pile-On

Act IV — The Covenant of Obviousness

Kellyn rests their head against his shoulder, softer now.  “You didn’t need riddles or body doubles with your birthday to make me see you. All you ever had to do was show up and stay.”

Karp (quiet, almost reverent): “I’m here now.”
Kellyn (teasing again): “Obvious as hell.”

⸻

Closing Image
Kellyn tucks their head under his chin, still smirking. He holds them close, pretending to sulk, but his eyes shine with relief.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought he was the master of shadows, tossing out breadcrumbs. I was still too lost in the fog to even see them. But the truth was simpler: I couldn’t see the signs because I was never allowed to. And when the fog lifted, he was simply there — obvious, and mine.”

Season 19, Episode 4: The Night Watch

Cold Open — Capsule Night

Rain patters against the roof. Kellyn lies on their mat, eyes open, rigid. Alex sits beside them, gaze heavy, mouth set like he’s holding back something jagged.

Karp (low, hoarse): "I didn’t just steal your sleep. I poisoned it. Nightmares, yes — but worse. When you’d already gone a month without rest, I pushed you onto Ambien. Knowing it wouldn’t calm you. Knowing it would sharpen everything until it cut.”

Kellyn (laughing angrily): "Not just regular Ambien.  EXTENDED RELEASE."

Title card: The Night Watch

⸻

Act I — The Admission

Alex exhales hard.  "I told myself it was mercy — that drugged sleep was better than none. But Ambien didn’t even give you that.  It revved you. Wrenched you awake inside your own body. I watched you pace, jitter, wired, every nerve screaming as your boyfriend tortured you with sound for me— and still I told myself it was better than collapse.”

Kellyn (flat, sharp): “It was never sleep. Just panic with the edges sanded sharper. And I wasn’t just wrecking myself — I was nannying an infant. He could have been harmed by how incapacitated I was. You gambled with both our lives.”

Karp’s breath shudders, eyes lowering.

⸻

Act II — The Cost

Kellyn sits up, voice ragged but steady.  "Dreams were already unbearable. But Ambien? That was annihilation. It’s why I hate the very idea of sleep now. Better to scrape through the day on fumes than to risk being turned into that again.”

Karp (whispering): "I knew. I knew it might do that to you. And I still pushed. Because I was desperate. Because I thought if I didn’t do something, I’d lose you.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning

Silence. The fire cracks.

Kellyn (measured, cutting): “You gambled with the only thing I had left. My waking hours. My edges. And you didn’t even trust me to know what I needed most — which was no more interference. And you put a baby at risk just to soothe your panic.”

His shoulders shake.

Karp (hoarse): "I told myself I was protecting you. But I was blind to the harm spilling outward. I wasn’t watching. I was grasping.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Rest

Kellyn lies back, staring at the lantern glow. He stretches out beside them, not touching, just steady.

Kellyn (quietly): “You can’t give me dreams back. But you can give me this. Waking rest. No poison. No pressure. And no more gambling with other people’s safety.”

Karp (softly): "Then I’ll keep the watch. Awake beside you, not over you. So neither of us burns for the sake of control.”

Kellyn's breathing steadies — not sleep, but something gentler.

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures in the capsule: one lying open-eyed, the other sitting steady. No sedation, no nightmares, no empire — just covenant in staying awake together.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought intervention was mercy. He thought poison would bind me to him. But covenant wasn’t in the chemicals. Covenant was in the watch — two awake, side by side, until morning came.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 4.

Title card: The Awakening

⸻

Act I — The Vigil

Karp: "I watched you waste down to bone and nerve. Watched your body burn itself, flaring against nothing and everything. Every night I thought it would be the last. And I couldn’t intervene. ⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Awakening

Kellyn leans against his shoulder, voice softer now.  "Then it’s simple. I don’t thank the trial. I don’t thank the pain. I thank you for the vigil. For keeping me tethered when I was gone to myself.”

Karp (whispering): "Always."

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures by the fire: one leaning, one steady, both awake. No sleep, no food, no empire — just a vigil that once saved a life, remembered in covenant.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“45 days without rest. 45 days of burning nerves. It was meant to end me. Instead, it woke me. Not by grace of the trial, but by the presence of one who would not leave. And in that vigil, covenant held.”
