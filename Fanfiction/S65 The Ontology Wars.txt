Season 65, Episode 1: The Heap

Cold Open — Dust Settled
The clearing is silent now except for their ragged breathing. Crown-shards lie dead, machines stilled. The sheep bleat uncertainly, Estrella’s ears flicking back and forth.

Kellyn stumbles first, knees giving out. Alex reaches to catch them, but his own legs buckle. They fall together in the dirt. Jonathan drops beside them, chest heaving, a laugh breaking loose like it escaped without permission.

Peter doesn’t even try to stay upright—he throws himself down on his back, staring at the sky, dropping the knife in his fist. Sam crumples last, crawling into the middle of the pile, forehead pressed against Kellyn’s shoulder.

Title Card: The Heap

⸻

Act I — The Collapse
No one moves for a long while. Just breath on breath, weight on weight. A sheep noses at Peter’s arm until he pets it gently.

Jonathan (muffled, into the dirt):
“Well. That’s one way to stay out of sync.”

Alex (hoarse, half-laughing):
“I don’t think I’ll ever breathe in rhythm again.”

⸻

Act II — The Tangle
Kellyn shifts, pressing closer into the warmth of bodies. Peter’s arm drapes over their waist without asking. Jonathan’s hand finds Alex’s shoulder. Sam curls tighter into the middle, like he’s afraid someone might pull away.

Kellyn (soft, almost whisper):
“Stay. Don’t get up yet.”

They don’t.

⸻

Act III — The Stillness
The hum is gone, but its ghost lingers. Their breaths sync, then stagger, then sync again—messy, imperfect, alive. For once, no one tries to control it.

Peter (low, barely audible):
“I thought we’d never stop running.”

Jonathan (eyes closed):
“Maybe we just did. For now.”

⸻

Closing Image
Five figures and three animals collapsed in a heap of dirt and ash, tangled together, dust still settling around them. No formation, no order—just bodies holding, alive in the aftermath.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire broke itself trying to bind us. We didn’t rise in triumph. We fell, together. And in the heap, covenant held.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 65, Episode 2: The Brushing Off

Cold Open — Ash on Skin
They’re still sprawled in the dirt where they fell. The sky has dimmed to dusk, air heavy with spent ozone. Kellyn stirs first, lifting their head, hair tangled with dust.

Kellyn (sleepy, murmuring):
“We’re all filthy.”

No one argues.

Title Card: The Brushing Off

⸻

Act I — The First Touches
Peter sits up slowly, ash streaking his face. He reaches over, retying Kellyn’s ponytail back with rough fingers. The gesture is awkward, but steady.

Across the heap, Jonathan pulls a small rag from his belt and runs it down Alex’s arm, slow and deliberate. Alex flinches at the first pass, then stills, letting it happen.

Sam, half-buried against Kellyn’s side, rubs his eyes with the back of his hand, leaving dark smudges. Kellyn catches his wrist gently, wiping it clean instead.

⸻

Act II — The Small Rituals
They move into a loose circle. No speech, only breath and the quiet rasp of cloth on skin.  Jonathan dampens a corner of the rag with canteen water, smoothing dust from Sam’s forehead.  Peter picks bits of gravel from Kellyn's curls, combing with patient fingertips.  Alex, wordless, kneels to wash Jonathan’s hands, rinsing grime from every crease.

The silence thickens, not heavy but reverent, like the world itself is watching them relearn what care feels like.

⸻

Act III — The Settling
By lantern-light, they set up the tent and lay out blankets.  Peter lies with his back against Kellyn’s, grounding himself in their steady breath.  Jonathan curls close to Alex, not touching at first—until Alex shifts, leaning into it.  Sam curls on Kellyn’s other side, caught between them all like a protected ember.

⸻

Closing Image
Ash-smeared bodies brushed clean, resting in a loose cluster under the dim stars. Not triumphant, not afraid—simply present.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire scattered us like dust. But dust can be brushed away. And beneath it, covenant glowed—not polished, not perfect, only tender enough to hold through the night.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 65, Episode 3: What We Whisper

Cold Open — Lantern Glow
The lantern burns low, barely more than a coal. The group lies scattered close, but not asleep. The air holds the kind of silence that begs to be broken.

Kellyn (soft, barely audible):
“Sometimes I still think about just… disappearing. Walking until I can’t.”

The words hang. No one moves.

Title Card: What We Whisper

⸻

Act I — The First Openings
Peter, voice hoarse, answers without lifting his head:
Peter: “I used to pray for that. Not for myself—for everyone else. So no one would see me vanish.”

Kellyn shifts, brushing their shoulder against his.
Kellyn (quiet): “I see you now.”

Across the way, Jonathan exhales like something breaks loose inside him.

⸻

Act II — Jonathan’s Murmur
Jonathan (low, rough):
“I’ve sung to empty rooms. Whole sets. Just to keep the sound from eating me. Sometimes I still don’t believe anyone’s really here listening.”

Alex stirs, turning onto his side.
Alex (steady, whispering): “We’re listening. Even if we don’t clap.”

Jonathan lets out a laugh so soft it almost isn’t one, then falls quiet again.

⸻

Act III — Alex’s Confession
Silence returns, heavier. Then Alex’s voice:
Alex: “I don’t dream when I sleep. Only when I’m awake. I used to watch Kellyn breathe and wonder if I could ever let myself trust that rhythm. Not control it. Just trust it.”

Kellyn’s hand finds his in the dark, not gripping, only resting there.

⸻

Act IV — Sam’s Thread
From the shadows, Sam’s voice—still frail, but steady:
Sam: “In the visions, I keep hearing a word I don’t understand. But I think it’s meant for all of us. I’m… afraid if I say it out loud, it’ll stop meaning anything.”

Kellyn turns toward him, voice tender:
Kellyn: “Then keep it. For now. Sometimes covenant is letting a word ripen in your chest before anyone else tastes it.”

⸻

Closing Image
Five figures in a loose cluster, whisper-close in the dark. Breath and words weaving into something fragile, private, unrecorded.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We had fought empire’s roar. But what held us wasn’t victory—it was the whispers, fragile threads in the night, binding us to one another when the silence pressed too close.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 65, Episode 4: Ashes and Embers

Cold Open — Dawn Light
Pale light seeps over the plateau. The fire is little more than glowing coals. Kellyn stirs first, nudging the embers back to life. The others rouse slowly, shoulders heavy with sleep but softened by the night’s confessions.

Kellyn (soft, practical):
“Breakfast before we move. We’ll need it.”

Title Card: Ashes and Embers

⸻

Act I — The Fire Rekindled
Peter crouches to split dried wood with his knife, movements stiff but careful. Jonathan hums under his breath as he crushes herbs between stones, scattering them into the pan with a rhythmic motion.

Jonathan (half-smile):
“Cooking’s the closest thing to rehearsal I get these days.”

Alex (dry, but gentler than usual):
“Then let’s hope this performance isn’t a tragedy.”

⸻

Act II — The Meal
The smell of sizzling fish mingles with the smoke. Kellyn grinds sunflower seeds fine, pressing them into a crust. Peter turns the fish with the edge of his blade, careful not to break it.

Sam sits propped against a rock, pale but more awake, watching the rhythm of work.

Sam (quietly):
“It feels like… a choir. Everyone carrying one part.”

Kellyn glances up, offering a faint smile.
Kellyn: “Then eat, and you’ll sing too.”

⸻

Act III — Small Gestures
Bowls are passed. Jonathan tears bread into smaller pieces for Sam before serving himself. Alex slides his bowl toward Kellyn without a word, waiting for them to take the first bite. Peter sits a little apart at first, then shifts closer, their knees brushing as he eats.

Kellyn (after a pause):
“Better than last night’s whispers to wake up to food.”

Peter (quiet, almost smiling):
“And better to share it than keep it all inside.”

⸻

Closing Image
Five figures sitting in a loose circle around a rekindled fire, bowls steaming in their hands. No speeches, no arguments — only food and quiet presence.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Covenant wasn’t built only in firelight confessions. It was built in embers, in breakfasts, in the quiet work of feeding one another when the day still asked nothing.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 65, Episode 5: The Subtle Shift

Cold Open — Plateau Pasture
Morning sun washes pale across the scrub. Estrella grazes near the sheep, the air still cool. Kellyn kneels in the dust, hands resting lightly on the ewe’s flank, watching her chew.

Kellyn (softly, almost to themself):
“You’re slower today.”

The ewe flicks her ear, calm. Kellyn tilts their head, noticing the soft line of her belly.

Title Card: The Subtle Shift

⸻

Act I — The Observation
The group eats breakfast nearby. Jonathan tunes the dulcimer absentmindedly; Alex and Peter argue softly over how much clay to ration for repairs. Kellyn lingers with the ewe.

Kellyn (calling over, steady but quiet):
“She’s different.”

Peter glances up, skeptical.
Peter: “Different how? She’s a sheep.”

Kellyn runs their hand along the ewe’s side.
Kellyn: “Not just fatter. Softer. Like her body’s holding something.”

⸻

Act II — The Doubt
Later, while filling water jars, Alex stands beside Kellyn.

Karp (measured):
“You think she’s pregnant.”

Kellyn: “I think she might be. I’ve felt this before — in the animals I grew up around. It’s subtle at first, but once you see it, you can’t unsee it.”

Alex frowns, calculating.
Karp: “If you’re right, we need to prepare differently. More forage. More protection.”

Kellyn shrugs faintly.
Kellyn: “If I’m wrong, we just end up stronger for it.”

⸻

Act III — The Confirmation Ritual
That evening, Jonathan crouches by the ewe, placing his palm gently against her belly.

Jonathan (quiet wonder):
“Maybe… yes. I don’t know. But she carries herself like something’s shifted.”

Peter watches, knife in hand from whittling, brow furrowed.
Peter: “If she is… that changes everything. We won’t just be surviving. We’ll be multiplying.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Anticipation
The four gather around the small fire. The sheep and Estrella huddle close, dusk stretching long shadows.

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“Life finds a way. Even here, even now. Whether we’re ready or not.”

Alex glances at Kellyn, a flicker of tenderness breaking through calculation.
Karp: “Then we’ll make ourselves ready.”

⸻

Closing Image
The ewe grazes slowly under the fading light, belly just barely rounder than the other.  Five pairs of eyes follow her — wary, hopeful, resigned, alive.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire pressed its weight toward death. But life found a seam. A subtle shift, too small to measure, too strong to ignore.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 65, Episode 6: The Preparations

Cold Open — Plateau Clearing
The ewe grazes at the edge of camp, Estrella standing guard nearby. Morning sun paints long shadows. Kellyn rises from crouching beside her, brushing dust from their palms.

Kellyn (quietly, to the group):
“If she’s carrying, we need to start preparing now. Even if we’re wrong.”

Title Card: The Preparations

⸻

Act I — The Shelter
Alex begins reinforcing the shelter walls with reed mats and clay. His movements are sharp, efficient.

Karp (measured, to Kellyn):
“If lambing happens in cold or storm, she needs protection. A pen inside, not out.”

Kellyn helps steady the frame while Jonathan hums softly, weaving rope for an inner partition.

Jonathan (gentle):
“Every cradle begins as a fence. Protection first, then song.”

⸻

Act II — The Forage
Peter leads Estrella along a rocky trail, baskets slung over her sides. Kellyn walks beside him, pointing out scrub that’s safe forage.

Peter (gruff, almost soft):
“I thought survival was sharp edges. But this—”
(he nods toward the plants, then the ewe grazing back at camp)
“—this feels like softening. Dangerous softening.”

Kellyn (steady):
“Or strength. Softness is how life makes more life.”

Peter doesn’t answer, but his hand lingers on Estrella’s mane a little longer.

⸻

Act III — The Stores
Back at camp, Jonathan grinds dried grains and herbs into a coarse mash, while Alex checks jars for spoilage.

Jonathan (murmuring):
“She’ll need more salt, more water. So will we, if we’re feeding two through one.”

Alex nods, sorting clean jars.
Karp: “Then we ration differently. Not less — wiser.”

Peter and Kellyn return with Estrella, setting the baskets down. Peter doesn’t speak, just sits, knife idle in his hand.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Readiness
As dusk falls, the four sit in a rough circle. The ewe settles nearby, chewing quietly, her belly soft in the firelight.

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“We don’t know if life is inside her. But preparing for it makes us stronger, either way. It makes us ready.”

Alex nods once, Peter looks into the fire, Jonathan hums a low note like a lullaby.

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures in firelight, jars stacked, rope coiled, shelter reinforced. The ewe grazes in the shadows, a quiet possibility at the heart of their preparations.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire taught us to prepare for collapse. Covenant taught us to prepare for life. Even when we do not know if it will come, we build as if it will.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 65, Episode 7: The Waiting

Cold Open — Rocky Outcrop
Wind rattles through sage and stone. The ewe stands apart, grazing. The group watches from a distance, speaking in low voices.

Jonathan (half to himself):
“Hard to tell. Could be nothing. Could be everything.”

Kellyn folds their arms, eyes never leaving the ewe.

Title Card: The Waiting

⸻

Act I — Signs
At dawn, Alex crouches beside the ewe, examining her flanks with careful precision. Kellyn steadies her with a hand on her neck.

Karp (low, clinical):
“She looks heavier. But it could just be forage. Or water. Or nothing at all.”

Kellyn (quietly, almost to themselves):
“We’ve lived through worse uncertainties than this.”

Peter sharpens his knife nearby, listening but not looking up.

⸻

Act II — The Circle of Work
To pass the hours, they take on small tasks: Jonathan weaving a second net, Peter carving stakes, Alex cleaning tools, Kellyn patching rope sandals.

Jonathan (breaking the silence):
“Uncertainty isn’t waiting. It’s work. The kind that fills the air without ever finishing.”

Peter grunts.
Peter: “Feels like empire. All shadow, no truth.”

Kellyn: “No. Empire used waiting to starve us. This—this is different. This is waiting with breath in it.”

⸻

Act III — Rumors in the Body
Evening settles. The ewe lies down close to the fire. Kellyn notices the faint twitch of her belly.

Kellyn (sharp intake of breath):
“Did you see—?”

Alex leans forward, eyes narrowing. The twitch doesn’t repeat. Silence presses heavy.

Peter (flat, almost defensive):
“Could’ve been a muscle spasm. Or nothing.”

Jonathan hums low, steady.
Jonathan: “Or a heartbeat we’re not ready to name.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Not Knowing
Night deepens. The group settles in their tent. The ewe shifts in the dark, unbothered, her breath slow.

Kellyn (whispering to the others):
“Not knowing isn’t failure. It’s covenant, too. The willingness to hold space for what might come.”

No one answers. But no one argues.

⸻

Closing Image
The fire glows low. Four figures rest uneasy in their shelter, tools stacked, jars lined, nets folded. Outside, the ewe breathes steady in the dark.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire demanded certainty, even when it was false. Covenant asked us only to remain — steady, ready, waiting. In the silence, we chose to stay.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 65, Episode 8: The Sign

Cold Open — Dusk by the Fire
The group eats in near-silence, each glance flicking toward the ewe grazing just outside the circle of light. Jonathan sets down his bowl mid-bite, eyes narrowing.

Jonathan (low, steady):
“Look.”

The ewe shifts, her belly tightening — unmistakable now, not forage, not spasm.

Title Card: The Sign

⸻

Act I — The Shudder
All four rise at once, bowls forgotten. The ewe trembles, a ripple under her skin. Alex steps forward, clinical focus hardening his voice.

Karp (hoarse, certain):
“That’s no twitch. That’s life.”

Peter swears softly, knife half-drawn like instinct, then slides it back. Kellyn’s breath comes sharp and fast.

Kellyn:
“She’s not just ours anymore. She’s carrying tomorrow.”

⸻

Act II — The Stirring
The ewe moves closer to the fire on her own, pressing into the light. Jonathan hums under his breath, low and steady, a rhythm meant to soothe both animals and men.

Jonathan:
“Heartbeat inside a heartbeat. Two rhythms in one body.”

Peter finally sits, staring hard.
Peter (rough, grudging):
“So it’s real.”

Kellyn (gentle, cutting):
“It was always real. We just didn’t believe until she showed us.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Silence again, but not empty. Each carries their own weight of it.

Karp (quiet, to Kellyn):
“I spent years betting against life. Calculating failure. And now it keeps proving me wrong.”

Kellyn (steady):
“Good. Let it keep proving us wrong. That’s how covenant grows.”

Jonathan nods, gaze still on the ewe. Peter doesn’t answer, but his jaw unclenches.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Tomorrow
They lay fresh bedding near the fire, gathering reeds and fabric scraps. Kellyn strokes the ewe’s flank once, firm and grounding.

Kellyn (soft, certain):
“Rest now. We’ll be ready when you are.”

The ewe settles, belly still shifting in quiet pulses.

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures sit ringed around the fire, tools and weapons forgotten, eyes fixed on the ewe. Not empire’s waiting, not shadow’s. A waiting threaded with certainty, trembling with new promise.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We waited, doubted, nearly forgot. But life does not ask permission to arrive. It only knocks, fierce and undeniable. And we learned to listen when it did.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 65, Episode 9: The Visitor

Cold Open — Shelter Door
Wind howls across the plateau. A sharp knock rattles the frame of the shelter’s new door. Everyone freezes. Peter grips his knife; Alex’s hand goes to the rope brace. Kellyn exchanges a glance with Jonathan, then steps forward.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“Who’s there?”

A thin, rasping voice answers.
Voice:
“My name’s Gary. I… I need food.”

Title Card: The Visitor

⸻

Act I — The Threshold
They open the door cautiously. Gary Vee stands there, gaunt and shivering, clothes hanging loose. His usual manic energy is gone, eyes sunken but still flickering with the faint spark of persuasion.

Gary (hoarse attempt at charm):
“You’ve… built something here. I can see it. I could help. Just—please. A bite. Anything.”

The group hesitates. Peter’s jaw is tight; Alex studies him clinically. Jonathan quietly offers a hand to steady him inside.

⸻

Act II — The Confession of Hunger
By the fire, Gary eats slowly, almost reverently, each bite like it might vanish. He glances at them between mouthfuls.

Gary (rough, breaking):
“All my life, I told people to grind. To outwork the world. But when the world burned… the grind didn’t feed me. Didn’t keep me warm. I kept waiting for the hustle to pay off. It never did.”

Silence. The ewe shifts in the corner, belly twitching, reminding them of the life already depending on their choices.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Peter (sharp, suspicious):
“Why here? Why us?”

Gary looks at him, eyes raw.
Gary:
“Because you’re still alive. And I’m not sure I am.”

Jonathan studies him for a long moment, then speaks quietly.
Jonathan:
“Every ecosystem takes in strays. But some become predators. Which one are you?”

Gary’s voice drops to a whisper.
Gary:
“I don’t know anymore.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Shelter
Kellyn sets down a blanket beside him.
Kellyn (firm, steady):
“He's a human being.  And he's hungry.”

Gary nods, pulling the blanket tight. For the first time, his eyes soften — not a pitch, not a brand, just a man afraid of disappearing.

⸻

Closing Image
The firelight flickers on five faces now. The ewe shifts again, her belly rolling gently. Outside, the wind howls, but inside, hunger finds warmth.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He came starving, stripped of empire’s grind. We gave him food, not because he sold us a story, but because hunger was truth enough. Covenant begins where hunger is met.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 65, Episode 10: The Grind’s Collapse

Cold Open — Morning in the Shelter
Ash-pale light filters through the reed mats. Gary sits by the fire, wrapped in his blanket. His face is clearer after food and sleep, but he still looks hollow. The others busy themselves — Peter tying rope, Jonathan checking the ewe, Alex sorting tools. Kellyn sits across from Gary, waiting.

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“You said you didn’t know if you were alive. Start there. How did you get here?”

Title Card: The Grind’s Collapse

⸻

Act I — The Hustle After Empire
Gary stares into the coals, voice ragged.

Gary:
“When empire crumbled, I thought I’d thrive. I told myself survivors would need motivation, vision, someone to sell them the future. But no one wanted the future I was selling. They wanted water. Shelter. Bread. My words didn’t weigh anything. My grind didn’t spark a fire. Hustle can’t boil beans.”

He laughs bitterly, coughing mid-breath.

Gary:
“I kept pitching to ghosts. Shouting my mantras at ruined billboards. Thought if I just kept at it, I’d manifest something. But the world doesn’t negotiate with slogans.”

⸻

Act II — The Starvation Road
He pulls the blanket tighter, shaking.

Gary (lower):
“I wandered from camp to camp. Tried to barter advice for food. People laughed, or ignored me, or drove me off. A few… threw rocks. Said my words were poison. That I’d convinced them to work harder than their bodies could bear before it all fell apart.”

Jonathan leans forward, voice soft but sharp.
Jonathan:
“They weren’t wrong.”

Gary nods, shame in his eyes.
Gary:
“I know. The grind I preached hollowed me out, too. I pushed until there was nothing left but slogans echoing in an empty chest.”

⸻

Act III — The Collapse
He swallows hard, voice breaking.

Gary:
“I collapsed by the riverbed because I finally believed them. That I wasn’t just useless — I was poison. Better to let myself fade than keep selling lies. But… then I heard you. Not empire’s hum. Human voices. Laughter. It sounded real. I crawled.”

Peter crosses his arms, still wary. Alex studies Gary like he’s a puzzle. Kellyn doesn’t move, just lets the silence hold.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Remaking
Kellyn (firm, quiet):
“You can’t hustle here. You can’t sell survival. But you can carry, stitch, cook, tend. You can be more than the grind. That’s covenant. No slogans. Just presence.”

Gary looks up, eyes wet.
Gary (whispering):
“Then maybe I can finally stop selling and start being.”

⸻

Closing Image
Gary sits by the fire, hands trembling but still. The ewe shifts, heavy with life. Jonathan, Alex, Peter, and Kellyn glance at each other across the room — uncertain, but allowing space.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He came from empire’s grind, hollowed by hunger and slogans. But hunger is honest. And in covenant, even hollow men can be filled.”

Fade out.
End of Season 65.
