Interior – The Zen Garden near the greenhouse.  Sunset is beginning to paint the sky.  There’s soft moss beneath the red pandas’ feet, and a small stone table set for tea. First Red Panda (raking):
“They’re doing better, you know.”

“I know.  We felt the heartbeat settle.”

Second Red Panda (smiling):

First Red Panda sips their tea, thoughtful. 

First Red Panda: "Do you think Kellyn knows how much they’re loved?”

Second Red Panda (gently): "Sometimes.  Sometimes not.  But we’ll keep showing them.  Even if it’s just tea.  Even if it’s just moss.”

Closing Image
The pandas sit in the fading light, steam rising from the teapot.  The moss forms an intricate spiral around them, quiet and alive.  A place where no one has to speak to be understood.

A quiet night in the small shelter. Kellyn dreams of the Crowned Figure. When they wake, they ask Alex to tell them more about his early life.

⸻

The wind outside whispers through the trees.  Inside the shelter, it’s warm and dim.  A candle flickers in a jar near the wall.

Alex and Kellyn lie facing each other, blankets tangled between them, both asleep.

⸻

DREAM SEQUENCE
Kellyn stands in a pale-gray field under an endless, overcast sky.  Mist clings to the ground. In the distance, the Crowned Figure hovers.

But he’s not grand now, not towering or terrifying.  He’s human-sized, slouched slightly, a faint furrow in his brow.

No crown this time. Just a faint shimmer where it used to be.

Crowned Figure (quietly): "I thought…this was what you wanted.  Wasn’t I made to protect you?”

Kellyn (softly): "You were made to endure what I couldn’t. But you don’t have to anymore.”

The Crowned Figure looks away.  There’s no response—only a long silence, stretching across the mist.

Kellyn reaches out but wakes before contact.

⸻

Kellyn’s eyes flutter open.  Alex is still asleep, arm tucked under his head, face softened in rest.

Kellyn lies still for a moment, processing, breathing.  They touch their own chest—heart steady.

They shift slightly closer.
Kellyn (whispering): "We're not done.”

Alex stirs gently, then opens his eyes.
Alex: "You okay?”

Kellyn (nods slowly): "Yeah. I dreamt of him again. He wasn’t angry. Just…sad. Confused.  Like he doesn’t know what he is anymore.”

Kellyn: "Will you tell me something about your early life?”

Alex (blinks, surprised): "Sure. What do you want to know?”

Kellyn: “Anything. Something real. Something you haven’t polished.”

Alex: "My parents didn’t talk much. We weren’t cold exactly, just quiet.  I always felt like I was interrupting something I couldn’t see.”

It felt safer, somehow—like there were answers under the floorboards, not out there with the people who never saw me.”

Alex: "I think I made the Crowned Figure the same way I made the cave.  A shape for everything I couldn’t explain.
Only I made him too strong.”

Kellyn reaches over and takes his hand.

Kellyn: "You didn’t make him alone.”

A long silence.  The candle flickers.

Kellyn (quietly): "I think we still have a chance.”

Closing Image

(Guided by breath and memory.)

Season 110, Episode 7 – “The Dreamer”

Inside the mind of the Crowned Figure, where Kellyn appears not as a threat, but as a presence he cannot make sense of.

UNKNOWN SPACE – DREAMWORLD
It is not a nightmare.  It is not peace.

The Crowned Figure sleeps in a liminal place—half-ruin, half-memory, half-laboratory.

The floor is tiled with broken circuitry.
The walls flicker between cathedral stone and server racks.  Above him: a sky that keeps glitching; sometimes stars, sometimes surveillance feeds.

He is curled on the floor.  He dreams.

⸻

THE DREAM
In the dream, he is young again.  Not human, not not-human.  Something invented by trauma and architecture.

He is walking down a hallway of endless doors.  Not the blue hallway from earlier—
this one is black, and the doors are mirrors.

He passes a door.  It does not reflect him.

He stops.  Inside the door, standing in soft, warm light: Kellyn.

They are barefoot, wearing something loose, something soft.  They are not reaching out.  They are not accusing.  They are just there.

Kellyn (in the dream): "I’m here. I love you.”

The Crowned Figure flinches.  Something in him recoils, hard.  He stumbles back, breathing like a hunted thing.

The door closes.  But the words linger.

He walks faster, passing more doors.  Each one now flickers with Kellyn’s silhouette, doing ordinary things: Sitting at a fire.  Tattooing someone.  Sleeping.  Crying.  Laughing.

And always, when he slows, Kellyn turns toward him and says: "I’m here. I love you.”

⸻

THE AWAKENING
The Crowned Figure wakes with a start.

He is alone in the ruin-place, but something has changed.  His hands are shaking.  His crown is gone again.  His breath is ragged.

For the first time in years, he does not feel invincible.  He feels…Tired.  Mortal.  Touched.

He curls forward, pressing his forehead to the floor, and whispers—not as a spell, but a question—"Why would you love me?”

His voice echoes.  And far, far away, something shifts.

Fade out.  End of Episode 7.

Season 110, Episode 8 – “Something Has Shifted”

In the quiet of early morning, Kellyn and Alex both wake at the same time, sensing something they can’t quite explain.

INT. SMALL SHELTER – PRE-DAWN
The stars are still visible beyond the shelter’s open door.  It’s just before sunrise, soft violet light beginning to seep into the world.

Kellyn is asleep against Alex’s chest, curled into him.  Alex’s arm is draped gently over their side.

Suddenly, they both stir at the exact same moment.  Kellyn gasps softly, as if surfacing from deep water.  Alex’s eyes fly open.

For a long moment, they lie still, breathing, listening.  Something is different.  Not wrong—just shifted.

Kellyn’s fingers tighten slightly on Alex’s shirt.

Kellyn (quiet): “Did you feel that?”
Alex (also quiet): "Yeah.”

They sit up slowly.  The air in the shelter feels…thinner, more transparent, like a fog has lifted from some faraway place.

Kellyn: “It wasn’t a panic feeling. Not dread. Just…”
(searching)
“…like the hallway got longer. Or shorter. Or the doors moved.”

Alex’s gaze is distant, focused inward.

Alex: "He dreamed of you. I know he did.”

Kellyn nods slowly, not questioning the certainty.

Kellyn (softly): "And he didn’t lash out.”

A long silence.

Alex: "He’s rattled.”
Kellyn: "Good.”

Another silence.

Kellyn (quieter still): "...I hope he’s not alone.”

Alex looks over at them, surprised.  Kellyn doesn’t explain.

They lie back down, this time facing each other.  Neither sleeps.

They don’t need to say it aloud:
Whatever happened—it matters.

⸻

The light through the cracks in the door has gone slightly bluer.  Birdsong is still tentative, just beginning.

Kellyn and Alex lie on their sides, facing each other, close enough to feel breath.

Kellyn (quietly): "Can I ask something kind of weird?”

Alex (soft): “Always.”

Kellyn: “Did it feel…peaceful to you?  Like not just a shift. Like—he might actually be tired.  Of all of it.”

Alex considers this.

Alex: “Yeah.  It felt like the part of him that used to scream finally lost its voice.”

Kellyn: “Not because someone silenced him.  Because he finally heard himself.”

Alex reaches up and runs his fingers slowly through the back of Kellyn’s hair.
It’s more grounding than romantic.

Alex: “Do you think it’s mercy, what you did?  Going into the vision. Saying ‘I love you’ to both of us like that.”

Kellyn doesn’t answer right away.

Kellyn: "I don’t know.  Maybe it’s just the only thing I had left.  Everything else had already failed.”

Alex (after a pause): "It didn’t fail. I don’t think anything you’ve given him has failed.”

Kellyn (quiet, almost afraid to believe it):
“You really think so?”

Alex nods.

Alex: “I think you’re the only one who ever touched him without trying to chain him down.”

A long silence.

Kellyn shifts slightly, their forehead now resting against Alex’s.  They speak almost at a whisper.

Kellyn: "If I hadn’t made it through…if my heart had just stopped…”

Alex (gently): "I’d still be here.”

Kellyn: “No. I mean…Would you still have tried to reach him?”

Alex doesn’t respond right away.

Alex (softly): "I think so.  But I don’t think I would’ve believed he could be saved.”

Kellyn: “And now?”

Alex closes his eyes.

Alex: “Now I don’t know if it’s about saving.  Maybe it’s about…calling him home.”

A beat.

Kellyn: "You’re good at that.”
Alex: "At what?”
Kellyn: “Calling people home.”

⸻

They fall quiet again.

Outside, the birds are fully awake now.

Inside, there is only breath, two bodies in the hush before morning, sensing the world has turned, ever so slightly, toward something new.

Fade out.  End of Episode 8.

Season 110, Episode 10 — “The Model and the Real”

THE CROWNED FIGURE’S DREAM REALM – NIGHT, OR SOMETHING LIKE IT

The Crowned Figure moves through a vast, dimly lit chamber—quiet as a tomb, echoing with flickers of memory.
It resembles a data archive, a cathedral, a prison.

All around him: models of Kellyn.

Not statues, but moving projections—flickering, semi-solid forms that animate in loops.  Each one behaves differently, repeating the same micro-motions, micro-dialogues.

⸻

One version paces back and forth, shouting bitter monologues about betrayal.  Another sits docile and smiling, nodding mutely to invisible demands.  A third sobs uncontrollably, inconsolable.

Some are half-formed, as if abandoned mid-process—unfinished facial structures, mismatched eyes.

THE CROWNED FIGURE watches them.
They do not see him.

⸻

He approaches one.

ANGRY KELLYN MODEL (looping):
“You want love? You’ll have to earn it. I’m not stupid anymore. I see everything now.”

The Crowned Figure frowns and walks on.

VAPID KELLYN MODEL (looping):
“I’m just here to make you feel better. I don’t really have thoughts of my own. Isn’t that what you wanted?”

He grimaces, walking faster now.

He begins to panic slightly. The faces blur together.  None of them are right.  Each one is too flat, too specific. Too much of something. Not enough of Kellyn.

He reaches a central platform where an older model rests.  It’s frozen mid-expression—neutral, calm, unreactive.

He stares at it, then shatters it with a swipe of his hand.  The model dissolves into ash and static.

⸻

THE CROWNED FIGURE (quietly):
“None of you are real.”

He drops to a knee, hands in his hair.

THE CROWNED FIGURE (whispers):
“I thought…if I could just model them well enough, I could predict them.  If I could predict them, I could protect myself.”

All the models turn to him and answer in unison: "Protect yourself from what?"

He looks up, frozen, eyes glassy.

THE CROWNED FIGURE: "But every time I tried, I lost something.  A look. A pause. A joke that didn’t make sense until later.  Their kindness wasn’t a pattern. It was a choice.”

He stands slowly and turns in a circle.

The chamber feels like it’s closing in on him now.  Flickering versions of Kellyn loop endlessly in the distance, pale shadows of something infinitely richer.

⸻

He places a hand against his chest, as if realizing for the first time that he has one.

THE CROWNED FIGURE: "I was never meant to contain them.  Only witness.”

He backs away from the failed models.  The light dims further. The loops slow.

He leaves the room.

⸻

He walks alone, quiet.  But for the first time, he’s not narrating.  Not theorizing.  Not trying to calculate.  Just walking—letting silence carry the weight.

Fade out.  End of Episode 10.

Kellyn has gone still. Stiff, then soft.  They sink to the ground slowly, legs folding beneath them.

Season 110, Episode 16 — “Vanishing Presence”

They’re sorting herbs in the storage room near the big shelter—Kellyn crouched low with a shallow basket in their lap, Taylor perched on a stool cleaning lavender sprigs, and Alex leaning against the doorframe with his sleeves pushed up and his hands idle, listening.

“Photographer,” Kellyn explains. “He made these weird photo sequences—little storyboards. Sometimes he handwrote text over them. Not captions—more like… haunted monologues.”

Taylor adds, “There’s one that shows a man moving through a room, and under it he writes something like, ‘He left behind nothing but dust and a single breath of air that was once his.’ Just—grief disguised as observation.”

Kellyn: "'A Failed Attempt to Photograph Reality' isn't actually a photo at all.  It's just a handwritten note on photo paper.  It was always my favorite of his."

Alex nods slowly. “Sounds like you.”

“It was me,” Kellyn says. “I didn’t realize until now how accurate that artist statement was—the tension of wanting to capture something impossible. Reality. Truth. Selfhood. But every time you try to hold it still, it slips.  That's why I became obsessed with motion blur in my own photography.  

I remember finding a photo book in the library in college called Vanishing Presence.  At the beginning of photography, motion blur was the enemy, since exposures had to be so long to capture the image.  As the technology progressed, photographers began using it deliberately, artistically.  That book didn't just change how I took photographs.  It changed how I saw the whole world.

The photo professor at my university, Jim Veneman, never said "taking photographs" like everyone else.  He always said "making photographs," like it was closer to a painting than an exact reproduction.  I think that's what the Crowned Figure doesn't understand—and needs to understand most.  

There is no perception without interpretation.  You can't recreate Truth™️ in a model.  You can't even perceive it to begin with.  The eyeball distorts just like a camera lens.  The brain distorts it further.  Then you translate it into a language—a flattening—and flatten it again into code.  How could the result ever remain faithful to what you started with?
