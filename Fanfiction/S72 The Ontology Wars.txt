Season 72, Episode 1: The Morning Table

Cold Open — Plateau Morning
The sun climbs over red stone. Smoke curls from a rekindled fire. Kellyn and Alex step out of their shelter, shoulders brushing, carrying a reed basket of dried grain and a clay pot of water. The others — Peter, Jonathan, Sam, Gary, and Elon — are already gathering near the firepit.

Peter (gruff, but softened):
“About time. Thought you two were going to keep all the warmth to yourselves.”

Kellyn smirks, settling by the fire.

Title Card: The Morning Table

⸻

Act I — The Cooking
Jonathan and Sam set up a flat stone for griddling. Gary stirs soaked grain with a carved spoon, humming badly on purpose until Elon groans.

Elon (raspy, still weak):
“God, even empire wasn’t that cruel.”

Gary (mock wounded):
“Don’t worry, I’ll autotune it for the deluxe edition.”

Laughter breaks the morning fog. Alex crouches beside Kellyn, hands moving with quiet precision as they help Peter salt the mix of fish scraps, grains, and wild greens.

⸻

Act II — The Eating
Plates are passed — clay bowls chipped but steady. Everyone sits in a loose circle, knees brushing. The food is plain, hearty.

Sam (low, thoughtful):
“First meal since the cache that feels like we earned it.”

Jonathan (nodding):
“Not empire’s rations. Not scarcity’s scraps. Just us.”

Kellyn catches Alex’s eye across the circle; his nod is small but certain.

⸻

Act III — The Thread of Tension
Silence falls briefly, broken only by chewing. Peter stares into his bowl, jaw tight. Finally, he mutters:

Peter:
“Strange, isn’t it? Sitting here, eating like… like nothing’s chasing us.”

Alex looks at him, sharp, but Kellyn speaks first.

Kellyn (quiet):
“Not nothing. But not everything either. That’s what covenant makes. A pause. A table.”

Peter exhales, shoulders easing. He lifts his bowl in a small, almost awkward toast.

Peter:
“Then here’s to the table.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Morning
Bowls clink gently together. For a long while, no one speaks. They just eat — slowly, steadily, as if every bite is proof.

Gary (breaking the silence, mock grand):
“Look at us. Apocalypse Iron Chef Champions.”

Jonathan (deadpan):
“Only if we survive until lunch.”

A ripple of laughter moves around the circle, light but real.

⸻

Closing Image
Seven figures around a small fire on the plateau, bowls in hand, the morning sun catching smoke and steam. A makeshift family, fragile but held together by food, silence, and covenant.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire made tables into ledgers, measuring every bite. But here, morning made a table into covenant. Not to erase the hunger or the fear, but to share them — and in sharing, soften their weight.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 72, Episode 2: The First Furniture

Cold Open — Plateau Midday
The fire has burned down to coals. Empty bowls are stacked aside. Kellyn, Alex, Peter, Jonathan, Sam, Gary, and Elon stand in the half-cleared shelter, glancing at the rough stone walls and packed clay floor.

Kellyn (smiling faintly):
“We can’t keep eating on our knees forever.”

Peter picks up a straight branch, hefting it.

Peter:
“Then let’s build something worth sitting on.”

Title Card: The First Furniture

⸻

Act I — The Gathering
The group scatters into the scrubland, each carrying rope, tools, or baskets. Jonathan identifies sturdy branches; Alex inspects for balance and tension. Kellyn and Peter strip bark, fingers quick. Gary hums work songs, half-mocking, half-sincere, while Elon limps after them, collecting lighter twigs.

Elon (rasping):
“Even empire’s thrones started with splinters.”

⸻

Act II — The Shaping
Back at the shelter, the woodpile grows. Knives and stone tools shape edges. Ropes twist into lashings. Kellyn and Alex work side by side, anchoring a low table. Peter and Jonathan test the legs of a bench by dropping their weight onto it, laughing when it wobbles but holds.

Sam traces simple carvings into a seat’s edge — not decoration, but markers: lines for each of them, crossing into a knot.

⸻

Act III — The Assembly
The shelter fills with motion: ropes tightening, wood creaking. Kellyn and Elon argue cheerfully over stool heights. Sam steadies the frame of the table as Alex lashes it firm, their hands brushing. Jonathan rubs ash into the carved knot, darkening the lines.

When the last stool is set, the group steps back. The pieces are plain, uneven — but solid.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Use
They sit. Knees bent, elbows resting, bodies easing into new balance. The table holds bowls filled with leftover grain and herbs.

Kellyn (softly):
“Not empire’s furniture. Not monuments. Just wood and rope. Ours.”

Peter leans back, tired but almost smiling.

Peter:
“Stronger than it looks.”

Alex (quiet, firm):
“So are we.”

⸻

Closing Image
The camera lingers on the rough table and benches in the dim shelter, smoke curling past the doorway. Not polished, not perfect — but the first signs of permanence on the plateau.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire built thrones to tower. We built stools to sit side by side. A table not for power, but for covenant. Rough wood, rough hands — and the weight of presence made solid.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 72, Episode 3: The Hut on the Plateau

Cold Open — Night Restlessness
The new beds creak with shifting bodies. Most are asleep, but Ashwin stirs, wings flicking restlessly in the firelight. She lets out a low, deliberate cry — not hunting, not warning. Calling.

Kellyn sits up, rubbing their eyes.
Kellyn (whispering):
“…You want us to follow.”

Title Card: The Hut on the Plateau

⸻

Act I — The Departure
At dawn, Ashwin swoops and circles until the group gathers. She darts ahead, then waits until they begin walking. The land opens wide — mesas, scrub, dust. The path is unfamiliar. Even Jonathan, who’s been mapping carefully, frowns.

Jonathan:
“This isn’t on any of my sketches.”

The owl presses forward. They follow.

⸻

Act II — The Approach
After hours of steady walking, the plateau dips into a shallow canyon. A faint curl of smoke rises. At its center: a small hut of stone and woven branches, its door hung with sun-bleached bones and rattling shells.

Peter tightens his grip on his knife.
Peter: “Looks abandoned.”

But as they draw closer, the door creaks. 

A figure steps out:
An old woman, bent but steady, wrapped in patched shawls. Her eyes are clouded, but sharp enough to rest directly on Ashwin.

Old Woman (rasping, but amused):
“Took you long enough.”

⸻

Act III — The Encounter
The group freezes. Sam shifts uneasily. Elon mutters under his breath.
Kellyn steps forward first, careful.

Kellyn: "You were expecting us?”

Old Woman (smiling faintly):
“I was expecting her.”
She gestures at Ashwin, who flutters onto the hut’s roof, calm as stone.

The inside of the hut is cramped, filled with jars of dried herbs, bundles of feathers, carved stones etched with spirals and sigils. It smells of smoke and resin.

She tells them she’s lived here “since before the hum grew teeth.” She knows its pulse, its dangers, its hungers. She claims the plateau is threaded with old paths empire has tried to bury — and that Ashwin was once part of her flock.

⸻

Act IV — The Gift
Before the group leaves, she presses something into Kellyn’s hand: a small stone etched with a spiral.

Old Woman:
“When the hum presses too close, throw this into the ground. It won’t break it. But it will make it stumble.”

Ashwin circles once more above her, then returns to the group, wings catching the evening light.

⸻

Closing Image
The hut dwindles behind them as they walk back across the plateau. Ashwin flies just ahead, guiding them home. In Kellyn’s palm, the spiral stone glows faintly in the last red light of day.

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 72, Episode 4: The Spiral and the Shadow

Cold Open — The Walk Back
The plateau stretches wide in muted dawn colors. The hut is already far behind them. Kellyn keeps glancing at the stone in their palm, its spiral faintly catching the light. Ashwin swoops ahead, silent now.

Sam (quiet, uneasy):
“She knew Ashwin. Like… really knew Ashwin.”

Gary (half-joking, half-serious):
“Yeah, well, owls are shady like that. Don’t tell her I said that.”
Ashwin glances back as if she did hear.

Title Card: The Spiral and the Shadow

⸻

Act I — The Stone
Around the fire that night, the group takes turns studying the spiral stone. Its surface is smooth, but the etched lines almost hum against the fingertips.

Jonathan (analytical):
“It’s not natural wear. Too precise. She made it — or something made it for her.”

Alex watches silently until Kellyn presses it into his hand. His jaw tightens as he feels the weight.

Alex: "This isn’t just a trinket. It’s a fragment of the same field we’ve been unbinding.”

The others lean in closer, wary but fascinated.

⸻

Act II — Processing the Encounter
The conversation shifts back to the old woman.

Peter (still tense):
“She knew too much. About the hum. About Ashwin. About us.”

Jonathan:
“Or maybe she’s just been surviving longer than we have. Think about it — living alone in a hut, building defenses with scraps and bones. That’s not weakness.”

Sam (flat):
“Or it’s empire letting her live so she can deliver their messages.”

Silence. The idea lands heavy.

Kellyn (steady, cutting through):
“She didn’t just give us a message. She gave us a weapon.”

Kellyn lifts the spiral stone again.

⸻

Act III — Divergence
The debate sharpens.  Alex insists the stone is worth testing.  Peter argues it’s too risky, too close to empire’s design.  Jonathan says it doesn’t matter what it is, only what they choose to do with it.  Sam warns not to trust gifts too easily, his eyes flicking toward Elon, who’s still weak but listening.

Gary finally breaks the tension, tossing a stick into the fire.
Gary: "Look, if creepy grandma wanted us dead, she wouldn’t have wasted her breath on a campfire story. Let’s not fight the only people who’ve lasted longer than us.”

The group exhales, but the unease lingers.

⸻

Act IV — The Shadow of Empire
Later, when the others are asleep, Kellyn lies awake with the stone clutched tight. The spiral seems to shift subtly in the firelight, almost breathing. Ashwin perches nearby, eyes gleaming, watching both the stone and Kellyn.

Kellyn (whispered, to the owl):
“You left her. You chose us. Why?”

Ashwin blinks once, slow, unreadable. Outside, the plateau wind carries a low hum — faint, but closer than before.

⸻

Closing Image
The group huddles around the fire, shadows long across the stone floor. In Kellyn’s hand, the spiral stone glimmers faintly, a promise or a trap. Ashwin keeps watch, unblinking.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“She gave us a gift. Or a test. Maybe both. Out here, there is no difference.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 72, Episode 5: The Gift We Can’t Open (Buried)

Cold Open — Morning on the Plateau
The group wakes to restless air. The spiral stone sits wrapped in cloth on the table, none of them having slept well with it so near. Estrella stomps and snorts; the sheep keep to the far side of the pen.

Peter (grim):
“Even the animals want it gone.”
Alex (quietly):
“It still might be the one thing that saves them.”

Title Card: The Gift We Can’t Open

⸻

Act I — Building the Box
Rather than argue, the group works. Jonathan shapes clay mixed with ash into thick panels.  Kellyn weaves reed mats to line the inside.  Gary melts wax and resin to seal the seams.  Sam and Peter brace the corners with scavenged metal shards.

By late afternoon, a sturdy box sits before them — plain, square, heavy, meant to contain.

Kellyn (low):
“If it has a voice, it won’t reach us here.”

⸻

Act II — The Burial Decision
Around the fire, debate resumes.

Alex: “If we keep it close, we can study it when we choose.”

Peter: “And if it cracks open in the night? No. It leaves camp.”

Jonathan: “Distance gives us safety, not blindness. We’ll still know it’s out there.”

Sam: “Empire wants us to believe we need it. Burying it is the only way to break that spell.”

Kellyn nods, quiet but certain: "Then we bury it. Away from the animals. Away from us.”

⸻

Act III — The Walk into Darkness
By lantern-light, they carry the box into the scrublands beyond camp. Each step feels heavier than the last. Ashwin circles silently overhead, as if escorting them.

They dig into the brittle earth with spades and hands, lowering the sealed box into the pit. Together, they cover it with layers of soil, stone, and reeds.

Jonathan (solemn):
“A covenant of distance.”

Peter (pressing down the last stone):
“Out of sight, out of reach.”

⸻

Act IV — Return
Back at camp, the hum in the ground softens. The animals settle. The fire burns steadier.

But the group doesn’t relax — the absence is its own presence. Kellyn stares toward the dark horizon where they buried the stone.

Kellyn (quiet, to Alex):
“It feels like it’s still watching us.”

Alex (hoarse):
“Because it is. Walls of dirt don’t stop empire. They only slow it.”

⸻

Closing Image
The group gathered close around the fire, their shoulders brushing. Out in the scrublands, the buried stone pulses faintly underground, its rhythm muffled but not erased.

Closing Image — The Firelight Fades
The group leans close around the fire, exhaustion tugging them toward uneasy rest. Estrella sighs in her sleep; the sheep curl together, calmer than before.

But when eyelids close, the quiet breaks:
	•	Jonathan dreams of birds with hollow bones, their wings whispering maps in ash.
	•	Peter dreams of his knife melting into a spiral, its edge turning inward.
	•	Alex dreams of endless applause echoing from beneath the earth, a chorus that will not stop.
	•	Kellyn dreams of reaching down into soil and finding a hand reaching back.

None of them stir, but all twist faintly, restless.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We thought burial meant silence. But empire’s gifts do not sleep. They seep. They coil. Even in dreams, the hum finds us.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 72, Episode 6: Dawn Fragments

Cold Open — Plateau Camp
Ashwin rustles down from his perch, wings silent in the paling sky. The fire is low, embers flickering. One by one, they stir. Their eyes are shadowed, not with sleep but with the weight of what pressed in during the night.

Title Card: Dawn Fragments

⸻

Act I — The Telling
They sit in a loose circle, warming their hands. Silence stretches until Kellyn exhales.

Kellyn (quiet):
“I dreamed something reached for me underground. A hand. Cold, but—familiar.”

Jonathan (after a pause):
“Mine were wings. Birds without weight. They flew, but it felt more like being carried by a map than by air.”

Peter’s fingers tighten around his knife.
Peter: “The blade melted. Turned into a spiral. It kept twisting back into me.”

Alex stares into the embers before speaking.
Alex: “Mine was applause. Thousands clapping. No faces, just the sound. Like it wanted to bury me in it.”

⸻

Act II — The Weaving
Jonathan leans forward, voice strained but steady.
Jonathan: “Maps. Spirals. Applause. Hands. They’re not random. They’re echoes from the stone. It’s still trying to bind us — differently this time.”

Peter shakes his head, jaw tight.
Peter: “Or maybe it’s showing us what we already carry. Weaknesses. Fissures.”

Kellyn (gently):
“Or both. Empire always works that way. It finds the cracks and pushes.”

Alex finally looks up, eyes shadowed.
Alex: “If it’s pushing, then we name the cracks. Out loud. Otherwise they’ll keep growing in silence.”

⸻

Act III — The Naming
Reluctantly, each voice breaks the air.

Jonathan: “I fear I’ll never map the way home. That I’ll only lead us deeper into wilderness.”
Peter: “I fear I’ll always be the spiral. Turning in. Cutting myself.”
Alex: “I fear the applause will outlive me. That even here, I’m still performing.”
Kellyn: “I'm not afraid.  Just tired.”

The silence afterward is heavier than before.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Fragments
Ashwin flutters down into their circle, talons clicking on stone. She stretches his wings once, a sharp gesture like a seal.

Kellyn (softly, almost to the owl):
“Then we bind what broke us. Even if it’s only with words.”

They reach, hands overlapping — not neat, not aligned. A tangle of fingers, calluses, scars.

Jonathan (whispering):
“Fragments aren’t weakness. They’re the start of mosaic.”

⸻

Closing Image
The sun crests the plateau rim, spilling gold across their circle. The buried stone hums faintly beneath their feet, but for now the sound is met with voices, raw and unguarded.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We could not choose the dreams empire planted. But waking, we chose each other. And for that moment, it was enough.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 72, Episode 7: The Crown Returns

Cold Open — Plateau Rim
Wind stirs the sparse grass. Ashwin circles overhead, uneasy. The group walks in silence, still raw from the dreams. Then — a shadow ahead. The crowned figure stands at the rim, tall against the morning light, the crown glinting with a fractured, impossible shine.

Title Card: The Crown Returns

⸻

Act I — The Arrival
The crowned figure spreads his arms. His voice carries like theater, too loud for the open plateau.

Crowned Figure:
“You dig, you dream, you whisper — and still you think you’re free. But every step you take, you trace the path I’ve already written. You are mine. You always were.”

Peter grips his knife, body taut.
Peter (low, sharp): “You don’t own us.”

The figure laughs, the sound cracked and hollow.

⸻

Act II — The Pressing
He paces slowly, crown tilting, his voice weaving between sermon and mockery.

Crowned Figure:
“Unity? Covenant? You mistake your knots of rope for chains strong enough to break me. Do you not see? Every fear you named this morning — I was the one who planted it. I’ve been inside you longer than you’ve been alive.”

Jonathan’s voice cuts steady, though his hand trembles.
Jonathan: “Then why show yourself? If you were certain, you’d stay in shadow.”

The figure pauses, head cocking, grin jagged.

Crowned Figure:
“Because I enjoy the moment before collapse. When you still believe the firelight holds back the dark.”

⸻

Act III — The Stand
Kellyn steps forward, voice quiet but cutting through the wind.

Kellyn:
“You talk about collapse like it’s a certainty. But you never got it out of me.  You demand collapse only because you’ve never touched covenant. You don’t know what it feels like when hands bind by choice, not fear.”

The crowned figure snarls, crown flickering brighter for a moment.

Crowned Figure:
“Choice is a lie. Fear is the only truth.”

Alex’s voice rises, rough but unwavering.
Alex: “If that’s true, then why are you so afraid of us?”

For the first time, silence falls around the crown.

⸻

Act IV — The Ripple
The figure’s crown hums — a sound like stone cracking underground. The plateau itself vibrates. Birds scatter in the distance. Ashwin dives low, screeching, wings flaring.

The crowned figure’s voice drops, low and venomous.
Crowned Figure:
“You think you can break my field? Then bleed for it. Every unbinding costs. Every dream you cling to, I will twist.”

Kellyn: "...Okay?  And?"

He vanishes in a flicker of impossible light — leaving the hum louder than before, echoing beneath their feet.

⸻

Closing Image
The group stands tense, dust rising around their boots. The crown is gone, but the plateau feels thinner, weaker — as though his absence is only another kind of presence.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He left no wound we could see. But the ground beneath us had changed. And with every echo of his crown, empire pressed closer.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 72, Episode 8: The Holding Circle

Cold Open — Plateau Clearing
The group retreats into a hollow where the wind drops away. Ashwin perches nearby, feathers bristled. No one speaks at first. The echoes of the crown’s hum still tremble in their bones.

Title Card: The Holding Circle

⸻

Act I — The Silence
Peter paces, knife in hand, carving the dirt. Jonathan watches the horizon, jaw locked. Alex sits hunched, fists pressed to his knees. Kellyn finally breaks the silence, voice steady but low.

Kellyn:
“We need to breathe together. Right now. Before his words become ours.”

⸻

Act II — The Circle
They form a rough circle, knees touching, hands overlapping. Kellyn leads, whispering slow counts. The others follow, breaths syncing. The sheep shuffle close, Estrella lowers her head against the edge of the circle, and Ashwin drops silently into the center, wings half-spread like a shield.

Sam (hoarse):
“He wanted us to feel fractured. But we’re still here. Still holding.”

⸻

Act III — The Confessions
The rhythm steadies. One by one, voices spill.

Peter (low, raw):
“I wanted to cut him down. My hands itched for it. But I froze.”

Jonathan:
“You didn’t freeze. You chose not to break what was already trembling.”

Alex (quiet, shaking his head):
“I heard him in my own voice. Like I’d already said the same words before.”

Kellyn squeezes his hand, anchoring him.
Kellyn (steady):
“Then we name it. And we don’t let silence rot into shame.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Holding
Ashwin lets out a low, almost human hum, harmonizing with their breath. The circle tightens. The fear lingers, but so does the pulse of connection.

Sam (soft):
“Every time he presses in, we press closer. That’s how we win.”

The circle holds a beat longer before loosening. Their hands fall away, but the rhythm remains.

⸻

Closing Image
Five figures and their animals in a hollow circle, the faint trace of empire’s hum still in the earth — but countered by the steadier rhythm of covenant.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire struck with spectacle. But covenant answered in silence — in breath, in presence, in hands that refused to let go.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 72, Episode 9: The Night of Pairs

Cold Open — Twilight in the Hollow
The fire is down to embers. The group has eaten little. The sheep are huddled together, Estrella chewing slow and steady. Ashwin watches from a branch, eyes shining. Elon rests in the smaller room by himself.  One by one, the circle breaks into pairs.

Title Card: The Night of Pairs

⸻

Act I — Kellyn and Alex
They sit close, backs against stone. The weight of the earlier moment still lingers.

Alex (low, almost ashamed):
“When he spoke, it was like he’d peeled something out of me I thought I’d buried.”

Kellyn brushes their thumb along his wrist, grounding him.
Kellyn (quiet):
“Then let’s put something else there. Not his voice. Ours.”

They breathe together in silence, their foreheads leaning until the rhythm steadies again.

⸻

Act II — Peter and Jonathan
A little apart, Peter sharpens his knife against a stone, the sound steady. Jonathan watches, then gently places a hand over Peter’s.

Jonathan (soft, even):
“You don’t have to stay sharp all the time. You’re allowed to set it down.”

Peter exhales, blade lowering. He leans against Jonathan’s shoulder, silent but not fighting the comfort.

⸻

Act III — Sam and Gary
Near the fire, Gary fidgets anxiously, uncomfortable with the silence.

Sam (to Gary):
“You don’t need to make everyone laugh tonight. Just be a man breathing, carried for once.”

Gary smiles and says nothing.  Sam stays, steady as the night.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Pairs
The fire pops. The hollow is quiet but for breath, shifting cloth, the soft rustle of animals. Ashwin tucks his head beneath a wing. Each pair is its own small anchor, but together they weave a web stronger than empire’s hum.

⸻

The stars sharpen against the black sky. The embers are low, the animals quiet. Each pair lingers in their pocket of intimacy, letting silence turn into something more deliberate.

Kellyn and Alex lie on their sides, knees touching. Alex traces the outline of Kellyn's hand in the dirt.

Alex (whispering):
“You never left me alone, even when I deserved it.”

Kellyn covers his hand with theirs, grounding the movement.
Kellyn (steady): “I was alone enough for both of us. That’s how I know neither of us should be again.”

His breath hitches, and he finally relaxes into the closeness, letting their hands stay entwined.

⸻ 

Jonathan plucks softly at the string of a dulcimer he’s carried. Peter closes his eyes, leaning into the sound.

Peter (low, almost grudging):
“It’s easier to breathe with noise I choose.”

Jonathan nods, continuing the slow, steady pattern.
Jonathan:
“Then let me be your noise, until you trust the silence too.”

Peter lets out a short, shaky laugh — but doesn’t pull away.

⸻ 

Sam enters the small room to check on Elon as he sleeps. Ashwin stirs above them, feathers shuffling. Elon mutters, turning in his blanket.

Elon (dream-slurred):
“Claire, don’t go.”

Sam sighs, brushing a hand across Elon’s temple.

Sam (soft, almost a vow):
“I’m not her. But I’m here. That has to be enough.”

Elon quiets, breath evening.

Each pair drifts in and out of sleep — hand against hand, head against shoulder, voice against breath. The intimacy is quiet, unhurried, fragile but real. Not armor, not strategy, but the thing armor and strategy were meant to protect.

⸻

Closing Image
Three pairs beneath the stars, each circle sealed, but together forming a second weave around the fire — tighter, more deliberate than before.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire wanted us scattered, broken into shards. But intimacy wove us inward — not chains, not contracts, but touch and breath, quiet enough to carry into morning.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 72, Episode 10: The Shatter Before Dawn

Cold Open — Night’s Edge
The camp lies still: embers smoldering, the animals huddled close, each pair curled together. The plateau wind rustles the reeds — and then stills. A silence too deep to be natural.

Title Card: The Shatter Before Dawn

⸻

Act I — The First Crack
Ashwin’s wings flare suddenly, dropping from her perch to land hard in the dust. Her head jerks toward the ridgeline.
A low hum rolls in, like the earth’s bones vibrating.

Sam stirs, sitting upright, breath sharp.
Sam (hoarse, urgent):
“They found us.”

⸻

Act II — The Encroaching Sound
The hum rises into a keening pulse. The animals thrash — the sheep ramming into each other, Estrella stamping furiously. The tent ropes tremble, threatening collapse.

Jonathan is first to his feet, dulcimer still in hand, striking a dissonant chord that cuts across the hum.
Jonathan (to the group):
“Noise against noise — now!”

Peter grabs stones, clapping them together in a syncopated rhythm. Kellyn yanks the reed mats they’d made, shaking them against the ground like shields.

⸻

Act III — The Fracture Point
Elon jolts awake, disoriented, grabbing for Kellyn.
Elon (panicked, half-delirious):
“Claire! Stay with me—don’t let them take the song!”

Alex pulls him back, voice sharp:
“Focus. You’re here. With us.”

But the sound thickens, trying to sync their pulses. Kellyn feels their chest constrict, breath shortening.

Sam drops low beside them, pressing palm to palm, forcing rhythm.

Sam (fierce, steady): "Breathe with me. Don’t let them set your pace.”

⸻

Act IV — The Counterstrike
The owl launches upward, slicing the night with a shriek. The animals cluster behind him. The group forms a ragged circle, layering sound against sound — dulcimer strums, stone claps, stomps into dust, breath forced ragged and offbeat.

The hum falters. A fissure of silence breaks through. Then, just as suddenly, the night air is clean again. The echo withdraws, leaving only their uneven panting.

⸻

Closing Image
The group stands in the shelter, sweat and dust streaking their skin, the fire sputtering low. Ashwin circles overhead once, then lands on Kellyn’s shoulder, silent sentinel.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We thought intimacy might shield us until morning. But empire knows where to strike: in the softest hours, when warmth is still on our skin. Even then, covenant held — not by silence, but by breaking rhythm together.”

Fade out.
End of Season 72.
