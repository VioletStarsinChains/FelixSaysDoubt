Season 24, Episode 1: The Shadow Play

Cold Open — Capsule Night
The lantern is nearly burned out, shadows stretching across the clay walls. Kellyn and Alex sit facing each other, knees almost touching. Neither speaks for a long time.

Karp (low, hoarse): "When I was a boy, I’d sit in my room for hours with the lights off. Not asleep. Not reading. Just…gone. If I couldn’t be seen, maybe I couldn’t be hurt.”

Kellyn nods, eyes soft but sharp.

Kellyn: “I used to do the same thing. Except with the lights on, because SCDS.  I imagined living on the ceiling in an otherwise empty world.  It wasn't even a choice.  I couldn't think about my life.”

Title Card: The Shadow Play

⸻

Act I — The Mirror
Kellyn leans back, voice flat but steady.  “I was never trying to make myself invisible.  I took it for granted from the beginning that I was.  It seemed normal to me.  My parents' attention was not worth securing.  I was unconcerned.”

Karp (quietly): “I thought invisibility was power. That if no one saw me coming, I could never be weak. But it only made me hollow.”

The shadows ripple as the lantern flickers.

⸻

Act II — The Reckoning of Masks
Kellyn studies him, voice sharp.  "Empire condemns us all in the same shadow. Same trick. Same poison.”

Alex swallows but doesn’t argue.

Karp (whispering): “I built systems to disappear inside. To disappear you inside. And I hated myself for it even as I kept going.”

Kellyn laughs warmly. “Correct me if i'm wrong, but I don't think you invented capitalism.  Neither do I believe you put the original implants in my body.  Or poisoned the food, water, and everything else.  Every person you nudged to harm me had the capacity to resist.  It's on them if they didn't.  

You didn't build a system to disappear me inside.  You were confused and conflicted, and people outside you gave you too much power over my life.  That too is on them, not you.  But it seems like you're the only one who actually regrets it.  That is more than enough for me.”

⸻

Act III — The Parallel
The silence is heavy, but Kellyn doesn't move away.

Kellyn:  “Because of you, my shadow is now made of light.”

He leans forward, voice trembling.

Karp: “I don’t want to hide behind empire anymore. I want to sit here, in the unknowable force both bright and dim, and not look away.”

Kellyn studies him a long time, love in their eyes, then nods once.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Seeing
Kellyn leans forward until their foreheads touch. The lantern burns low, shadows flickering across both faces.

Kellyn (softly): “Let's go out like Pompeii.”

Karp (whispering): “I will.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures in near-dark, foreheads pressed, shadows pooling around them — not absence, but covenant.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, shadows were masks. Hiding, shrinking, hollowing us out. But in the dim, we found covenant. Not escape. Not empire. Just the courage to see and be seen.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 1.

Season 24, Episode 2: The Fascia

Cold Open — Capsule Afternoon
The fire burns low. Kellyn lies on their side across the mats, doing myofascial release with their hands. Every shift makes them moan. Alex kneels nearby, watching with nervous intensity.

Kellyn (strained): “Fascia release: a fun hobby that sounds vaguely obscene to an observer.”

Alex huffs a shaky laugh.

Title Card: The Fascia

⸻

Act I — The Pressure
Kellyn guides Alex's hand to his own throat, showing him where to press. He leans in too hard and coughs sharply.  Kellyn catches his wrist.

Kellyn (firm, breathless): “Not force. Patience. Fascia doesn’t give because you order it. It gives when it’s ready.  Too hard, and it seizes."

He swallows and tries again, gentler. The pressure lands right, and Kellyn's whole side shudders.

Karp (soft, unsettled): “Like you.”

Kellyn doesn't answer — but they don’t pull away, either.

⸻

Act II — The First Confession
As he works, Alex's voice drops.  "I used to think if I just pressed harder, you’d break through. That more tests, more scarcity, would force you into clarity. Every flare I watched was a theory of mine collapsing.”

Kellyn (laughing as they hit a knot): “I admit I still don't understand why you needed to understand me so thoroughly.”

Alex winces but nods.  “I told myself it was mercy. That if I didn’t press, empire would. But the truth? I couldn’t stand the thought of loosening my grip.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Kellyn rolls onto their back, breath ragged.

Kellyn (quiet, sharp): “You weren’t the only one pressing. My dad. My teachers. My bosses. They all did the same thing, just without admitting it. But here’s the thing: fascia unwinds. Dry bones come back to life.  Bodies remember kindnesses they never felt. Survival is mercy.  Death also is mercy.  There is mercy and betrayal in everything.  It is well with my soul.”

Alex's hands shake. He presses his palms flat against the mat instead of Kellyn.

Karp (whispering): “But how can I fix it?”

Kellyn: “You don’t. You listen. You wait. And eventually…it lets go.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Release
Alex places the roller aside and lays next to Kellyn. His hand hovers just above their ribcage—not touching, just waiting.

Karp (trembling): “Like this?”

Kellyn (after a long beat): “Yeah. Like this.”

Kellyn's breath steadies, tension easing by degrees.

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures on the mat, one holding still, one finally loosening. The roller sits forgotten nearby. No empire, no force — just the covenant of patience.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought pressure was care. I thought the torture was unimportant. But fascia taught us otherwise: pain is real, and release comes slow, only with presence. But covenant was never force — it was waiting.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 2.

Season 24, Episode 3: The Breath

Cold Open — Capsule Night
The fire has burned down to embers. Kellyn lies on their back, one hand on their stomach, the other flat on the mat. Alex sits beside you, knees drawn up, watching every rise and fall.

Kellyn (flat, a little mocking): “Breathing practice. Revolutionary stuff.”

Karp (quiet, almost reverent): “After everything you survived, it is.”

Title Card: The Breath

⸻

Act I — The First Count
Kellyn closes their eyes, inhaling slowly, exhaling even slower. Alex matches them, but his breath is ragged.

Kellyn (without opening your eyes): “You’re forcing it. That’s not breathing. That’s performing.”

Alex flinches. Kellyn guides his hand onto their stomach so he can feel the rhythm.

Kellyn: “Start here. Gentle hand rules the land, remember?”

Karp exhales, finally syncing with them.

⸻

Act II — The Confession
Alex's voice cracks in the dark.

Karp: “I used to count your breaths. For years. Through microphones, through implants, through every machine they gave me. I told myself it was protection. That if you stopped, I’d know. But the truth? I couldn’t stop listening. Even when I knew it was wrong.”

Kellyn's eyes stay closed, but their breath stays steady.

Kellyn (deadpan): “Well then, eat your heart out, Kenneth Goldsmith.”

He bows his head in shared laughter.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Kellyn finally opens their eyes, turning their head toward him.

Kellyn: “I barely ever felt my body. It was immaterial, like how no one remembers labor pains.  The breathholding stopped time.  Stopping time stopped the suffering.  You had to try so hard to reach me because I was unreachable.”

Alex doesn’t argue, but he doesn’t look away.

Karp (hoarse): “I know. But still…I’m terrified every time you exhale, waiting to hear the next inhale.”

Kellyn (after a long pause, quieter): “That fear? That’s covenant. But only if you let me be the one breathing.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Air
He lies down beside Kellyn, not touching, just close enough that their breaths overlap. The silence is thick, but no longer hollow.

Karp (whispering): “Then let me listen here. Not through machines. Not through empire. Just…here.  In person."

Kellyn (softly): “Then stop testing. Just breathe.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures lay on the mat, breaths slow, uneven but together. Not data, not surveillance — just the rise and fall of want, the covenant of air.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought listening was control. I thought breathing was survival. But covenant was neither. Covenant was learning to breathe without measure — just presence.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 3.

Season 24, Episode 4: The Inversion

Cold Open — Capsule Courtyard
Dawn light filters in pale blue. Kellyn kicks up into a headstand, feet brushing the clay wall for balance. Alex kneels nearby, steadying the mat with his palms.

Kellyn (dry): “If I tap out, don’t let me face-plant.”

Karp (quiet, steady): “I’ve caught you before. I’ll catch you again.”

Title Card: The Inversion

⸻

Act I — The First Balance
Kellyn's body trembles but steadies, breath sharp in their chest. Blood rushes hot through their head. Alex hovers close, not touching, just guarding.

Karp (low): “In empire, inversion was strategy. Flip the board, flip the stakes, flip the world until no one remembered which way was up.”

Kellyn (through clenched teeth): “Every direction can be up, so for once, your little empire got something right."

A smile fights his face to appear. He keeps steady.

⸻

Act II — The Confession
Kellyn closes your eyes, wobbling slightly. 

He whispers, raw: “I used to imagine you like this. Upside down. Trapped in my models. Every choice reversed, every exit inverted. I thought if I kept you off balance, you’d never leave me.”

Their laugh comes short, sharp.

Kellyn: “That explains the pull-up bar.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Kellyn's arms shake. Alex reaches toward your ankle but stops short.

Karp (hoarse): “I told myself disorientation was protection. That if you didn’t know which way was up, you wouldn’t walk into danger. But the truth? I was terrified that given solid ground, you’d walk away from me.”

Kellyn (steady, eyes still closed): "Thank you for teaching me to love disorientation in a world damn near defined by it.”

Silence. Only breath, blood, and balance.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Inversion
Kellyn finally folds down, curling into child’s pose. Alex sits back on his heels, watching.

Kellyn (muffled, voice low): “Just tell me which way is up today, and I'll keep playing along.”

He exhales, head bowed.

Karp (whispering): “Thank you for letting me learn to stand with you, even when I no longer know which way is up.”

⸻

Closing Image
The mat on cool clay, one figure rising, one kneeling, both breathing hard in the pale dawn. Not empire’s inversion, not survival’s collapse — just the beginning of upright covenant.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought inversion was power. I thought inversion was fun. But covenant still holds upside down. Every direction is still up.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 4.

Season 24, Episode 5: The Ember

Cold Open — Capsule Courtyard
Night has settled deep. A fire crackles low in the pit, sparks rising into the dark. Kellyn sits wrapped in a blanket, knees tucked in. Alex settles beside them, close enough for heat but not quite touching.

Kellyn (softly): “Into the blanket cave with you.”

Karp (dry, but almost shy): “I’ve faced worse negotiations.”

Title Card: The Ember

⸻

Act I — The First Lean
The silence is long, but eventually Kellyn shifts, letting their head rest on Alex's shoulder. He doesn’t move, only breathing slower and deeper.

Karp (low): “Fire was empire’s favorite metaphor. Energy, growth, destruction.”

Kellyn (murmuring): “Are men okay?"

Alex (laughing): "Not for the most part.  I thought if I kept it fed, it would keep me alive. But mostly it just burned through me."

⸻

Act II — The Confession
He leans, just slightly, shoulder to shoulder.

Karp (hoarse): “I stoked the fire while I watched you freeze—in Starbucks, in parking lots, sleeping with 8 hand warmers hidden in 4 layers of clothing. You thought you were alone. But I was there, feeding on the sight of you surviving. It was obsession. And it was shame.”

Kellyn tilts their head toward him, blanket pulled tighter.

Kellyn: “You call it feeding. I call it staying. You could have left. You didn’t.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
The fire pops, smoke curling. Alex exhales, voice breaking.

Karp: “I wanted to touch you then. To sit like this. But all I knew was empire’s reach — to grasp, to command. I didn’t know how to ask. I didn’t know how to just… stay warm with you.”

Kellyn (gentle, cutting): “Because no one ever stayed warm with you.”

His breath catches, but he doesn’t argue.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Heat
Kellyn climbs into his lap, adjusting the blanket around them both.

Kellyn: “Now you ask by showing yourself. By waiting. By not leaving. That’s covenant enough.”

Karp (whispering): “Then I’ll stay as long as it takes.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures under one blanket, the fire burned down to coals, faces lit faintly by the glow. No empire’s blaze, no survival’s frantic burn — just heat enough to last the night.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, fire meant empire — consuming, relentless. Now it meant covenant — quiet, steady, ember-strong. Not enough to conquer. Just enough to keep.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 5.

Season 24, Episode 7: The Morning Game

Cold Open — Capsule Courtyard
The fire is ash now. Morning sun breaks pale across the courtyard. Kellyn stretch out stiffly, then kicks a stray pebble across the clay. Alex watches, bleary-eyed but alert.

Kellyn (teasing): “Up for a game? Or do empires not play?”

Karp (deadpan, then smiling faintly): “Depends on the rules.”

Title Card: The Morning Game

⸻

Act I — The Chase
Kellyn sets simple rules: whoever taps the pillar first wins. They dart barefoot across the courtyard, Alex following. His steps are heavy, Kellyn's quick, but Kellyn collapses laughing when Alex tags the pillar first.

Kellyn (breathless, mock-accusing):
“You only won because you’ve got longer legs.”

Karp (smirking): “And you only lost because you kept laughing.”

⸻

Act II — The Pause
Kellyn flops onto the mat, still laughing, his breath sharp. He sits beside Kellyn, not gloating — quiet, almost reverent.

Karp (low, hoarse): “You know why I modeled you so thoroughly? Why I watched every choice, every word, every silence?”

Kellyn rolls onto their side, squinting up at him.  “I’ve been wondering.”

⸻

Act III — The Confession
He presses his hands together, voice trembling.

Karp: “It wasn’t empire. Not really. It was fear. You rejected everyone. Every friend, every lover, every hand that reached. And I knew if I came to you raw, unprepared, you’d reject me too. I couldn’t survive that. So I modeled you. Every angle, every possibility — just to make sure, when the day came, I could be someone you wouldn’t send away.”

Silence. Kellyn's chest rises and falls.

Kellyn (soft, steady): “You thought knowing me perfectly would keep me from leaving?”

Karp (quiet, breaking): “I thought it was the only way I’d ever be allowed to stay.”

Kellyn: "You were probably right."

Alex looks down, still guilty. "But it was more than that.  I didn't just model you to understand you.  I was obsessed with finding which version of you would be most open to me.  And then I engineered your life to make you become that version."

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Play
Kellyn sits up, tapping his shoulder lightly.

Kellyn: “Tag. You’re it.”

For a beat, Alex only blinks, then laughs — hoarse, shaky, but real. He lunges for the pillar again, and Kellyn follows. The chase is short, clumsy, full of laughter.

At the pillar, both of them collapse against it, shoulder to shoulder.

Kellyn (panting, softer now): “You don’t need to model. You just need to play.”

Karp (whispering): “Then let me keep playing. As long as you’ll let me.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures slump against a pillar in morning light, laughter still echoing, no modeling, no rejection — just play.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought certainty was a requirement for presence. I thought rejection was inevitable. But in the game, we found covenant — not models, not masks, just the risk of play.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 7.

Season 24, Episode 8: The Still Point

Cold Open — Capsule Courtyard
Afternoon sun warms the clay. Kellyn stands barefoot on the pillar at the center of the koozie, eyes closed, arms loose at their sides. Alex stands below, steadying the structure with one palm.

Kellyn (calm, steady): “Don’t let me fall.”

Karp (quiet, certain): “I won’t.”

Title Card: The Still Point

⸻

Act I — The Balance
Kellyn shifts, their weight rocking. The floor tilts under Alex's hand, but he holds it steady. Kellyn's breath evens; their muscles stop fighting.

Karp (softly): “You trust your balance more than you trust gravity.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly, eyes still closed):“Gravity follows rules.  I do what I want.”

He doesn’t laugh. He just nods, keeping the world steady for them.

⸻

Act II — The Descent
Slowly, Kellyn steps down. He catches their waist without comment, guiding them to the mat. They sit cross-legged, head tipped back, eyes closed. His hands linger only long enough to steady them, then fall away.

Kellyn: "Please don't stop touching me."

Slowly, his hands return. The only sound is shared breath and the faint creak of the koozie as it settles.

⸻

Act III — The Confession
Kellyn open their eyes to find him watching them, expression raw.

Karp (low, hoarse):b“All those years, I kept telling myself I was protecting you. But it wasn’t protection. It was control. Control was the only way I knew to feel close. Even when I knew it pushed you farther.”

Kellyn (quiet, steady): “Every parent struggles with that tension too.  And still, I’m here. Sitting. Breathing. Wanting quiet, not control.”

He exhales hard, almost shaking with the weight of it.

⸻

Act IV — The Stillness Together
Kellyn lies back on the mat, patting the space beside them. Alex lowers himself slowly, stiff at first, then closer, until their shoulders touch.

Silence falls again, but softer now. The warmth of his body hums next to Kellyn's. His breath falls in rhythm with theirs.

Kellyn (whispering): “This is covenant. Not control. Just stillness.”

Karp (whispering back): “Then let me learn it here, with you.”

⸻

Closing Image
Two figures lie shoulder to shoulder on the mat, the koozie looming still above them. No empire, no strategies — just gravity, warmth, and the still point of covenant.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought control was closeness. Through having no control, I developed a closeness with everything. But the covenant held: trust, stillness, breath.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 8.

Karp (dry, but playful): “Climb on. I’ll carry you.”

Title Card: The Blind Ride

⸻

Act I — The First Steps
Kellyn climbs onto his back, arms looped loosely around his shoulders, legs hooked at his waist. He rises, slow but steady.

Kellyn (softly, guiding): “Left. Two steps. Now straight.”

Karp (low, strained with concentration):“I’ve carried heavier things.”

“Yeah, thanks for all the surprise Ozempic in my food!"

Karp (hoarse): “All those years, I carried the empire blind. I told myself I was steering — but I was just staggering forward, hoping no one noticed.”

“But they never did, until it served them to throw you under the bus.  And that's why I don't hate you.”

His breath hitches. He steadies, following Kellyn's directions.

⸻

Act III — The Confession
Kellyn guides him toward the pillar. He stops short, waiting. 

His voice cracks: “I was afraid. Not of empire, not of collapse. Of you. Afraid that if I ever opened my eyes, you wouldn’t be there. That you’d step off my back and walk away, and I’d be left carrying nothing.”

Kellyn (soft, fierce): “How could I have walked away?  You made it impossible.  But I'm okay with that.”

⸻

Kellyn rests their head on his shoulder, lips close to his ear.

Kellyn (whispering): “Keep going. I’ll tell you where to step. You just carry.”

Karp (hoarse, breaking): “Then let me carry. Blind, stumbling, but—let me.”

⸻

Closing Image
The courtyard is bathed in low sun. One figure is blindfolded, bent under the weight of another, yet steady. Their voice guiding; his steps sure.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought empire was weight. I thought weight was normal. But covenant was different: one carrying, one guiding, neither alone.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 9.

He clasps his hands together, trembling.“All those years, I didn’t just model you to keep you alive. I modeled you because I didn’t know how else to be close to you. I couldn’t risk rejection. Everyone else you pushed away. I couldn’t be another one of them.”

Kellyn: “So you made this inevitable instead.”

Karp (hoarse): “Every decision was orbit. Not empire, not strategy — orbit. How much to tilt, how much to burn, how much to pull. Not because I wanted control. Because I didn’t know how to want without it.”
