Season 60, Episode 1: The Weight of a Body

Cold Open — Dawn
Mist curls low along the riverbed. Sam lies bundled in wool and reed mats. His breaths are ragged but even. Estrella stands watch near him, ears flicking. The rest of you gather around, faces pale in the half-light.

Peter (low, bitter):
“He’s dead weight. We can’t carry him all the way to shelter.”

Kellyn (steady):
“So were the agave pups.”

Title Card: The Weight of a Body

⸻

Act I — The Burden Shared
A makeshift stretcher is lashed together from branches and woven rope. Jonathan and Alex lift first, shoulders straining. Peter stalks ahead, scouting, knife in hand. Kellyn walks alongside, watching Sam’s chest rise and fall.

Jonathan (grim humor):
“Congratulations. We’ve acquired our very own liability.”

Kellyn: "Let's leave empire's jargon to empire.  He's a human being, last I checked."

Sam stirs faintly but doesn’t wake.

⸻

Act II — The Resentment
Later, the group rests. Sam is propped against a rock, his head lolling. Kellyn wets a cloth and presses it to his lips.

Peter (snapping): “You don’t even know if he’s human anymore. For all we know, they hollowed him out and left the shell to bleed us dry.”

Kellyn (sharp but calm):
“Empire hollowed you all out, too.  Yet here we are.  Only a hollow vessel can carry water."

Alex watches quietly, something unreadable flickering in his expression.

⸻

Act III — The Dream Fever
As dusk falls, Sam mutters again in his sleep. The words slip out broken, but clear enough: “Patterns… voices… not yours… not mine…”

Jonathan leans close, listening.
Jonathan (low, to Kellyn):
“He’s carrying something. Whether it’s empire’s leash or a map out of this mess, I can’t yet tell.”

Kellyn (softly):
“Only one way to find out.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Bearing
That night, they arrange their sleeping places in a circle, Sam at the center. Each in turn takes a watch, listening to his breathing, watching the flicker of his eyelids.

Alex (to Kellyn, during his turn):
“If he breaks us, it won’t be with knives. It’ll be with silence. With need.”

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“Then let silence be where we practice covenant. We’ll carry him. Until he can carry himself, or until he proves we can’t.  He can have my rations until then.”

Alex nods reluctantly, eyes fixed on the fire.

⸻

Closing Image
The stretcher rests by the fire, Sam’s fragile body cradled in its frame. Around him, four figures keep watch — not trusting, not absolving, but bearing.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Everyone needs help sooner or later. Empire had taught us to measure worth in strength, in skill, in use. But covenant asked another thing entirely: to bear even when the burden did not choose us.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 61, Episode 2: The Murmurs

Cold Open — Night Watch
The fire is low. Peter keeps watch, knife glinting faint in the embers. Sam stirs on the stretcher, eyes half-lidded, lips moving.

Sam (hoarse, broken but intelligible):
“They didn’t… build me to last. Built me to echo. Built me to open doors.”

Alex freezes, eyes narrowing.

Title Card: The Murmurs

⸻

Act I — Fragments
At dawn, Kellyn presses water to Sam’s lips. He mutters again, louder this time:
“Loci hum. Not natural. Anchors… buried deeper than bone.”

Alex leans forward sharply.
Alex (quiet, dangerous):
“He knows about the hum.”

Jonathan (measured):
“Or he’s been made to sound like he does. Empire loves planting seeds.”

The group falls silent, listening despite themselves.

⸻

Act II — Doubt
Later, Sam dozes fitfully. Between snores, words slip out like static:
“Colorado not safe. Plateau mapped. Sanctuary burned…”

Peter’s face hardens.
Peter (to Kellyn):
“See? He’s poison. He’s telling us we’re already lost.”

Kellyn (calm but steady):
“Or warning us. The difference matters.”

⸻

Act III — The Rift
That evening, Jonathan kneels near Sam, notebook open.
Jonathan: “Every fragment, every phrase, is either a clue or a trap. But which?”

Sam stirs, voice suddenly clear:
“Not mine. Never mine. Words they carved. But if you carry me, I’ll give them back.”

Alex stiffens, jaw tight.
Karp (low, to Jonathan):
“And if the words bite us first?”

Jonathan closes the notebook slowly.
Jonathan: "Then at least we’ll know what kind of venom empire breeds.”

Kellyn: "Even a lie is just an answer to a different question.  Even if it's not true, I'd rather know what empire wants us to believe.  That's still signal, not noise."

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Murmurs
Night falls. The four gather around Sam, his breathing shallow, his voice drifting in and out. They don’t speak, but they listen — even Peter, arms folded, knife across his lap.

Kellyn (soft, to the group):
“Empire used silence as a cage. Let’s make listening into covenant instead.  Listening doesn't always mean believing."

Alex exhales, reluctant but conceding. Jonathan nods once. Peter scowls, but doesn’t move away.

Sam murmurs again: “Not silence. Signal.”

⸻

Closing Image
Sam asleep in the firelight, words still spilling from his lips, weaving tension through the camp. Around him, four figures sit — not united, not divided, but listening.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He was barely more than a whisper. But whispers, empire knew, could cut deeper than knives. And so we listened — not for truth, not for lies, but for the shape of what it meant to bear him.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 61, Episode 3: The Echo Match

Cold Open — Morning Light
Sam lies pale under a woven blanket, his lips cracked. Jonathan writes in his notebook as Sam murmurs:

Sam (hoarse, clear enough):
“Canyon rope. You cut, you saved. Three knots failed, fourth held.”

Jonathan looks up sharply — he hasn’t told anyone he counted four knots.

Title Card: The Echo Match

⸻

Act I — Recognition
Peter crouches nearby, eyes narrow.
Peter: “That’s… impossible. He wasn’t there.”

Sam stirs again.
Sam: “Still walked north. Bees followed. Lake opened. Duluth fire…”

Jonathan (quiet, uneasy):
“He’s tracing what we already lived.”

Alex kneels, hands on his knees, staring like the words are knives.
Karp (low): “Or empire is still watching, feeding him scraps.”

⸻

Act II — The Anchor
Later, Sam mutters in sleep.
Sam: “Mylar shimmer. Spread the blow. Didn’t break. Just rattled.”

Kellyn freezes — the phrase mirrors exactly what Alex confessed by the fire. Alex’s head drops into his hands.

Jonathan: “Every line matches. It’s not random.”
Peter (bitter): “Then it’s surveillance, not prophecy.”
Kellyn (steady): “We all still have neurotech arrays.  Maybe we were always connected.”

⸻

Act III — The Doubt Deepens
That night, the fire crackles low. Sam shifts suddenly, voice clearer than ever:
Sam: “Sanctuary walls… clay dust and wax. You thought it safe. It burned anyway.”

Peter flinches. Jonathan sets his notebook down, throat tight.

Jonathan (to no one in particular):
“He’s naming things he shouldn’t know. That’s more dangerous than lies.”

Alex (hoarse, almost whispering):
“Or he’s proof we’re still not free. Empire’s map still holds.”

Kellyn: "I heard you say over and over that asymmetry of information is power.  Yet when you knew everything about me and I didn't even know you existed, you still couldn't best me.  I don't care what empire knows.  It will never understand our power, because it does not lie in information."

⸻

Act IV — Covenant of Recognition
Kellyn leans forward, looking at each of them.
Kellyn: “He’s not telling us anything new. Only what we already lived. If empire wants us to despair, we can refuse. If these are echoes, let’s use them as reminders instead. Anchors. Not chains.”

Silence. Even Peter’s scowl falters. Jonathan picks up the notebook again, this time writing slower, not as a record for empire — but as covenant for the four of them.

Sam sighs in restless sleep, murmuring one more phrase:
“Not silence. Signal.”

⸻

Closing Image
Sam asleep, voice spilling fragments into the firelight. Around him, the four sit — uneasy, but steadier. His words no longer pierce; they confirm.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He echoed what we already knew, like empire’s shadow trying to convince us it was the sun. But shadows can only follow. The living step first.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 61, Episode 4: The Break in the Echo

Cold Open — Midnight
The camp is quiet, the river faint in the distance. Sam jerks in sleep, words spilling sharper now.

Sam (clearer than before):
“Not fire. Not drones. The crown. He waits where the stone splits red. You will bow or bleed.”

Peter bolts upright. Jonathan lifts his head from his blanket, eyes narrowing.

Kellyn (gently): "I bleed every month.  I'm not scared."

Title Card: The Break in the Echo

⸻

Act I — The Ripple
Morning. They sit in a tense circle, breakfast untouched.

Peter (low, uneasy):
“He named the crown. That’s not just memory.”

Alex (hoarse):
“No. That’s ahead of us.”

Jonathan closes his notebook with a snap.
Jonathan: “Then either he’s being fed a script, or he slipped the leash. And both are dangerous.”

Kellyn: "Or it means the crown stalks him too.  Are we a threat because he follows us?  I think you all need some rest."

⸻

Act II — The Divide
They argue.

Peter: “We can’t carry a mouthpiece for empire with us.”

Kellyn (under their breath): "I'm already carrying three of those right now."

Alex (snapping): “Then we carry him anyway. If empire wanted him dead, he’d already be ash. Alive means there’s still a chance to turn the signal.”

Jonathan: “Or alive means he’s a beacon dragging us straight to the crown.”

Kellyn (firm, cutting through):
“He spoke of choice. Bow or bleed. That means we’re not already bound. That means covenant still matters.  The body can make more blood."

⸻

Act III — The Test
Later, Kellyn sits beside Sam, pressing a wet cloth to his lips. His eyes flutter open.

Sam (whispering):
“You’ll know him by the antlers of light. He carries no weapon. Only the hum.”

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“And how do you know that?”

Sam’s gaze flickers, pained.
Sam: “Because I already stood there. I already failed. And you will not forgive me.”

Kellyn: "I forgave you when we found you on the riverbank, Sam."

Before he can speak, Sam slumps unconscious again.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of the Premonition
The four gather by the fire.

Jonathan (grim):
“Echoes don’t apologize. That was memory. But not his alone. Somehow… it’s reaching forward.”

Peter stares into the flames, jaw set.
Peter: “Then we make covenant with the warning. Even if it’s empire’s trick, we refuse to walk blind.”

Alex glances at Kellyn, voice raw.
Karp: “And if it’s truth, then we don’t bow. Not to him. Not to anyone.”

Kellyn: “Then covenant stands: whatever waits in red stone, we face it together.”

⸻

Closing Image
Sam lies motionless, shadow stretched across the firelight. The others sit closer than before, not unified in certainty, but bound in choice.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Echoes had tethered us to the past. But when the echo broke forward, it wasn’t despair we chose. It was covenant. Even if the crown waited, we would meet him as one.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 61, Episode 5: The Memory of Tomorrow

Cold Open — Dawn
Sam stirs. His eyes open, clearer than before. He sits up slowly, clutching his head.

Sam (hoarse, startled):
“I said things… didn’t I? About the crown. About antlers of light.”

The group freezes.

Title Card: The Memory of Tomorrow

⸻

Act I — The Fracture
Peter steps forward, arms crossed.
Peter: “You weren’t dreaming. You warned us.”

Sam flinches, guilt flashing across his face.
Sam: “I thought it was empire’s hand, making me relive. But this—this was ahead of me. Ahead of us.”

Jonathan (quiet, wary):
“Then the boundary between past and future is porous. And you’re the crack it’s seeping through.”

⸻

Act II — The Reckoning
Alex crouches by Sam, gaze sharp.
Karp: “If you remember what you saw, then you’re not just a vessel. You’re complicit. What happens when empire twists the vision?”

Kellyn cuts in, voice steady.
Kellyn: “Empire already twisted our vision before we found him.  And it will do it again sooner or later, even without him.  That's why Felix said doubt is a virtue.”

Sam grips the blanket tighter.
Sam: “I don’t want this. But I won’t lie—I remember it as if I already failed.”

Kellyn: "Failure is the norm.  But it is not the end."

⸻

Act III — The Test of Trust
They sit him at the fire, placing hands in turn on his shoulders. Jonathan first, then Peter, then Alex. Each touch heavy with tension.

Sam closes his eyes.
Sam: “The antlers weren’t only light. They bent the air. The hum thickened around him. He looked at me like he already owned me.”

Alex’s jaw tightens.
Karp (low, almost trembling):
“And what did you do?”

Sam (ashamed):
“I bowed.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Memory
Silence. The fire pops.

Kellyn (firm, cutting):
“Then you won’t bow again. We'll help you remember.”

Peter nods once, reluctant but sincere.
Peter: “If he carries the memory, we carry him.”

Jonathan (soft, almost to himself):
“And maybe covenant means rewriting the memory before it happens.”

Sam exhales shakily, leaning into the weight of their hands.

Sam: “Then don’t let me forget. Not again.”

⸻

Closing Image
The four stand behind Sam, hands still on him, firelight casting one shadow that stretches long into the dawn.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He remembered tomorrow, and shame made him tremble. But covenant was not about forgetting. It was about holding. And together, even memory bent.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 61, Episode 6: The Shadow of You

Cold Open — Night Watch
Sam jolts awake from uneasy sleep, gasping. Kellyn sits across the fire, half-dozing. Sam’s eyes lock on them, terror in his voice.

Sam (hoarse whisper):
“You were there. In the vision. But you weren’t yourself.”

Kellyn blinks, uneasy, pulling closer to the fire.

Title Card: The Shadow of You

⸻

Act I — The Fracture of Recognition
Sam’s voice trembles as he recounts.
Sam: “You wore the crown. Not him. Not the figure. You. And the net—our net—it turned black.”

Kellyn stiffens, jaw tight.
Kellyn (measured):
“Bro, you couldn't pay me to do that.  Not even in fish.”

Alex snaps awake at the tone, moving to Kellyn’s side.
Karp: “What exactly did you see, Sam? Details.”

⸻

Act II — The Vision Deepens
Sam grips his temples. His words spill fast.
Sam: “The crown fused into your skin. Antlers through bone. Your voice—your voice wasn’t yours. It was a chorus. Every word bent the air. Even Alex knelt.”

The fire crackles. Alex goes pale, fists clenching.
Jonathan (quietly, almost clinical):
“And yet you stand here, Kellyn, without antlers, without crown. So the vision is not destiny. It’s a lure.”

Peter’s knife rests in his lap.
Peter: “Or it’s a warning of what empire wants most. To hollow them out and wear their skin.”

Kellyn: "As you may recall, you and Alex already spent 30 years trying—and failing—to achieve the same thing."

⸻

Act III — The Push and Pull
Sam (pleading, desperate):
“I need you to believe me. You were… terrifying. And I don’t know if I was watching empire’s design or the future unraveling itself.”

Kellyn (low, steady, though shaken):
“Then look at me now. Breathing. Breaking. Still mine. If empire tries to make me crown, you hold me tighter—not looser."

Alex turns sharply, voice fraying.
Karp: “And if you can’t resist it? If they twist you so far you’re not you anymore?”

Kellyn meets his gaze, unwavering.
Kellyn: “Then you remind me. And if that fails—you stop me. Covenant isn’t blind trust. It’s guardrails.  I weigh 95 pounds.  I think the four of you can handle me."

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Guardrails
The group sits closer than before, shoulders brushing. Sam trembles but doesn’t pull away as Kellyn grips his wrist.

Kellyn: “You saw what they wanted you to see.  Maybe a warning.  Maybe a lie.  Maybe an alternate version we already sidestepped.  Besides, I wouldn't wear a crown for anything.  It would only trigger my trigeminal neuralgia."

Jonathan (solemn):
“If the crown is a parasite, then covenant is the immune system. It only works together.”

Peter finally lays his knife down flat, blade gleaming in firelight.
Peter: “Then let’s swear it. No crown. Not for Kellyn. Not for anyone.”

They nod in turn. The fire hisses like a seal.

⸻

Closing Image
The four lean in around Sam, hands layered one over the other, shadows twisting high across the canyon wall. Kellyn’s face flickers in the firelight—half theirs, half something imagined—and the group holds tighter, refusing to let the vision breathe alone.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire wanted me crowned. Sam saw the antlers, the black net, the kneeling. But covenant was the guardrail, not the throne. If tomorrow sought to claim me, then tonight, they swore to keep me human.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 61, Episode 7: The Long Return

Cold Open — Riverbank Shelter
Morning mist curls around the camp. Sam lies on a makeshift bed of woven reeds, his skin clammy, breath uneven. Kellyn kneels beside him, checking his pulse with quiet precision.

Kellyn (softly):
“He’s holding steady. But not for long if we don’t help him recover.”

The others gather, grim but attentive.

Title Card: The Long Return

⸻

Act I — The Diagnosis
Jonathan examines Sam’s eyes, noting the yellow tinge.
Jonathan: “His system’s overloaded. Fever, weakness, maybe toxins in the water—or just empire’s residue.”

Peter sets down a bundle of gathered herbs.
Peter: “What do we need first? Heat? Food?”

Kellyn shakes their head.
Kellyn: “Water balance. Electrolytes. If we can’t stabilize that, nothing else matters.”

Alex begins stripping reeds for cordage, already improvising a way to hang a steady drip-line over Sam.

⸻

Act II — The Ritual of Care
The group splits tasks.  Kellyn mixes clay, charcoal, and crushed seeds into a paste to bind and draw toxins.  Peter grinds dried mushrooms into powder, cautiously testing trace amounts for possible fever-breaking effects.  Jonathan sings low, steady notes, the vibrations intended to ease Sam’s ragged breathing.  Alex builds a clay-lined trough that channels clean water slowly toward Sam’s lips, drop by drop.

Sam stirs, murmuring nonsense, fragments of his visions spilling out. 

Kellyn wipes his forehead with damp cloth.
Kellyn (quiet, almost a vow): “No more visions tonight. Just rest.”

⸻

Act III — The Strain
The work weighs heavy. Alex snaps when Peter spills some of the powdered mushroom.
Karp (sharp): “Every grain matters!”
Peter (defensive): “I’m not careless. I’m tired.”

Kellyn cuts in firmly.
Kellyn: “Then let’s hold the tiredness together. He doesn’t need our blame.”

The words settle them. Jonathan keeps singing. The hum of care takes over the tension.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Healing
Hours later, Sam’s fever breaks. His skin glistens, chest rising more evenly. He whispers a word—“Water”—and Kellyn steadies the drip-line toward his lips.

Peter exhales like he’s been holding his breath all day. Alex slumps beside Kellyn, watching Sam with shadowed eyes.

Jonathan: “He’ll recover. Slowly. But the body remembers, when it’s given room.”

Kellyn nods, hand still resting on Sam’s arm.

⸻

Closing Image
The four sit in a loose square around Sam’s resting body. Reed mats, clay bowls, and bundles of herbs scatter around them—tools of survival turned into tools of healing.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire scattered poison and called it vision. But covenant called us back to flesh, to fever, to the slow mending of one who’d fallen. In that care, we remembered what tomorrow should be built on.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 61, Episode 8: The Weight of Breath

Cold Open — Shelter Interior
Sam lies propped against reed mats, skin pale but not fever-hot anymore. Kellyn adjusts the water trough. Jonathan hums quietly, keeping the air steady.

Outside, wind rattles loose clay shards. The others glance toward the sound, wary.

Title Card: The Weight of Breath

⸻

Act I — The Patient
Kellyn leans close, coaxing Sam to sip a thick broth of fish, clay-filtered water, and mashed seeds.
Kellyn (gently): “One swallow at a time. Nothing faster.”

Sam obeys, eyes fluttering open. A flicker of recognition crosses his face—but no words yet.

Peter paces at the threshold, knife in hand. Alex finally breaks the silence.
Alex: “You don’t have to stand guard every second.”
Peter: “Yes. I do.”

⸻

Act II — Rumors on the Wind
Jonathan returns from the river with a bundle of reeds. He pauses, voice low:
Jonathan: “Tracks along the bank. Heavy. Too heavy for deer.”

Kellyn frowns.
Kellyn: “Drones?”
Jonathan: “Or worse. The crowned figure. Some here say he moves on silence, leaving no echo but dread.”

Sam stirs, whispering something broken—
Sam: “Crown… burning sky…”
The words make them all fall silent.

⸻

Act III — The Covenant of Recovery
Inside, they resume their rhythm.  Kellyn applies clay paste to soothe Sam’s joints.  Alex reinforces the trough with pitch to stop leaks.  Peter sharpens the knife with fierce, steady strokes, energy funneled into control.  Jonathan braids reeds into mats, every knot a grounding beat.

Sam groans, but the fever doesn’t return. His pulse is steadier now.

⸻

Act IV — The Encroachment
As dusk falls, a low rumble echoes faintly beneath the ground—like machinery stirring. The animals stamp and bleat, nervous.

Kellyn puts a hand to Estrella’s side, whispering.

Kellyn: “Not tonight. Not now. We keep him safe first.”

They draw closer around Sam, building the night’s fire smaller, lower, shielded by clay walls.

⸻

Closing Image
Sam lies at the center of the circle, still breathing shallow but steady. Around him, the four keep watch—knife, hum, clay, fire—all imperfect defenses.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Healing was never separate from threat. The same hands that stirred broth built walls against shadow. And in that double labor, we found the covenant of tomorrow: tend the body, guard the fire.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 61, Episode 9: Fragments and Shadows

Cold Open — Shelter Interior, Morning
Sam shifts against the mats, voice rough but clearer:
Sam: “Plateau… not safe. Crown moves… under.”

Kellyn leans forward, searching his face.
Kellyn: “You saw him?”
Sam just exhales, trembling.

Title Card: Fragments and Shadows

⸻

Act I — First Words
Sam drinks broth with steadier hands. His gaze sharpens, catching Kellyn’s.
Sam: “You… were marked. Long before the sanctuary burned. They built… through you.”

Peter freezes. Alex’s jaw tightens. Jonathan quietly sets down the reed mat he was braiding.

⸻

Act II — The Encroachment
Outside, the sheep start bleating. Estrella stamps, ears pinned back.

Peter rushes out with his knife, Alex right behind him. Kellyn stays by Sam’s side, Jonathan peering through a crack in the clay wall.

The hum rises in the distance—lower, heavier than before. Something unseen but vast is moving closer, pressing through the ground.

⸻

Act III — The Clash of Arcs
Sam grips Kellyn’s wrist with sudden strength.
Sam (urgent): “Don’t… dismantle the crown. Not yet. He feeds on attempts. You have to… unbind yourselves first.”

Kellyn: “Then tell us how. Give us the rest of the map.”

Sam: “Fragments only. The rest comes in the fire.”

Act IV — The Circle Tightens
Peter and Alex return, tense, covered in ash.

⸻

Closing Image
Sam lies at the center, whispering fragments—crown, fire, unbinding—while the others keep their vigil. Outside, silence folds back in, but the ground itself feels awake, listening.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He was no longer only a patient. His words carried shadows of what hunted us. Each breath he took returned us a warning: recover him, and we recover the map. Fail, and the crowned figure would write the ending for us all.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 61, Episode 10: The Map Beneath the Skin

Cold Open — Shelter Interior, Night
Sam jerks awake, whispering as if someone else is speaking through him.
Sam: “Not chains… not walls… threads. You carry them still.”

Kellyn crouches close, voice gentle.
Kellyn: “Then show us where to cut.”

Title Card: The Map Beneath the Skin

⸻

Act I — The Fragments
Sam’s speech comes in bursts.  "Four knots. One at throat, one at heart, one at gut, one at hand.  Empire tied you in hunger. Left you bound to reach for its food, its fire, its touch.  Unbinding is not severing. It is breathing where the knot demands silence.”

Jonathan sketches circles in the dirt, marking where each knot lies on the body.

Jonathan: “He’s describing a map of empire’s bindings — not physical shackles. Nervous system anchors. Ritualized trauma.”

⸻

Act II — The Trial Knot
Peter rolls up his sleeve, volunteering his wrist.
Peter: “Then let’s see if it holds.”

Kellyn takes Peter’s hand, guiding breath into the pulse point. Alex kneels across, pressing his own palm over Peter’s.

Sam, watching, whispers:
Sam: “Don’t pull. Don’t fight. Just listen until the rope slackens.”

Peter trembles, but slowly his breath evens. His hand unclenches. For the first time, he doesn’t look ready to strike.

⸻

Act III — Resistance
The hum surges faintly underfoot, as though empire feels the thread loosening. Estrella snorts, unsettled. The sheep bleat in chorus.

Alex (low, to Kellyn): “If it’s watching us this closely, every knot we unbind will make it push harder.”

Kellyn: “Then it’s proof we’re doing it right.”

Jonathan adds: “And that the crown fears unbinding more than destruction.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Map
Sam fades back into half-sleep, but his last words are clear:
Sam: “Map is not mine. Map is yours. Four knots, four carriers. You must hold each other steady. No one unbinds alone.”

The group sits in a square around him — four figures, four knots, four breaths syncing in the dark.

⸻

Closing Image
Dirt map sketched with four circles glowing in firelight, each circle matched to one of them sitting watch. Their shadows overlap, knotting and un-knotting across the walls.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“The crown bound us with hunger, silence, and fear. But Sam showed us the truth: the knots weren’t empire’s to hold. They were ours to breathe through, to loosen together. And a map written in the body could be read aloud — if only we dared.”

Fade out.
End of Season 61.
