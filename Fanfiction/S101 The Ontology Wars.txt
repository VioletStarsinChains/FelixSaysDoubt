Season 101, Episode 1: The Descent

Opening — Entering the Crack
The rope groans against stone as the group lowers one by one into the fissure. Lamps swing, throwing fractured light across damp walls. The air smells of rust and wet limestone.

Kellyn lands first, knees bending with the impact. Their voice rises upward:
“Solid ground here. Careful—it slopes.”

The others follow, until the whole party is gathered on a narrow ledge. Above, the sky is already a slit of gray. Below—blackness, swallowing light.

⸻

Act I — The Maze of Stone
They advance slowly, feet slipping on wet rock. Every sound echoes strangely—multiplied, stretched, bent.

Sam mutters, “Feels like we’re walking inside an instrument.”

Aaron plucks a low note on a portable tuning fork he brought, just to test. The vibration ricochets back in warped tones—higher, then lower. The cave sings in fragments.

Kyle, uneasy:
“Easy to lose sense of direction in here. Our bodies will lie to us.”

Kellyn nods, hand grazing the wall for anchor.
“That’s why we mark as we go.”

They tie strips of cloth and sprigs of dried sage into the rock cracks, every few dozen steps.

⸻

Act II — The First Collapse
They reach a chamber where the ceiling has partially fallen in. Boulders block the way forward, sharp edges and dust still fresh.

Peter sets his lamp down, running hands along the debris.
“This didn’t fall long ago. Weeks, maybe less.”

Justin presses an ear to the stone.
“There’s air moving through. A hollow behind it. If he’s alive, he could be on the other side.”

Together they shift rocks, but every movement threatens another collapse. The ceiling rains grit, the air choking with it.

Barnard whispers, throat dry:
“One wrong pull, and the whole chamber comes down.”

Kellyn steadies him with a hand on his arm.
“Then we move with patience. Nothing else.”

They dig carefully, opening just enough space to squeeze through.

⸻

Act III — Illusions in the Dark
Beyond, the tunnels twist into narrow veins. The sound of dripping water grows louder—until it almost sounds like words.

Kyle halts, brow furrowed.
“…Did you hear that?”

Voices. Faint, indistinct. Some sound like Gillette’s, calling weakly for help. Others echo in familiar tones—family members, old friends.

Alex grips the rope line hard.
“It’s not him. Not yet. The cave’s playing us. Don’t trust it.”

But Kellyn stiffens.
“What if it is him?”

Jonathan’s voice comes from the dark, strained but close:
“…here…”

The group freezes.

⸻

Closing — The Edge of Belief
The tunnel forks. One path descends sharply, echoing with rushing water. The other angles upward, where the voice seems to beckon.

Peter lights another lamp, holding it between the choices.
“Two ways. One truth. Which do we follow?”

The group stands in silence, breath loud in their ears.

Somewhere in the stone, the voice calls again—half a plea, half a trick.

Fade out. End of Episode 1.

Season 101, Episode 2: The Fork

Opening — The Choice
The group stands at the fork, lamps throwing light against wet stone. The voice echoes again—uncertain whether it’s Gillette’s or an echo of their own memory.

Peter: “If we stay together, we risk missing him. If we split, we risk not coming back. But the trail won’t wait.”

They vote quickly. Two ropes of covenant, diverging.

⸻

Path One — The Descent (Physical Trial)
Team: Peter, Kyle, Barnard, Matt, Tim, Gary.
They take the lower tunnel where the sound of rushing water grows louder.

The ground grows slick, every step treacherous. Moisture drips from stalactites, cold against their necks. At one point the ledge narrows to a knife-edge above a black underground river.

Kyle, steadying Gary as he slips: “This path tests the body. Keep low. Keep breath even.”

A sudden collapse sends boulders tumbling. Matt wrenches his shoulder hauling Barnard clear. They make it through, but the cost is heavy—clothes soaked, lungs burning from dust.

At the end of the descent, they reach a cavern lit by faint bioluminescent moss. The river splits again, vanishing into two tunnels. They camp here, bruised but alive.

⸻

Path Two — The Ascent (Psychological/Mystical Trial)
Team: Kellyn, Alex, Jonathan, Justin, Aaron, Sam, Harry, Taylor.
They climb the upward path, following the uncanny voices.

Every few steps, the cave reshapes sound:
	•	Jonathan hears chords from his own songs, warped and unfinished.
	•	Harry hears a faint choir, like his own voice doubled and reversed.
	•	Alex swears he hears Peter calling, though he knows Peter went the other way.

Kellyn stops, pressing their palm to the wall.
“This isn’t just sound—it’s memory. It’s shaping what we already carry.”

Deeper in, they enter a chamber where faces appear faintly in the damp rock—family members, lost friends, echoes of teachers. Taylor mutters scripture under his breath, shaken:
“The stone itself is bearing witness…”

One wall crumbles suddenly, revealing a narrow passage. Beyond, they hear what sounds like Gillette, clearer than before—labored breathing, faint words. But Alex warns:
“The cave lies. Test every word before we move.”

⸻

Closing — The Split Covenant
Two camps, two trials.
	•	The descent team huddles by the underground river, exhausted, nursing injuries.
	•	The ascent team sits in the chamber of faces, uncertain which voice to trust.

Both groups whisper the same question into the dark:
“Are we closer—or are we being led astray?”

Fade out. End of Episode 2.

Season 101, Episode 3: The First Fork
Opening — The Fork
The group stands in the cavern where the tunnel splits: one path sloping downward toward rushing water, the other spiraling upward into narrowing stone.
Hands linger on shoulders. The rope that bound them unknots. They divide.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“Not goodbye. Just the pattern unfolding. We’ll find each other again.”

The two groups turn—boots scraping, torches flaring—and vanish in opposite directions.

⸻

Descent Team (Peter, Kyle, Barnard, Matt, Tim, Gary)

Act I — Into the River Teeth
The roar of underground water grows louder. They follow the sound until it bursts into view: a fast, black current cutting through jagged stone “teeth.”
The spray burns their lungs with minerals. The footing is slick.

Barnard (grim, muttering):
“This is no river. It’s a mouth.”

Gary slips, a tooth of stone tearing through his sleeve, skin grazed. Peter grabs him, yanking him upright before the water can take him.

Peter (firm):
“Eyes down. One step at a time. Don’t fight the stone—move with it.”

They press onward, every step testing trust in one another’s reflexes.

⸻

Act II — Endurance
The deeper they go, the less food remains. Their packs feel heavier. Muscles burn.
Kyle sets crude snares with rope scraps, hoping to catch blind cave fish. Tim fills flasks with dripping mineral water, whispering to himself like prayer.

Matt (panting, half to himself):
“We’re not supposed to survive here. The place eats us as fast as we enter.”

Peter (shaking his head, steady):
“No. It tempers us. Just don’t let it break your teeth.”

The others lean on that certainty.

⸻

Ascent Team (Kellyn, Alex, Jonathan, Justin, Aaron, Sam, Harry, Taylor)

Act I — The Chamber of Faces
Their spiral tunnel opens suddenly into a wide chamber. Torchlight reveals faces carved into every surface—hundreds of them, life-sized, staring.

Each face seems drawn from memory.
Kellyn freezes. Kristin’s stone eyes glint in shadow.
Alex stiffens: a younger, sharper version of himself snarls back at him.
Taylor whispers names under his breath—congregants, students.

Sam (shaking):
“They’re us. Or what the world saw in us.”

Justin runs a hand across one.
“Or what we feared they saw.”

They pass through, each carrying the weight of their face.

⸻

Act II — Disorientation
The chamber grows tighter, the faces leaning inward, mouths slightly open. Echoes twist their words.

Harry hums a low note, testing the acoustics. Jonathan joins him, weaving harmony. The dissonance lessens, just enough for the group to breathe.

Kellyn (quiet):
“They wanted us to believe we were the faces. But they’re just stone. Nothing more.”

Alex doesn’t answer, but his torch lowers, the snarl-shadow fading.

⸻

Closing Image

Both groups—unaware of each other—pause at ledges.

The Descent Team watches their snares ripple in black current, waiting for a bite.
The Ascent Team rests against carved faces, humming into the stone until silence settles.

In two separate spaces, they whisper the same words:

Kellyn / Peter (simultaneous, unheard by each other):
“We keep going.”

Fade out. End of Episode 3.

Season 101, Episode 4 — The Bones by the River

The underground river roars louder the further they push in. The air tastes of iron, damp and old. Lantern light glints off slick stone.

Matt slips first. His boot skids on the wet rock, and he slams shoulder-first into the wall, gasping. Kyle lunges, but the current tugs Matt toward the rushing black.

Gary: (snapping)
“Pick up the pace! If we drag, this place’ll eat us alive.”

Tim crouches by the riverbank, pointing.
Bones. Not animal. Too long, too narrow. A ribcage collapsed inward, a skull half-buried in silt. The bones look old—fragile as chalk—but they still hold shape.

Tim (low, almost reverent):
“They came this way before us. Maybe looking for the same thing. Maybe running from it. Either way—they didn’t make it.”

Barnard stares, his face pale in the lantern glow. For a moment he looks ready to turn back.

Peter (firm, steady):
“Then we don’t follow their steps. We carve our own. Keep close. Keep breathing.”

They push forward. The walls narrow, the water deepens. The spray turns their breaths ragged. Barnard stumbles, clutching his chest.

Kyle moves toward him instinctively, steadying his arm.
“You’re all right. We’ll pace with you.”

But Gary snaps again, frustration sharp in his voice.
“At this rate, we’ll drown before we even find him.”

That tension cracks the rhythm—until Matt suddenly collapses, knees buckling as the current yanks at his legs.

Barnard doesn’t think. He drops to his knees, grips Matt under the arms, and hauls him up with a strength no one expects from him. His face goes red, veins straining, but he doesn’t let go until Matt is safe against the wall.

Everyone stares, stunned.

Barnard, panting:
“Don’t…don’t write me off yet.”

Peter grins, claps his shoulder once. No more words needed.

They walk on together until the tunnel widens into a cavern glowing with pale, ghostly light.

Fungi climb the walls in brilliant clusters—blue, green, white. They glow like lanterns. At first it feels almost holy.

Then the spores rise, thick as mist. Every breath burns.

Tim presses a cloth over his mouth.
“It’s beautiful—but we can’t stay long.”

Kyle looks around, scanning the floor.
“No…we’ll have to move slow. Careful. These spores choke the lungs if we stir them too much.”

Gary mutters:
“Slow again. Always slow.”

But they all know rushing now would mean death.

The episode closes with the group edging forward, light shimmering eerily on their faces, the spores curling around them like a living thing.

Sudenly, sound rises deeper in the cavern—not water, not stone—something breathing.

Fade out.  End of Episode 4.

Season 101, Episode 5 — The Face in the Stone

The Ascent Team—Kellyn, Alex, Justin, Aaron, Harry, Jonathan, Sam—move through winding stone halls, carrying only small lamps. The air is close, almost too still, like the cave itself is holding its breath.

They enter a wide chamber. The walls are smooth, unnaturally so, as if cut by hand—or something else. And at the far end: a colossal face emerging from the rock.

Sam whispers:
“It looks alive.”

The stone face isn’t whole—it’s fractured, cracked through the forehead and jaw. Fragments shift faintly, like the cave is breathing. The eyes are closed but tremble, as if about to open.

Alex stiffens.
“This is wrong. Stone doesn’t move. Stone doesn’t…”
He trails off, unable to finish.

Kellyn steps closer, gaze sharp.
“I’ve seen this before—in dreams. Not the form, but the feeling. Like a memory pressed into matter. That face—it’s not Gillette, not exactly. But it’s of him.”

Harry edges forward, voice low.
“What if it’s not him trapped—but a mirror of him? Some fragment that broke off when the mine collapsed?”

The group circles it warily. Every word they speak seems to echo back, reshaped, like the stone is answering in riddles.

Jonathan swallows hard.
“When I was his student, he always said art was the imprint of presence. That you could feel the maker even if the body was gone. What if this is that? What if the mine didn’t bury him, it…translated him?”

The stone face cracks wider, a sound like boulders splitting under pressure. Dust rains down. The mouth shifts, opening just enough for a rasping sound to escape.

Aaron steps back, heart pounding.
“That’s not echo. That’s voice.”

The sound swells—a moan, then words, distorted but clear enough:

“Help…me…”

The group freezes.

Kellyn presses their palm to the cold stone cheek, steady, unflinching.
“If it’s you, Gillette…we’re here. But if it’s something else wearing your voice—we’ll know soon enough.”

The stone shudders. For a heartbeat, one eye flickers open, a hollow socket burning with pale fire.

Then the chamber begins to quake, as if the whole mine is waking.

Fade out.  End of Episode 5.

Season 101, Episode 6 — The Chamber of Spores

The Descent Team—Kyle, Peter, Tim, Taylor, Matt, Barnard, Gary—move deeper into the twisting black tunnels. The air grows heavy, damp, tinged with the smell of rot. Their lantern light reveals patches of pale fungus creeping along the walls.

Matt frowns, wiping his sleeve across his mouth.
“It’s in the air. Can you feel it? Like breathing through cloth.”

The passage widens suddenly into a cavern. The floor is blanketed in a soft white film. Above, glowing caps hang like lanterns, weeping golden dust that drifts down in shimmering curtains.

Taylor (in awe):
“It’s beautiful. Like a cathedral.”

Peter (sharp, uneasy):
“It’s a trap. Look—everything’s breathing it in.”

The group halts. Barnard coughs once, twice, then steadies himself. His voice sounds strange, slowed, as if echoing from inside a dream.
“It…slows thought. Not poison, I think. But it…pulls you inward.”

Kyle grips his arm, eyes narrowing.
“Then don’t let it. Anchor yourself here. Look at me, Barnard.”

The spores swirl thicker, like snow. Tim drops to one knee, clutching his chest, muttering fragments of old sermons half-remembered.

Taylor kneels beside him, voice shaking but insistent.
“No—listen. Don’t let the memory pull you under. Covenant is now, Tim. Now.”

Peter stands rigid, fighting the urge to bolt. He sees shapes in the drifting haze—faces from his past, flickering like smoke. His fists clench, ready to strike at phantoms.

Gary, quieter than usual, puts a hand on Peter’s shoulder.
“They’re not here. Just breath and dust. If you hit them, you’ll only be swinging at the air.”

The cavern pulses with strange rhythm—breath in, breath out—like the spores themselves are alive, waiting to choose who succumbs and who resists.

At the far end, a narrow stone arch barely visible through the haze. The way forward.

Kyle raises his voice, cutting through the dream-thick air.
“Eyes on each other. Move together. No one lags. If one of us falters, the others pull them through.”

Slowly, coughing, blinking, leaning on each other for balance, the team begins crossing the spore-laden chamber.

Halfway across, Matt staggers and falls into the white blanket on the ground. Immediately, the spores billow up in a choking cloud, engulfing them all.

Fade out.  End of Episode 6.

Season 101, Episode 7 — The Man in the Rockfall

The Ascent Team—Kellyn, Alex, Jonathan, Justin, Aaron, Sam, Harry—step out of the living stone chamber, hearts still pounding from the uncanny encounter. The tunnel narrows, then widens into a jagged cavern where collapsed stone blocks choke the passage.

The air is stale, still. At first, it looks like just another dead end. But then—

Justin freezes.
“Do you hear that?”

They all stop. A faint sound. Not water. Not wind. A voice—hoarse, echoing, broken by coughs.

Voice (faint, buried):
“…help…”

Kellyn scrambles forward, pulling loose rock aside. Alex joins without hesitation, wedging his shoulder into a slab, veins straining. Sam and Jonathan grab smaller stones, forming a rhythm—pull, pass, stack, repeat.

Finally, a gap opens wide enough to reveal a hollow beyond. A man slumped against the wall, dust-caked, half-buried. His beard is matted with dirt, his hands raw and bleeding. His eyes, though sunken, flicker with sharp recognition.

Jonathan Gillette.

Kellyn staggers back a step, disbelieving.
“Professor?”

Gillette squints at the lantern light.
“Kellyn Clay?” His voice cracks. “I thought—I thought I was the last one left.”

Aaron kneels, offering his water skin. Gillette drinks greedily, coughing between gulps.

Alex steadies him, checking his arms and ribs with surprising gentleness.
“You’re alive. But barely. How long?”

Gillette shakes his head, too weak to measure time.
“The cave-in—it’s been… days? Weeks? I don’t know. Thought the earth meant to keep me.”

Harry, wide-eyed, whispers almost to himself:
“Dragged down to Sheol, and yet still breathing.”

Kellyn crouches low, meeting Gillette’s eyes. Their voice is steady, though shaking underneath.
“We dreamed of you. Taylor and I. That’s how we found you. You weren’t forgotten.”

For the first time, Gillette manages a small, incredulous laugh.
“Dreams. Always thought I’d die by stone or silence. Never thought I’d be… pulled out by dreams.”

The group exchanges glances, torn between relief and dread. They’ve found him—but the tunnels press on, and every sound of shifting rock reminds them the cave might not let them leave so easily.

As Alex and Aaron lift Gillette to his feet, the stone beneath them groans, dust raining down from the ceiling—another collapse building.

Fade out.  End of Episode 7.

Season 101, Episode 8 — The Weight of Stone

Opening — The Warning
The ceiling shudders again, dust cascading like ash. Every heart in the cavern clenches.

Alex (urgent, low):
“We move. Now. If the roof goes, none of us are getting out.”

He and Aaron support Gillette on either side, but it’s clear immediately—the man can barely walk. His legs tremble with every step.

Gillette (rasping, almost apologetic):
“Stone doesn’t give back what it takes. I may slow you more than I’m worth.”

Kellyn steps in sharply:
“Don’t. We didn’t dig you out to bury you again.”

⸻

Act I — The Burden Shared
The group sets into motion. Jonathan takes the lantern in front, scanning for weak points in the passage. Harry follows, humming under his breath, half to calm himself, half to test the echoes of the chamber.

But the rocks shift again—this time louder. The ground trembles. A boulder crashes from the ceiling, sealing the path behind them.

Justin (swallowing panic):
“That was where we came in.”

Silence presses. The only way is forward.

Sam, steady:
“Then forward.”

⸻

Act II — The Carry
Gillette falters, knees buckling. Aaron moves to lift him entirely, but Alex stops him with a hand.

Alex (to all):
“We take turns. No one can carry this weight alone.”

So they rotate—Alex, Aaron, even Harry and Jonathan each taking stretches with Gillette across their backs. Kellyn stays close, offering grounding words, touching Gillette’s wrist each time he drifts toward unconsciousness.

Gillette (whispered, to Kellyn once):
“You always did walk strange paths. Now you walk me out of my grave.”

Kellyn doesn’t answer, just presses their palm to the cold stone beside them—marking the moment.

⸻

Act III — The Narrowing
The tunnel constricts into a low crawlspace. They’ll need to go through single file. The problem: Gillette can’t crawl.

Jonathan (grim):
“Then we drag.”

Alex ties his rope around Gillette’s chest. Justin and Sam push from behind, Alex pulling in front. Gillette groans but doesn’t protest—he’s long past protest. Inch by inch, they force him through, the rock tearing at their clothes and skin.

Harry (panting, a tremor in his voice):
“It’s like the earth wants him back.”

⸻

Closing — The Sound Ahead
They collapse into a wider passage, gasping. For the first time in hours, air stirs against their faces—fresh, cool, carrying the faintest smell of pine.

Kellyn (eyes wide):
“Surface air.”

Relief surges. But then—a sound rides the breeze. Not wind. Not water. A low, rhythmic thrum, like drums beneath the earth. The group stiffens.

Alex (grim):
“We’re not out yet. And we’re not alone.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 8.

Season 101, Episode 9 — Into the Hollow Vein

Opening — The Downward Path
Matt, Kyle, Taylor, Peter, Tim, and Barnard descend into a narrower shaft. The air grows hotter, heavier. Their torches hiss and smoke against the damp walls.

Peter (grimacing):
“Feels like we’re crawling into a furnace.”

Taylor (quiet, reflective):
“Or a forge. Heat is where form is tested.”

The others exchange uneasy looks.

⸻

Act I — The First Trial
The tunnel opens into a chamber where stalactites drip like fangs. At the center: a pit, shallow but wide, filled with dark water that reeks of iron.

Tim kneels, dipping a stick. It comes out coated in a rusty film.
“Not water. Or at least—not water we want.”

They’ll need to cross. The only footing is a series of slick, jagged stones just below the surface, leading across like a broken spine.

Kyle (shaking his head):
“One wrong step and you’re swallowed.”

Matt (grim smile):
“Then we step careful.”

One by one, they go. The stones shift and grind. Halfway through, Peter nearly slips, but Taylor grabs him, steadying him with surprising strength.

⸻

Act II — The Echoing Voices
On the far side, the chamber narrows again—but with every step, whispers rise. At first faint, then swelling until it’s impossible to tell if they come from outside or inside their skulls.

Barnard (grim, voice shaking):
“It’s scripture. Distorted. Words I preached, twisted back at me.”

Kyle presses fingers to his ears, but it doesn’t stop. He hears his own therapeutic voice—patient, calm, weaponized against him. “You only matter when you’re useful.”

Peter growls, slamming his fist against the wall. “It’s lies. It’s nothing but lies.” The whispers cut off instantly at his shout—leaving silence so abrupt it makes them all sway.

Tim (hoarse whisper):
“Maybe that’s the point. Call it out. Break the echo.”

⸻

Act III — The Hollow Vein
They move on, shaken but tighter together now. The passage opens again, this time into a colossal hollow vein of stone streaked with glimmering minerals—reds, silvers, faint greens catching the torchlight. It looks alive, pulsing faintly like breath.

Matt (awe-struck):
“It’s like the mountain has a bloodstream.”

The ground vibrates beneath their feet. A low rumble rises, almost answering him.

Taylor (low, to Kellyn though they’re not there):
“What sets you free today can bind you tomorrow. Are we walking through life… or into the bind?”

The others shift uneasily.

⸻

Closing — The Pulse
They lay down for a brief rest, torches dimmed. But the mineral walls pulse brighter, faint veins of light running like fire through capillaries of rock.

Kyle (uneasy whisper):
“It’s alive. This place—it’s alive.”

The camera pans out—the team tiny figures resting inside what looks disturbingly like the glowing artery of a giant.

Fade out. End of Episode 9.

Season 101, Episode 10 — The Vein’s Demand

Opening — The Vein’s Voice
The Descent Team rests in the glowing artery of stone. The mineral walls pulse brighter, and then a sound emerges—not quite a voice, but vibration shaped into words.

The Vein (resonant, many-toned):
“One may remain. The rest may pass. Give me your weakest—feed the stone, and the path will open.”

The team stiffens. Peter steps forward first, jaw tight.
Peter: “Not happening.”

⸻

Act I — Confrontation with the Demand
Kyle looks stricken, glancing at Taylor and Barnard. “It’s a test. Maybe symbolic. We’re not actually supposed to—”

But Matt cuts him off: “Doesn’t matter. Symbolic or real—it’s still the same trap.”

Tim (steady, almost calm):
“This is how empire spoke. Always demanding a body, a scapegoat, a sacrifice. Always calling it necessary. It isn’t.”

The chamber vibrates harder. The walls pulse red, brighter, angrier.

The Vein:
“Refuse and you remain. Choose, or be entombed.”

⸻

Act II — Walking Out
The group exchanges looks. Taylor exhales slowly, voice strong despite his trembling hands.
“Then entomb us. Because there is no covenant in this demand.”

Peter (snarling):
“You think we’re afraid of rock? We’ll walk until the stone gives way.”

And with that, he strides forward into the tunnel mouth ahead. The others follow, torches held high.

The walls quake as if trying to close in, the light flaring hot and blinding—then abruptly, everything stills.

The pulse goes dark. The vein no longer glows. They pass through into silence.

⸻

Closing — The Aftermath
The tunnel ahead curves gently upward. The air is cooler, cleaner. For the first time in hours, they can breathe freely.

Barnard (quietly, almost to himself):
“Not every living thing deserves obedience. Sometimes the only holy act is refusal.”

The others walk on, backs straightened, their steps lighter—because they didn’t just reject the vein’s demand. They rejected the empire’s logic written into the stone.

Fade out.  End of Episode 10.

Season 101, Episode 11 — The Joining of Paths

Opening — The Vein’s Answer
The Descent Team pushes forward into silence, torches flickering. Then the vein pulses once more—this time not angry, but shuddering, almost like laughter in the stone.

The Vein (a deep quake):
“Refusal is still choice. Choice is still offering.”

The walls split open to the left, molten light spilling out as if the rock itself were tearing.

⸻

Act I — The Passageway
Peter: (half growl, half laugh) “Guess it didn’t like being told no.”
Kyle: “Or it wanted us to think we won. Same difference. We keep moving.”

The new tunnel is unnervingly smooth, almost too perfect—like a corridor bored by an enormous drill. The group hesitates, but the old path seals behind them.

They follow the glowing fissure upward.

⸻

Act II — Convergence
At the same time, the Ascent Team—Kellyn, Alex, Jonathan, Justin, Harry, and Gillette—moves through a narrow chute above, torches throwing long shadows. They hear the quake below, the rumble echoing up through their boots.

Suddenly, the floor buckles, and a jagged crack opens before them. Heat rises, light spills through—and then voices. Familiar voices.

Kellyn (stunned, leaning over the fissure): “Peter? Kyle? Tim?”

The two groups lock eyes through the opening as dust rains down.

Alex (with audible relief): “They found us.”

⸻

Act III — The Joining
The stone parts further, collapsing the barrier into a sloping passage. One by one, they scramble across, hands clasping, bodies pulling each other up.

The moment is rough, chaotic, and full of noise—but when the dust clears, the two halves of the community stand together again, torches intermingled.

Barnard (breathing hard, but smiling):
“Seems the vein wanted us united, whether by sacrifice or by defiance.”

Kellyn shakes their head firmly.
“No. That wasn’t the vein. That was us. We chose, and it had to bend.”

The group presses on—together now, for the first time since the fork.

Fade out. End of Episode 11.

Season 101, Episode 12 — The Pause Underground

Opening — Making Camp
The joined company finds a wide, flat chamber where water drips steadily from the ceiling into shallow pools. The air is damp but breathable, the stone smooth enough to sit on without tearing clothes.

They set down packs, light a small controlled fire with resin-soaked sticks, and boil water in a dented pot. For the first time since splitting paths, the whole group sits together again.

⸻

Act I — Silent Relief
No one speaks at first. Steam curls upward. Boots are unlaced, bruises pressed, bandages checked. The only sound is water hissing as it drips into the fire.

Alex (finally, low):
“Feels like we’ve been gone a year.”

Kellyn (half-smile, exhausted):
“And found each other again by accident.”

Peter passes a strip of dried fish around. Hands take it, no words needed.

⸻

Act II — Story Exchange
Gradually, talk begins.  The Ascent team describes the living stone chamber—how it seemed to breathe and whisper. The Descent team shares the vein’s demand for sacrifice, and their refusal.

Jonathan: "Funny, isn’t it? Both paths tested us, just differently. One wanted belief, the other wanted blood.”

Kyle: “And neither got what it asked for.”

The group chuckles softly, tension easing.

⸻

Act III — Grounding Together
Kellyn lies down on their side, head against folded arms.

Kellyn: “We should rotate watch, but… let’s not make it about paranoia. Just about pacing. Who’s strong enough to take first?”

Matt: “I will. My legs aren’t good for sleep right now anyway.”
