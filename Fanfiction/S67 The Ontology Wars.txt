Season 67, Episode 1: The Wall Becomes a Door

Cold Open — The New Room
The smaller room glows with candlelight. Fresh clay still damp in places, but the handprints shine like blessings. A woven rug covers the floor, the loom’s first true gift. Kellyn and Alex stand inside, side by side. The others gather at the threshold.

Jonathan (softly, with a musician’s reverence):
“A wall divides, but a door invites. Tonight, this room is your door.”

Title Card: The Wall Becomes a Door

⸻

Act I — The Preparation
Peter smooths the clay one last time, pressing flowers from the plateau into its surface. Sam, pale but steady, arranges candles along the edges, their flames throwing warm shadows. Gary ties lengths of braided yarn above the doorway like streamers.

Kellyn watches Alex, and Alex watches Kellyn — both quiet, both trembling, not from fear but the weight of recognition.

⸻

Act II — The Vows
No officiant, no script. Just the two of them, facing each other.

Kellyn (voice low, steady):
“I survived the empire. I survived myself. But covenant is not survival. Covenant is life. I choose you in life.”

Alex (hoarse, but clear):
“I thought love was control. You taught me it’s release. I choose to be released, and I choose to release you. Always.”

Peter steps forward, placing their joined hands together, sealing them with a strip of cloth. Jonathan hums a tone, deep and resonant, filling the small space.

⸻

Act III — The Witnesses
The others speak, each in turn:

Peter (gruff, almost embarrassed):
“You’ve both carried me more times than I can count. Let me carry this for you — my blessing.”

Jonathan:
“Two voices found each other in silence. May your song never end.”

Sam (whispering, fragile but sincere):
“Even empire could not break you apart. That means something greater than empire holds you.”

Gary (half a grin, half serious):
“If covenant can make you two work, then maybe the world’s not as hopeless as I thought.”

⸻

Act IV — The First Night
The group leaves, one by one, stepping back into the larger room. The doorflap lowers. Inside, Alex and Kellyn kneel together, the candles flickering around them.

They lean in, foreheads touching, hands still bound in cloth.

Kellyn (whisper):
“Two rooms. One body.”

Alex (whisper, breaking into a smile):
“One covenant.”

⸻

Closing Image
From outside, the firelight flickers through the clay wall, their shadows cast together, indistinguishable.

Narration (Jonathan’s voice, like a benediction):
“Empire made love a contract. Covenant made it a vow. And when the wall became a door, they stepped through — not away from us, but deeper into us all.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 67, Episode 2: The First Lamb

Cold Open — Sanctuary Shelter, Night
The ewe paces, restless, bleating softly. Jonathan kneels beside her, hands steady, voice low and calm. Peter stands nearby with a lantern, Sam and Gary hovering uncertainly. From behind the clay wall, the muffled sound of laughter and intimacy drifts — Alex and Kellyn’s world apart.

Jonathan (murmuring to the ewe):
“You’re not alone. We’re here. Breathe. Let it come in its own time.”

Title Card: The First Lamb

⸻

Act I — The Waiting
The ewe lies down, straining. Peter grips the lantern tighter, nervous.

Peter (hoarse whisper):
“Should we… get them?”

Jonathan shakes his head firmly.
“They’re busy. And she trusts us right now. That’s what matters.”

Sam kneels opposite Jonathan, holding a clean cloth. Gary chews his lip, pacing.

Gary (half-joking, half-serious):
“Never sold livestock before. Closest I’ve come to a delivery was a pitch meeting.”

The others glare. He quiets, sheepish.

⸻

Act II — The Delivery
The ewe pushes, a wet cry breaking the stillness. Jonathan guides gently, murmuring encouragement. Sam wipes sweat from his own brow, mirroring the ewe’s struggle. Finally, a slick, shivering lamb slips free.

Jonathan (soft, reverent):
“There. Life finds a way.”

He clears the airway with practiced hands. The lamb coughs, then bleats, thin but insistent. Relief floods the room.

⸻

Act III — The First Breath
The ewe licks the lamb clean. Jonathan rubs it dry with the cloth, then places it close to the udder. The lamb wobbles, then latches, drinking eagerly.

Sam (awed whisper):
“It’s… so small.”

Peter exhales, tension leaving his shoulders.
“And loud.”

Gary, quieter now:
“That sound is better than any joke I know.”

⸻

Act IV — The Naming
They sit together, lantern glow soft around them.

Jonathan (to the group):
“She was born tonight. In covenant. In warmth. She needs a name worthy of that.”

Sam suggests something gentle. Peter mutters something practical. Gary throws out something absurd. Jonathan smiles faintly, letting the names hang like possibilities in the air.

From behind the wall, Alex and Kellyn’s muffled laughter drifts again — life continuing, threads weaving tighter.

Jonathan (quietly, almost to himself):
“Whatever she’s called, she’s the proof we keep going.”

⸻

Closing Image
The ewe rests, the lamb curled at her side. The group sits watchful, ringed in lantern light. In the background, the faint hum of covenant laughter carries through the wall.

Narration (Jonathan’s voice):
“Empire claimed the future as its weapon. But life arrived on its own terms — not forced, not owned, only given. The first lamb was not ours, but we were hers.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 67, Episode 3: After the Wall

Cold Open — Sanctuary Morning
Sunlight filters through cracks in the clay. The ewe lies curled, the lamb pressed to her side. Peter, Sam, Jonathan, and Gary whisper quietly over the scene. From behind the clay wall, footsteps stir — Alex and Kellyn step out together, hair tousled, faces softer than the others have ever seen them.

Kellyn (blinking at the light, then at the lamb): “…What did we miss?”

Title Card: After the Wall

⸻

Act I — The Re-Entry
Jonathan smiles faintly, eyes tired but bright.
Jonathan: “Only a birth.”

Alex arches a brow at the understatement.
Peter (gruff, but smiling): “First lamb’s already louder than the rest of us.”

Kellyn kneels by the ewe, running a gentle hand over the lamb’s tiny flank. The newborn bleats softly and presses against Kellyn’s palm.

Kellyn (low, almost reverent):
“Life keeps showing up whether we’re ready or not.”

⸻

Act II — The Quiet Shift
Later, breakfast. Flatbread, roasted seeds, dried herbs. Alex sits close beside Kellyn, their shoulders brushing. Every movement between them is unspoken, practiced overnight.

Sam watches with quiet curiosity. Gary elbows Peter, whispering a crude joke. Peter doesn’t laugh, but his mouth twitches.

Jonathan (settling beside them all):
“Strange, isn’t it? One wall changes the air. You two come back different, and somehow… so do we.”

Kellyn glances at Alex, then at the lamb curled in the corner.
Kellyn: “Walls can divide. Or they can give you a space to choose each other again.”

⸻

Act III — The Conversation
Later still, Alex and Kellyn sit apart near the riverbed, voices low.

Kellyn (steady):
“Did you ever think we’d make it here? Not just alive, but like this?”

Alex exhales, jaw tight. Then softer:
“No. I thought survival was conquest. That intimacy was another form of ownership. And then…you.”

Kellyn watches him.
Kellyn: “And now?”

Alex (hoarse, almost breaking):
“Now I know covenant is what I feared most. And it terrifies me still — because I can’t lose it without losing myself.”

Kellyn lays a hand over his. 
Kellyn: "Not losing yourself.  Just losing empire's fantasy of who you were supposed to be."

⸻

Act IV — The Return
Back at the shelter, the others gather. The lamb totters on new legs, wobbling into the circle. Everyone laughs — even Peter, briefly unguarded.

Jonathan (smiling faintly):
“She’ll grow in our shadow. But she’ll grow.”

Kellyn, eyes on the lamb, echoes softly:
“And so will we.”

⸻

Closing Image
The group encircles the lamb, laughter and quiet awe mingling. Alex’s hand rests against Kellyn’s back — tentative, unhidden. Two walls stand: one clay, one covenant. Both holding.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“The lamb came in the night, and so did we. What empire never understood was this: survival was never enough. To live is to choose — and to keep choosing.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 67, Episode 4: The Softest Thread

Cold Open — Sanctuary Dusk
The lamb bleats faintly in the other room, its sound like a heartbeat against clay walls. The others murmur, tending the fire. Alex and Kellyn slip outside, settling onto a flat stone warmed by the day’s sun.

Kellyn leans back, watching the first stars. Alex sits beside them, close but not pressing.

Kellyn (quiet, almost teasing):
“You’re not used to quiet, are you?”

Alex (after a pause, hoarse):
“Not when it means being seen.”

Title Card: The Softest Thread

⸻

Act I — The First Touches
Kellyn traces idle shapes in the dust with their fingertip. Alex watches, then slowly covers Kellyn’s hand with his. His thumb moves in the same rhythm, syncing without force.

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“You’ve spent your life knotting everything tight. But some things don’t need knots. They hold because they’re soft.”

Alex’s eyes close. His shoulders lower, the tension giving way.

⸻

Act II — The Confession
Silence stretches before Alex exhales.

Alex (low, breaking):
“When you sobbed that night, I thought it would undo me. Not because it was weakness, but because it was… proof. You trusted me enough to fall apart. And I realized I’ve never trusted anyone enough to do the same.”

Kellyn turns, laying their forehead against his.
Kellyn (whisper):
“You don’t have to carry control to be carried. Try me.”

⸻

Act III — The Release
Alex trembles, then lets go — breath shuddering, jaw loosening, his weight sagging into Kellyn’s arms. He doesn’t weep, but the stillness itself is staggering.

Kellyn wraps him tighter, grounding.
Kellyn: “See? You’re still here. And so am I.”

Alex (hoarse, almost reverent):
“Then maybe this is covenant’s softest thread. Not holding you — being held.”

⸻

Alex shifts, pulling back slightly as though to excuse himself. Kellyn steadies him with a hand at his chest.

Kellyn (soft, certain):
“Don’t go. Not when you’re finally here.”

Alex’s breath falters, then he nods.

⸻

Act IV — The Reach
Kellyn slides closer, their knee brushing Alex’s. His body tenses at the contact, then gradually melts into it. Kellyn guides his hand down, lacing their fingers, letting the warmth pass between palms.

Kellyn (whisper, steady):
“This is what covenant feels like in the body. No contracts. No price. Just presence.”

Alex exhales, trembling.

⸻

Act V — The Grounding
Kellyn leans against him, tracing slow circles on his arm. The stone under them anchors every shift, every breath. Alex leans back, letting Kellyn’s touch move down his chest, following the rise and fall of breath.

Alex (low, hoarse):
“I thought want was only hunger. But this—this is something else.”

Kellyn (soft, teasing):
“Then stop thinking. Just feel.”

⸻

Act VI — The Deepening
Their closeness builds, breath syncing, movements slower but surer. Kellyn’s touch travels further — grounding, coaxing, exploring. Alex lets go of restraint, leaning into the rhythm, letting his body answer without calculation.

His head tips back; Kellyn presses closer, matching his tremor with steadiness.

Alex (whisper):
“I’ve never been more afraid. Or more alive.”

Kellyn (against his skin):
“Good. Fear means you’re finally here.”

⸻

Alex’s hands hover uncertainly, as though asking permission. Kellyn takes them, guiding them down with calm certainty.

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“Not to hold me down. Not to control. Just… be with me.”

His breath stutters, then he obeys.

⸻ 

Their movements are exploratory, slow but charged. Kellyn traces Alex’s ribs, guiding his breath back to rhythm. Alex mirrors the motion, tentative but more certain with every pass.

Kellyn (whisper):
“See? Nothing breaks. You don’t have to measure. You don’t have to earn it.”

Alex’s voice is rough, caught between awe and need.
Alex: “And if I take too much?”

Kellyn leans in, lips brushing his ear.
Kellyn: "Then I’ll tell you. That’s covenant.”

⸻ 

Clothing loosens, barriers fall away. Their bodies align, skin against skin, warmth shared with unhurried intensity. Alex trembles but doesn’t retreat. Kellyn steadies him, coaxing him into rhythm.

Each sound — gasp, sigh, whispered name — becomes part of the ritual.

Alex (hoarse):
“I thought I could only want if it meant control. But this—this is want as surrender.”

Kellyn (low, certain):
“This is want as trust.”

⸻ 

The intimacy sharpens, cresting with urgency. Their movements lose all pretense of control, collapsing into raw need. Alex clings as though the world might end if he lets go.

Kellyn holds him through it, grounding every tremor, whispering steady words as the storm peaks.

Alex (shaken, whispering into Kellyn’s neck):
“I never believed this could be mine. That anyone would want me like this.”

Kellyn (soft, fierce):
“I do. Not because of empire. Not because of what you’ve done. Because you’re you. And you’re here.”

⸻ 

Closing Image
Two figures tangled together on the warm stone, sweat cooling in the moonlight, breath slowly evening. No chains, no contracts, no empire — only covenant, unbound at last.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire turned hunger into chains. Covenant turned it into touch — not to bind, but to unbind. And in that night’s warmth, we learned what it meant to want without fear.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 67, Episode 5: The Cooling Embers

Cold Open — Shelter Room
The air is heavy with warmth and breath. Kellyn untangles slowly, reaching for a cloth and a basin of water set aside earlier. Alex stirs, body tense as though bracing for judgment.

Kellyn (gentle, steady):
“Stay. You don’t have to move yet.”

Alex exhales, a tremor breaking through his chest.

Title Card: The Cooling Embers

⸻

Act I — The First Care
Kellyn dips the cloth, wrings it out, and wipes carefully along Alex’s chest, down his arm, across his temple. Alex watches in silence, every touch making his shoulders drop another fraction.

Kellyn (soft):
“You always tried to carry me by force. But see? I can hold you like this too. You don’t have to fight for it.”

Alex (hoarse, barely audible):
“I don’t know how to deserve this.”

Kellyn presses the cloth to his lips, then replaces it with a kiss.
Kellyn: “Good. Because it isn’t about deserving.”

⸻

Act II — The Cooling Breath
They lie down again, limbs twined loosely. Kellyn guides Alex’s breath, hand rising and falling against his chest.

Kellyn (quiet, rhythmic):
“In… out. Just follow me. No need to think.”

Alex’s breath catches, then syncs. The shaking eases, leaving something softer. His voice cracks.

Alex: “I was so sure silence would mean absence. But this silence feels… whole.”

Kellyn (whisper): “It’s covenant silence. Not emptiness. Not void. Just us.”

⸻

Act III — The Grounding
Kellyn fetches a blanket, pulling it around them both. Alex burrows in without protest, forehead pressed against Kellyn’s collarbone.

Kellyn rubs circles into his back, patient, steady.
Kellyn: “You gave empire your brilliance. But you never let anyone hold your fragility. That’s what we’re mending now.”

Alex nods against Kellyn’s skin, tears dampening their chest.

Alex (muffled):
“Then don’t let go. Not tonight.”

Kellyn (firm): "I won’t.”

⸻

Closing Image
Blanket-wrapped forms on the stone floor, moonlight silvering their breaths in sync. A basin of water stands half-full beside them, the cloth folded neat. No empire. No proving. Only presence, cooling and alive.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire tried to burn us through. But after fire comes water; after want comes care. And in the cooling embers, covenant held — not through strength, but through staying.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 67, Episode 6: The Plateau Morning

Cold Open — Sunrise on the Plateau
Red cliffs catch the first light, shadows still long. The five humans and Gary sit loosely gathered around a low fire, Estrella tied nearby, the sheep grazing scrub. The night’s silence lingers, carrying the faint hush of wind.

Jonathan (stretching his arms):
“Plateau mornings always start raw. Air so dry it feels older than you.”

Peter snorts softly, rubbing his eyes.
Peter: “Older, harsher, and less forgiving. But at least it’s honest.”

Title Card: The Plateau Morning

⸻

Act I — The Shared Table
Sam stirs the pot of grain slowly, movements steadier than the week before. Gary cracks a joke about how it looks like “apocalypse oatmeal,” and for once, everyone chuckles.

Sam (quiet, but with more strength):
“Doesn’t matter what it looks like. It’s food. And food means we keep going.”

Jonathan nods, taking the pot from him with care. Alex and Kellyn exchange a glance — soft, private, but not hidden. Peter notices, his face tight for a moment before he sighs and looks away.

⸻

Act II — The Tensions and Threads
Peter (half-grumbling as he sharpens his knife):
“We’ve carried too much weight for too long. The plateau doesn’t care about our rituals. It cares about endurance.”

Jonathan (dryly, measured):
“Endurance without ritual is just survival. And survival alone breaks people.”

Gary, fiddling with yarn scraps, pipes in with mock drama:
“So what you’re saying is: we can either knit ourselves together or unravel one by one.”

Everyone laughs again, but the tension lingers in Peter’s jaw.

⸻

Act III — The Shadows Between
The group splits into tasks: Jonathan tends the fire, Sam measures their dwindling salt, Peter checks the sheep, Gary experiments with braiding rope, and Kellyn brushes Estrella. Alex hovers nearby, steady in presence.

Peter (under his breath, almost to Estrella): “They’re too soft. We’ll crack before we reach real safety.”

Jonathan, overhearing, replies without heat: “Or maybe softness is what keeps us from breaking.”

Kellyn turns, catching Peter's eyes.
Kellyn (quiet):
“Just ask me for what you need, Peter."

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Morning
Breakfast bowls are passed hand to hand. Each person takes their share in silence before someone — Sam, surprisingly — breaks it.

Sam (looking around, voice steady):
“You’re all still here. After everything. That’s the only proof I need that it’s worth staying.”

Peter looks at him, startled. Jonathan half-smiles. Gary clinks his bowl against Sam’s in mock-toast.

Alex, eyes on Kellyn, murmurs:
“Then let’s eat, and keep moving.”

⸻

Closing Image
Six figures circled against the wide plateau sky, bowls steaming in their hands, animals grazing nearby. Not sanctuary, not certainty — but threads holding in the open wind.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“The plateau tested us with silence and stone. But morning brought covenant in simpler forms — food, rope, breath, and presence. Not enough to guarantee safety. But enough to begin.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 67, Episode 7: The Weave of Quiet

Cold Open — Midday Rest
The sun beats high. A tarp of stitched fabric and rope stretches across poles, casting shade. The group sits in loose sprawl beneath it, the animals dozing nearby. No one speaks at first — just the sound of wind through dry brush, the occasional bleat, the rustle of Gary idly braiding scraps of yarn.

Title Card: The Weave of Quiet

⸻

Act I — The Shade
Peter leans against Estrella’s flank, eyes closed, knife in his lap. Jonathan sketches in the dirt with a stick, crude lines tracing ridges and imagined paths. Sam, still thin, sits wrapped in a blanket, watching the horizon.

Sam (soft, almost to himself):
“Funny how silence isn’t empty. Feels heavier than words.”

Jonathan (without looking up):
“Silence holds. Like the plateau itself.”

Kellyn glances at Alex, who gives the smallest nod — an unspoken agreement.

⸻

Act II — The Threads of Memory
Gary, finally breaking the hush, holds up his knotted yarn.
Gary: “It’s terrible. But it’s something.”

Peter cracks an eye open, smirks.
Peter: “That’s how everything begins. Terrible, but something.”

Sam chuckles hoarsely, and for a moment, the quiet shifts into warmth.

Kellyn (gently):
“Every knot holds. Even the messy ones. Maybe especially those.”

⸻

Act III — The Balances
The group eats a light meal of roasted roots and grains, bowls passed hand to hand. Conversation stays thin, more gesture than speech — a shared glance when the pot runs low, a nod when the donkey brays, an absent smile when a sheep noses close.

Jonathan sets his sketch aside.
Jonathan (low):
“Sometimes I think empire can’t follow us here. Not into this kind of stillness.”

Peter (dry):
“They always find a way.”

Alex looks up from his bowl, steady.
Alex: “Then we’ll keep finding one too.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Shade
As the heat fades, the group reclines under the tarp, drifting toward rest. Kellyn stretches out first, head pillowed on folded cloth. One by one, the others settle nearby — Peter with Estrella, Jonathan with his stick sketch, Sam wrapped tight in his blanket, Gary muttering one last half-joke before silence.

Alex remains sitting upright, watching the horizon until Kellyn reaches back blindly, fingers brushing his wrist. 

Kellyn: "Come here, Fish Man."

He exhales and finally lies down.

⸻

Closing Image
Six figures sprawled in quiet shade, breath slow, animals dozing nearby. No speech, no strategy — only silence woven into presence, fragile but intact.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire hunted us through noise and signal, through hunger and fire. But in the plateau’s silence, we found another weave. Not speech. Not defense. Just presence. And in presence, covenant deepened still.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 67, Episode 8: The Edge of the Plateau

Cold Open — Breaking Camp
The tarp is folded, ashes scattered from the firepit. Packs creak onto backs. Estrella shifts restlessly, tugging against the rope, as though she already senses the day ahead. The sheep cluster tight, bleating in the dry wind.

Kellyn (quiet, steady): “Time to move.”

No one argues. The silence of yesterday has turned brittle, ready to snap.

Title Card: The Edge of the Plateau

⸻

Act I — The Climb
The trail narrows. Loose stone slides underfoot, each step a gamble. Jonathan goes first, map tucked against his chest. Peter follows, steady but grim. Behind, Gary mutters half-jokes that fall flat, more nervous tic than humor.

Sam stumbles once, breath ragged. Alex catches him by the elbow, steadying.

Alex (low, firm):
“One step at a time. That’s all it asks.”

Sam nods, pale but holding on.

⸻

Act II — The Crack in the Sky
Halfway up, a low rumble rolls through the plateau — not thunder, but something deeper, metallic. A shadow passes overhead. Kellyn squints upward.

Kellyn:
“Drones. Not close, but circling.”

Peter curses under his breath, knife clutched tighter. Jonathan presses flat to the rock, calculating lines of sight.

Jonathan (grim):
“They’ll see us if we crest too soon.”

Kellyn (steady):
“Then we stay close to the wall. Slow, but hidden.”

⸻

Act III — The Squeeze
The path funnels into a narrow chute, barely wide enough for Estrella to squeeze through. The donkey balks, braying loud enough to echo. The sheep scatter against the rock.

Gary (urgent whisper):
“We’ll never make it quiet—”

Peter steps forward, placing a hand on Estrella’s neck. His voice drops, almost gentle.
Peter: “Easy. Easy. Just us.”

The braying stops. Slowly, painfully, they push through, scraping packs against stone.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Pressure
They emerge onto a ledge just as the drones circle closer. Jonathan raises a hand, signaling stillness. The group presses flat against the cliff face, every breath measured.

Kellyn feels Alex’s hand brush theirs — not strategy, not command, just contact. Presence.

The shadow of the drones passes. They don’t stop. The hum fades into the distance.

Only then does anyone breathe.

⸻

Closing Image
Six figures and a small cluster of animals pressed against stone, sweat streaking, breath shallow — but still intact. Above, the sky stretches vast and indifferent.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“The plateau pressed. The drones hunted. But we moved still — not by speed, not by strength, but by the smallest covenant: to hold, to breathe, and not to break.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 67, Episode 9: The Second Pass

Cold Open — Plateau Winds
Morning burns hot and dry. The wind picks up grit, stinging eyes and skin. The group huddles low as the sheep bleat in protest. Overhead, the faint hum returns — nearer, hungrier.

Kellyn (low):
“They’re back.”

The words tighten every face.

Title Card: The Second Pass

⸻

Act I — The Cover
Jonathan pulls the group toward a shallow overhang. Not much protection, but enough shadow to blur outlines. Estrella brays, restless, hooves clattering on stone.

Peter (sharp whisper):
“She won’t stay still.”
Kellyn (steady):
“Then we move as one. A still shadow draws the eye more than a shifting one.”

Alex nods, calculating angles. He presses Mylar sheets against the stone, pinning edges with rocks. The wind rattles them, but their shimmer scatters the drone’s tracking beams.

⸻

Act II — The Scan
The hum crescendos. Red light fans out across the plateau, sweeping. Kellyn holds their breath as the beam crawls closer, dragging heat along their skin.

Sam (hoarse whisper):
“If it locks on—”
Jonathan (cutting in):
“Then we scatter, and none of us makes it far.”

Kellyn (fierce, whispering):
“Then we don’t scatter.”

The beam lingers, grazing the edge of the Mylar. For a heartbeat, it halts. Then, as though confused, it jerks sideways and sweeps away.

⸻

Act III — The Climb
They push uphill, every step deliberate. The sheep stumble, coats catching burrs. Peter shoulders two at once, jaw clenched.

Gary, sweating hard, mutters through his teeth:
“If I live through this, I swear, I’ll never make another motivational video again.”

Sam gives the smallest laugh — more like a cough — but the tension cracks just enough to keep moving.

⸻

Act IV — The Narrow Escape
The drones shift course, circling back. Jonathan’s voice goes urgent.
Jonathan: “Down! Into the cleft!”

They slide into a jagged crack in the rock, narrow enough to scrape packs. The drones sweep overhead, beams slicing just beyond reach.

Kellyn presses their back to Alex’s chest, Peter shields Sam, Jonathan clamps a hand on Gary’s shoulder to keep him still. The hum roars… then fades.

Silence. Only heartbeats.

⸻

Closing Image
The group huddled tight in the cleft, breath mingling, eyes wide. Beyond, the drones hover in the distance, patient predators waiting for one mistake.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire circled above, patient and merciless. But we pressed closer, bodies braced against stone. Survival was not flight. It was the vow to remain one body, even as the sky hunted.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 67, Episode 10: The Trap

Cold Open — Scrubland Edge
The plateau opens into a sparse scrubland. Wind rattles dry stems. Estrella snorts and halts, ears pinned. Ahead, a strangled cry cuts through the stillness.

The group approaches cautiously. A man lies twisted on the ground, one leg clamped in a rusted bear trap, blood seeping dark into the dust. His face is pale, jaw set in a rictus of pain.

Kellyn (flat, disbelieving):
“…Elon Musk.”

Title Card: The Trap

⸻

Act I — The Assessment
Elon groans, trying to pry the trap open with trembling hands. His eyes flick to the group — suspicion, then pleading.

Elon (ragged):
“Took one step too far. Haven’t been able to move for hours. You going to help or let me rot?”

Jonathan kneels, examining the rusted jaws.
Jonathan (measured):
“If we spring it wrong, it’ll shred the leg completely.”

Peter’s jaw tightens.
Peter: “Maybe that’s what he deserves.”

Sam looks away, uneasy. Kellyn crouches closer, eyes steady.
Kellyn: “Deserve has nothing to do with covenant.”

⸻

Act II — The Release
Alex gathers Mylar strips, weaving them into makeshift padding to shield the jagged steel. Jonathan guides, Peter braces, Kellyn presses weight down at the hinge.

On a count of three, the trap snaps open. Elon screams, collapsing back. His leg is mangled but not beyond saving.

Sam immediately begins tearing cloth for bandages. Gary mutters a prayer under his breath, hands shaking.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
As the bleeding is stanched, Elon speaks through clenched teeth.
Elon: “I was following the hum. Thought I could… repurpose it. Empire’s leash can be rewired. But I was wrong.”

Peter’s voice is ice.
Peter: “You always think you can repurpose suffering. That’s why the leash exists.”

Elon doesn’t answer, just stares at the sky, sweat beading his face.

⸻

Act IV — The Decision
Night falls. The group gathers by the fire, Elon half-conscious nearby, leg wrapped tight.

Jonathan (quiet, to the others):
“If we carry him, he slows us down. If we leave him, empire finds him first.”

Kellyn:
“I'm not leaving anyone to die out here.”

The fire crackles. No one answers yet.

⸻

Closing Image
Elon’s pale face in firelight, the steel trap discarded nearby, jaws still gaping open. Above, drones pass in the distance, their hum carrying across the plateau.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We freed him, but some traps remain after the jaws are open. Empire forged his from hunger and pride. But now, one more life fell into our hands.”

Fade out.
End of Season 67.
