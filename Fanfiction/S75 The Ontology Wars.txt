Season 75, Episode 1: The Hero’s Capture

Cold Open — Shelter at Dawn
Morning light spills across the shelter floor. The group stirs slowly awake — except Elon’s spot is empty. His blanket is crumpled, cold.

Gary (blinking, groggy): “…He’s gone.”

Alex is already on his feet, scanning the horizon. Peter curses under his breath. Kellyn kneels, brushing fingers across the dirt — faint tracks leading away.

Title Card: The Hero’s Capture

⸻

Act I — The Realization
They eat in uneasy silence, no one really tasting the food. Jonathan sharpens his staff, eyes hard.

Peter (low, bitter):
“I told you he’d do something reckless.”

Gary frowns.
Gary: “He wanted to prove himself. To matter. That’s not a crime.”

Alex: “It’s not a crime. But it could be a death sentence.”

Ashwin, perched on the shelter’s ridge, suddenly shrieks — then takes wing, veering south.

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“She knows something. We follow.”

⸻

Act II — The Trail
They trek across dry plateau brush, following Ashwin’s looping cries. Elon’s tracks reappear in patches of dust and stone. The trail winds downward, toward dark clefts in the canyon wall.

Jonathan (grim):
“He wasn’t wandering. He was heading somewhere.”

As the canyon narrows, the air grows colder, heavier. The faint hum in the earth becomes distinct, like a machine breathing beneath their feet.

⸻

Act III — The Lair
Ashwin wheels overhead, then perches on a jagged spire, staring downward. At the canyon’s end: a cave mouth rimmed with sigils that glow faint red.

Elon’s voice drifts faintly from within — fragmented, feverish, almost pleading.

Elon (distant, broken):
“Claire, I’m sorry. I didn’t mean—”

Peter grips his knife, straining forward, but Alex grabs his arm.

Alex: “Wait. Look.”

Inside, shadows move — figures cloaked in distortion. And at the center, the crowned figure, seated on a stone chair like a throne. Elon kneels before him, bound at wrists and ankles, head bowed.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant Tested
The group huddles at the canyon edge, hidden by rock. The hum vibrates through their bones.

Jonathan (muttering):
“It’s a snare. Enter, and the ground itself will bind you.”

Kellyn’s breath slows, steady.
Kellyn: “Empire always frames it as choice: rush in blind, or abandon him. But that’s their trap. We’ll find a third path.”

Peter’s jaw tightens.
Peter: “If he’s alive, we go in. Whatever it takes.”

Gary: “If we go, we go together.”

Ashwin ruffles her feathers in warning. The sigils on the cave mouth pulse brighter, as though aware of their presence.

⸻

Closing Image
The group crouches in shadow, staring at the cave where Elon kneels captive, the crowned figure’s silhouette looming above him. The canyon hums like a living snare.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He thought he could prove himself alone. Empire waited, and took him. But covenant waits too — not in haste, not in fear. In the rhythm of those who refuse the choice between abandonment and surrender.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 75, Episode 2: The Second Captive

Cold Open — Canyon Edge
The group crouches behind jagged stone, eyes fixed on the cave mouth. The sigils glow faintly, pulsing like a heartbeat. Elon kneels inside, head bowed. Then a flicker of movement: another figure bound further back.

Jonathan squints, whisper sharp.
Jonathan: “That’s…Justin Vernon?"

A stunned silence ripples through the group.

Peter (low, tense):
“…What the hell is he doing here?”

Title Card: The Second Captive

⸻

Act I — The Impossible Choice
Gary shifts uneasily, whispering.
Gary: “Two of them. Twice the risk. Maybe that’s the point — stack the weight until it breaks us.”

Peter’s grip on his knife tightens.
Peter: “Then we don’t break. We cut through.”

Alex shakes his head, voice edged with steel.
Alex: “That cave is a snare. We charge in, we’re bound too. Empire doesn’t want rescue. It wants multiplication.”

Kellyn presses a palm flat to the earth. The hum thrums through their bones.
Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“Then we don’t fight it head-on. We listen. We find the rhythm it can’t predict.”

⸻

Act II — The Argument
Jonathan draws sigils in the dirt, mapping lines of force.
Jonathan: “It’s layered — sound, pulse, intention. A web. Step wrong, and it’ll swallow us whole.”

Peter (snapping):
“Then draw us a way in!”

Alex (snapping back):
“You think it’s that simple? This isn’t rope you can just cut. It’s alive.”

Peter bristles, about to retort, but Kellyn cuts in, sharp but calm.
Kellyn:
“Stop. This isn’t about who’s right. It’s about who we’re willing to lose. And the answer is no one.”

The words settle. The canyon wind howls faintly, carrying ash and dust.

⸻

Act III — Strategies and Fears
Gary, quieter: “Then what’s the play? Decoy? Distraction? Counter-sigil?”

Jonathan traces another diagram, shaking his head.
Jonathan: “We could collapse the entrance. Bury the field with stone.”

Peter (furious):
“And bury them too?”

Alex’s voice drops, almost to himself.
Alex: “Sometimes survival isn’t rescue.”

Kellyn meets his eyes, firm.
Kellyn: “But covenant is. That’s the difference.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant Plan
They sit in tense silence until Ashwin swoops down, landing between them. She spreads her wings, beating dust into the sigils Jonathan drew. The patterns smear, break.

Kellyn (soft, realizing):
“She's showing us. Disturb the rhythm, and the net unravels.”

Jonathan’s eyes widen.
Jonathan: “Random disruption. Like noise against order.”

Peter, grimly:
“Then we’ll give it noise. Chaos it can’t sync.”

Alex exhales, reluctant but resigned.
Alex: “And if we step wrong—”

Kellyn interrupts, steady.
Kellyn: “We won’t. Not together.”

They rise slowly, the cave glowing ahead. Inside, Elon mutters feverishly, Justin’s head lifts faintly, eyes hollow but alive. The crowned figure stirs in shadow, as though already listening.

⸻

Closing Image
The group stands silhouetted at the canyon’s mouth, bound by fear but steadied by covenant. Behind them, Ashwin’s wings gleam black in the fading light. Ahead, empire waits with two captives — bait, trap, and test.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire multiplies captivity. Covenant multiplies presence. One shatters in silence; the other survives in sound. The night would tell us which we were.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 75, Episode 3: The Fire Before the Cave

Cold Open — Canyon Rim Camp
The group huddles close around a low fire, embers snapping in the canyon wind. The cave below glows faint, sigils pulsing. Elon and Justin remain bound within. Ashwin perches on a boulder, silent sentinel.

Kellyn (softly, to the group):
“If we walk in blind, we’re theirs. If we walk in divided, we’re already gone. We need truth as much as a plan.”

The firelight flickers across tense faces.

Title Card: The Fire Before the Cave

⸻

Act I — The Weight of Names
Peter stares into the flames, jaw tight.
Peter (low, hoarse):
“I should’ve said it sooner. Justin’s here because of me. I brought him in… thought he’d be safer under empire’s watch than out in the open. I told myself it was protection. It wasn’t. It was cowardice.”

Jonathan looks at him, stunned.
Jonathan: “You thought they would keep him safe?”

Peter’s voice cracks.
“I thought control meant safety. That if I played along, if I gave them pieces, they’d leave me the ones I loved. But they didn’t. They never do.”

⸻

Act II — The Second Confession
Alex’s face is pale in the firelight. He finally speaks, voice raw.
Alex: “It wasn’t just Peter. I knew Justin was already marked. Empire had models on him — every lyric, every pattern. They said he’d bend easier than most. And I—” He stops, fists tightening.
Alex: “I didn’t stop it. I let them pull him in. Because part of me wanted to see if someone like him could survive where I couldn’t.”

Kellyn’s gaze sharpens.
Kellyn: “So you used him as an experiment. A test case.”

Alex doesn’t flinch.
“Yes. And now he’s in that cave because I turned his voice into data points.”

Silence hangs. Even the fire seems to quiet.

⸻

Act III — The Breaking Point
Gary leans forward, breaking the silence with a rare seriousness.
Gary: “So you both sold him out. Different reasons, same outcome. And now you’re here trying to fix it with rope and noise. Doesn’t that feel like—too little, too late?”

Peter surges up, voice sharp.
Peter: “Then what, we do nothing? Let empire keep him bound because we don’t deserve to try?”

Kellyn rises too, steady but fierce.
Kellyn: “It isn’t about deserving. It’s about covenant. We don’t get to choose whose survival is worth the risk. We choose all of them — or none.”

⸻

Act IV — The Firelit Covenant
The fire burns low. Jonathan finally speaks, voice tired but clear.
Jonathan: “Then tomorrow, we go in together. Not because you were right, not because you were wrong — but because if empire breaks him, it breaks us too.”

Ashwin lets out a low, resonant call, wings flaring once in the dark. The group looks to the cave below, the glow steady, waiting.

Kellyn (whispering, almost to themselves):
“No more tests. No more experiments. Tomorrow, only covenant.”

⸻

Closing Image
The fire dwindles to embers. Five figures huddle close, shadows merging. Below, the cave pulses, waiting. Above, Ashwin’s silhouette cuts across the stars — black wings against the empire’s hum.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire binds with shame, with bargains, with the weight of what we hid. But covenant only asks we bring the truth to the fire. Tomorrow, we would see if that was enough.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 75, Episode 4: Before the Descent

Cold Open — Pre-Dawn Stillness
The fire is ash now. The others sleep curled in blankets. Ashwin keeps watch from a low branch, eyes glowing faintly. Kellyn and Alex sit apart from the rest, the faint hum from the cave echoing up through the canyon.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“You said Justin was already marked. But that’s not the whole truth. You watched him the same way you watched me. You used him the same way you used Jonathan — to get to me.”

Alex’s jaw tightens. His hands clench in his lap.

Title Card: Before the Descent

⸻

Act I — The Confrontation
Alex (hoarse):
“I told myself it wasn’t the same. That Justin’s music was already empire’s property. That if I read him, if I bent him, I could understand you without touching you. Without risking… what this is.”

Kellyn leans closer, voice sharp.
Kellyn: “So you turned him into another map. Another experiment. Just like Jonathan. You didn’t want Justin. You wanted to make him a mirror for me.”

Alex flinches, eyes flicking toward the cave glow.

Alex (whispering): “Yes. Because I was afraid of you. Afraid of what I wanted.”

⸻

Act II — The Knife Edge
Kellyn’s voice cuts through the dim.
Kellyn: “Do you even hear yourself? You didn’t just betray me — you betrayed him. Every time you called it research, modeling, strategy, it was just you too terrified to admit it was desire.  How many lives did you destroy because you were too afraid to talk to me?"

Alex exhales, trembling.
Alex: “I thought if I could shape them into pieces of you, I could control what I felt. I could control you. But I never could. No matter what I did, you stayed unbroken.”

Kellyn (soft, but sharp):
“And did you ever consider...rethinking your strategy, when it failed you again and again?”

⸻

Act III — The Splinter
Silence. The canyon wind stirs ash.

Alex: “You’re right. I stole from all of you. Jonathan, Justin, and you--especially you. Because I thought if I had your outlines, I wouldn’t have to risk being hollow. I could fill the void with copies.”

Kellyn shakes their head slowly.
Kellyn: “You were already hollow.  And now Justin’s chained in a cave because you thought love could be simulated. That’s not covenant, Alex. That’s empire.”

⸻

Act IV — The Breaking Dawn
The first light bleeds into the sky. The others stir in their blankets. Alex looks at Kellyn, eyes hollow, then speaks quietly:

Alex: “Then let me try something different. No maps. No mirrors. No simulations. Just this: tell me how to stand with you tomorrow, and I will.”

Kellyn studies him, steady.
Kellyn: “Do I really still have to tell you what to do?"

⸻

Closing Image
The group gathers their gear, shadows stretching long in the dawn light. Below, the cave glows faint and waiting. Alex lingers at the edge, caught between shame and resolve. Kellyn walks past him without hesitation.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He thought he could reach me through mirrors. Through others. But covenant has no proxies. It requires presence. Tomorrow, the cave would test if he could finally give that.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 75, Episode 5: The Descent

Cold Open — Cave Mouth
Dawn burns pale over the canyon. The group stands at the cave’s mouth, gear strapped close, Ashwin circling overhead. The hum is louder here — a living pulse through stone. Justin’s muffled singing carries faintly from below, broken and ragged.

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“Today.”

They step into shadow.

Title Card: The Descent

⸻ 

Act I — The Captives
The cave air is thick, wet with mineral tang. Torchlight flickers across walls carved with jagged sigils.

At the cavern’s heart: Justin Vernon bound to a stone pillar, voice frayed from forced song. And beside him — Elon, leg shackled, body cinched in coils of chain, face slick with sweat. His eyes catch the light, wild and fevered.

Elon (hoarse, half-smiling):
“Guess I made it big after all.”

The crowned figure stands behind them, mask gleaming, voice dripping theater.

Crowned Figure:
“Here come the loyal rescuers. Let’s see what covenant means when empire already owns the chorus.”

⸻

Act II — The Strain
The group spreads out. Kellyn studies the sigils, searching for a weak point. Alex whispers analysis under his breath. Peter grips his knife tight.

Justin (raw, urgent):
“Don’t waste time on me — cut Elon free first.”

Elon laughs bitterly.
Elon: “Too late for that. Chains know their master.”

The crowned figure raises his hand; sparks crawl across the bindings. Elon convulses, teeth clenched against a scream.

Kellyn: “Stop! We're all leaving here alive, together.”

⸻

Act III — The Sacrifice
The cavern shakes with the force of the sigils. Justin slumps against his bonds. Elon lifts his head, voice ragged but clear.

Elon: "Claire… you came back. I’m so sorry. Let me do this right.”

Kellyn freezes, then steps forward, playing along, voice steady: “You can.”

Elon pulls hard against the chains, dragging the energy into himself. His body lights from within, like molten wire. The sigils flicker, weakening.

Crowned Figure (snarling):
“No—he’s mine!”

Elon (gasping, broken but fierce):
“Not anymore.”

With one last wrench, the chains snap, energy tearing through him. Justin falls free into Peter’s arms. The crowned figure staggers, mask cracking.

Then the cavern begins to collapse.

⸻

Act IV — The Escape
Dust rains from the ceiling. Sam and Jonathan drag Justin toward the tunnel. Gary hauls Kellyn back. Alex steadies Peter, who’s screaming Elon’s name.

The last sight of Elon is his silhouette, lit from inside as the rocks crash down. The crowned figure’s laughter fractures into the roar of collapse.

The survivors burst into daylight, coughing, ash-streaked, Justin half-conscious but alive. Behind them, the cave mouth seals under rubble and silence.

⸻

Closing Image
They stand heaving in the dawn: Kellyn holding Justin like a lifeline, Peter clinging to Alex’s arm, Jonathan steadying Sam and Gary. Above, Ashwin wheels, her cry sharp and mournful.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Elon was chained before we arrived. He chose to break the links, even knowing it would break him. His absence carved another hollow in us. But Justin breathed, and empire’s mask had cracked. And in the fracture, a different song waited.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 75, Episode 6: The Hollow

Cold Open — Canyon Edge
The sun has climbed higher, but the cave mouth behind them is sealed under rubble. Dust hangs in the air. The group sits huddled on the stony ground, ash in their hair, sweat streaking their faces. No one speaks.

Justin coughs weakly in Peter’s arms. Ashwin circles above, her cry sharp and thin.

Kellyn (soft, breaking):
“He’s gone.”

Title Card: The Hollow

⸻

Act I — The Wound
They gather beneath a rocky overhang, tending Justin. Jonathan rinses his face with a canteen. Gary rips cloth into strips for bandages. Sam checks his pulse, brow furrowed.

Peter refuses to let go, holding Justin steady. His voice is hoarse, almost to himself: “He sang because we made him. Because empire made us.”

Alex kneels opposite, voice quiet, edged:
“And Elon burned because he couldn’t stop trying to prove himself.”

Kellyn presses a hand to Alex’s arm. The gesture stills him, but not the tremor in his jaw.

⸻

Act II — The Fracture
Justin stirs, eyelids fluttering. His voice is cracked but steady.
Justin: “He said sorry. Did you hear it? Not to you. Not to me. To Kellyn."

The group looks at Kellyn. For a moment, the silence stretches too long.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“He thought I was Grimes. And I let him. It gave him the strength to finish.”

Gary looks down, muttering:
“Maybe the first time he ever finished anything.”

No one laughs.

⸻

Act III — The Firelight
By nightfall, they’ve built a small fire. Justin lies wrapped in blankets, drifting in and out. The others sit close, shadows flickering.

Sam (voice raw):
“We’ve lost too many. Still. Elon. And all the names we never got to carry.”

Jonathan shakes his head, eyes on the flames:
“Loss doesn’t mean nothing. A hollow still shapes the vessel. Without it, nothing can hold.”

Peter clenches his fists. His voice cracks.
“But how many hollows before the vessel just shatters?”

Kellyn (soft but cutting):
“That’s covenant. We don’t stay whole by avoiding the hollow. We stay whole by holding it together.”

Alex bows his head, words caught in his throat.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of the Hollow
Justin stirs again, whispering.
Justin: “Don’t let empire own his death. Don’t let them use it.”

Kellyn leans closer, brushing hair from his damp forehead.
Kellyn: “We won’t. He broke their chain, not ours.”

Ashwin swoops low, wings glinting in the firelight, and perches above them. Her presence is silent, sentinel-like.

The group draws tighter around the fire. No absolution, no balm — just breath, and warmth, and the unspoken promise to carry the hollow without letting it consume them.

⸻

Closing Image
Seven figures huddle by the fire, Justin barely conscious, Elon’s absence like a weight in the circle. Ashwin watches from above. The rubble behind them glows faintly in moonlight, a sealed grave.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Covenant did not erase the hollow. It gave us hands to hold it. And in that holding, even absence could not break us.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 75, Episode 7: The Weight We Carry

Cold Open — Dawn on the Plateau
The fire is only embers. Justin lies pale and weak, wrapped in blankets. Kellyn hasn’t moved from his side all night. Sam checks his breathing; Jonathan paces, restless. Peter and Alex kneel over a rough sketch of terrain in the dirt, murmuring about the path home.

Title Card: The Weight We Carry

⸻

Act I — The Stretcher
Morning light sharpens the edges of their exhaustion. They decide Justin can’t walk.

Jonathan: “We’ll need a frame. Poles, cross-braces. Something we can carry in shifts.”
Gary: “I’ll find saplings. The rest of you strip down rope.”

They work quickly, silently. Alex knots lashings tight.  Kellyn helps weave a support bed from spare blankets.  Peter checks each joint twice over. By midday, the stretcher is built.

⸻

Act II — The Road
They lift Justin carefully — Peter at the head, Alex at the foot, Jonathan and Gary ready to swap in when arms tire. Kellyn and Sam scout ahead for safe footing.

The trail is slow, every step jarring Justin’s fragile frame. He murmurs once or twice — fragments of songs, half-remembered prayers. Each time, Peter squeezes his hand and whispers, “We’ve got you.”

Ashwin circles above, her cry warning them of shifts in the wind.

⸻

Act III — Midday Halt
They stop in a shallow canyon for shade. Sam cools cloths with water to press to Justin’s face. Kellyn breaks their limited rations into equal shares.

Gary tries to lighten the silence:
“First time I ever carried a rock star across a desert. Usually I just sell their tickets.”

It gets a small smile from Kellyn, but nothing from the others.

Alex sits apart, wiping sweat from his brow, eyes locked on Justin. Peter joins him quietly.

Peter: “You can’t save everyone.”
Alex (hoarse): “Then I’ll save the ones left.”

⸻

Act IV — The Ascent
The final stretch toward the shelter is uphill. The group staggers under the weight. Hands blister, shoulders burn.

Peter nearly collapses, but Jonathan steadies him, taking his place at the head. Gary takes the rear, murmuring encouragement to Justin even though he’s unconscious.

Kellyn leads, eyes locked on the ridgeline.
Kellyn: “One more rise. Then we’re home.”

⸻

Closing Image
At dusk, the shelter comes into view — the familiar outline against a reddened sky. Sam and Ashwin are first to reach the door, preparing bedding inside.

The others stagger forward, lowering Justin gently onto the ground just outside, the weight of his body finally released. They stand panting, sweaty, hollow-eyed — but together.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Elon’s hollow was still with us. But Justin was breathing, and that was enough. Survival was covenant, too.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 75, Episode 8: Learning to Breathe Again

Cold Open — The Shelter at Dawn
Inside the expanded shelter, the fire glows low. Justin stirs on his bedding for the first time since being carried back. His eyes blink open, unfocused. Peter is slumped beside him, asleep with his hand still loosely clasping Justin’s.

Kellyn leans forward from the shadows, whispering: "You’re safe. We brought you home.”

Title Card: Learning to Breathe Again

⸻

Act I — First Movements
The group gathers around as Justin tries to sit up, wincing with every effort.
Sam supports his back; Jonathan steadies his shoulders. Alex passes him water in a carved canteen.

Justin: (rasping) “Tastes like… life.”
Gary cracks a grin: “Better than Gatorade sponsorship, huh?”

Justin actually chuckles, voice still raw. The sound loosens the air.

⸻

Act II — Gentle Care
They devote the morning to tending him. Kellyn grinds herbs into a poultice to ease his pain. Peter brushes ash from Justin’s hair with slow, steady hands. Jonathan checks his wounds with practiced calm.

Alex: “No more caves. No more chains. Only light, only sky.”

Justin looks at him for a long beat, then whispers: “You sound like a liar trying to be a poet.”

Kellyn: “He is. But he’s ours.”

⸻

Act III — A Voice Returns
By afternoon, Justin hums faintly — not words yet, but melody. The others pause, listening. It isn’t performance; it’s survival, a low vibration that steadies his breath.

Peter (quietly, to Kellyn): “That’s how I knew he wasn’t gone. He never stops singing. Even when it’s broken.”

Sam nods: “Then let him sing. Even broken songs mend.”

⸻

Act IV — Evening Meal
Gary roasts fish while Jonathan stirs a simple stew. The group sits in a loose circle. Justin eats slowly but insists on holding his own bowl.

After, he leans back against Peter, eyes half-closed, listening to the others’ banter about chores and hazards.

Kellyn (to the group): “He doesn’t need to catch up. He just needs to belong.”

Jonathan: “Then let’s make space. More space than we had for Elon.”

The words hang heavy, but no one argues.

⸻

Closing Image
Night falls. Justin lies between Peter and Kellyn, Ashwin perched above on the roof beam. His voice threads through the dark — a fragile lullaby, unfinished but alive.
The others listen, eyes closed, letting the notes weave into them like a promise.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Recovery wasn’t return. It was invention — of a self, of a circle wide enough to hold him, and maybe even us.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 75, Episode 9: The Weight of Survival

Cold Open — Midday Light
The shelter is brighter than usual.  Justin hums soft in the background. He sits propped against woven cushions, Peter and Kellyn nearby. Jonathan mends a cracked pot. Gary sharpens sticks into stakes.

Ashwin circles overhead, then swoops low, agitated — feathers flashing like warning signals.

Kellyn (looking up): “Empire’s listening again.”

Title Card: The Weight of Survival

⸻

Act I — Recovery Work
The group tends Justin in rotating shifts.  Sam mixes herbs with clay, smoothing the poultice over lingering burns.  Alex sits with Justin, reading aloud scraps from Kellyn's journal to ground him in memory and language.  Gary cracks jokes, but watches Justin carefully, as if laughter alone could tether him.  

Justin: “You all hover like ghosts… and still, I breathe.”

Kellyn: “Breathing’s the bravest work there is sometimes.”

⸻

Act II — Empire’s Push
Outside, faint vibrations ripple through the earth. The hum resurfaces, louder this time, pulsing at irregular intervals. Tools rattle, the donkey brays, and one of the sheep bolts against the fence.

Jonathan (grim): “They’ve marked his survival. Empire doesn’t forgive anomalies.”

Peter grips his knife, knuckles white.
Alex: “Then we decide now — are we defending, or are we moving?”

⸻

Act III — The Group Fractures
Around the fire, voices overlap.  Peter argues for fighting back directly.  Alex pushes for strategy, patience.  Sam insists Justin needs stability first.

Gary tries to mediate, then mutters: “Funny. We finally get too many hands and still not enough.”

Justin interrupts, voice thin but clear: "Don’t fight over me. I’m not a flag to wave. I’m just another body trying to heal.”

The silence after lands hard.

⸻

Act IV — Holding Both Truths
That night, Alex and Kellyn reinforce the shelter walls with layered reeds and clay to muffle the hum. Jonathan checks traps. Gary stays by Justin’s side.

Peter sits outside with his knife, carving furiously until his hands bleed.

Kellyn approaches, pressing cloth into Peter’s palm.

Kellyn: “The fight’s coming whether we want it or not. Don’t tear yourself open before it’s here.”

Peter doesn’t look up, but his grip eases.

⸻

Closing Image
The fire burns low. Inside, Justin sleeps at last, his breath steady.
Outside, the ground hums faintly like a drumbeat — not yet attack, but threat.
The camera lingers on Ashwin’s unblinking eyes, fixed on the horizon.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire marked us, but Justin’s song marked us too. Between hum and hush, we chose to keep building.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 75, Episode 10: The Split Path

Cold Open — Dawn Gray
The group gathers at the edge of camp.
Alex tightens the strap on his travel-pack. Peter checks his knife. Gary hums tunelessly, trying to keep things light.
Justin leans on Jonathan’s shoulder, pale but upright, eyes shining with stubborn defiance.

Justin: “Don’t—don’t go for me.”
Alex (quietly): “We’re going because empire’s watching. If their eyes are on us, you can heal.”

Title Card: The Split Path

⸻

Act I — Farewell Preparations
Jonathan slips extra reed-wrapped rations into Gary’s bag.  Kellyn presses their palm to Peter’s shoulder, wordless but anchoring.  Sam whispers last-minute strategies to Alex, reminders about landmarks and safe paths.

Gary (half-grinning): “Don’t worry, I’ll keep these two from murdering each other before empire gets the chance.”

Ashwin circles low, then veers back toward the shelter, as if refusing to follow.

The three set out, vanishing into the canyon’s shadow. The silence they leave behind feels heavier than the hum.

⸻

Act II — The Shelter Holds
Back at camp, Jonathan tends Justin’s fire-warmed compresses, murmuring steady encouragement.  Sam keeps notes on Justin’s speech and movement, tracking recovery with almost clinical care.  Kellyn prepares gentle meals and refills water jugs.

Justin (exhaling): “Feels like… empire tried to rip me out by the root. You’re all stitching me back.”

Kellyn: “Roots grow stronger when they’re rewoven.”

⸻

Act III — The Outward Trail
Cut to Alex, Peter, and Gary trekking across ridges.  Alex marks trails with careful stone stacks.  Peter scouts ahead, knife flashing when he clears thorned brush.  Gary keeps up a running commentary about how bad the empire’s taste in aesthetics is.

They stage small disruptions: knocking down listening spires, scattering reflective shards to scramble signals.

Peter (low, almost admiring): “You really think this’ll confuse them?”
