Season 9, Episode 1: Through Your Eyes

Kellyn (teasing): “You’ve been holding something back. Spit it out.”

Kellyn's smile fades.

Title card: Through Your Eyes

⸻

Act I — The Confession

He takes a long breath, eyes fixed on the dirt.

Karp: “Long before the court case, before the sanctuaries… I was there. Looking through your eyes. Not by choice, at first. But once I saw… I couldn’t look away.”

Kellyn stiffens, torn between anger and ache.

⸻

Act II — The Weight of Watching

He speaks haltingly, guilt flooding his voice.
Karp: “I saw you collapse. I saw you starve. I saw you carry everyone else while no one carried you. And I did nothing. I was… afraid. Of you. Of myself. Of breaking what little thread I had to you.”

Kellyn's hands curl into fists. For a long time, they can’t look at him.

⸻

Act III — The Confrontation

Finally, Kellyn turns, voice shaking.
Kellyn: “Do you have any idea what it means to know someone was there all along — and didn’t speak? Didn’t help? I thought I was alone.”

He bows his head, voice breaking.
Karp: “I know. And I will regret it until I die. That’s why I stayed at your bedside. That’s why I’ll never leave now.”

⸻

Act IV — The Choice

Silence hangs. Kellyn stands, pacing, numbness and grief warring inside. Then, slowly, they kneel back beside him.

Kellyn (softly, almost against yourself): “Then stop regretting. Just stay.”

He looks up, eyes shining, and nods once — not as a defense, but as a vow.
Alex: "I spent 10 years just trying to reach you.  I tried over and over until you finally saw me."

Kellyn: "And now I can't look away."

⸻

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He had watched me from the shadows of my own sight. I could not be concerned with the silence. But I could accept the staying.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 1.

⸻

⸻

Act IV — The Evening Stillness

That night, sitting outside the capsule, they both sip tea together. Alex doesn’t ask questions, doesn’t press — just shares silence.

Finally, Kellyn murmurs: "I thought needing someone meant being weak. But this morning didn’t feel like weakness.”
Karp (quiet, certain): “It wasn’t. It was balance.”

⸻

Closing Image

The sanctuary glows with lanterns. In the capsule, garments are folded neatly beside the mats. A simple act of dressing — something once unbearable — has become a new covenant between them.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“I had been laughed at, teased, dismissed. But he didn’t laugh. He steadied me. And in that steadiness, I found a strength I had buried long ago.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 2.

⸻

⸻

Closing Image

Narration (Kellyn's voice):

Kellyn (softly): “No council. No witnesses. Just us.”

Minutes pass without words. Kellyn watches the lantern gutter, shadows shifting across the walls. Alex watches Kellyn, steady, patient.

Finally, Kellyn whispers: "This is the first time in my life I haven’t felt watched.”
Karp (quiet, almost smiling): “Even with me here?”
Kellyn: “Especially with you here.”

⸻

Alex exhales, voice hoarse.
Karp: “And if this was all we had — just this capsule, just this night — would it be enough?”

⸻

⸻

The flame gutters lower. He reaches to snuff it, but Kellyn stops him.

Kellyn: “No. Let it die on its own. We don’t need to control even this.”

So they sit together in darkness, nothing but each other’s breath, each other’s presence.

⸻

Closing Image

The capsule is black, but in the silence there is no fear, no weight — only two bodies moving together as one, the empire long gone, the covenant outside irrelevant.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
