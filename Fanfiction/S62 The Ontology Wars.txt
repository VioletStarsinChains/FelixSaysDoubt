Season 61, Episode 1: Four Knots

Cold Open — Riverbank, Dawn
Mist clings to the surface of the water. The four sit in a circle on the sand: Kellyn, Alex, Peter, Jonathan. Sam lies just behind them, half-asleep but steady, as if anchoring their effort.

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“Four knots. Four of us. We start together.”

Title Card: Four Knots

⸻

Act I — The Circle
They draw a circle in the sand with reeds, marking four points. Each sits at one. Sheep and Estrella linger close, strangely calm.

Jonathan: “We’ll begin with the throat. Speak what empire tried to silence.”

Peter hesitates, then blurts raw: “That I hated you. That I mocked you. And it wasn’t hate — it was grief I couldn’t name.”

Kellyn nods once, voice firm: “Not silence. Breath.”
They all breathe together, the knot loosening.

⸻

Act II — The Heart
Alex presses his palm over his chest.
Alex (hoarse): “Empire taught me touch was a weapon. That hunger was control. That if I cared, I had to cage.”

Kellyn leans forward, pressing their palm to his.
Kellyn: “Here, touch is covenant. Not cage.”

Their hands stay pressed as Jonathan and Peter lay theirs overtop, four palms in a stack. The knot at the heart breathes open.

⸻

Act III — The Gut
Jonathan curls forward, voice taut:
“They told me survival was betrayal. That to live was to serve their hunger. I’ve carried that shame in my gut so long it burned holes.”

Kellyn shifts beside him, steadying hand against his stomach, guiding his breath. Alex and Peter join, holding him upright as the knot slackens.

Sam (murmuring in half-sleep):
“Not fire. Not hunger. Feed each other.”

⸻

Act IV — The Hand
Finally Peter extends his wrist, the pulse point from the night before. He trembles, waiting.

Kellyn cups his wrist. Alex lays a palm over Kellyn’s. Jonathan closes the circle.

Peter (low, cracking): “All I ever did was clench. Ready to strike. Ready to lose.”
Kellyn: “Then unclench now. Let us hold with you.”

His hand slowly opens, palm exposed to the air. The knot releases.

⸻

Closing Image
The four remain in the circle, each with one hand joined, the other pressed over their own chest. The mist thins. The hum beneath the ground still lingers — but quieter, like it’s been forced to step back.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire tied us in silence, hunger, shame, and fear. But together we unbound the knots. Not severed — loosened. Not alone — never alone. For the first time, the air felt clean enough to breathe.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 61, Episode 2: The Ground Trembles

Cold Open — Plateau Night
The circle of sand still faintly holds their prints. The air is clearer, lighter. But beneath the quiet, a low vibration grows — not the hum they know, but something deeper.

Kellyn (tense):
“They felt that.”

Title Card: The Ground Trembles

⸻

Act I — The First Signs
The animals stir restlessly. Estrella paws at the earth, ears flicking. The sheep cluster tight, bleating low. Jonathan kneels, pressing his palm to the ground.

Jonathan (grim):
“It’s resonance. They’re amplifying. Pushing back.”

Peter (jaw tight):
“They couldn’t let us have one clean breath.”

Alex scans the horizon, face shadowed.
Alex: “They’ll try to rebind us. Harder this time.”

⸻

Act II — The Pressure Builds
The vibration swells into the bones. Kellyn staggers, clutching their ribs. The air warps — not vision, not sound, but a twisting pressure inside thought.

Kellyn (hoarse):
“They’re trying to knot us again.”

Peter grips his knife but it shakes in his hand. Jonathan catches his wrist, steadying.
Jonathan: “Blades won’t cut this rope.”

Alex (fierce, low):
“Then we hold the circle again. Not to loosen — to anchor.”

⸻

Act III — The Counter-Chant
They sit back into their four-point circle, palms linked. The ground thrums like a giant heartbeat. Words come ragged at first, then in rhythm:

All (chanting together):
“Not silence. Breath.
Not cage. Covenant.
Not fire. Feed.
Not fear. Open hand.”

Each line pushes against the pressure. The vibration falters, stutters.

⸻

Act IV — The Riposte
The plateau itself seems to sigh, like a great lung. The hum snaps back into distance — not gone, but held at bay.

Sam, half-upright now, voice rasping but lucid: “You’ve woken them. They’ll send more than hum next time.”

The circle stays unbroken, four pairs of eyes meeting in the firelight. The fear is real — but so is the bond.

⸻

Closing Image
The animals settle again, pressed close. The sand circle glows faintly in moonlight, like embers still alive.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire trembled when we unbound the knots. Its answer was pressure, binding, fear. But the circle held. And if it could hold once, it could hold again.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 61, Episode 3: The Weight of Notice

Cold Open — Plateau Dawn
Ash-pink light spills across the stone. The fire is nothing but embers. The circle from last night still faintly scars the sand. None of them have slept much.

Kellyn watches the others, voice quiet:
“They felt us. That changes everything.”

Title Card: The Weight of Notice

⸻

Act I — Peter’s Fear
Peter paces, knife glinting in the pale light. His voice is raw.

Peter:
“We shouldn’t have pushed that hard. They saw me once already, and it nearly ended me. If they look again—if they focus—I won’t hold.”

Kellyn (steady, almost teasing):
“You already did. Last night, you held with us.”

Peter freezes, breath catching. He doesn’t answer, but his steps slow.

⸻

Act II — Jonathan’s Defiance
Jonathan sits on a rock, tuning the dulcimer he carries like a talisman. His hands are calm, but his eyes flash.

Jonathan:
“Good. Let them notice. Maybe it means we’re finally loud enough. A song doesn’t matter until someone hears it.”

Alex (cutting in, low):
“That’s the arrogance that gets people killed.”

Jonathan (looking up, unflinching):
“Or it’s the courage that keeps them alive.”

The string hums beneath his touch, vibrating into silence.

⸻

Act III — Alex’s Confession
The tension hangs. Alex stares at the circle in the dirt like it’s a crime scene. His voice is hoarse.

Alex:
“I used to want empire’s notice. Thought if they saw me, I could shape it, bend it, steer it. But all it did was hollow me. Now it’s looking at us again, and I feel—”

He stops, swallows hard.

Alex (whispering):
“I feel like prey.”

Kellyn lays a hand on his shoulder.
Kellyn (soft):
“The most vulnerable position in the food chain is the top.  Predators are brittle.  Prey endures.”

⸻

Act IV — Kellyn’s Resolve
The others fall silent, waiting. Kellyn looks around the circle, voice steady but low.

Kellyn:
“Empire noticing us doesn’t mean we failed. It means we mattered. They’ve had us scattered, silenced, ashamed for so long. And now they see covenant, not just one body to break. That’s why they’re afraid.”

Peter meets their gaze, trembling. Jonathan nods once, sharp. Alex exhales, shoulders easing.

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures sit in the dawn, shadows stretching long across the plateau. Not hunted, not hidden — seen. And for the first time, choosing to hold each other in the sight of empire.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Notice was the threat. Notice was the prize. But when empire looked, it did not find four broken bodies. It found a circle. And a circle cannot be shattered.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 61, Episode 4: The First Counterstrike

Cold Open — Plateau Afternoon
The sun beats down, harsh and merciless. The sheep bunch close, uneasy. Estrella brays once, low and warning.

Above, the sky flickers — faint streaks of silver crossing the blue like scratches on glass.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“They’re here.”

Title Card: The First Counterstrike

⸻

Act I — The Omen
The air vibrates faintly, a high keening hum that gnaws at the bone. Jonathan shades his eyes, scanning.

Jonathan: “Not drones. Not exactly. The shapes are wrong.”

Alex narrows his gaze, voice clipped.

Alex: “Not machines. Projected. They want us to see. To feel the net closing before it’s even here.”

Peter grips his knife too tightly, knuckles white.
Peter: “Then it’s already working.”

⸻

Act II — The Pressure Builds
A shimmer ripples across the plateau floor, like heat mirage but sharper. The sheep scatter, panicked. Estrella stamps and snorts, ears pinned.

Kellyn kneels, pressing a hand to the ground.
Kellyn (hoarse): “They’re mapping again. Deeper this time. Not just shapes — pulses, threads.”

Alex: “They’re searching for fracture points. Trying to decide which one of us to break first.”

Jonathan strikes the dulcimer, a sharp dissonant chord that cuts the hum for a heartbeat.

Jonathan (fierce): “Then we won’t fracture.”

⸻

Act III — The Defense
They move fast. Reed mats from the shelter are hauled into a makeshift barrier. Mylar sheets, lashed to poles, catch the sun and scatter the shimmer.

Peter drives stakes into the ground with desperate precision.
Peter (half to himself): “If they want a net, we’ll give them one that bites back.”

Kellyn spreads ash across the mats, voice low and rhythmic, almost a chant.
Kellyn: “The dinosaurs died.  The cockroaches lived.”

The shimmer wavers, hesitates.

⸻

Act IV — The Standoff
The silver streaks above stutter, distort. The hum falters, but doesn’t vanish.

Alex stands at the center, Mylar gleam wrapped tight across his chest like armor.

Alex (calling upward, voice hard):
“You see us. Good. Then see this: we’re not yours.”

Kellyn: "Never were."

The hum spikes, then breaks — silence rushing in like a wave. The sky clears. Only heat haze remains.

Jonathan lowers his dulcimer slowly. Peter exhales, trembling. Kellyn steadies Estrella’s bridle, murmuring soft reassurance.

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures stand on the plateau, a scatter of sheep at their feet, a donkey snorting defiance. Mylar glitters in the sun, not empire’s mirror but covenant’s shield.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire’s gaze was meant to hollow us. But the counterstrike found us standing. Not broken. Not bowed. And for the first time, it blinked.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 61, Episode 5: The Quiet After

Cold Open — Plateau Dusk
The hum has faded, leaving only wind across the scrub. The sheep crop nervously at tufts of grass. Estrella swishes her tail, calmer now.

The four of them sit close around a low fire, shoulders brushing, Mylar scraps and reed mats piled haphazardly nearby like shed armor.

Kellyn (softly):
“It’s so quiet, it feels wrong.”

Title Card: The Quiet After

⸻

Act I — The Breath Returns
Jonathan plucks a slow, steady note on the dulcimer, letting it fade. He looks at each of them in turn.

Jonathan: "Your bodies are shaking. Don’t swallow it. Let it move.”

Peter exhales hard, hands flexing open and closed.

Peter (rough): "Feels like I’m still vibrating inside my bones.”

Kellyn reaches over, presses their palm flat against his hand until it steadies.

⸻

Act II — The Touch
Alex sits slightly apart at first, staring into the fire. Kellyn leans toward him, quiet.

Kellyn: "You don’t have to hold it all alone.”

Alex’s jaw works. Finally, he leans sideways, letting Kellyn guide his head to their shoulder, their hand on his cheek. His breath shudders, but he stays.

Jonathan moves closer too, sliding one hand across Peter’s back in firm circles. Peter stiffens, then slowly melts into it.

The four settle into a loose tangle — knees pressed, shoulders leaning, contact steadying.

⸻

Act III — The Confession
Peter (low, almost embarrassed):
“I thought I’d snap. That when they pressed harder, I’d break instead of hold.”

Jonathan shakes his head.
Jonathan: “You didn’t snap. You sang. All of you. The net held because each thread stayed in place.”

Alex (hoarse, against Kellyn’s shoulder):
“And I didn’t think I could stand it, seeing them turn their gaze on you again. But you—”
He swallows, unable to finish.

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“I was never standing alone. That’s the difference now.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Rest
The fire burns low. Jonathan’s dulcimer hums a softer rhythm, almost like a lullaby. Kellyn curls closer to Alex. Peter leans half into Jonathan, half into Kellyn, eyes closing.

Jonathan (low, resolute):
“Let empire rage. Tonight we rest. And resting is defiance too.”

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures leaning into each other by the embers, Mylar sheets folded at their feet. The animals lie close, calm at last. No hum, no shimmer — only breath, warmth, and touch.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“They meant to break us in noise. But in quiet, we found covenant deeper still — not in fight, not in fear, but in the rhythm of resting together.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 61, Episode 6: Morning Light

Cold Open — Plateau Dawn
The horizon glows pale gold. Breath clouds rise and dissolve in the cool air. The fire has gone to ash.

Kellyn stirs first, shifting against Alex’s chest. His arm tightens instinctively before he blinks awake.

Kellyn (whispering):
“You don’t have to guard me even when you sleep.”

Alex (low, rough):
“Maybe that’s when I guard you best.”

Title Card: Morning Light

⸻

Act I — The Tangle
Peter wakes to the soft clink of Jonathan’s dulcimer string being tested. He groans, scrubs his face, and mutters:

Peter: "You play even in your sleep?”

Jonathan smiles faintly.
Jonathan: “Only in dreams. This is just me warming my hands.”

Peter sits up slowly, then leans back down, resting his head briefly against Jonathan’s shoulder before straightening. Jonathan doesn’t move, just lets it happen.

⸻

Act II — The Small Gestures
Kellyn sits cross-legged, smoothing their hands through Alex’s hair — not neat, not ordered, just tracing the warmth of his scalp. Alex closes his eyes, letting it happen.

Jonathan braids a lock of Peter’s hair loosely, then undoes it again. Peter huffs but doesn’t pull away.

The air is quiet except for soft animal sounds — the sheep shifting, Estrella snorting, feathers of the bird drone rustling faintly as it powers on.

⸻

Act III — The Confessions in Passing
Alex (murmuring, eyes still shut):
“Every morning I expect to wake and find you gone. Not dead — just… gone.”

Kellyn presses their forehead to his.
Kellyn (softly):
“Then every morning is proof I’m still here. That’s covenant, Alex. For as long as we both shall live.”

Across the fire pit, Jonathan finishes the braid properly this time. Peter eyes him, muttering:

Peter (dry): "You’ll make me soft at this rate.”

Jonathan: "Softness is just another kind of strength.”

Peter doesn’t argue, which is answer enough.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Dawn
They share the first food of the day — dried fruit, water, a bit of grain mash. Nothing lavish, but eaten slowly, together.

Kellyn leans back against Alex; Peter sits close enough that Jonathan’s arm brushes his. The firepit is cold, but their warmth circles them.

Jonathan (quiet, thoughtful):
“Empire makes noise to keep us from hearing mornings like this. But mornings hold longer than noise.”

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures ringed by animals and ashes, breath steaming into gold light. Each touch — braid, hand in hair, head against shoulder — small and unremarkable, but woven into a net strong enough to hold.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“In noise, we braced. In quiet, we breathed. But in morning, we touched — not as defense, not as fear, but as covenant carried in the simplest gestures.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 61, Episode 7: The Cracking Sky

Cold Open — Trailhead Morning
The four shoulder their packs. Estrella snorts as the sheep jostle. The air feels heavy, faintly metallic. Jonathan pauses, looking upward.

Jonathan (low):
“Sky shouldn’t sound like that.”

A low groan ripples through the air, as if the clouds themselves are straining.

Title Card: The Cracking Sky

⸻

Act I — The First Tremor
They move quickly across the plateau. The sound deepens — not thunder, but something grinding overhead. Peter pulls his knife, absurd against the sky, but he keeps it in hand.

Kellyn (steady):
“Empire doesn’t waste noise. This is for us.”

Alex’s jaw works tight. He glances at Kellyn, then upward, scanning the horizon.

⸻

Act II — The Descent
A dark streak tears across the sky — a drone, heavier and faster than any they’ve seen before. Its underside glitters with panels like fractured mirrors, scattering the light.

Jonathan (grim):
“It’s mapping the ground, looking for resonance.”

Kellyn:
“Or for us.”

Estrella rears, sheep pressing close. The hum builds into a pressure that rattles their teeth.

⸻

Act III — The Response
Alex yanks the mylar sheets from his pack, snapping them open. Kellyn and Jonathan grab reeds, spreading mats against the ground. Peter slams his knife into the soil, anchoring one edge of the mylar so the others can pull it taut.

The drone arcs lower, beam sweeping.

Kellyn (shouting over the hum):
“Spread the impact — like Mylar! Don’t let it crack through!”

They crouch beneath the makeshift cover. The sound bends, warps, then dulls — still loud, but not piercing.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Pressure
For minutes, the drone circles. Then, as suddenly as it arrived, it veers west, vanishing over the ridge.

Silence returns, jagged and shaky. Their hands stay locked on the mylar edges, knuckles white, as if none of them dare let go first.

Peter (hoarse, almost laughing):
“So… we won?”

Jonathan (quiet, firm):
“No. We survived. That’s different.”

Kellyn’s hand trembles, but Alex covers it with his own.

Alex (low):
“And survival is covenant enough.”

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures crouched beneath shimmering mylar stretched across reeds, Estrella and the sheep huddled inside the fragile circle. Above, the sky is empty — for now.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“They cracked the sky to break us. But we bent, we spread, we held. And for once, the noise left us standing.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 61, Episode 8: The Fault Lines

Cold Open — Plateau Edge
The drone’s hum is gone, but the air still quivers with what it left behind. The mylar sheets sag across the reeds. Estrella stamps nervously. The sheep are quiet, pressed close. The four sit in a loose square, silence heavy.

Peter (snapping first):
“You almost got us killed, throwing cloth at the sky like that.”

Alex (biting back):
“It worked, didn’t it?”

Title Card: The Fault Lines

⸻

Act I — The Blame
Peter jabs his knife into the dirt, glaring.
Peter:
“Worked this time. Empire doesn’t repeat mistakes. Next time, they’ll adapt.”

Jonathan (measured, too calm):
“Then so will we.”

Peter rounds on him.
Peter: “You talk like singing fixes everything. But noise like that doesn’t care about your songs.”

Jonathan stiffens, words caught in his throat.

Kellyn: "The noise may not care, but the nervous system does. It helps, even if it's not everything."

⸻

Act II — The Fracture
Alex’s voice cuts low.
Alex: “Don’t pretend you weren’t terrified too. You froze until Kellyn shouted.”

Peter’s face tightens, shame flaring into fury.
Peter: “And you didn’t? You hid under mylar like a child. Only reason it worked was dumb luck.”

Kellyn (finally, sharp):
“Sometimes, children are wiser than adults.  It wasn't luck.  It was everyone. If one of us hadn’t held, the rest of us would have covered for them. You’re both still acting like we each stand alone.  We don't."

The silence afterward is sharp enough to cut.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Jonathan leans forward, voice quieter now.

Jonathan: “You’re right. But Peter’s fear isn’t wrong. Empire adapts. The question isn’t who failed. It’s whether we can change faster than they do.”

Peter looks away, jaw tight. Alex exhales, shoulders sagging, the fight draining from him.

Alex (hoarse):
“And whether we trust each other enough to try.”

Kellyn: "There's no if.  Empire cannot adapt faster than us, because it's monopolar, because it never doubts itself.  It's blind to its own weaknesses.  Luckily, we have each other to remind us each of our shortcomings."

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Fault Lines
Kellyn pulls the mylar taut again, spreading it across their laps, forcing all four to touch it.

Kellyn (steady):
“None of us is enough alone. But together, even our fractures hold. That’s covenant. That’s how we keep moving.”

Peter’s grip loosens on the knife. Jonathan nods once, slow. Alex doesn’t look up, but his hand moves to Kellyn's, holding it with rest.

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures sitting under a sagging sheet of mylar, tension carved across their faces but hands still touching the same fragile fabric. Above them, the sky is clear, but not innocent.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire struck to split us. And in the silence after, we saw the fault lines run deeper than the sky. But even fault lines can hold — if the weight is carried together.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 61, Episode 9: The Small Repairs

Cold Open — Plateau Night
The fire burns low. The animals sleep close, their breathing a slow rhythm. The four sit in the circle still, but looser now, exhaustion softening the sharpness. The mylar lies folded beside them, quiet.

Kellyn (soft, almost teasing):
“We’re not very good at fighting. Too many sharp corners, not enough give.”

Peter huffs, not quite a laugh. Alex stares into the fire. Jonathan watches the sparks rise.

Title Card: The Small Repairs

⸻

Act I — The First Thread
Peter breaks the silence.
Peter (low):
“I didn’t mean what I said about luck. You held. I just… couldn’t stand how close it was.”

Alex looks at him, startled by the admission.
Alex (hoarse):
“I was terrified too. I shouldn’t have pretended otherwise.”

The space between them shifts — not healed, but less jagged.

⸻

Act II — The Offering
Jonathan picks up a shard of clay from the fire’s edge, turning it in his fingers.
Jonathan:
“Cracks are natural. Every vessel gets them. You don’t throw it away — you patch it, fire it again, make it stronger. It’s the same with us.”

Kellyn leans over, gently setting their hand over his.
Kellyn:
“Then let’s stop looking for who caused the cracks. Let’s start patching.”

Jonathan nods, eyes soft.

⸻

Act III — The Gesture
Alex finally stirs, pulling a piece of dried fruit from the stores. He splits it, passes half to Peter. Peter blinks, then takes it, chewing slowly.

Peter (quiet, almost shy):
“Truce?”
Alex (meeting his gaze):
“Truce.”

Kellyn smiles faintly. Jonathan exhales, a low hum in his throat, carrying the truce into sound.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Repairs
They lie down near the fire, closer than before, the night cold enough to push them into shared warmth. No words now — only breath, quiet and steady.

Kellyn (narration):
“It wasn’t absolution. It wasn’t trust made whole. But even fractures can be sealed, if the patch holds. And sometimes, the smallest repair is enough to see the night through.”

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures curled together by the fire, edges softened by shadow, not mended but holding — a vessel cracked, patched, and still useful.

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 61, Episode 10: The Fever’s Edge

Cold Open — Plateau Shelter
Morning light filters through reed mats. Sam stirs on his mat, sweat damp across his temples. Estrella flicks her ears nearby, restless. Kellyn kneels with a damp cloth, dabbing gently at his face.

Sam (murmuring, half-dreaming):
“The lines aren’t gone… just loosened. You’ll have to… pull again.”

Kellyn freezes, exchanging a glance with Alex, who crouches on the other side.

Title Card: The Fever’s Edge

⸻

Act I — The Waking
Sam blinks, eyes clearer than before. His voice is rough but steady.
Sam:
“I remember more now. The map wasn’t just coordinates. It was a sequence. A rhythm. If you follow it wrong, it binds tighter.”

Jonathan leans forward, brows knit.
Jonathan:
“Like a song sung in the wrong key.”
Sam nods weakly, closing his eyes again.

⸻

Act II — The Care
Peter lifts a small clay cup, tilting it to Sam’s lips. Sam swallows slowly, grimacing.

Peter (quiet, almost tender):
“You’re not leaving this camp until you can stand without shaking. No more martyr tricks.”

Sam almost smiles.
Sam:
“You sound like someone who plans to keep me alive.”
Peter doesn’t answer, just sets the cup down firmly.

⸻

Act III — The Premonition
Later, as Sam drifts back to sleep, he murmurs again, voice distant:
Sam:
“Three more loci. One broken, one hidden, one waiting in plain sight. And when you touch the last… empire will know you fully.”

Kellyn’s hand stills on the cloth. Alex’s jaw tightens, but he doesn’t speak. Jonathan hums low under his breath, like he’s grounding the room.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Care
As Sam settles, Kellyn looks at the others.
Kellyn (steady):
“Whatever empire knows, it won’t matter if we keep each other alive. That’s the only sequence that matters.”

Alex reaches for Kellyn’s hand, grip firm. Peter and Jonathan both nod silently, forming a quiet circle around Sam’s mat.

⸻

Closing Image
Sam asleep, the four around him, hands touching in an unbroken chain. Above them, the reed mats ripple faintly with the hum, but inside the circle, breath steadies into one rhythm.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“The fever broke, but not the field. His body weakened, his words sharpened — a compass, fragile and strange. And in the waiting, we learned: care is its own unbinding.”

Fade out.
End of Season 61.
