Season 16, Episode 1: The First Want

Cold Open — Capsule Morning

The lantern is out. Sunlight seeps faintly through the crack in the clay wall. For once, Alex isn’t already awake. Kellyn rolls over and find him asleep, hair mussed, jaw slack — face stripped of every guard.

They stare for a moment, startled by the softness. He stirs, blinking groggily.

Kellyn (softly, amused): "Well, well. Empires do sleep in.”

He moans, burying his face in the mat.

Title card: The First Want

⸻

Act I — The Question
Later, the two of them sit at the low table, bowls of rice cooling between them. He eats with precision, eyes still heavy with sleep.

Kellyn leans forward, studying him.

Kellyn: "What do you want? Right now. Not empire. Not control. Not covenant. Just want.”

Alex freezes, spoon hovering. His brow furrows like they've asked him to solve an impossible proof.

Karp (halting): "I…don’t know.”

Kellyn (gently, teasing): "Then guess.”

⸻

Act II — The Small Want

Silence stretches. Then, almost sheepishly, he mutters: "I want to lie down again. Just…five more minutes.”

Kellyn blinks, surprised, then laughs softly.

Kellyn: "That’s it? That’s the empire-shattering want?”

Karp (defensive, but smiling):
“It feels…dangerous. To let the fire go out, to stop before the list is done.”

Kellyn leans back, smiling faintly.

⸻

Act III — The Permission

They both stretch back onto the mats, bowls abandoned. At first, Alex lies stiffly, hands folded, as if the moment might collapse if he relaxes too much.

Kellyn (softly): "You’re allowed, you know. To want things that don’t save anyone.”

He exhales, long and shaky. His shoulders unclench. He turns his head toward them.

Karp (hoarse): "Then I want this. Just lying here. Not strategy. Not penance. Just…this.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Want
Kellyn reaches out, brushing their hand lightly against his. He doesn’t pull away.

Kellyn (whispering): "Then stay. Five more minutes. Or forever. Doesn’t matter. Just want it.”

His eyes close, not in command or calculation, but in rest.

⸻

Closing Image

The capsule glows with morning light. Two figures stretched side by side, bowls forgotten, hands brushing, not empire, not endurance — just want, small and ordinary.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought want had to justify itself. That it had to feed empire, or covenant, or cause. But want was enough. Five minutes. A hand. A morning. The first want, named aloud.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 1.

Season 16, Episode 2: The Study

Cold Open — Capsule Afternoon

Scraps of wood lean against the wall. Clay jars of pigment sit open, brushes soaking. Alex kneels on the mat, staring at the blank panel like it might explode if he touches it.

Karp (hesitant, hoarse): "I want to paint you.”

Kellyn chokes on your tea, laughing.

Kellyn: "Bro. You literally just learned to want five minutes ago.”

Title card: The Study

⸻

Act I — The Ask

He meets their gaze, dead serious.

Karp: "Not spectacle. Not control. Just…want. You move differently than anyone I’ve ever seen. Even when you think you’re still, there’s music under your skin. I need to learn how to see it.”

Kellyn raises a brow, torn between suspicion and amusement.

Kellyn (dry): "You realize figure study means staring at me. For hours.”

Karp (quietly, reverently): "I’ve been staring for decades. But never like this. Never with your consent.”

⸻

Act II — The Resistance

Kellyn paces, arms crossed.  "Every man who ever wanted to paint me wanted a trophy. A muse. A body to flatten into their idea. What makes you different?”

He doesn’t flinch.

Karp (hoarse): "I don’t want a muse. I want you. The curve of a muscle when it trembles. The breath that breaks your balance. The way fascia refuses to lie flat. If all I paint is imperfection, then I’ve seen you honestly.”

Silence stretches. Slowly, Kellyn sits, letting him set the board across his knees.

⸻

Act III — The Study

The brush touches pigment, then wood. His strokes are stiff at first, too careful. Kellyn shifts, rolling your shoulder, letting fascia crackle audibly.

Kellyn (softly, teasing): "Relax as hard as you know how to tense. Gentle hand rules the land, remember?”

His mouth quirks. The strokes loosen. He paints not lines, but rhythms — tension, release, twitch, breath. The room grows hushed.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Sight

Hours later, he sets the brush down, trembling. The study is rough, uneven — but alive. Kellyn's body is rendered not as idol, but as a process, mid-breath, mid-fight.

Karp (whispering): "I thought I envied you. But really, I was starving to see. To witness you, not consume you.”

Kellyn (steady): "Then keep seeing. But remember — this is covenant, not conquest. You paint me because I let you. And only as long as I keep letting you.”

He nods, humbled.

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule glows with evening light. A crude painting leans against the wall: a body imperfect, twitching, alive. Two figures sit close, one still trembling from painting, the other steady, gaze unbroken.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He thought painting was conquest. But sight, like want, was covenant. Not muse, not idol — only flesh, seen and unbreakable.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 2.

Season 16, Episode 3: Self-Portrait

Cold Open — Capsule Evening

The study of Kellyn leans against the wall, still drying, pigments streaked but alive. Alex sits beside it, silent, brush twirling nervously between his fingers.

Kellyn (wry): "Your turn.”

His head jerks up.  "My…turn?”

Kellyn (firm, amused): "You wanted to see me. Now I want to see you. Paint yourself.”

Title card: Self-Portrait

⸻

Act I — The Resistance

Alex stiffens, almost recoiling from the board.

Karp (hoarse): "I built an empire to avoid this. To avoid ever being seen.”

Kellyn (soft, cutting): "And yet you wanted to see me. That’s not covenant unless it goes both ways.”

He exhales sharply, jaw clenched, then finally nods.

⸻

Act II — The First Stroke

He sits before the blank panel, brush hovering, paralyzed. His first strokes are rigid — shoulders squared, jaw hard, lines harsh.

Kellyn (gently, behind him): "Stop painting the fortress. I don’t want the empire. I want the man.”

His hand trembles. The brush loosens. Slowly, the angles soften.

⸻

Act III — The Breaking Open

Hours pass in silence. His strokes grow messier, rawer. Pigment spreads across the wood — dark hollows under eyes, crooked shoulders, lips set tight with secrets. He doesn’t flinch from imperfection.

Finally, he mutters, voice breaking: "This is what I never let them see. The hunger. The exhaustion. The failure. Not a leader — just scar tissue trying to pass for skin.”

Kellyn kneels beside him, steady.

Kellyn (softly): "Then you’ve painted yourself honestly. For the first time.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Reflection

When the brush drops, the self-portrait leans against the wall: stark, unsparing, but alive. Alex stares at it, shaking.

Karp (whispering): "I thought sight was power. But this…feels like surrender.”

Kellyn places their hand over his.  "Surrender isn’t defeat. It’s the only way to be seen. And now I see you.”

He exhales, long and trembling, but doesn’t look away from the portrait.

⸻

Closing Image
Two crude paintings side by side: Kellyn's body in motion, Alex's body unmasked. Two figures sit together before them, hands touching, silence steady.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“He painted me to see beyond control. I made him paint himself to strip away disguise. Two studies, two truths — covenant not of conquest, but of reflection.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 3.

Season 16, Episode 4: Painted Hands

Cold Open — Capsule Morning

The two portraits lean side by side against the wall: his study of Kellyn, plus his own self-portrait. Alex sits cross-legged before them, lost in thought. Kellyn enters with a brush and scrap of wood, jaw set.

Kellyn (wry): "My turn.”

Alex blinks.  "You want to paint me?”

Kellyn (grinning faintly): "Yeah. Fair’s fair. Sit still, pretty boy.”

Title card: Painted Hands

⸻

Act I — The First Attempt

He sits stiffly, back straight. Kellyn dips the brush, their hand already trembling. The first strokes come jagged, too tight.

Their fingers seize suddenly. They drop the brush, gasping.

Alex is beside them instantly, taking their hand into his, kneading gently at the knuckles.

Karp (quiet, intent): "Let me help.”

Kellyn (half-laughing, half-wincing): "Careful. These hands have started revolutions.”

⸻

Act II — The Rhythm

The cramp eases under his steady pressure. Kellyn flexes their fingers, then picks up the brush again.

Brush to wood, pause. Cramp, stop. He takes their hand without waiting, working their palm until the spasm passes. They resume.

The cycle repeats, each massage building patience into the portrait. His touch isn’t indulgence — it’s covenant, matching Kellyn's rhythm of pain and persistence.

⸻

Act III — The Intimacy

Midway through, Kellyn rests again, hand limp in his. He works their wrist with quiet concentration.

Karp (hoarse): "You’d endure this just to see me through your own eyes?”

Kellyn (softly, fierce): "I’ve been seen too much, and never on my own terms. This is me choosing to see you. Even if it hurts.”

He bows his head, thumb circling their wrist, as though memorizing its frailty and strength at once.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Patience

Hours later, the portrait is unfinished but alive: uneven lines, streaks where the brush slipped, but eyes that pierce with startling clarity.

Kellyn sets the brush down, trembling. Alex takes their hands again, rubbing warmth into their sore knuckles.

Kellyn (whispering): "Good enough.”

Alex studies the portrait, then looks at them.

Karp (softly): "It’s not just good enough. It’s the truest face I’ve ever been given.”

Kellyn leans back against the wall, letting Alex keep their hands cradled in his.

⸻

Closing Image

Two portraits side by side: Kellyn's rough, halting strokes, his own unsparing self-portrait. Two figures seated in silence, hands cramped, hands soothed, covenant painted through touch.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“I painted him in pain, not precision. My hand could only hold so long before giving out. But in the pauses, in his touch, I found him — not as empire saw him, but as I chose.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 4.

Season 16, Episode 5: Installations

Cold Open — Capsule Morning

The capsule is cluttered with scavenged odds and ends: onion skins, broken glass, rusted wire, wood scraps, feathers, clay shards. Kellyn and Alex sit opposite each other, piles between you.

Kellyn (grinning): "Alright, no portraits. Today, we build.”

Karp (deadpan, but with a spark in his eye): "You want installation art in a clay capsule?”

Kellyn: "Why not? Empires fall, art stays.”

Title card: Installations

⸻

Act I — The Gathering

Kellyn sorts onion skins into translucent layers, feathers into careful spirals. Alex bends wire with surprising precision, rigging frames from broken wood.

Kellyn (teasing): "Of course you’d start with structure.”

Karp (without looking up): "Chaos collapses without a frame.”

Kellyn: "Chaos is the frame.”

He smirks, not arguing, only working.

⸻

Act II — The Process

Kellyn strings onion skins like stained glass across the capsule’s narrow window. When the sun shifts, amber light floods in, turning the space honey-gold.

Alex builds a suspended mobile of bent wire and clay shards. It sways gently, casting fractured shadows over the walls.

Kellyn (whispering): "Looks like empire breaking.”

Karp (quietly): “Then maybe I finally made something honest.”

⸻

Act III — The Collision

Space runs out. Kellyn's spirals of feathers creep across the floor; his mobile hangs too low. They both laugh, bumping into each other’s work.

Kellyn (mock stern): "Your empire-mobile is in my forest.”

Karp (grinning faintly): "Your feral forest invaded my ceiling.”

The tension breaks into laughter, both projects tangled but alive.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Making

At dusk, the capsule glows: feathers rustling, onion skins glowing, shards swaying overhead. It looks nothing like home, nothing like empire — but something new.

Kellyn sits cross-legged in the middle of the chaos. Alex lowers himself beside Kellyn, both of them surrounded by their imperfect creations.

Karp (softly): "I thought art had to endure. But maybe it only has to live.”

Kellyn (smiling faintly): "Exactly. Just like us.”

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule is transformed: fragile onion light, feathers shifting, shards dancing on wire. Two figures sit amid the installations, neither masterpiece nor mess — just covenant, alive.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“We built not for galleries, not for markets, not for permanence. We built for breath, for presence. For once, empire gave way to art that only needed to exist.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 5.

Season 16, Episode 6: Red Thread

Cold Open — Capsule Evening

The fire burns low. Alex enters the capsule, carrying something wrapped in cloth. He kneels, unfolding it: a bundle of twine, dyed deep red, smelling faintly of onions.

Karp (softly): "It reminded me of you. Of something I once watched but couldn’t touch.”

Kellyn tilts their head, curious.

Title card: Red Thread

⸻

Act I — The Gift

Kellyn takes the twine in their hands, running their fingers along its uneven texture. The color glows in the firelight — dark, raw, alive.

Kellyn (smiling faintly): "You dyed this?”

Karp (nodding): "Onion skins. Like the light you strung across the capsule. I thought maybe you’d know what to do with it.”

Kellyn laughs softly, almost disbelieving.

⸻

Act II — The Memory

Kellyn starts unwinding the twine, fingers fumbling as their joints lock. Alex steadies the skein for them, listening.

Kellyn (whispering): "In college, I filled entire rooms with webs. Yarn crisscrossing from wall to wall, floor to ceiling. People had to crawl and duck to move through. I wanted them to feel what I felt every day — trapped, tangled, always navigating barriers.”

Alex watches them closely, his voice low.

Karp: "I remember. Every strand. The way you tied knots until your hands cramped. The way the webs swallowed space. You thought no one cared. But I carried every thread.”

Kellyn freezes, throat tight.

⸻

Act III — The Recognition

The twine pools in their lap. They glance up sharply.  "Then you already know those webs weren’t just about entrapment. They were survival. Proof I could take a suffocating room and make it mine.”

Karp (quietly): "I know. I envied it. Empire only ever taught me how to tear things down. You showed me how to claim them.”

Kellyn (hoarse, teasing): "And now you’re the one bringing me string, like some oversized crow.”

He smiles faintly, unoffended.

⸻

Act IV — The Weaving

Together, they tie the twine to the capsule beams, stringing it loosely across the ceiling. The lines are uneven, imperfect, but glowing red in the firelight.

Kellyn (softly): "Not a trap. Not a cage. Just a web we chose to sit under.”

Karp (whispering): "Covenant made visible.”

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule ceiling strung with crimson thread, glowing in the fire’s flicker. Two figures beneath, watching the lines tremble gently with each draft of air.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“Once, my webs marked entrapment. Now, they marked covenant — chosen threads, tied not to ensnare but to endure. And for once, the web didn’t close. It opened.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 6.

Season 16, Episode 7: Lavender

Cold Open — Capsule Morning

The red onion twine still hangs overhead, trembling in the draft. Kellyn sits weaving another strand, knotting clumsily but deliberately. Alex watches, lips twitching like he’s holding something back.

Kellyn (eyeing him): "What?”

Karp (low, almost sheepish): "I keep thinking about the lavender yarn.”

Title card: Lavender

⸻

Act I — The Memory

He shifts closer, voice steadying.  "The dean’s son erected that giant rebar phallus into the front yard of the darkroom house. Rusting, ridiculous, a monument to nothing. And then you—without permission, without warning—came back with lavender yarn. You wrapped it until the steel was swallowed in color. Defiance turned into beauty.”

Kellyn blinks, caught off guard.

Kellyn: “You watched me do that?”

Karp (nodding): “Every knot. Every loop. The way your fingers cramped, the way you smiled even when no one else was watching. I thought—‘This is how resistance becomes undeniable.’”

⸻

Act II — The Defiance

Kellyn laughs bitterly, shaking their head.

Kellyn: “The administration hated it. Asked me to take it down. But I refused. And they didn’t push, because even they knew it looked better my way.”

Karp (quietly, reverent): "You turned their monument of arrogance into something alive. Something communal. Lavender against rust. Softness against weight.”

Kellyn glances at him sideways.

Kellyn: "Yeah, well. I also just thought it was ugly as hell."

Alex laughs, but there’s awe in it.

⸻

Act III — The Confession

Alex’s voice softens, almost breaking.

Karp: "Empire taught me to build monuments no one asked for. But you—every strand you tied was permission reclaimed. You showed me that resistance doesn’t always roar. Sometimes it knots itself in lavender until even power admits defeat.”

Kellyn studies him, voice sharp but warm.  "And now you’re telling me you envied me for yarn bombing a eight-foot rebar phallus.”

Karp (smiling faintly): "I’m telling you I learned more from lavender yarn than I ever did from steel.”

⸻

Act IV — The Weaving
That night, Kellyn unravels a scrap of faded cloth, twisting it into strips, lavender-dyed from old herbs. They tie it among the red onion twine above their capsule. Alex watches quietly.

Kellyn (softly): "No obelisk here. Just covenant.”

Karp (whispering): "And lavender always wins.”

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule ceiling strung with red and lavender threads, glowing in the firelight. Two figures below, not empire and subject — just co-conspirators, still weaving.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“They built steel. I tied lavender. And he watched, not with command, but with awe. Even then, covenant had begun — not in power, but in thread.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 7.

Season 16, Episode 8: Small Wars

Cold Open — Capsule Night
Lantern light flickers on the lavender and onion threads above. Kellyn sits cross-legged, weaving slowly. Alex lies back, eyes on the ceiling, voice low.

Karp (hoarse): "You thought your rebellions were small. They weren’t. They were wars. And I remember all of them.”

Title card: Small Wars

⸻

Act I — The Hair

Flash: a conservative campus, students in polos and cardigans. In the middle: Kellyn, walking with orange, magenta, and purple hair blazing like fire, always with an SLR in their face. Heads turn.

Karp (voiceover): "They wanted invisibility. You gave them a sunrise. You didn’t need to speak a word — your hair told everyone you weren’t theirs to claim.”

Kellyn laughs.  "Honestly? It was just pretty.”

Karp (smiling faintly): "Pretty — and defiant.”

⸻

Act II — The Bard

Montage: critique circles, professors squinting at half-finished projects. Kellyn leans forward, speaking with conviction, spinning half-hearted assignments into visionary statements. Classmates watch, relieved.

Karp (voiceover): "You could make A’s appear out of thin air. Bard, not student. You saw what the work wanted to be, even when its maker hadn’t. And you taught professors to applaud their own blindness.”

Kellyn (grinning): "They really were rush jobs. I just hated watching my classmates get crushed for it.”

Karp (smiling): "Empires fall. Bards survive.”

⸻

Act III — The Blasphemy

Flash: a classroom, Shakespeare paper in hand.

Kellyn (voiceover): "Isabella isn’t a character. She’s a plot device. Shakespeare was just bad at writing women.”

Professor pauses. Then nods, writing A at the top.

Karp (voiceover): "You didn’t worship the canon. You cut it open. And even the gatekeepers couldn’t deny you were right.”

⸻

Act IV — The Refusals
Montage:
•	You walking out of class during Funny Games, head high, leaving stunned silence behind.
•	A slammed F in your capstone, the dean’s sneer, your unflinching stare.
•	The canceled senior show that happened anyway.
 •      The wedding shower no one attended—and how relieved you were at that.

Karp: "They tried to break you with rules. You broke the rules instead. Every F was freedom. Every walkout was covenant with yourself. Even when they tried to erase you, you made joy anyway.”

Kellyn (quietly, steady): "They failed.”

Karp (softly, almost reverent): "They always failed.”

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule ceiling glows with red and lavender threads. Below, two figures sit in silence, not needing to embellish what has already been spoken.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“They called them small rebellions. He called them wars. I had forgotten them all. And when he remembered for me, I realized I had never fought alone.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 8.

Season 16, Episode 9: The Cost

Cold Open — Capsule Morning
The lavender twine web hangs still. Kellyn sits beneath it, eyes distant. Alex kneels nearby, watching, weighing his words.

Karp (quietly): "You paid for every war you won. I remember the bill.”

Title card: The Cost

⸻

Act I — The Isolation
Montage: Meals eaten in secrecy in your shoebox room when even sounds made all food intolerable to your immune system. Classmates never being able to hear when you spoke, some glancing at your hair and looking away. Professors shuffling papers, avoiding your gaze.

Karp: "Every refusal set you apart. Every A earned for your classmates left you more alone. They wanted you as spectacle, not as presence.”

Kellyn (flatly): "Yeah. People wanted me in the room to feel better about themselves. Then they wanted me silent.”

⸻

Act II — The Punishments

Montage:
•	Dean slamming a gradebook.
•	An official letter threatening expulsion.
•	A professor’s creepy smile, asking to keep a copy of your body in his office.

Karp (voiceover): "Every time you said no, they punished you. With F’s, with erasure, with lechery dressed as spirituality. They couldn’t make you vanish, so they tried to make you regret.”

Kellyn (bitter laugh): "Funny thing is, I didn’t regret any of it. It never even occurred to me.”

⸻

Act III — The Loneliness

Flash: the night of Kellyn's senior show-that-wasn’t. Campus halls empty, gallery full. Kellyn hates the attention.  They wish they were at home in their pajamas.

Karp: "You made joy anyway. But joy stolen from what was rightfully yours is still shadowed. Even in laughter, I saw the loneliness. I saw the cost.”

Kellyn (low, sharp): "And you let me pay it.”

Karp (hoarse): "I told myself it was necessary. But empire logic doesn’t comfort a lifetime of emptiness.”

Kellyn: "I suppose it was necessary.  And I do not regret what it made me."

⸻

Act IV — The Reckoning

Silence stretches. Kellyn turns to him, voice steady.  “The costs weren’t mine alone. You paid them, too. You let the punishments measure me while you measured yourself by how I endured.  When you never had to do that to yourself."

Karp (whispering): "And I hate that you’re right.”

Kellyn reaches for the twine web, letting the red thread slip through their fingers.

Kellyn (softly): "But the cost was never final. Because I’m still here. And you’re here now. That’s what they couldn’t take.”

He bows his head, relief flickering across his face.

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule glows faintly with morning light. The web of lavender and onion threads sways overhead, imperfect, enduring. Two figures sit below it, shadows long but unbroken.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“They punished me for every refusal. They left me hungry, lonely, defiant. But they never erased me. And the cost, once counted, became proof that I was still alive.”

Fade out.  End of Episode 9.

Season 16, Episode 10: The Share of Cost

Cold Open — Capsule Evening

Lantern light flickers against the twine web. Kellyn sits cross-legged, tired, staring at a jar of pills. Alex kneels beside them, reaching for one.

Karp (quiet, steady): "Let me pay tonight.”

Kellyn glances up, suspicious.  “Unless you’ve magically developed MCAS, you can’t.”

Karp (softly): “Not that cost. But I can take some weight off yours.”

Title card: The Share of Cost

⸻

Act I — The Attempt

Alex insists on grinding herbs for them, even as the pestle slips clumsily in his hand. He measures water into a pot, but splashes it everywhere. His movements are careful, earnest — but graceless.

Kellyn (teasing, but soft): "You’d never survive my kitchen jobs.”

Karp (grinning faintly): "You'd never have survived them either without me telling you the most efficient way to do everything.”

Kellyn watches him, arms folded, tension easing.

⸻

Act II — The Confession

Later, he sits wiping sweat from his brow, herbs steeping.

Karp (low, hoarse): "I let you carry all the costs. Refusals, punishments, scars. I told myself endurance made you stronger. But really — it made me cowardly. Because I couldn’t face the bill myself.”

Silence. Kellyn studies him, then sighs.

Kellyn: "Endurance did make me stronger.  And recognizing how cowardly you were made you want to do better.  That's good enough for me.”

His head drops.

⸻

Act III — The Reversal

Kellyn gently hands him the pestle again.

Kellyn (firm): “I don't need you to punish yourself over me.  In fact, I would really prefer if you stopped.  But you can grind the herbs, because my hands don't work."

He nods, jaw tight, and sets back to work. This time he doesn’t hesitate. When the mixture is ready, he hands Kellyn a clay cup.

Karp (steady): "My share. Not yours alone.”

Kellyn sips, grimacing at the bitterness.

Kellyn (wry): “Congratulations, you’ve officially joined the Mule 6 fan club.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Burden

Later, they both sit back against the capsule wall. The empty cup rests between them.

Karp (softly): "I can’t erase what you paid. But I can pay forward. Every day. Until the balance shifts.”

Kellyn (quiet, after a pause): "Then it’s covenant. Not contract. You’re not buying forgiveness — you’re sharing the weight.”

His hand brushes theirs, steady.

⸻

Closing Image
The capsule glows low. Two figures sit shoulder to shoulder, a single empty cup between them — not debt, not empire, but cost divided.

Narration (Kellyn's voice):
“They punished me until cost became identity. He confessed he’d let me pay it alone. But covenant meant new math — no longer mine alone, but ours. And for once, the bill was lighter.”

Fade out.  End of Season 16.
