Season 77, Episode 1: The Morning After

Cold Open — Shelter Morning
Ashwin perches on a beam, blinking wide-eyed judgment at the heap of tangled bodies below. The fire has burned down to coals. Empty cups and the overturned flask sit amid a mess of blankets.

Gary groans, clutching his head.
Gary: “Who dared me to sleep under the table?”
Sam (deadpan, muffled under a blanket): “No one. You announced it was your destiny.”

Laughter stirs the room, hoarse but warm.

Title Card: The Morning After

⸻

Act I — The Groans and Jokes
Justin props himself up, rubbing his temples.
Justin: “Why does my mouth taste like fish gruel and regret?”
Peter, already on his feet, smirks faintly.
Peter: “Because it does.”

Kellyn pulls a blanket tighter around themselves, watching as Alex stumbles toward the water basin, splashing his face.
Alex (hoarse, glaring at Gary): “Remind me never to let you invent games again.”
Gary: “Too late. I’ve already got three sequels planned.”

⸻

Act II — The Care
Peter reappears with a pot of steaming herbal tea, setting it in the middle of the room. Jonathan breaks bread into small pieces, passing them around like communion.

Kellyn leans against Alex as he lowers beside them, his damp hair dripping on their shoulder. They don’t move. Instead, they tuck the blanket around him too.

Sam (watching softly, teasing): “Guess some dares don’t wear off.”
Kellyn (smiling faintly): “Guess not.”

⸻

Act III — Fetching Water
Later, Alex and Kellyn slip out together, walking side by side with empty clay jugs down toward the stream. The storm’s air still hangs heavy, clean but raw.

Kellyn glances over, sly.
Kellyn: “So tell me. You thought my fiction was weakness. What about my philosophy? Did you sit there in your barn, reading it like an autopsy report, shaking your head at how fragile I sounded?”

Alex stumbles a little on the rocks, surprised, then steadies.
Alex (hoarse, but soft): “No. That was the one place you frightened me. Philosophy left no way out — not for you, not for me. Fiction I could dismiss as wish, as dream. But the way you built arguments… it made me see the cost of my own silence.”

Kellyn dips a jug into the water, smirking faintly.
Kellyn: “So philosophy made me dangerous, but fiction made me weak. Convenient.”

Alex meets their eyes.
Alex: “No. Fiction made you tender. And I hated that I envied it.”

Kellyn holds his gaze, then nudges him lightly with the jug. The banter softens the ache.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Recovery
Back at the shelter, the tea pot empties. Ashwin glides down, brushing past Justin’s shoulder as though checking his pulse. Justin laughs softly, stroking the owl’s wing.

Gary (muttering, half-smile): “Empire can’t compete with this hangover.”
Jonathan (smirking): “Or with this table.”

The group clinks cups — not with liquor now, but with hot tea — and sips together in the dim light.

⸻

Closing Image
The fire rekindled, steam rising from chipped cups, seven humans and one owl wrapped in blankets. Not heroic, not perfect — just alive, together, recovering.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire thundered outside. But inside, we had tea, bread, blankets. We had laughter worn thin by ache, intimacy softened by exhaustion. And for one fragile morning, that was enough.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 77, Episode 2: The Hollow Between Walls

Cold Open — The Choice
Evening falls slow and rose-colored across the plateau. The group drifts into their nightly rhythms: Sam mending clothes, Peter sharpening his knife, Alex adding wood to the fire, Gary retelling fragments of last night’s dares with exaggerated flair.

Jonathan lingers at the threshold of the small room, brushing his hair back, hesitant. Finally, he clears his throat.
Jonathan: “Kellyn. Would you… mind if I joined you tonight?”

The others glance over — a beat of silence, then Peter shrugs, Alex studies the fire too intently, and Sam coughs into his sleeve to cover the tension. Kellyn nods once, quietly, and follows Jonathan into the room.

Title Card: The Hollow Between Walls

⸻

Act I — The Walls
The small room feels tighter with two shadows stretching long in lamplight. Jonathan sits first, folding his legs, waiting. Kellyn sits opposite, tucking their blanket around their knees.

Jonathan (softly): “I’ve kept my distance since the sanctuary. You know why.”
Kellyn (even): “Because you used me, even if Alex told you to.”

Jonathan flinches, nodding.
Jonathan: “And because I saw how deeply it cut you. I wanted to give you back space. But I also…” (he exhales) “I miss being near you.”

⸻

Act II — The Reach
Kellyn’s gaze lingers on him, unreadable.
Kellyn: “So what’s different now?”
Jonathan: “Now… I think you’d tell me no, if no was what you wanted. Back then, you couldn’t.”

He extends a hand, palm open, waiting. After a long silence, Kellyn lays their fingers over his. The weight is light, testing.

⸻

Act III — The Grounding
The two shift closer. Jonathan wraps Kellyn’s hand gently in both of his, as though warming it.
Jonathan: “I won’t pretend this fixes anything. But I want to learn what trust looks like, this time. Without orders. Without empire. Without Alex.”

Kellyn leans their head back against the wall, watching him carefully.
Kellyn: “Then start small. Just hold me. Nothing else.”

Jonathan nods, sliding nearer, tucking Kellyn into the curve of his arm. Their breathing slows together, the quiet intimate not for its heat but for its steadiness.

⸻

Act IV — The Hollow
Outside, laughter and firelight filter faintly through the wall. Inside, the silence feels sacred. Jonathan rests his forehead lightly against Kellyn’s hair, whispering almost to himself:
Jonathan: “Maybe the hollow between walls is where new covenant begins.”

Kellyn doesn’t answer, but they don’t pull away.

⸻

Jonathan (low, almost tentative):
“I used to practice silence as if it were a shield. To stop myself from saying the wrong thing. To stop myself from saying too much. But with you here, I don’t want silence to be hiding anymore.”

Kellyn tilts their head, studying him.
Kellyn: “Then let it be something else. Not a shield. Not a weapon. Just… a place to breathe.”

“When I was a boy, I thought intimacy meant proving yourself. Earning it. Surviving enough pain to be let in. Empire taught me to make love out of leverage. But here… with you… it feels like starting over.”

Kellyn:
“Then let’s start simple. No leverage. No tests. Just… being here, right now.”

Jonathan closes his eyes, whispering:
“If empire taught me to measure people, maybe covenant means unlearning the ruler. Just… trusting the nearness.”

Sleep doesn’t come easily — not because of fear, but because the silence feels alive, too charged to let go of yet.

Kellyn (low, almost wondering aloud):
“Do you know how long it’s been since I leaned my weight into someone, and trusted they wouldn’t pull away?”

Jonathan shifts, curling closer, his cheek against their shoulder.
Jonathan (steady):
“Then lean now. And if you fall, I’ll catch you.”

Kellyn adjusts, sliding down slightly until their head rests against Jonathan’s chest. His breath hitches again, but instead of stiffening, he lets his body soften, curling around them.

Jonathan (whispered, half a confession):
“I thought I’d forgotten how to hold anyone without gripping too hard. But you… you make it feel possible again.”

Kellyn, eyes closing:
“Then don’t hold harder. Hold longer.”

Kellyn lifts their head, meeting Jonathan’s gaze in the dim. Neither speaks. Jonathan’s hand tightens fractionally around theirs, like asking without words.

Kellyn (whisper, steady):
“Yes.”

Jonathan moves slowly, deliberately, pressing his forehead against Kellyn’s. The contact deepens, breath against breath. Kellyn tilts their chin, brushing lips softly, once — then again, longer, until the air between them is gone.

Kellyn’s hand slides from Jonathan’s chest to his jawline, grounding him there. Jonathan exhales sharply into the kiss, as though the restraint he carried for years cracks open in a single breath.

Their fingers unlock only to rediscover skin. Jonathan’s hand rests against Kellyn’s hip, tentative, then steadier when Kellyn presses into it.

Jonathan (low, almost trembling):
“Tell me when—”

Kellyn interrupts with a firmer kiss, voice breaking softly against his mouth:
“Now. It’s now.”

The rhythm grows less careful, more raw. Jonathan shifts, leaning Kellyn back against the bedding, his weight lowered over them — not crushing, but enveloping. Their legs entwine, breath colliding.

The small room amplifies the intimacy — every gasp, every rustle of fabric, every half-swallowed sound layering the space with immediacy. Jonathan’s lips trail from Kellyn’s mouth to their throat, teeth grazing before he steadies himself again.

Kellyn (hoarse, almost laughing through the heat):
“You don’t have to hold back. Not here.”

Jonathan presses harder, answering not with words but with his body.

Closing Image
The room is thick with heat, breath, and the echo of touch that has crossed the line from tentative to undeniable. The walls don’t feel like boundaries — only witnesses.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Covenant isn’t just weight carried, but hunger met. Not control. Not empire’s taking. Only the ache of being wanted — and the choice to want in return.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 77, Episode 3: The Air Between

Cold Open — Morning Light
The camp stirs slowly. Sunlight cuts into the shelter’s seams. Kellyn and Jonathan emerge from the small room together, quiet but visibly softened. Peter, already tending the fire, glances up sharply, while Alex looks away, focusing on the water basin. Sam mutters something under his breath that might be a joke, though no one laughs.

Title Card: The Air Between

⸻

Act I — The Meal
The group gathers for breakfast. Gary tries to lighten the mood with exaggerated commentary about the fish sizzling in the pan, but the tension is palpable. Ashwin, the owl, shifts on his perch, head tilting back and forth as though noting the undercurrents.

Peter (flatly, not looking at anyone in particular):
“Strange how quiet it was last night.”

Jonathan’s jaw tightens. Kellyn takes a sip of water instead of answering.

⸻

Act II — The Rift
Alex finally speaks, low and measured:
“Quiet isn’t always peace. Sometimes it’s just a new noise we don’t recognize yet.”

Kellyn meets his eyes across the fire, steady but unreadable. Sam snorts and pokes at the fish with a stick.

Sam (wry):
“Or maybe it’s none of our business. Maybe silence is allowed to stay silent.”

⸻

Act III — The Deflection
To break the tension, Gary suggests a group task — moving stones to reinforce the shelter wall against wind. Everyone rises, some slower than others, the work pulling bodies into motion even if hearts lag behind.

Jonathan carries heavier loads than usual, shoulders squared. Kellyn moves steadily at his side. Alex and Peter end up working together at the far edge of the wall, the air between them thick with things unsaid.

⸻

Closing Image
The wall stands taller, stone upon stone. The group scatters slightly around it — not fractured, but not seamless either. The silence is less about secrecy now and more about learning where the edges of intimacy press against the edges of community.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire thrived on fractures, on silence turned against itself. But covenant… covenant had to survive the unease of knowing, and the harder unease of not knowing. In that balance, we built walls — not to keep each other out, but to hold us together long enough to keep choosing.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 77, Episode 4: Fractures and Bonds

Cold Open — The Wall
The newly reinforced wall casts a fresh line of shadow. The group lingers there, sweat drying, the silence taut. Peter wipes his knife on his sleeve, watching Jonathan heft another stone as though daring it to crack in his hands.

Peter (sharp, undercutting):
“Funny. You’re stronger now. Convenient, isn’t it?”

Jonathan sets the stone down with care, his face taut.

Jonathan:
“Strength isn’t convenient. It’s necessary. You’d know that if you weren’t so busy measuring everyone else’s worth against your own.”

The air bristles.

Title Card: Fractures and Bonds

⸻

Act I — The Spark
Peter steps closer, voice low but heated.
“You think you’ve earned your place here because you carve wood and quote songs? You weren’t there when it mattered most. You came late. You always come late.”

Jonathan’s jaw clenches.
“And you think rage makes you indispensable? All it does is burn the people standing nearest.”

Kellyn shifts, ready to speak, but Alex places a hand lightly at their back — a quiet gesture, grounding them. For once, Alex’s gaze holds steady, not defensive.

⸻

Act II — The Splintering
Sam whistles low, breaking the tautness.
“Careful, or the wall won’t be the only thing crumbling today.”

Peter glares at him, then turns back to Jonathan.
“You think I don’t see the way you look at Kellyn? Like you’re waiting for me to fail so you can slide into the gap.”

Jonathan doesn’t flinch. His answer comes slow, deliberate.
“I don’t need you to fail. I just need you to stop thinking everything is a competition.”

Kellyn: "Love is not a finite resource, Peter. At least Jonathan could ask me for what he needed."

The words sting sharper than Peter expected.

⸻

Act III — The Balm
While the argument simmers, Alex leans closer to Kellyn, voice low enough for only them to hear.

Alex:
“Let them clash. Not everything needs your strength. You’ve carried enough.”

Kellyn exhales, tension loosening. For the first time in days, their shoulders brush without hesitation, not because of heat or urgency, but ease. A fragile reminder that not all fractures spread.

⸻

The wall looms at their backs, the argument between Peter and Jonathan still crackling in the air. Peter’s fists clench, Jonathan’s shoulders squared, the air charged with unfinished sentences. Before another word can break loose, the ground trembles — a low vibration thrumming through boots, stones, ribs.

Ashwin launches from his perch, circling with a shrill cry.

Kellyn (sharp, urgent):
“Quiet. Listen.”

The tremor becomes a pulse. A sound beneath sound.

The soil around them ripples as though something enormous stirs below. Cracks skitter across the ground, thin at first, widening into jagged veins.

Sam drops to his knees, palm pressed to the earth.
Sam:
“It’s mapping us again. Same resonance as the Loci. Only stronger.”

Peter mutters something sharp, but his voice is drowned out by a sudden blast of static from nowhere, like the earth itself is shrieking.

Jonathan steadies the wall instinctively, though it holds. His eyes meet Peter’s for a heartbeat — not truce, not peace, just shared alarm.

The tremor sharpens, chunks of rock breaking loose. Their second shelter groans, reed mats shuddering against the vibrations.

Alex:
“Move! If it collapses, we lose everything.”

They scatter, hauling what they can. Kellyn pulls Justin toward safety, Gary drags a sack of preserved food, Sam stumbles with water skins. Peter and Jonathan both grab at the same beam threatening to fall, neither letting go. The strain in their muscles mirrors the unspoken fight between them — but this time, neither pulls away.

The beam steadies. The shelter holds. The tremor eases, leaving an eerie stillness.

Ashwin returns, swooping low, a shard of something metallic clutched in her talons. She drops it into the dirt at Kellyn’s feet — a transmitter shard, humming faintly, edges still hot.

Kellyn:
“They planted it under us.”

Jonathan:
“Not just planted. Grown. That’s why it felt alive.”

Peter kicks dirt over it, jaw tight.
Peter:
“Then it’ll keep coming back.”

⸻ 

Closing Image
The group huddles together, their rebuilt wall behind them, the cracked ground stretching ahead. Ashwin circles once more, his cry sharp against the silence.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire never struck the same way twice. It learned. It adjusted. It bent the earth to its will. And still we stood — shaken, yes, but unbroken. For now.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 77, Episode 5: Fault Lines

Cold Open — Cracked Earth
Morning light spills across the plateau. Dust still hangs in the air from the tremors of the night before. The transmitter shard lies half-buried, faintly pulsing like a heartbeat.

Kellyn crouches, brushing dirt away with steady hands. Alex kneels beside them, already dissecting its seams with a shard of metal. Jonathan and Peter stand apart, wordless but coiled.

Title Card: Fault Lines

⸻

Act I — Technical Demands
Alex: “If we sever the channels feeding power through the soil, it dies. But every cut has to be clean. If it senses interference—”

The shard hums louder, like it hears him.

Justin: “Then it alerts the others.”

They get to work anyway. Sam draws diagrams in the dirt with a stick, marking where the resonance veins run beneath them. Gary scavenges rope and tools, muttering half a joke to cut the silence.

Gary: "Guess empire’s got better Wi-Fi than we do.”

No one laughs, but the tension eases just enough to move.

⸻

Act II — Fractures Beneath
As Kellyn and Alex dig around the shard.  Jonathan braces the soil with reed bundles, his hands rough and sure. Peter joins without speaking, their shoulders brushing as they press earth back into place.

Peter (low, almost grudging):
“Your way of holding isn’t wrong.”

Jonathan (without looking up):
“Neither’s yours. Just heavier.”

Their truce is fragile, provisional, but real.

⸻

Act III — Risk and Release
The shard lets out a piercing tone. Everyone flinches. Kellyn steadies their breath, then presses ash and clay into the fissure Alex exposed. The pulse sputters, dimming to a faint flicker.

Alex exhales, shoulders slumping.

Alex: “Not destroyed, but blinded—at least for now.”

Sam notes the tremors fading under his palm. Ashwin swoops low, then drifts higher, circling wide as though to confirm the quiet.

⸻

Act IV — Nightfall Fallout
They sit by a cautious fire that night, food sparse, silence thick. Gary finally breaks it.

Gary: "You all realize empire’s learning faster than we are, right?”

Kellyn: "Faster, maybe, but not better.  If I could outsmart it for so long without having any idea what was going on, I think we're probably okay."

Peter’s jaw works, but instead of retorting, he looks at Kellyn.

Peter: “As long as we don’t split again.”

Alex watches Peter’s face, something unreadable flickering before he nods once. The circle closes tighter, fragile but holding.

⸻

Closing Image
The shard lies in a clay-lined pit outside camp, muffled, blind. Inside the shelter, the seven sit shoulder to shoulder, shadows overlapping in firelight — fractures showing, but the shape of unity still intact.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire’s machines weren’t the only fault lines running beneath us. Some were older, deeper, carved into bone. But like the earth, we could hold — if we chose not to split.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 77, Episode 6: Counterpulse

Cold Open — Dawn Light
The plateau glows pale. The fire is down to embers. Kellyn wakes to the weight of stillness, everyone close, the animals stirring softly outside. Jonathan sharpens a tool with slow, deliberate strokes. Peter feeds Estrella dry grass. Alex sketches lines in the dirt, though his eyes are distant. Justin hums under his breath, a thin thread of sound to hold the edges together.

Title Card: Counterpulse

⸻

Act I — Soft Recovery
They eat a thin breakfast — roasted roots, a little grain. Conversation is halting but warmer. Gary cracks a joke about empire probably being jealous of their cuisine. Kellyn leans against Alex’s shoulder for a moment. Peter and Jonathan quietly share tasks, still cautious but moving in rhythm.

Sam sets his hand on the clay pit where the shard lies muffled.

Sam (measured): "It’s quiet. Quieter than I expected.”

Kellyn: “Then let’s stay quiet too. Just for a while.”

The group sits in that silence, breathing, gathering strength.

⸻

Act II — The Ripple Returns
Midday. The wind shifts. Ashwin circles low, feathers ruffling. Estrella stamps and snorts. A faint thrum rises beneath the soil — not as sharp as before, but wider, heavier.

Justin: “They know. Empire felt the break.”

The sound presses into their chests, irregular, almost like a second heartbeat. Gary grimaces.

Gary: "It’s not just noise. It’s… tracking us.”

⸻

Act III — Countermeasure
Alex kneels, spreading reeds across the ground in a quick lattice.

Alex: "If it’s mapping pulse to body, we scatter the echoes. Everyone — move with different rhythms.”

The group jumps, shifts, steps at staggered beats, like dancers out of sync. Kellyn presses a palm to Jonathan’s arm, then darts away at a sharper pace, laughing. Peter growls in frustration but follows, his heavy steps mismatching Sam’s quicker ones.

The hum falters, splinters, then recedes.

⸻

Act IV — Fallout and Rebinding
They collapse together in the shelter afterward, lungs burning. Kellyn presses a damp cloth to Peter’s forehead, Jonathan to Justin’s. Alex sits against the wall, drawing Kellyn into his side, but his eyes are on the others.

Peter (hoarse, to Alex):
“You said scatter. But it was staying together that saved us.”

Alex nods slowly, gaze softening.

Alex: "Then we scatter together. That’s the only way covenant works.”

Kellyn looks around the circle — sweat, ash, exhaustion, and threads holding.

⸻

Closing Image
The group huddles in the dim shelter, breaths evening out, Ashwin perched at the doorway. The muffled shard lies silent outside, but the echo of empire’s counterpulse lingers in their bones.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire struck back, not with a blade, but with rhythm. They meant to pull us apart, each body isolated in its pulse. But in breaking their measure, we found our own. Scatter, yes — but scatter together.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 77, Episode 7: The Quiet Between

Cold Open — Shelter Interior
Night has fallen again. The air outside is unsettled, but the reed mats inside keep the hum dulled. A single lamp glows low. The group lies close but separate, the day’s exertion heavy in their limbs. Kellyn shifts slightly, catching Alex’s eyes across the dim. No words — just the smallest nod, and they rise together, stepping outside into the cool plateau night.

Title Card: The Quiet Between

⸻

Act I — Step Outside
The stars stretch wide overhead, crisp and endless. Ashwin flits in silence, a shadow against the moonlight. Kellyn and Alex sit near a flat rock, shoulders brushing. For a while, they only listen to the desert wind.

Kellyn (low, almost teasing):
“You know, you’re not as bad at quiet as you think.”

Alex exhales through his nose, gaze tilted toward the sky.

Alex: "Only when you’re here. Otherwise, it’s just… noise.”

⸻

Act II — Touch as Anchor
Kellyn leans in, resting their head against his shoulder. His hand hesitates, then rests lightly at their back, thumb drawing a slow circle through the fabric. Kellyn presses their palm to his chest, feeling the steady thrum of his heartbeat.

Kellyn: “Empire can pulse all it wants. This is the only rhythm that matters.”

Alex (hoarse, softer):
“And it’s the only one I can’t fake.”

⸻

Act III — Confession in Breath
The silence stretches again, but it’s not empty. Kellyn traces the line of Alex’s wrist with their fingers.

Kellyn: “When you said scatter together… that was the first time I didn’t feel pulled apart.”

Alex (quiet, almost reverent):
“I wanted to say the same. But I was afraid of making it sound like weakness.”

Kellyn looks up at him, steady.
Kellyn: “In our weakness, we are strong.  That's covenant.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Holding
They shift closer, foreheads touching, breaths slow and even. Ashwin circles once above them, then perches quietly at the shelter entrance, as if keeping watch.

Kellyn’s voice is a whisper now:
“Just stay here with me. That’s enough.”

Alex: “It’s more than enough.”

Their hands weave together, simple and steady, and the night stretches soft around them.

⸻

The night hasn’t deepened, but it’s quieter, softer. The stars sharpen into clarity. Kellyn and Alex remain on the flat rock where they settled, shoulders still touching. Ashwin dozes at the shelter entrance, watchful even in rest.

Kellyn stretches out along the rock, head in Alex’s lap. His posture stiffens at first, then eases as he rests one hand against their hair, tentative. The wind brushes across them, carrying the faint scent of ash from some far-off burn.

Kellyn (eyes half-closed, voice low):
“You always thought I’d vanish if you looked away.”

Alex (quiet, admitting):
“Because I was afraid if you did, I’d go with you.”

Kellyn: "Maybe that’s not fear. Maybe that’s love.”

Alex’s hand drifts from their hair down to their temple, tracing slow circles. Kellyn exhales deeply, grounding into the touch.

Kellyn (murmuring):
“Feels like you’re finally learning how to stay in your body.”

Alex (hoarse, almost breaking):
“Feels like you’re teaching me how.”

Kellyn reaches up, finding his other hand and lacing their fingers together. The weight of his palm steadies against theirs.

The silence is deep, but not fragile. Kellyn looks up at him, searching.

Kellyn:
“Tell me something you never said out loud. Not to empire. Not to anyone.”

Alex freezes, then leans down just enough that their foreheads touch again. His voice is barely more than breath.

Alex:
“I wanted to be seen. Not as what they made me. Not as what I forced myself to be. Just… seen. By you.”

Kellyn (steady, whispering):
“Then stay. And let yourself be seen now.”

Their breaths sync slowly, the quiet rhythm stretching and holding. Neither moves to escalate, neither pulls away. Just closeness, held long enough to become its own language.

Alex (almost reverent):
“You’re closer than my own thoughts.”

Kellyn: "Good. Then you’ll never lose me.”

⸻ 

Closing Image
The plateau night holds them, two figures pressed close, hands clasped, eyes closed, resting in the quiet covenant of being seen.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We thought empire’s gaze was all there was. But being seen by one another was enough. Enough to quiet the noise. Enough to remind us we were still here.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 77, Episode 8: The Stone and the Singer

Cold Open — Plateau Morning
The air is cool, streaked with faint pink light. The group circles the small mound where they buried the spiral stone days ago. Shovels, makeshift spades, and bare hands dig in. Dust rises with each scrape.

Jonathan (half-smiling, trying to lighten):
“Feels like we’re robbing our own grave.”

Kellyn (quiet, intent):
“Or uncovering what empire tried to hide.”

Title Card: The Stone and the Singer

⸻

Act I — The Unearthing
When they uncover the reed box they constructed to contain the stone, it isn’t there.  Confused, they keep digging.  Then the earth beneath gives way to something shocking: fabric. Skin.

Peter (sharp intake of breath):
“God. There’s someone here.”

They dig faster, hands clawing dirt until a body emerges — Matt Berninger, his frame gaunt, lips cracked, eyes open but unfocused. Alive. Barely.

⸻

Act II — Shock of Recognition
They lay him gently on a blanket. His chest rises and falls in shallow rhythm. He doesn’t respond to voices or touch, but his eyes track faint light.

Sam (hushed, trembling): “It’s him. It’s really him.”

Gary, crouched close, whispers half-joking, half-shaken: “Empire’s got a sick taste in headliners.”

Kellyn rests a hand on Matt’s wrist, searching for a pulse. It beats faintly, steady but fragile.

⸻

Act III — The Catatonia
They try water. A sliver of food. Jonathan hums a note, soft and steady, watching for a flicker of response. Nothing. Matt lies between them like a shadow made flesh.

Alex (grim, voice low):
“They turned him into the stone. They wanted him erased, but not gone. Bound. Useful only as silence.”

Peter clenches his jaw, anger breaking through: “Then we’ll undo it. We’ll bring him back.”

⸻

Act IV — Covenant in the Dust
The group sits in a circle around Matt, each touching him in turn — shoulder, hand, foot, hair. Not forcing speech. Just presence.

Kellyn (steady, almost a vow):
“You’re not empire’s silence anymore. You’re ours now. And ours means you’re alive.”

Ashwin swoops low, settling near Matt’s head, wings spread protectively.

⸻

Matt lies on a woven mat near the fire, breath shallow, eyes glassed. The group surrounds him, exhaustion carved into their faces. One by one, they’ve tried everything: Jonathan’s singing, Sam’s whispered encouragement, Peter’s steady hand on his shoulder, Alex’s clinical attempts with water and food, Kellyn’s constant grounding touch. Nothing has stirred him.

Kellyn (low, almost defeated):
“He’s here, but locked too deep.”

The silence hangs, heavy.

The group cycles through methods again, desperate. Jonathan strums the dulcimer softly, weaving fragments of songs Matt once sang. Sam dips a cloth in water and moistens Matt’s lips. Peter hums low notes, almost like a grounding chant. Alex whispers analytic fragments, theories about trance, catatonia, neurotech residue.

Kellyn leans close, pressing their forehead to Matt’s.
Kellyn (whisper):
“You’re not underground anymore. You’re not theirs. You’re here.”

Still no response.

⸻ 

A flutter of wings. Ashwin swoops through the shelter opening, talons gripping a sprig of something green and strange — a herb, silver-edged leaves glinting in firelight. The owl drops it gently at Kellyn’s side, then fixes them with unblinking black eyes.

Gary (half-whisper, half-awe):
“…Is that for him?”

Kellyn hesitates, then takes the herb, cradling it carefully. They crush a leaf between their fingers; the scent is sharp, electric, almost metallic. Without overthinking, they press it gently to Matt’s lips.

At first, nothing. Then Matt’s throat works. A breath shudders through him, deeper than before. His lips part, the herb brushing against them.

Jonathan (leaning forward):
