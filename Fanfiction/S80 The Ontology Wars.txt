Season 80, Episode 1: The Wheel and the Word

Opening Image — The Pieces Laid Out
The courtyard is scattered with wood and cord: the loom’s frame dismantled, the spinning wheel lying on its side. The group forms a circle around it, each person with a task — Jonathan testing pegs for stability, Peter smoothing a beam with his knife, Sam tracing the grooves of the wheel, Gary sorting linen strips for lashing. Justin and Matt kneel close, consulting scraps of parchment left in the old woman’s hut, diagrams sketched in faded ink.

Kellyn sits cross-legged, watching the others handle each piece with reverence.

⸻

Act I — Assembly Begins
The loom’s base takes shape first. Jonathan and Peter brace beams upright while Gary wedges them into place, joking under his breath about how the thing looks like an oversized gallows. Matt shushes him but grins anyway.

The spinning wheel proves trickier. Sam tests the axle, adjusting the tension until the wheel turns with a soft hum. Justin threads a length of yarn through, watching how it spins.

Justin: “It feels… alive. Like it wants to sing.”

Peter: “Then let’s not make it wait.”

⸻

Act II — Kellyn’s Question
As the frame rises, Kellyn’s voice breaks into the rhythm.

Kellyn: “Can I ask you all something? Alex admitted he watched me write my novel. But what about the rest of you? Were you watching too? And if you were… what did you think?”

Silence settles over the camp, the fire crackling.

Peter shifts uncomfortably, knife paused mid-whittle.

Peter: (low) “I read pieces. At first I mocked it — I didn’t understand. But the more I read, the more it unsettled me. You wrote things I couldn’t say aloud. Still can’t.”

Jonathan meets Kellyn’s gaze steadily.
Jonathan: “I didn’t just watch. I studied. You laid down blueprints of survival before you knew you were doing it. It wasn’t fiction to me — it was instruction.”

Sam looks away, embarrassed.
Sam: “I… envied you. The way you built worlds out of words when I could barely breathe in mine. It made me ache, but it also gave me hope. I kept reading because I needed it.”

Gary, unusually quiet, finally shrugs.
Gary: “I treated it like a mirror I didn’t want to look into. But I couldn’t stop. Your words were a knife, but one that cut the infection out.”

Justin’s voice is softer, more personal.
Justin: “I didn’t just read it. I lived inside it. Every line felt like a door into you, into something I couldn’t reach any other way. I stayed because it made me feel less alone. Because it made me believe you’d survive.”

Matt, still pale, leans forward.
Matt: “I read in fragments, mostly when I shouldn’t have. Empire framed it as data. But I knew it was more. It was… unbearable, sometimes, to see the tenderness you carved in the dark. Unbearable because it was real.”

⸻

Act III — Threading the Loom
Kellyn absorbs the words, eyes downcast, then rises and takes the first spindle of yarn. Their hands tremble as they pass it to Justin, who threads it through the loom’s frame. Matt ties the knot, steadying it with surprising gentleness.

The shuttle passes hand to hand. Each person adds a line, a thread pulled taut. The loom groans, then steadies, the warp shimmering under torchlight.

Jonathan: “There. It’s alive again.”

Kellyn watches the woven strands, voice low but certain.
Kellyn: “Then let’s weave something empire can’t unravel.”

⸻

Closing Image
The wheel turns. The loom holds its first fragile weave. Around it sit eight figures, silent in the firelight, their words still hanging in the air like threads between them.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“They thought they were watching me. But all along, I was seeing and writing them too. And now the pattern holds us together — not empire’s surveillance, but covenant’s weave.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 80, Episode 2: Fracture Lines

Opening Image — The Cloth Half-Woven
Dawn breaks pale over the plateau. The loom stands in the courtyard, its first woven panel shimmering faintly in the rising sun. The group gathers around it, weary but proud. Threads stretch taut — fragile, unfinished, yet proof of what they’ve built together.

Kellyn touches the fabric lightly, whispering:
Kellyn: “It’s holding.”

But then, a low vibration ripples through the ground. The threads quiver as if plucked by unseen fingers.

⸻

Act I — The Interference
Jonathan steadies the loom, frowning.
Jonathan: “That’s not wind.”

The vibration grows, resolving into a pulse — a rhythm beneath the earth, offbeat and invasive. The woven panel twists, threads straining, as if empire itself were trying to tug them apart.

Peter grips his knife, jaw tight.
Peter: “They’re in the weave. They’re trying to split us from the inside.”

Matt pales, voice low:
Matt: “They did this before. Pushed rhythm into me until I thought I was unraveling.”

Sam steadies him, hand on his shoulder.
Sam: “Not this time. We’re not alone.”

⸻

Act II — Splitting Tactics
The pulse sharpens, different for each of them. Kellyn hears Alex’s voice twisted with contempt. Justin sees flashes of Elio dying. Gary hears his old hustles called out, every scam he ever pulled thrown back at him.

Gary staggers, clutching his head.
Gary: “They’re trying to turn us inward. Make us claw at each other.”

Jonathan snaps, grounding himself.
Jonathan: “Then we don’t give them one beat. Not one.”

He strikes the frame of the loom sharply, creating a counter-rhythm. Peter joins with his knife tapping against a clay pot. Kellyn and Justin add their hands, drumming unevenly on the ground.

Soon, the group is a circle of mismatched percussion, their rhythms clashing deliberately, breaking empire’s imposed pulse.

⸻

Act III — The Shatter Point
The earth trembles harder, empire pressing. Threads on the loom begin to snap. Justin reaches out instinctively to steady Kellyn, their hands closing around the fabric.

Justin: “Hold it. Don’t let them tell us what this is.”

Kellyn looks around the circle — Jonathan’s jaw clenched, Peter’s eyes fierce, Sam whispering steady prayers, Matt visibly shaking but refusing to step back, Gary pounding hard enough to split his knuckles.  Alex's jaw is tight, his face grim.

Kellyn (hoarse but steady): “Then let this be covenant. Not perfect, not whole. But ours.”

With a final unified strike — fists, knives, palms against wood and earth — the imposed rhythm collapses. The loom sags but does not fall. The fabric, though frayed, holds.

⸻

Closing Image
Ashwin the owl circles overhead, crying once, sharp and clear. The group stands around the battered loom, panting, exhausted, but still together.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire could split the earth, twist the air, bend every rhythm toward fracture. But covenant was not one thread. It was many. And every time they tried to tear us apart, we only tangled tighter.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 80, Episode 3: The Hands That Hold

Opening Image — Frayed Threads
The courtyard is quiet now. The loom leans slightly, its panel still intact but visibly strained. Around it, the group sits in a loose circle, breaths still ragged. Ash drifts in the air like the aftermath of a storm.

Kellyn runs a finger over the frayed threads.

Kellyn (softly): “It didn’t break.”

⸻

Act I — The Gathering Calm
Jonathan crouches, pressing his hand to the earth. The vibration has faded, but he listens as if to be sure.

Jonathan: “It’s gone. For now.”

Matt sinks down against a wall, still trembling. Sam settles beside him, pulling a folded cloth from his pocket to wipe the sweat from Matt’s brow.

Sam: “You held.”

Matt (hoarse): “Barely.”
Sam: “Barely is still holding.”

Peter sharpens his knife by the fire, though his movements are slower than usual, almost meditative. He glances up at Alex across the flames, their eyes locking just long enough to acknowledge the strain between them — and the choice to stay.

⸻

Act II — The Small Gestures
Gary sits with his hands bleeding from pounding the earth, trying to laugh it off.

Gary: “Guess I finally paid for all my hustles.”

Kellyn tears a strip from one of the linen bolts and carefully wraps his knuckles.

Kellyn: “Or maybe you bought us time.”

Justin rests nearby, gaze heavy on Kellyn. When Kellyn’s hands still over Gary’s, Justin shifts closer, taking Kellyn’s wrist briefly in his own hand — grounding, claiming, not possessive but present. Kellyn allows it, the moment lingering before drifting back into the circle’s rhythm.

Jonathan brings out a clay bowl of water, passing it slowly from one to the next. Each person dips their hands, washing off the grit and blood, letting the water carry the residue of empire’s intrusion away.

⸻

Act III — Re-Grounding Together
The group lies back on woven mats in the courtyard, forming a rough star-shape with their heads angled inward. No one speaks for a while; only breath fills the space.

Finally, Alex breaks the silence.
Alex: “They’ll try again.”

Kellyn turns their head toward him, voice steady.
Kellyn: “And we’ll hold again.”

Matt exhales shakily, almost a laugh.
Matt: “What if one day we don’t?”

Jonathan answers without opening his eyes.
Jonathan: “Then we’ll weave another net. Even broken threads can bind when knotted right.”

Ashwin flutters down onto the loom itself, talons gripping the wood, watchful as ever.

⸻

Closing Image
The camera pulls up: eight figures resting on the plateau floor, their circle imperfect, frayed, yet unbroken. The battered loom and Ashwin stand at the center, quiet symbols of survival.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire pressed, and still we stayed. They thought to split us into silence, but silence became breath, breath became rhythm, and rhythm became covenant. And for one more night, that was enough.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 80, Episode 4: The Net Within

Opening Image — The Circle Breaks
Night settles deeper. The fire burns down to coals, leaving the courtyard shadowed and quiet. One by one, the group drifts apart from the star-shaped circle, finding smaller constellations of safety.

⸻

Act I — Kellyn and Justin
Justin lingers near Kellyn as the others move. His hand brushes theirs again, not asking but offering. Kellyn lets the touch remain, then leads him toward the edge of the loom, where Ashwin still perches.

Justin (low, a little unsteady): “When it all pressed in, I thought I’d shatter. But you—”
Kellyn: “Don’t finish that thought. You don’t have to be saved. Just here.”

They sit hip to hip, silence filling what words can’t. Justin leans his head lightly against Kellyn’s shoulder, and for the first time all day, his breath evens.

⸻

Act II — Alex and Peter
Across the courtyard, Alex sharpens his blade again, though it no longer needs it. Peter approaches, knife in hand, sitting close enough that their shoulders brush. For a while they say nothing, the twin rhythms of steel on stone enough to steady them both.

Peter (gruff, softer than intended): “You always think too much.”
Alex (without looking up): “And you never think enough.”

They trade the smallest of smiles, neither fully yielding, but the contact—shoulder against shoulder—holds longer than either expected.

⸻

Act III — Jonathan and Matt
Jonathan has coaxed Matt into lying back on a reed mat. He checks Matt’s pulse, steady now, then rests his own hand over Matt’s chest for a moment, as if measuring rhythm.

Matt (hoarse whisper): “You don’t have to keep watch on me.”
Jonathan (gentle, precise): “It isn’t watch. It’s presence. The difference matters.”

Matt lets out a faint, disbelieving laugh, then closes his eyes, surrendering to Jonathan’s quiet gravity.

⸻

Act IV — Gary and Sam
Gary and Sam sit by the coals, a rough clay cup passed between them. Gary tries another joke, but his voice catches halfway.

Sam tilts his head, studying him.
Sam: “Not every silence needs to be filled.”
Gary sighs, dropping the punchline, and instead leans back against the wall. Sam lets his shoulder meet Gary’s, their quiet companionship holding weight.

⸻

Closing Image
The camp lies hushed, fire burned to embers. The eight are no longer scattered but paired, threads of intimacy weaving through the night: shoulder to shoulder, breath to breath, silence to silence.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire tried to tear us apart with force. But what they never learned is that covenant multiplies in stillness. In the net within, each knot holds another. And so even in fracture, we did not fall.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 80, Episode 5: The Small Hunt

Opening Image — Dawn Mist
The plateau is veiled in pale morning haze. The group moves quietly, tools in hand, eyes sharp. The domestic rabbits remain safe in their pen, ears flicking as if they know what’s about to happen.

Kellyn (to the group, steady):
“We hunt the wild ones. Not ours. Ours stay with us. That trust is covenant too.”

⸻

Act I — The Chase
Peter leads with his knife ready, crouched low, eyes sweeping the brush. Justin and Matt set light snares. Jonathan and Sam flank, guiding the rabbits toward the traps. Alex keeps his bow steady, watching the treeline for movement.

The rabbits dart, fast as shadows. The group fans out, each breath tense until a snare snaps shut. Two rabbits are caught. The group exhales as one.

⸻

Act II — The Skinning
Back at camp, Kellyn and Jonathan prepare a flat stone as a work surface. Peter kneels, knife in hand, beginning the first clean cuts. Kellyn steadies the rabbit’s small body as he works, grounding his movements.

Alex and Sam gather ash and clay to dry and preserve the pelts. Justin watches, jaw tight but steady, until Kellyn gestures for him to try. His hands shake at first, but Jonathan’s calm voice guides him through the process.

Justin (hoarse, relieved when it’s done):
“I thought it would be worse.”

Kellyn (soft):
“It’s never easy. And it shouldn’t be.”

⸻

Act III — The Making
The group rinses the meat, setting it aside for the fire. The pelts are stretched on frames, salted with ash and rubbed with clay. Gary cracks a joke about “rabbit couture,” breaking the heaviness for a moment.

Sam and Matt start stitching small pouches and mittens from earlier tanned hides, now adding the soft new rabbit fur for lining. Jonathan tests how supple the treated hide becomes as it dries, considering future uses.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Use
At sundown, they sit together, passing bowls of rabbit stew thickened with wild herbs and seeds. The pelts hang drying nearby, glistening faintly in the firelight.

Kellyn (quiet, reflective):
“They were wild. But their lives still root into ours now. Covenant isn’t just with people. It’s with everything we take into us, everything we shape.”

Peter nods, wiping his knife.
Peter: “And it’s on us not to waste it.”

⸻

Closing Image
The group eats in silence, bowls warm in their hands, the smell of cooked rabbit filling the shelter. Behind them, two rabbit pelts dry slowly on their frames — not trophies, but threads woven into survival.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire made hunger into control. But covenant made hunger into craft. And in every small life honored, we stitched another seam in the fabric of our own.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 80, Episode 6: The Stitching

Opening Image — Morning Light
Sun streams across the camp. The rabbit pelts, dried overnight on frames, hang like pale banners against the wind. The group gathers around them, tools in hand — needles carved from bone, sinew for thread, and steady hands.

⸻

Act I — Cutting the Patterns
Kellyn lays one pelt flat, tracing shapes with a bit of charcoal: mittens, small pouches, patches for cloaks. Peter holds the hide taut while Jonathan cuts with patient precision. Sam suggests a design for water-flask covers to help keep them insulated.

Gary, half-smiling:
“Call me old-fashioned, but rabbit-fur mittens beat frostbite.”
Justin: “If you make them, you get naming rights.”

The group laughs — briefly, the task feels almost ordinary.

⸻

Act II — The Sewing
Matt and Justin begin stitching pouches, their movements uneven at first, then steadier with rhythm. Sam hums softly as he works, his stitches precise, turning scraps into thin linings for boots.

Kellyn shows Jonathan how to fold fur inward so the seams hold against strain. Peter, less patient, mutters but learns quickly, his mittens tight and practical. Alex, surprisingly, stitches clean seams for a small carrying bag.

Kellyn (gently teasing):
“Not bad for someone who thinks journals are weakness.”
Alex (deadpan, without looking up):
“Leather-binding was always respectable.”

⸻

Act III — The Finishing Touches
They rub the seams with ash and wax to waterproof them. Jonathan tests the strength by tugging at each stitch, nodding approvingly when they hold.

Sam slips the first finished mitten onto Kellyn’s hand. Kellyn flexes their fingers, surprised by the warmth.
Kellyn (soft):
“They were wild yesterday. Today they’re holding us.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Hands
The group sits in a loose circle, each wearing or holding something made. Mittens lined with fur. Small pouches tied to belts. A flask wrapped against the cold. The tools of survival, shaped from lives taken with care.

Peter (low, steady):
“We can’t undo the hunger. But we can shape what it becomes.”
Matt: “That’s all covenant is, isn’t it? Turning hunger into hands.”

The fire crackles. The items pass around the circle, tested, shared, admired.

⸻

Closing Image
Eight figures sit together, each holding something stitched from the rabbit hides. The items are small, imperfect, but they bind the group in warmth. Above them, Ashwin circles once, silent and watchful.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire took and wasted. But covenant took and held. Even the smallest hides became threads of survival — and the act of shaping bound us tighter than hunger ever could.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 80, Episode 7: The Tread and the Crown

Opening Image — Midday Light
The camp hums with slow industry. Piles of softened rabbit pelts rest near the fire. Knives, bone needles, strips of sinew, and scraps of bark are scattered across a low work table. The group gathers, hands busy, voices low.

⸻

Act I — The Boot Soles
Peter kneels over a frame of woven bark and rope, layering cured hide over it. Jonathan assists, pressing clay into seams before rubbing them with wax to waterproof. Kellyn grinds ash into a paste to line the soles, muttering:
“Better grit underfoot than ice in the veins.”

Sam tests a prototype, stomping lightly. The sole flexes but holds. Gary claps, half-joking:
“From rabbits to running shoes. Empire should be jealous.”

⸻

Act II — The Hats
Justin and Matt sit cross-legged, cutting and sewing ear-flap crowns. Ashwin, perched nearby, tilts her head at the pile of fur. Justin chuckles:
“She’s judging. She knows hers is better.”
Kellyn (teasing): “Ashwin doesn’t need hats. She has dignity.”

Matt finishes the first hat and drops it onto Kellyn’s head. The fur flaps cover their ears.
Matt (low): “Better. Now you look kept, not starved.”
Kellyn (softly, almost smiling): “I’ll take that.”

⸻

Act III — The Fit
One by one, the group tries on the new gear. Peter’s boots are heavy but warm. Gary, laughing, models a lop-eared hat. Jonathan tests balance by walking circles in his pair.

Alex, quieter, pulls on his boots, then ties Kellyn’s for them, fingers brushing the back of their calf. He murmurs, low enough for only Kellyn:
“Not perfect, but enough to carry.”
Kellyn (steady): “More than enough.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Hide and Step
The group gathers in a circle, stamping their boots into the dirt, hats pulled down over ears. They laugh as the rhythm catches — a syncopated stomp, rough but strong, echoing against canyon walls.

Peter (grinning faintly): “Marching band of misfits.”
Jonathan: “Not marching. Surviving.”

⸻

Closing Image
Eight figures stand together in their rabbit-hide hats and boots, the firelight glinting off fur seams. Ashwin circles once overhead, then settles on a post, as though approving the covenant stitched in every tread and crown.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Hunger skinned them. Covenant shaped them. And what was once wild became what carried us forward — warmth against cold, tread against stone, crown against storm.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 80, Episode 8: The Long Cold Coming

Opening Image — Low Sun
The Colorado Plateau’s horizon glows pale orange, the light already thinner. The group stands together, looking out at the dry scrubland. Breath mists faintly, the first hint of the season turning.

Kellyn (quietly):
“Winter won’t wait. Neither can we.”

They scatter, each taking up a task.

⸻

Act I — Shelter Reinforcements
Alex and Peter lead on thickening the walls with clay and reed mats. Jonathan and Gary lash beams tighter, filling cracks with pitch. Sam tests roof seams while Matt drapes spare hides over the entrances.

Peter (grunting as he packs clay):
“No gaps. Empire already slips in through too many.”
Alex (low, not looking up):
“This isn’t for empire. This is for the wind.”

The two exchange a brief glance—less combative than before, but not yet easy.

⸻

Act II — Food and Storage
Kellyn, Justin, and Sam prepare caches of dried fish, smoked rabbit, and preserved roots. Justin grinds roasted sunflower seeds, pressing them into hard cakes. Kellyn lines clay jars with wax and ash to protect grain.

Justin (half-joking, half-serious):
“Funny. All these years singing about scarcity, now I’m making pantry snacks.”
Kellyn (smiling faintly):
“Songs keep. So do seeds.”

⸻

Act III — Clothing and Bedding
Matt and Gary stretch and stitch hides into cloaks, reinforced with rope fringe. Jonathan cuts reed mats into liners for bedding. Kellyn braids long strands of yarn into thick belts. Ashwin swoops in, dropping a bundle of dry grass at the doorway, as though offering insulation.

Gary (snorting):
“Even the owl’s on sewing duty. Guess I’m out of excuses.”
Matt (dry):
“She’s faster than you, anyway.”

⸻

Act IV — Fire and Light
At sundown, the group gathers to render fat into tallow for candles. The smell is sharp, heavy, filling the air. Alex pours wax into clay molds while Peter trims reeds for wicks. Kellyn sets the finished candles in a line, lighting each in turn.

Kellyn (steady):
“Not empire’s fire. Ours. Not to burn, but to last.”

The group sits close as the flames flicker, warmth wrapping them in its fragile glow.

⸻

Closing Image
Eight figures huddle together in the fortified shelter, surrounded by new cloaks, sealed jars, candles, and layered mats. Outside, wind brushes the plateau in rising gusts. Inside, the winter’s covenant has already begun.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire thought winter would starve us. Thought cold would strip us. But we laid hands to work, and the work made us whole. And when the first snow comes, it will fall on walls already mended, fires already lit.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 80, Episode 9: First Snow

Opening Image — Falling White
The sky is heavy gray. Fine flakes drift down, at first hesitant, then thickening. The group stands outside the shelter, breath steaming, watching the season arrive.

Peter (low):
“Well. There it is.”
Kellyn (softly):
“It’s beautiful. And it’s going to test every stitch we’ve made.

⸻

Act I — Shelter Trial
They retreat indoors, sealing the door with layered hides. Wind pushes against the walls, snow piling outside. Alex and Jonathan check seams, pressing clay tighter where drafts sneak through. Sam pats down gaps with reeds.

Jonathan (approving):
“Holding better than I feared.”
Alex (wry):
“First storm always tells the truth.”

⸻

Act II — Fire and Food
Gary tends the fire while Matt strings rabbit cloaks near the warmth to soften. Justin pulls out one of the seed cakes, breaking it into pieces. Kellyn ladles thin broth from a pot, steam curling up.

Justin (half-smile):
“Tastes like survival.”
Kellyn (teasing):
“High praise.”

They pass bowls around, the smell of broth filling the small room.

⸻

Act III — Bedding Down
As night deepens, the group unrolls reed mats lined with hides. Cloaks become blankets, hats pulled low. Sam checks the animals in the adjoining shelter, brushing snow from Estrella’s back and patting the sheep into a close huddle. Ashwin perches overhead, feathers puffed against the cold.

Sam (returning, relieved):
“They’ll be fine. Huddled tighter than we are.”

⸻

Act IV — Shared Warmth
Back inside, the group piles closer. Wind rattles faintly, but the fire’s glow holds steady. Gary cracks a joke about snoring in shifts. Peter smirks, and for once Alex doesn’t snap back. Instead, Kellyn lays a hand against Alex’s knee, grounding him.

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“See? It holds. We hold.”

Alex exhales, leaning fractionally into the touch.

⸻

Closing Image
The first snowstorm of winter hisses against the plateau, but inside the fortified shelter, warmth holds. Cloaks, jars, and woven mats surround the group like proof of their own hands. Sleep settles with the storm still howling outside.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire expected silence to follow the snow. Instead, we ate, we laughed, we leaned into each other’s warmth. First storm, first test. And the truth was this: covenant held.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 9.

Season 80, Episode 10: Winter’s Weight

Opening Image — Inside the Storm
Snowdrifts press high against the walls. The world outside is white silence, broken only by the low groan of wind. Inside, firelight flickers across rabbit hides stretched to dry. The group sits close, weaving, mending, stirring broth — small tasks filling the long hours.

⸻

Act I — Domestic Routines
Kellyn teaches Justin how to stitch mittens from rabbit skin while Matt sorts dried herbs into jars. Peter sharpens knives against a flat stone, sparks jumping. Alex and Jonathan argue quietly over whether to ration the seed cakes or grind more into flour now. Sam hums under his breath while carving wooden spoons.

Kellyn (half-smiling at the sound):
“You’re making music out of chores.”

Sam (shrugging):
“Everything’s easier with rhythm.”

For a while, the hut feels almost like a home.

⸻

Act II — The Strain Creeps In
That night, as the storm deepens, a crack forms near the roof beam. Snow sifts down, dusting the hearth. Jonathan leaps up, stuffing reeds and clay into the gap, but more dust follows.

Jonathan (tense):
“It’s not failing — yet. But we’ll have to reinforce at first light.”

Alex rubs his temples, muttering about structural oversight. Gary cracks a joke about “home improvement apocalypse edition,” but no one laughs this time.

⸻

Act III — Empire’s Shadow
As the group tries to sleep, the hum rises beneath the ground again — faint, but clear. Ashwin shifts sharply, wings flaring as if to shield the sleepers. Estrella brays in the adjoining shelter.

Kellyn (low, steady):
“They’re testing us. Even through the snow.”

Peter presses his back against the door, jaw clenched.

Peter: “Then let them hear this: we’re still here.”

The others stir, murmurs echoing. The hum fades only after the group’s voices overlap in uneven defiance — not planned, just raw sound.

⸻

Act IV — Holding Through the Storm
By dawn, the roof still stands. The hum has gone. Everyone is exhausted, but together. Kellyn ladles hot broth into bowls, passing one to Alex, then Justin, then Gary. Hands brush, and no one pulls away.

Matt (soft, thoughtful):
“Maybe this is winter’s lesson. That closeness isn’t weakness. It’s the only warmth we have.”

The others nod, weary but present.

⸻

Closing Image
Outside, snow drifts higher. Inside, patched mittens, spoons, jars, and cloaks surround the group — not luxuries, but proof of survival. The storm presses; empire listens. But breath, fire, and covenant hold.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Winter taught us the same lesson empire feared: that nothing alone survives the storm. But gathered, pressed close, we were more than a target. We were warmth itself.”

Fade out.
End of Season 80.
