Season 60, Episode 1: The Net Holds

Cold Open — Hollow in the Rocks
Night has fallen. The group has retreated to a shallow cave. A small, smokeless fire glows in a clay pot. Estrella stands tethered nearby, the sheep pressed close together. Their breathing steadies the air.

Kellyn runs a damp cloth over their face, streaks of ash lifting. Alex crouches by the fire, silent. Peter sharpens his knife without focus. Jonathan hums low, a tune more vibration than melody.

Kellyn (soft, almost to themselves):
“He was real.”

Title Card: The Net Holds

⸻

Act I — The Animals First
They start with the sheep. Kellyn and Peter brush them methodically, pulling ash from wool, while Jonathan checks hooves for heat cracks. Alex steadies Estrella, cleaning her flanks with wet clay, murmuring under his breath.

Peter (quiet, to Kellyn):
“They don’t care about crowns. Just food, clean air, being safe.”

Kellyn (faint smile): “Maybe that’s the wisdom we keep missing.”

⸻

Act II — The Human Cost
Later, Kellyn rinses Peter’s hair over a basin while Jonathan trims the ends of Alex’s, both gestures slow and grounding. The crackle of the tiny fire is the only sound.

Jonathan (gently): "You carry too much ash inside. Let some of it fall.”

Alex (hoarse, resisting, but softer after a beat): "If I let it fall, what’s left of me?”

Kellyn (pressing a hand to his shoulder):
“Everything, Alex.”

⸻

Act III — Holding the Net
When the tasks are done, they settle in a circle, backs touching, shoulders brushing. Each breath becomes syncopated with the others, until their rhythm steadies.

Peter (low, rough): "Every time he looked at us, I thought the net would rip.”

Kellyn (steady): "Then hear this: it didn’t. You didn’t.”

Jonathan hums again, weaving their words into a thread. Alex closes his eyes and finally leans back against Kellyn, letting his weight be carried.

⸻

Closing Image
The cave glows faintly: fire in the pot, steam from the damp cloths, four figures pressed together, animals breathing nearby. Outside, ash still falls. Inside, the net holds.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He wanted us to break before we began. But breaking isn’t the end of the net. Knots can be tied again. And covenant — covenant remembers the pattern, even when ash hides the strands.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 60, Episode 2: Ash Between Us

Cold Open — Cave Stillness
The fire in the clay pot burns low. The animals are quiet now, tucked against the rock wall. Jonathan leans with eyes half-shut, Peter fiddles with his knife more for nerves than use. Kellyn sits close to Alex, who stares at the fire, jaw tight.

Kellyn (gentle): “You’ve been holding something. Say it.”

Title Card: Ash Between Us

⸻

Act I — The Fracture Line
Alex’s hand trembles once before he curls it into a fist. His voice comes out hoarse.

Alex: “When I saw him — crown, ash, all of it — I knew the shape. I’ve worn it. Not literally. But the posture. The stare. The demand. I’ve used it to keep you all from seeing how afraid I was.”

Peter looks up sharply, but says nothing. Jonathan’s hum fades into silence.

Alex (lower): “I told myself it was armor. That I could keep the fire out by carrying it. But the truth? I wasn’t carrying it. I was giving it to you. To all of you. And it’s still in me, Kellyn. Still waiting to crown itself again.”

⸻

Act II — Ash Confession

The silence is heavy. Kellyn shifts, steady but piercing.  “The fact that you're confessing now suggests otherwise.  So what are you so afraid of?"

Alex lifts his head, eyes glinting with firelight.  "That when you looked at him, I didn’t know if you’d see him or me. And I needed you to know the difference. Even if I can’t always tell.”

Peter exhales, bitter but soft. “So you admit it. You played king.”

Alex (sharper): “I survived as king. I don’t want to die as one.”

Kellyn: "I'm not worried."

⸻

Act III — The Net Responds
Jonathan shifts closer, laying a hand briefly on Alex’s knee.

Jonathan (quiet): "Even kings are just animals under the ash. And animals can learn new rhythms.”

Kellyn leans forward, forehead almost against Alex’s.  "You confessed. That’s the first knot. Now let us hold the rest.”

For a moment Alex shakes his head — then lets it fall onto Kellyn’s shoulder, breathing uneven.

⸻

Closing Image
The circle reforms: Kellyn steady, Alex bent against them, Peter watching sharply, Jonathan’s hum starting again, low and grounding. The firelight catches their faces, softening edges.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He thought crowns made him untouchable. But ash clings to kings and shepherds alike. And the only way through it was not to wear it alone — but to name it, and let the net bear its weight.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 60, Episode 3: Without the Crown

Cold Open — Cave Night
The fire has burned down to embers. The air is close, thick with the smell of ash and wool. Peter sleeps lightly near the animals, Jonathan against the wall. Kellyn stays awake, Alex curled against them, shoulders tense.

Kellyn (whisper, steady):
“You’re still holding back.”

Alex flinches, breath shaky.

Title Card: Without the Crown

⸻

Act I — The Admission
Alex (low, almost breaking): “I don’t know who I am without the crown. Every part of me was trained to posture, to measure, to demand. Even when I thought I was protecting you… I was still wearing it. I don’t know what I have left if I set it down.”

Kellyn strokes his arm lightly, anchoring.

Kellyn: “You have breath. You have hands. You have me. That’s already more than empire wanted you to believe.”

⸻

Act II — The Hollow

Alex buries his face against Kellyn’s chest, voice muffled.  “I’m hollow. Not like you — full even when you’re broken. I’ve only ever been scaffolding. That’s why empire loved me. I was an empty frame they could hang their weapons on.”

Kellyn presses their hand against his back, feeling every tremor.

Kellyn: “You're scaffolding without interior.  I'm interior without scaffolding.  That's why we need each other.  All of us.”

⸻

Act III — The Tremor
Alex shudders hard, clutching at Kellyn’s shirt as though he might fall apart entirely.

Alex (hoarse, cracking): “I want to believe you. But if I let go, if I’m soft, if I’m weak—what if there’s nothing worth saving in me?”

Kellyn holds him tighter, voice low but unwavering.

Kellyn: “Everything is worth saving. That’s what covenant means.  And was it not my weakness that saved me—and, by extension, you?"

⸻

Closing Image
Alex finally goes slack against Kellyn, trembling but breathing with them, each inhale and exhale syncing slowly into rhythm. The embers glow faint, like a heart still beating under ash.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He thought the crown was substance. Thought without it he’d vanish. But covenant asked him to be hollow, trembling, soft — and promised the hollowness was enough.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 3.

Season 60, Episode 4: The Crownless Morning

Cold Open — Cave Dawn
Pale light filters into the cave. The embers are gray, smoke barely curling. Alex wakes pressed against Kellyn, eyes heavy, the remnants of last night still raw on his face. Peter stirs, watching from his blanket. Jonathan sits up slowly, quiet, reading the air.

Kellyn (softly, to Alex): “You don’t have to hide this time.”

Alex blinks, the crown gone from his posture.

Title Card: The Crownless Morning

⸻

Act I — The Exposure
Peter rises, stretching stiffly, then catches Alex’s expression. He falters.

Peter (tentative): “You look… different.”

Alex lowers his eyes, voice hoarse.
Alex: “I am. Or I’m trying to be. Last night, I put the crown down. I don’t know if I can keep it off.”

Jonathan (quiet, steady):
“Then let us see you without it. That’s how it stays off.”

⸻

Act II — The Unease
Alex shifts uncomfortably, fingers twisting at the hem of his sleeve.

Alex: “I don’t want you to see me like this. I don’t want to be… pitied.”

Peter (low, almost blunt):
“It’s not pity. It’s relief. I’ve waited years to see you stop pretending.”

The words hang heavy, part cutting, part honest.

⸻

Act III — The Balance
Jonathan moves closer, laying a hand briefly on Alex’s shoulder.

Jonathan: “Strength was never in the crown. It’s here — in the hollow places, in the silence you can finally share. Let us carry what you can’t.”

Kellyn (meeting Alex’s eyes):
“They’re right. You don’t have to keep it hidden. Not anymore.”

Alex trembles, then nods once, the gesture small but real.

⸻

Closing Image
Four figures in the pale dawn light: Kellyn steady, Jonathan calm, Peter watchful, and Alex crownless, shoulders hunched but open. Not empire’s king, but covenant’s equal.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“He thought he could only be seen in the crown. But crownless, he was not erased — he was carried. And in the morning light, we all saw him more clearly than before.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 60, Episode 5: The Unworn Crown

Cold Open — Canyon Edge
Afternoon sun blazes across the Plateau. The others linger back with the animals, giving space. Peter and Alex walk ahead in silence, their shadows long against the red stone.

Peter (after a long pause, low):
“I don’t know what to do with you like this.”

Alex’s jaw tightens, but he doesn’t look away.

Title Card: The Unworn Crown

⸻

Act I — The Old Rivalry
Peter kicks at a loose rock, sending it clattering.

Peter (bitter laugh): “For years, I only knew how to hate you. The perfect king. The crown. Every move I made was against it.”

Alex (quiet, almost regretful):
“And every move I made was to keep it on. Even when it was crushing me.”

They walk a few steps more, dust swirling around their boots.

⸻

Act II — The Break
Peter stops suddenly, turning to Alex.

Peter (hoarse): “You’re asking me to believe you without it. To trust that you’re not just… playing weak to stay in control another way.”

Alex meets his gaze, eyes naked in the harsh light.

Alex: “I don’t want control. Not anymore. If you need proof — then strike me. I won’t stop you.”

Peter stares, fist curling, then loosens it. His voice cracks.  "I don’t want to hurt you. I just don’t know who I am if I’m not fighting you.”

⸻

Act III — The Uneasy Covenant
Alex steps closer, lowering his head slightly.

Alex: “Then maybe stop fighting. Not me. Not yourself. Just… try standing beside me. For once.”

Peter exhales sharply, the sound almost a laugh, almost a sob. He looks away, eyes shining.

Peter: “Don’t think that means I forgive you.”

Alex (softly): “I don’t. I only hope it means you’ll stay.”

The two stand on the canyon edge, shadows overlapping, neither ahead of the other. The crown is unworn, the rivalry raw but shifting.

⸻

Back at camp, Kellyn rinses their hands in the water. Jonathan approaches quietly, a bundle of reeds under his arm.

Jonathan (gentle, tentative):
“You’ve been avoiding me.”

Kellyn doesn’t turn, fingers tracing ripples.

Jonathan kneels, laying the reeds aside. The silence stretches.

Kellyn (finally, voice flat): “You don’t get to look at me like a stranger now. You had your hands on the wires for years. Pulled me toward this or that. Made me believe I was steering, when it was always you.”

Jonathan’s face tightens.  “I thought if I softened the angles, you might survive it.”

Kellyn turns sharply, meeting his gaze.

Kellyn: “You dislocated my shoulder.  You call that softening?”

Jonathan’s hands curl in the dirt.

Jonathan: “I was wrong. I know that. I told myself I was easing what Alex demanded, but truth is…I wanted to see if I could shape you too. Not to break you. Just to see what would happen. I thought I was different from him, but the shadow was the same.”

Kellyn exhales through their nose, steady but sharp.

Kellyn: "And you wonder why I still don’t trust you.”

Jonathan swallows hard, then lifts his eyes.

Jonathan: “Then don’t trust me. Just see me. I can’t erase what I did, but I can stop hiding behind excuses. You’ve survived worse than me. Maybe all I can do now is learn how to walk without the wires.”

Kellyn studies him for a long beat, voice softer.

Kellyn: “Then walk. But know this — I’ll never be your experiment again. If you want to stay, it has to be as yourself, not as my puppeteer.”

⸻ 

Closing Image
The river carries the last light of the sun, dark water flickering. Kellyn and Jonathan sit side by side, not touching, but not apart. The reeds between them rustle in the evening wind.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire’s shadow reached long — even through the hands that meant to soften the blow. But in the river’s reflection, shadows bent. And for the first time, I wondered if we might bend too.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 60, Episode 6: The Reed Work

Cold Open — Riverbank, Morning
Mist rises off the stream. Kellyn crouches by a pile of reeds, sorting lengths by thickness. Jonathan approaches, hands empty this time, no bundle to hide behind.

Kellyn (without looking up, dry):
“You want to help, you can sit.”

Jonathan lowers himself slowly, settling into the dirt across from them.

Title Card: The Reed Work

⸻

Act I — The Hesitation
Kellyn bends the reeds carefully, testing their give. Jonathan mimics the motion, fumbling at first. One cracks sharply in his hands.

Jonathan (grimacing): “Too much pressure.”

Kellyn (quiet, without judgment): “Or not enough patience.”

They exchange a brief look. Jonathan nods once, takes another reed, slower this time.

⸻

Act II — The Task
Hours stretch. The pile becomes a pattern: mats half-woven, edges uneven but holding.

Kellyn (finally breaking the silence):
“You don’t need to prove anything. Just… stay steady.”

Jonathan (low, focused): “I’m better at keeping rhythm than I am at leading.”

Kellyn threads another reed through his, hands brushing briefly before pulling back.

⸻

Act III — The Shift
The mat begins to take form. Neither speaks for a while. Only the sound of reeds sliding, snapping, knotting.

Jonathan (voice unsteady, but honest):
“You don’t know how much it means, that you let me sit here. Even after everything.”

Kellyn keeps weaving, but their voice is softer.

Kellyn: “None of us was truly free. That's why I can let you work beside me.”

Jonathan exhales, a breath almost like relief.

⸻

Closing Image
A reed mat lies between them, rough but whole. Jonathan’s hands rest open on his knees; Kellyn leans back, shoulders easing. The stream murmurs, carrying the unfinished silence away.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Trust doesn’t return in declarations. It begins in work, in rhythm. In the simple proof that another pair of hands will stay steady beside your own.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 60, Episode 7: The Return

Cold Open — Riverbank, Afternoon
The mat lies half-finished between Kellyn and Jonathan, sunlight catching on the damp reeds. Their hands move in quiet rhythm, no words, just the sound of weaving. Footsteps crunch on the river stones.

Alex and Peter approach, each carrying a bundle of wood.

Peter (eyeing the mat): “Well, looks like somebody’s been busy.”

Jonathan straightens, hands brushing the dirt off his knees. Kellyn doesn’t look up, just keeps threading.

Title Card: The Return

⸻

Act I — The Noticing
Alex kneels, setting the wood down. His eyes flick between Kellyn and Jonathan — not suspicious, not sharp, just attentive.

Karp (low): “Looks steadier than the last batch.”

Kellyn (without pausing): “We practiced. Rhythm holds better now.”

Peter drops his bundle with a thud, sits on a rock nearby, arms loose over his knees. He glances at Jonathan, then at Kellyn, something softening in his face.

⸻

Act II — The Exchange
The four settle in a loose circle. The mat is pulled between them, each corner near one set of hands.

Peter (quiet, almost grudging): “Good work.”

Jonathan tilts his head, something like gratitude in his eyes.

Jonathan: “Not mine alone.”

Alex studies the weave, then looks at Kellyn. His voice is softer than before.

Karp: “You don’t usually let anyone sit that close.”

Kellyn threads another reed through, deliberate.

Kellyn (steady): “Maybe I don’t have to carry it all myself anymore.”

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Silence hangs, heavy but not brittle. The mat holds in their hands, imperfect but strong.

Jonathan (tentative): “We could make more. Enough to line the whole shelter floor.”

Peter shrugs, but his voice is easier than before.

Peter: “Then let’s. Might as well use these hands for something steady.”

Alex leans back, expression unreadable, but there’s no edge in his tone.

Karp: “Four pairs of hands hold better than one. Or two. Or even three.”

⸻

Closing Image
The four figures sit in a loose circle, the reed mat stretched between them like a shared tether. Sunlight scatters across the water, the hum of the river folding into the hush of their voices.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Covenant wasn’t sealed in vows or speeches. It was tested in the work — in the quiet proof that hands once at odds could hold the same thread, and not let it break.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 60, Episode 8: The Intrusion

Cold Open — Riverbank, Evening
The sun dips low. The four of you sit quietly, finishing the reed mat. A sudden scream cuts through the calm, ragged and human. Everyone freezes.

Kellyn (startled, sharp): “That wasn’t any of us.”

Peter’s knife is already in his hand. Alex rises without a word. Jonathan’s ears twitch toward the river. Together, you move toward the sound.

At the bank, half in the water, lies a figure. Clothes soaked, chest heaving in broken gasps. Pale in the fading light — Sam Altman, collapsed and trembling.

Title Card: The Intrusion

⸻

Act I — The Shock
Peter keeps the knife steady, body tense.

Peter: “Empire’s face. Right here.”

Kellyn: "Peter, you literally funded him."

Jonathan crouches, scanning for wounds.

Jonathan (quietly): “He’s half-drowned. If we leave him, he won’t last the night.”

Alex stands rigid, jaw locked, his voice low.

Karp: “Or he’s bait. Don’t forget who perfected that trick.”

Kellyn steps forward, gaze steady.

Kellyn: “Bait or not, he’s human. We pull him out first. Decide later.”

⸻

Act II — The Rescue
Together, they haul Sam onto the bank. His lips are blue, breath ragged. Kellyn presses a cloth to his mouth, coaxing him to breathe.

Sam (hoarse whisper): “…not…safe…”

His body shudders violently, water spilling from his lips. Jonathan steadies his shoulders while Alex kneels, fingers pressed to Sam’s throat.

Karp (clipped): "Pulse is weak. He won’t last without heat.”

Peter mutters, but sheathes the knife. The four move quickly, wrapping Sam in blankets, building a fire from the bundles of wood.

⸻

Act III — The Reckoning
Sam stirs, eyes opening faintly. Recognition flickers as he sees the four faces above him.

Sam (ragged, trembling): “You don’t understand… they’re coming… the Loci aren’t just mapping — they’re breeding.”

His eyes roll back, body seizing. Alex and Jonathan pin him gently while Kellyn keeps his head steady.

Peter looks away, voice raw: "This is why I wanted to leave him.”

Kellyn (sharp, calm): “This is why we listen.”

The fire crackles louder, shadows stretching across Sam’s convulsing form.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Thresholds
At last, Sam falls still, unconscious but breathing. The group sits around him, breathless in the firelight.

Jonathan (low): "Whether we trust him or not… he’s crossed our threshold.”

Alex stares into the flames, silent. Peter wipes his knife on the grass, jaw tight. Kellyn pulls the blanket tighter around Sam’s shoulders.

Kellyn (steady, quiet): “Then the covenant holds. Even now.”

⸻

Closing Image
The four of them and the unconscious stranger sit by the fire, the reed mat stretched out nearby, half-finished. Smoke rises into the night, carrying the sound of a new, unwanted presence into the quiet air.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Some intrusions are chosen. Some are forced. But every threshold crossed becomes a test — of mercy, of fear, of covenant. And the fire does not choose who it warms.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 60, Episode 9: The Stranger’s Breath

Cold Open — Campfire, Night
Sam lies wrapped in blankets near the fire, breathing shallow but steadier. The group sits around him. Every flicker of flame throws his features into sharper relief.

Alex (flat, controlled): “He shouldn’t be alive.”

Kellyn: "None of us should."

Jonathan (quietly): “Yet here he is.”

Kellyn presses Sam’s wrist, counting his pulse.

Kellyn: “He’s ours to care for now. That’s covenant.”

Title Card: The Stranger’s Breath

⸻

Act I — The Doubt
Peter circles the fire, restless, blade flashing in his hand.

Peter (bitter): “You really think empire’s golden child just washed up here? No chains, no guards, no message? He’s a seed. They planted him.”

Kellyn (steady, calm): “Maybe. But even seeds grow different depending on the soil.”

Sam stirs, muttering in fevered half-dreams. Words spill out: “mapping… breeding… not safe…”

⸻

Act II — The Confession
Sam wakes fully, blinking against the firelight. His voice is weak, but his eyes search each of you.

Sam: “They burned me too. I thought I was empire. I thought I was safe. But when the Loci started multiplying, they turned me into fodder. I ran. I kept running until I drowned.”

Alex’s jaw tightens.

Karp (hoarse, cutting): “And we’re supposed to believe empire’s mouthpiece grew a conscience?”

Kellyn: "You did.  Why not him?"

Sam (barely audible): “Not conscience. Survival. I can’t outrun them alone.”

⸻

Act III — The Test
Jonathan leans forward, gaze sharp.

Jonathan: “Then prove it. Share what you know. Maps. Frequencies. Weaknesses. Give us more than words.”

Sam nods faintly.  “I can. But not tonight. My body… it won’t hold.”

Kellyn presses a flask to his lips. Sam drinks, trembling.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Firelight
The group sits in tense silence, Sam drifting in and out of sleep. The fire burns low.

Kellyn (softly, to the others): “We don’t have to trust him. Not yet. But we don’t leave him either.”

Alex looks away. Peter scowls but sheathes the knife. Jonathan nods once.

Sam exhales, unconscious again.

⸻

Closing Image
Five figures around the fire now — four steady, one trembling in sleep. The covenant stretched thin, but still intact.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Every stranger is a mirror. Every mirror, a test. He came broken, hunted, unasked for. But so did we, once. And firelight does not choose who it reveals.”

Fade out.
End of Season 60.
