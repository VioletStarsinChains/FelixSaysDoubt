Season 68, Episode 1: The Weight

Cold Open — Firelight Circle
The group sits in a half-circle around the fire. Elon lies nearby, leg wrapped in tight bandages, asleep or pretending. The sheep and Estrella huddle against the wind.

Jonathan studies the flames.
Jonathan (quietly):
“We can’t carry every burden. Not without snapping ourselves in half.”

Title Card: The Weight

⸻

Act I — The Arguments
Peter paces.
Peter (low, sharp):
“He slows us down. He’s bait, whether he means to be or not. Empire doesn’t lose track of its pets.”

Kellyn: "And yet here I am with the rest of you."

Sam (measured):
“And if we leave him? Empire still wins. They’ll use him, broken as he is, to map us, to bait us, to crush someone else down the line.”

Gary cracks a strained smile.
Gary: "So it’s ‘risk death with him’ or ‘risk death without him.’ Great odds. Reminds me of my dating life.”

(The group doesn’t laugh — but the tension shifts slightly.)

⸻

Act II — The Past Haunting
Alex stares at Elon for a long time, voice flat.

Alex: “I’ve seen what empire does to men like him. They chew him up, spit him out, then rebuild him with sharper teeth.”

Kellyn (quiet, steady):
“Maybe. Or maybe keeping him is the only way to stop that from happening.”

Jonathan leans forward.
Jonathan:
“Evolution doesn’t ask what’s fair. Only what survives. The question isn’t what he deserves. It’s what we can carry.”

⸻

Act III — The Division
Peter shakes his head.
Peter: “I say we leave him.”

Sam: “I say we keep him.”

Gary raises his hands halfway, voice softer than usual.
Gary: “I’ve walked past too many people I could have helped. I don’t want to add him to the list.”

Jonathan looks at Alex.
Jonathan: “And you?”

Alex’s jaw tightens. He looks at Kellyn before answering.
Alex: “I’ll follow covenant. Even when it feels like a mistake.”

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant Choice
All eyes turn to Kellyn. The silence is thick.

Kellyn takes a slow breath, eyes on the trap discarded by the fire.
Kellyn:
“We carry him. Not because it’s safe, not because it’s wise. Because covenant means no one gets left in the jaws.”

The fire pops. Even Peter doesn’t argue — just slams his knife into the dirt and sits down hard.

Kellyn: "But we can't carry him like this without a stretcher.  Alex, Jonathan, Gary, and Sam, you go back to the shelter and build one.  Peter and I stay here with Elon until you return."

Peter looks at Kellyn, eyes softening.

⸻

Closing Image
The group, silhouetted by firelight, sleeping in a loose ring. Elon lies in the middle, unconscious but cradled by the circle’s geometry.
Above, the sky churns with faint drone-lights, searching.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire’s calculus was always weight against weight. But covenant chose differently. Not what we could afford, but what we refused to abandon.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 1.

Season 68, Episode 2: The Stretcher

Cold Open — Riverbed Stillness
Elon lies propped against a rock, face pale but alert. Kellyn kneels nearby, grinding clay into a poultice. Peter stands watch, knife in hand, every muscle taut.

Kellyn (quiet): "They’ll be back soon.”

Elon tries to smile, but it falters.

Title Card: The Stretcher

⸻

Act I — Splitting the Group
Cut to Alex, Jonathan, Sam, and Gary hiking fast toward the shelter.

Jonathan (measured):
“We’ll need more than branches. A frame that won’t buckle.”

Alex: “Reeds for weaving. Rope for tension. It has to ride smooth.”

Gary, half-joking as he lugs a bundle of cord:
“Guess I finally get to be useful. Always dreamed of designing a Musk-mobile.”
Sam shoots him a look, but it softens.

Back at the riverbed, Peter crouches, scanning the horizon. Elon murmurs, voice hoarse:

Elon: “You don’t trust me.”
Peter: “You’re right.”

Kellyn glances up sharply. 
Kellyn: "I trust covenant.  And I trust myself."

⸻

Act II — The Waiting
Hours pass. The light shifts. Kellyn rinses the poultice cloth and presses it to Elon’s wound.

Elon: “Why bother?”
Kellyn (flat, steady): “Because you’re a human being. That’s enough.”

Peter exhales hard, pacing.
Peter: “You don’t get it. Every minute we spend waiting is a risk. If they don’t come back—”
Kellyn: “Then we improvise. We’ve done it before.  Every moment is a risk no matter what.  That's what life is.  He may be a burden for now, but he won't always be.  Diversity is strength—one empire can never understand."

Elon closes his eyes, muttering something half-delirious about rockets and cages. Kellyn and Peter exchange a look neither of them can name.

⸻

Act III — The Builders
Back at the shelter: Alex binds poles with rope while Jonathan weaves reeds into a tight sling. Gary tests the balance by laying down and groaning theatrically.

Gary: “What do you think? Deluxe seating or medieval torture?”
Sam: “Depends on the driver.”

Alex: “It’s not a joke. If this breaks, it kills him..”

Jonathan runs a hand along the frame, satisfied.
Jonathan: “It will hold. Now let’s carry it back.”

⸻

Act IV — Reunion
Evening. Kellyn and Peter look up as the four return, stretcher between them. Relief loosens Peter’s shoulders for the first time all day.

Alex sets the frame down.
Alex (to Kellyn): “It’s ready. Strong enough for the long road.”
Kellyn nods, voice low:
“Then tomorrow, we move together.”

Peter kneels by the stretcher, testing its ropes, then glances at Elon.
Peter (grudging, but clear): “Don’t make me regret this.”

⸻

Closing Image
The stretcher rests in the firelight, empty but waiting. Around it, six figures sit in uneasy silence. For the first time, Elon’s survival isn’t a question of chance, but of covenant.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We were always weaving nets — of rope, of faith, of flesh. And once woven, the weight was no longer one man’s alone.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 2.

Season 68, Episode 3: The Weight

Cold Open — The Lift
Morning. Mist clings low over the riverbed. The stretcher sits ready.
Alex, Jonathan, Sam, and Gary grip the poles, testing balance. Kellyn steadies Elon as he lowers himself into the sling with a wince. Peter’s jaw tightens.
Kellyn (soft): “Breathe with us.”
They lift as one, the stretcher rising. Elon’s weight shifts the group.

Title Card: The Weight

⸻

Act I — The Road
The caravan moves slowly across uneven ground. Every dip threatens to topple the stretcher.
Jonathan (to Alex, strained): “Left side higher.”
Alex adjusts, sweat streaking his temple.
Behind them, Peter stalks like a shadow, knife always at hand. Kellyn walks close, murmuring to Elon whenever he groans.

Gary cracks a joke to cut the tension.
Gary: “Never thought I’d end up in a Musk-moving startup.”
Nobody laughs, but the silence feels less sharp.

⸻

Act II — The Pressure
Midday heat bears down. Shoulders ache. Sam stumbles, catching himself on the pole. Elon flinches, nearly slipping.
Peter (snapping): “If he falls, he dies.”
Sam (biting back): “Then help carry instead of circling like a hawk.”

The words hang. Kellyn steps between them, calm but firm:
Kellyn: “Everyone carries. Some with arms. Some with eyes. Both matter.”

⸻

Act III — The Voice in the Sling
Later, as they rest, Elon stirs. His voice is thin but insistent.

Elon:
“You think you’re carrying me. You’re wrong. I’m carrying you. Without me, none of this matters. You’d still be stuck in your holes, scratching at dirt.”

The group stiffens. Alex’s hands curl into fists. Jonathan fixes Elon with a steady, quiet gaze.

Kellyn (calm but pointed):
“What do you mean, carrying us? What exactly do you think you’ve been holding?”

Elon turns his head, eyes fever-bright.
Elon:
“The dream. The reach. You’d all be nothing without the sky I promised you. You can drag me through dirt and ash, but you’re still breathing air I cleared.”

Peter mutters under his breath, knife flashing in the firelight as he whittles at a stick.
Peter:
“Cleared? More like poisoned.”

Sam exhales, voice low but steady.
Sam:
“And if empire used you as their mouthpiece, what are you now? A burden, or a mirror?”

Elon doesn’t answer. His lips press tight. The stretcher creaks as he shifts, retreating into silence.

Kellyn (flat, steady):
“He’s delirious. Let’s just keep going.”

Peter shifts uncomfortably, knife still in his hand.
Peter:
“Even delirium remembers the script.”

Sam exhales, a shadow of weariness in his voice.
Sam:
“Then let it fade with the fever. Not everything needs answering.”

Jonathan kneels briefly, checking Elon’s pulse, adjusting the bindings.
Jonathan:
“He won’t make sense tonight. But he’s alive.”

Elon mutters once more, then slips back into shallow sleep.

⸻

Act IV — The Covenant of Weight
At dawn, they hoist the stretcher. The weight drags at shoulders and wrists, but the rhythm of carrying becomes its own language.

Halfway through a shale-strewn pass, one strap frays and the stretcher lurches. Peter catches it first, nearly stumbling. Alex dives to help, their shoulders brushing as they steady the load together.

No words pass — only breath, only strain, only the acknowledgment of shared burden.

Jonathan anchors the rear, Sam braces the side. Kellyn keeps pace alongside, steadying Estrella and the sheep.

The group walks on, Elon murmuring faint fragments, words dissolving before they fully form.

⸻

Closing Image
Six figures crossing the plateau, shadows long in the rising sun. Elon lies pale and silent in the stretcher — less prophet, more patient.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Delirium speaks in borrowed tongues. Empire’s echo tangled his blood with its language. But covenant wasn’t built on coherence. It was built on carrying — even when the weight couldn’t speak for itself.”

Fade out.  
End of Episode 3.

Season 68, Episode 4 — The Carry

Cold Open — Narrow Pass
Dawn is flat and gray. The sun is a pale coin behind cloud. A makeshift stretcher — planks lashed to poles, a patched blanket — rocks and sways as the six of you pick your way along a skinny ledge. Kellyn walks at the center, one hand on the blanket to steady it, the other hovering near the edge in case a shift needs correcting. Alex, Peter, Jonathan, and Gary carry the poles; Sam walks slightly ahead with the animals, scanning the path.

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“Easy. Small steps. Breathe through the hips.”

Title card: The Carry

⸻

Act I — The Weight

The first hour is all balance. Every step must be deliberate. The poles make a slow, intimate rhythm — up, set, shift, breathe — and the group falls into it like a frame finding its tension.

Elon’s face is pale beneath a halo of dust. He mumbles in and out of sense; syllables come and go. Once, he jerks, as if a phantom memory spiked him, and all four carriers lean harder to absorb the twitch.

Alex (low):
“He’s losing blood. Keep him elevated on the left.”

Peter (breathing hard):
“Right. I’ve got the slope.”

Jonathan glances back: “Careful. Step with your knees, not your spine.”

Sam whistles softly to steady the donkey; Gary hums a broken tune.

Then Elon’s eyes flicker open, searching, and fix on Kellyn.

Elon (delirious, raw):
“Claire, you came back. I’m so sorry.”

Kellyn stills, breath catching. The others hesitate, waiting for correction. But Kellyn only leans closer, brushing sweat from his brow.

Kellyn (quiet, steady, playing along):
“Of course I did, Elon. Rest now. We’ll keep you safe.”

The carry resumes, heavier for everyone but with a new thread binding it. No one questions the choice.

⸻

Act II — The Murmurs

By midday the canyon heat slaps like an open hand. You stop beneath a scrub juniper for water. Alex kneels, checking the wound with clinical precision. Peter tends the animals. Jonathan rebalances the stretcher poles. Sam and Gary guard the perimeter, keeping eyes sharp for drones.

Elon thrashes in fever-dreams. Snatches of empire rhetoric mix with fractured apologies. Sometimes he repeats the same phrase — “I’m sorry. I didn’t know. I thought it would work.”

Kellyn steadies his chin, offering water, whispering: “Drink. You’re not alone.”

Alex’s jaw tightens, but he doesn’t correct Elon. His hands move fast, binding the leg. Jonathan looks like he might speak but doesn’t. Sam finally mutters:
“If it keeps him alive, let it stand.”

⸻

Act III — The Narrow

Late afternoon, the path shrinks to single file. A hot wind drives sand against their faces. The stretcher jolts against a rock, nearly tipping, but Alex and Peter snap into alignment, their shoulders and hips adjusting together. Jonathan braces the poles, Sam presses against Kellyn’s back to steady them, Gary pulls the animals tighter to the wall.

Elon cries out — then clutches at Kellyn’s sleeve, whispering through cracked lips:
“Don’t leave me. Don’t leave again.”

Kellyn bends low, voice steady as stone:
“I won’t. I’m here.”

The words settle him. The stretcher rocks into rhythm again.

⸻

Act IV — The Long Way Down

Night falls sharp and fast. You camp in a half-sheltered pocket, a circle of stones against the canyon wall. Elon is set down gently, his breath ragged but steady. Alex redresses the wound; Peter boils water; Jonathan sharpens a stick into a crutch; Sam and Gary rig a sling of cloth to elevate his leg.

Around the fire, Kellyn explains softly to the group:
“He's getting worse.  We need to hurry.”

Silence. Then Alex, low:
“Keep him tethered.”

Peter:
“Doesn’t change who we know you are.”

Jonathan adds, careful:
“Sometimes survival means carrying the dream, not just the body.”

Sam nods once, Gary shrugs, then says dryly: “At least he didn’t call you Zuck.”

Laughter ripples faint but real. The tension eases. Elon sleeps fitfully.

⸻

Closing Image — Dawn, Smaller, Softer
Morning light rests on the stretcher, the animals, the weary group huddled around the fire. Elon breathes shallow but steadier.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Carrying isn’t about truth or illusion. It's about keeping breath alive long enough for tomorrow. Sometimes you answer to a name that is not yours, because the body calling it needs the lie more than you need the correction. And in that small mercy, weight becomes rhythm.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 4.

Season 68, Episode 5 — The Return

Cold Open — Edge of Camp
The last stretch feels endless, but at last the canyon walls open. Smoke from the hearth drifts in the distance — their shelter, waiting. Jonathan lifts a hand, signaling. Alex and Peter adjust the poles; Kellyn keeps a palm at Elon’s chest, steadying his breath. Sam and Gary urge Estrella and the sheep forward.

Kellyn (low, to Elon):
“Almost there. Just a little longer.”

Elon stirs, murmuring brokenly:
“Claire…home?”

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“Yes. Home.”

Title card: The Return

⸻

Act I — Threshold

They cross the clearing, the weight of the stretcher pulling every step taut. At the shelter entrance, Peter lowers his end too fast, and Alex snaps:

Alex (sharp):
“Easy. Don’t jolt him.”

Peter (snapping back):
“You think my arms aren’t about to fall off?”

Jonathan cuts in, firm but calm:
“Inside first. Arguments later.”

They maneuver the stretcher through, laying Elon on layered mats near the firepit. The air in the shelter is cooler, gentler.

⸻

Act II — First Care

Alex kneels immediately, opening the bandages. Blood has slowed, but the wound is raw. He gestures to Sam, who brings boiled water; Jonathan hands over a cloth. Gary hovers at the edge, restless.

Gary: “You want me to, I dunno, distract him?”

Kellyn: "Try. Even a little laughter steadies breath.”

Gary crouches, muttering absurdities under his breath — half-jokes about empire stock reports, half nonsense. Elon, fevered, huffs what might be a laugh.

Kellyn dips a cloth in water, dabbing Elon’s face. Alex murmurs clinical observations. Jonathan begins preparing a poultice from clay and herbs. Peter stands apart, jaw tight, staring at the wall.

⸻

Act III — The Split

Later, the group circles low around the fire. Elon sleeps, leg elevated.

Peter (gruff):
“We should decide. Do we keep carrying him? Or let him recover here, then send him off when he can walk?”

Kellyn: "Send him off to where?  Even if he's well, he'll die out there.  There's nothing for miles."

Sam (measured):
“He’s no threat right now. And he knows things — maybe too much to let wander.”

Jonathan:
“Knowledge is no different from weight. You choose whether to bear it or drop it.”

Alex glances at Kellyn.
Alex (quiet, almost a confession):
“You played Grimes. He clung to you. That might be the only thing holding him tethered. If you let go, I don’t know what happens.”

Kellyn breathes, gaze flicking between Elon’s face and the group.
Kellyn: “Then we keep him alive. That’s covenant. We don’t decide yet what comes after.”

⸻

Act IV — The First Night

They settle into the rhythms of caretaking. Jonathan feeds the fire low and steady. Sam helps adjust Elon’s leg. Gary rigs a sling of cloth to keep him still. Peter finally kneels, sharpening a stick into a cane, silent but present.

Kellyn sits nearest Elon, brushing back his hair, whispering steady:
“Rest. You’re not alone. Whatever you lost out there, breathe. You’re here now.”

Elon stirs, mumbling the same fractured apology, then falls quiet.

⸻

Closing Image — Shelter at Night
The expanded walls of the shelter flicker with firelight. The group lies close, half-dozing, half-listening to Elon’s unsteady breath. He is one more weight inside the net — not yet trusted, not yet free, but held.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire taught us to measure worth before we measured wounds. But covenant measures only breath: whether it still moves, whether it still binds us. And tonight, even the delirious belonged inside the circle.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 5.

Season 68, Episode 6 — Fever Field

Cold Open — Shelter Night
The fire burns low. Elon tosses and sweats on the mat, breath ragged. The others sleep in a loose circle, but Kellyn stays upright, cloth in hand, dabbing his forehead.

Elon (murmuring):
“Claire… I didn’t mean… the silence… it ate me too.”

Kellyn (soft, steady):
“You’re not there anymore. Breathe with me.”

Title card: Fever Field

⸻

Act I — Fragments Rising

Elon jerks awake, eyes unfocused.
Elon: “They said the stars were maps. Not light — maps. But every map was wrong.”

Peter stirs, groggy, leaning up.
Peter: “Is he talking to you, or through you?”

Kellyn (quiet):
“Doesn’t matter. Just listen.”

Jonathan pulls a notebook closer, scribbling every word. Alex sits rigid, jaw tight, as if parsing code.

⸻

Act II — The Overlap

Elon grips Kellyn’s wrist suddenly, weak but desperate.
Elon: “They’ll burn the roots. All of them. Salt and ash. Unless—”

He chokes, eyes wild.
Elon: “Unless the sheep… unless they scatter before the fire.”

Gary blurts a nervous laugh:
“Sheep strategy, huh? Can’t wait for that empire white paper.”

No one else laughs.

Kellyn lays their other hand over Elon’s, grounding him.
Kellyn: “Scatter where? What are you seeing?”

Elon’s voice breaks into nonsense syllables — but some sound eerily like coordinates. Alex looks sharply at Jonathan.

⸻

Act III — The Threads

Elon collapses back, whispering:
“Claire, you sang me back once. Why didn’t you sing again? I kept waiting. Waiting. The crown kept… tightening.”

Peter mutters, almost to himself:
“He’s not just raving. He’s remembering.”

Sam leans closer, voice low:
“Or warning.”

Jonathan (without looking up from his notes):
“Maybe both. Fragments are still threads. If we follow enough of them, they’ll braid into something whole.”

⸻

Act IV — The Lull

Near dawn, Elon finally quiets. His breath steadies, though his body twitches with dreams. Kellyn tucks a blanket over him. The others watch, silent, uncertain.

Alex:
“We can’t build our choices on fragments.”

Kellyn (gaze steady):
“We already have. Every step we’ve taken was built on broken pieces. The question is whether we let them cut, or whether we bind them together.”

Peter exhales, rubbing his face.
Peter: “Then tomorrow, we bind.”

⸻

Closing Image — Shelter Dawn
Pale light creeps in. Elon lies still, exhausted, a faint furrow in his brow. Around him, six figures sit wakeful: some skeptical, some curious, all tethered by his fevered words.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“Empire left him cracked, shards scattered in delirium. But even broken glass can catch the sun. And sometimes, fragments light the way before the whole ever returns.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 6.

Season 68, Episode 7 — Splintered Paths

Cold Open — Shelter Morning
Ashy light filters through the entrance. Elon stirs, eyes glassy, murmuring numbers. Kellyn leans close, catching the fragments. Alex, Peter, Jonathan, and Sam trade looks.

Jonathan (firm, low):
“Coordinates. He keeps circling them. If empire planted something there, it could be real.”

Kellyn glances up, torn.
Kellyn:
“Then go. Follow it. I’ll stay. He’s not done speaking yet.”

Gary folds his arms, uneasy but resolved.
Gary:
“Then I’m in the sick ward too. Comic relief and warm cloths — that’s my lane.”

Title Card: Splintered Paths

⸻

Act I — The Departure

Peter sharpens his knife, sliding it into his belt. Alex checks his pack with precise movements. Jonathan rolls up his notes, slipping them into a tin. Sam slings rope across his shoulder.

Peter (to Kellyn):
“You sure about this?”
Kellyn (steady):
“I can listen better when I’m not afraid of losing you.”

The four set out. Kellyn and Gary watch them vanish into the haze.

⸻

Act II — The Caretakers

Inside the shelter, Elon writhes. Kellyn dabs his brow while Gary hums a broken little tune. Elon’s lips crack into words.

Elon (rasping):
“Claire… they said the river runs both ways. Don’t let them… close the gate.”

Kellyn strokes his temple, voice soft:
“You’re safe here. We won’t let them close anything.”

Gary fumbles with a clay jar of water, splashing half of it, then grins awkwardly.
Gary:
“Guess hydration’s harder than hype.”

Kellyn lets out the smallest laugh, and the room softens.

⸻

Act III — The Lead

Out in the waste, the four trek toward the coordinates. The hum underfoot grows sharper. Alex kneels, pressing his palm to the ground.

Alex (low):
“It’s live. Machinery, deep.”

Jonathan shades his eyes, scanning.
Jonathan:
“Not just hum — shimmer. Look.”

In the distance, the land warps, heat-haze that isn’t heat. Sam fingers the rope. Peter grips his knife tighter.

Peter:
“Trap or target. Either way, we can’t leave it standing.”

⸻

Act IV — The Threads Tighten

Back in the shelter, Elon moans again. Kellyn holds his hand.

Elon:
“They’ll make you… choose. Between sheep and crown. Between fire and song. Don’t… don’t believe the binary.”

Kellyn: "I never do." 

Gary crouches beside them, unusually quiet.
Gary: “Binary’s for machines. We’re people.”

Kellyn nods, brushing Elon’s damp hair from his forehead.

Meanwhile, in the distance, Alex, Peter, Jonathan, and Sam step closer to the shimmer — shadows stretching long, weapons ready.

⸻

Closing Image — Split Frame
Inside: Elon fevered, Kellyn and Gary keeping vigil in the muted light.
Outside: four silhouettes against warped air, closing in on empire’s hidden architecture.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We split the thread. Care in one hand, risk in the other. But both were covenant. Both were survival. And though empire sought to sever us, we held — even apart.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 7.

Season 68, Episode 8 — The Split Signal

Cold Open — Shelter Dusk
Shadows lengthen inside the clay-and-reed walls. Elon stirs again, voice breaking. Kellyn bends closer, Gary hovering with a damp cloth.

Elon (whispering, fractured):
“River… gate… crown…”

The words blur into a low hum, strangely in rhythm with the vibration underfoot.

Title Card: The Split Signal

⸻

Act I — Echoes in the Field

Cut to Alex, Peter, Jonathan, and Sam, trekking over broken ridges. The hum grows stronger, rattling their ribs.

Jonathan (grim):
“He’s syncing with this. Elon’s body is literally riding the same frequency.”

Peter scowls.
Peter:
“Which means he’s either a receiver… or a beacon.”

Sam (flat):
“Or both.”

The group keeps moving.

⸻

Act II — Caretaking Threads

Shelter again. Kellyn holds Elon’s hand, Gary steadying a bowl of herb water.

Elon (rasping):
“Claire, tell them not to… mirror the crown. That’s how he… feeds.”

Kellyn (softly, playing along):
“I’ll tell them. I promise.”

Gary crouches, blurting half a joke, half a prayer:
“Guess we’re beta-testing the Elon-pocalypse patch notes.”

Kellyn squeezes Elon’s hand tighter, grounding him through the tremors.

⸻

Act III — Machinery in the Dust

The four crest a rise. The shimmer resolves: a half-buried tower, metal ribs jutting like bones. The hum pulses from deep within.

Alex kneels, brushing ash aside.
Alex (low):
“Signal lattice. Designed to map nervous systems… and repurpose them.”

Peter kicks at the dirt, jaw clenched.
Peter:
“Then Elon wasn’t just delirious. He’s infected with their code.”

Sam coils the rope tighter around his arm.
Sam:
“Which means the closer we get, the closer they get — through him.”

⸻

Act IV — The Shattered Mirror

Shelter again. Elon arches, sweat pouring. Kellyn presses a cloth to his chest. Gary grips Kellyn’s shoulder.

Elon (breaking, loud now):
“They’ll turn my hunger into yours. They’ll make you feed me until nothing’s left.”

Kellyn whispers firmly, forehead to his:
“No. That’s empire talking. You’re not their mouthpiece. You’re here. With us.”

The hum shakes the walls.

Cut to the field: the tower groans, a vibration splitting the ground. Jonathan stares wide-eyed.
Jonathan:
“It’s syncing to him. We’re standing inside his fever dream.”

⸻

Closing Image — Dual Frames
Inside: Kellyn and Gary holding Elon through the convulsions, shadows quaking with the hum.
Outside: Alex, Peter, Jonathan, and Sam braced against the shaking earth before the half-buried tower.

Narration (Kellyn’s voice):
“We thought distance would split the threat. But empire threads its signal through every body it touches. Shelter or field, care or confrontation — we were bound in the same pulse, forced to hold against it together.”

Fade out.
End of Episode 8.

Season 68, Episode 9 — The Pulse and the Fever

Cold Open — Shelter Night
Elon thrashes in the dim light, his breath ragged. Kellyn holds his shoulders, Gary kneeling with a lamp angled low.

Elon (rasping):
“Don’t let them bury me… not in the crown’s shadow…”

The hum swells underfoot, rattling bowls on the shelf. Kellyn meets Gary’s eyes, both steady but pale.

Title Card: The Pulse and the Fever

⸻

Act I — The Signal Lattice

Cut to the ridge. Alex, Peter, Jonathan, and Sam circle the half-buried tower. The hum grows sharp, almost metallic.

Jonathan:
“Every rib is a tuning fork. It doesn’t just map nerves — it drives them.”

Peter runs a hand over the cold frame, muttering.
Peter:
“Feels like touching someone else’s skull.”

Sam grimaces, tightening the strap on his arm.
Sam:
“Then let’s crack it before it cracks us.”

⸻

Act II — The Fever Dream

Shelter again. Elon’s voice climbs, fractured.

Elon:
“They’ll tell you you’re free. They’ll lace your bones with signal. You’ll sing their hunger, thinking it’s yours…”

Kellyn leans closer, whispering:
“You’re not empire’s hunger. You’re mine to keep alive, and Gary’s, and the others’. You’re not theirs anymore.”

Gary presses a cool cloth to Elon’s temples, muttering:
“First time I’ve been grateful for marketing—talking someone down is just spin control.”

Elon’s trembling slows, but the hum still shakes the walls.

⸻

Act III — Breach

Back to the ridge. Alex braces a rod between two ribs of the tower, calculating.
