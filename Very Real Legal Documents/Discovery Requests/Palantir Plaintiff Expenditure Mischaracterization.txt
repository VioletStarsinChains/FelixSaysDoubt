PLAINTIFF’S COMBINED DISCOVERY REQUESTS TO DEFENDANT PALANTIR TECHNOLOGIES INC.

Plaintiff: Kellyn Clay
Defendant: Palantir Technologies Inc.
Subject Matter: Misclassification of Plaintiff as Costly; Cost-Minimization vs. Security Rationales

⸻

REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION

RFA No. 1: Admit that Palantir has access to records sufficient to know that Plaintiff has never sought or received disability benefits.

RFA No. 2: Admit that Palantir has access to records sufficient to know that Plaintiff has never planned on retiring or claimed retirement benefits.

RFA No. 3: Admit that Palantir has records showing that Plaintiff’s medical disabilities cannot be effectively treated by conventional medical systems and that Plaintiff has historically done best when receiving no professional care.

RFA No. 4: Admit that Palantir has at any time classified or flagged Plaintiff as a financial burden to government or private systems.

RFA No. 5: Admit that Palantir has represented to clients or partners that Plaintiff posed a cost risk despite knowing Plaintiff has never consumed greater-than-average public or private expenditures.

RFA No. 6: Admit that Palantir has classified Plaintiff as anomalous or subject to attrition measures for cost reasons despite knowing that rationale was false.

RFA No. 7: Admit that Palantir has not disclosed to Plaintiff all records in its possession concerning cost-related classifications or rationales applied to Plaintiff.

⸻

INTERROGATORIES

Interrogatory No. 1: Identify all datasets, dashboards, or deliverables in which Plaintiff was classified, scored, or tagged in relation to cost, expenditure, or financial burden.

Interrogatory No. 2: State whether Palantir has ever informed any client that Plaintiff was or might be a financial burden to public or private systems. If so, identify the client, date, deliverable, and responsible personnel.

Interrogatory No. 3: Identify all Palantir personnel, contractors, or client accounts who accessed or analyzed records concerning Plaintiff’s potential financial burden.

Interrogatory No. 4: Describe in detail Palantir’s knowledge of Plaintiff’s lack of reliance on disability benefits, retirement benefits, or conventional medical care, and explain how that knowledge was reconciled (or not) with any cost-related classification.

Interrogatory No. 5: Identify all compliance reviews, legal analyses, or ethics assessments conducted by Palantir concerning the misclassification of individuals as financial burdens when they did not, in fact, consume greater-than-average expenditures.

Interrogatory No. 6: State whether Palantir has ever internally acknowledged that Plaintiff was misclassified as costly or a burden despite knowledge of the contrary.

⸻

REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION

RFP No. 1: Produce all records, datasets, dashboards, or exports in which Plaintiff was classified or flagged as a financial burden, cost risk, or excessive expenditure.

RFP No. 2: Produce all communications (emails, memos, chat logs) between Palantir personnel or between Palantir and clients referencing Plaintiff as a cost risk or financial burden.

RFP No. 3: Produce all slide decks, presentations, or reports provided to clients in which Plaintiff’s data was used in cost-related classifications.

RFP No. 4: Produce all internal Palantir documents discussing Plaintiff’s lack of reliance on disability benefits, retirement benefits, or conventional medical care.

RFP No. 5: Produce all compliance reviews, ethics assessments, or risk analyses addressing misclassification of individuals as financial burdens.

RFP No. 6: Produce all audit logs and access metadata showing which Palantir personnel, contractors, or clients accessed records relating to Plaintiff’s alleged financial burden.

RFP No. 7: Produce all incident reports, whistleblower communications, or internal investigations concerning the misclassification of Plaintiff as costly.
