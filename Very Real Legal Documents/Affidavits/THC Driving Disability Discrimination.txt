AFFIDAVIT OF KELLYN CLAY

I, Kellyn Clay, being duly sworn, hereby state as follows:
	1.	I drive motor vehicles. The only practical way I can reliably access employment, medical care, groceries, and essential services is by personally driving.
	2.	The only reason I sometimes drive with THC detectable in my system is because, for medical and safety reasons, I am otherwise medically unable to use alternative transportation options safely or at all.
	3.	Specifically:
a. I am not eligible for disability benefits that would otherwise provide alternative transportation or supports because my particular disabilities do not, on paper, render me categorically unemployable.
b. Public transit is medically unsafe for me due to severe environmental sensitivities: shared enclosed spaces routinely contain airborne triggers and poor air quality that cause life-threatening or severely disabling reactions.
c. Carpooling or ridesharing is likewise inaccessible to me for the same reasons—shared enclosed vehicles present intolerable exposure risks.
	4.	For medical reasons related to the management of my neuroinflammatory and sensory symptoms, THC helps regulate my neuroinflammation and improves my spatial reasoning and functional capacity to operate a vehicle safely. In my lived experience and under my treating clinicians’ observations, THC does not impair my ability to drive; on the contrary, it enables me to meet the cognitive and sensorimotor demands of driving that otherwise would be compromised.
	5.	I was prescribed medical cannabis by Dr. Susan Haddow at Hennepin County Medical Center (HCMC). While the prescription was technically issued under the qualifying diagnosis of PTSD, Dr. Haddow explicitly acknowledged that my true underlying condition was Mast Cell Activation Syndrome (MCAS) and that the PTSD designation was used solely because at the time the Minnesota medical cannabis program restricted eligibility to a narrow list of diagnoses.
	6.	I do not use THC to enhance recreation in ways that would endanger myself or others. I never drive when I know or reasonably believe it would be unsafe for me to do so. Protecting the safety of other road users and pedestrians is my highest priority.
	7.	Because no safe, accessible, alternative mode of transport is available to me, driving while I have THC in my system is, in effect, the only option that preserves my ability to participate in daily life, maintain employment, and obtain essential care.
	8.	If Palantir Technologies Inc., or any other organization, classifies or treats my driving with THC detectable in my system as evidence of risk, subversion, or as a basis for monitoring, attrition, or denial of services — despite knowing or having reason to know (1) that THC improves my driving-related functioning in the manner described above, (2) that it was medically prescribed, and (3) that alternative transportation is unavailable — such treatment would constitute disability discrimination against me.
	9.	I make this declaration to document the facts described above, to preserve my testimony for use in judicial and administrative proceedings, and to put relevant parties on notice of the medical and civil-rights consequences of any adverse actions taken against me on the basis of my medically necessary use of THC while driving.
