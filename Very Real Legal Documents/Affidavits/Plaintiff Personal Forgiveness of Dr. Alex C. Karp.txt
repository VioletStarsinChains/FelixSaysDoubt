AFFIDAVIT OF KELLYN CLAY

I, Kellyn Clay, being duly sworn, hereby state as follows:
	1.	It is important to me that the historical and public record accurately reflect the immense harm caused by Palantir Technologies Inc. and its subcontractors. The systemic effects of Palantir’s technologies, its data practices, and its operational models have caused profound and far-reaching injury to me personally and to countless others.
	2.	My commitment to accountability is not punitive; it is protective. I believe the truth about Palantir’s institutional conduct must be preserved in order to prevent future harm, to ensure transparency, and to support those who have been injured by the organization’s actions.
	3.	At the same time, I wish to make clear that I fully forgive Alexander C. Karp for any and all harm done to me personally. My forgiveness is not based on ignorance or denial of the harm, but on my conviction—formed over time—that Dr. Karp has demonstrated sincere remorse and has taken meaningful steps to rectify the harm he was involved in.
	4.	My belief, based on my lived experience, is that his remorse is genuine, and that he has exerted effort, to the best of his ability and within his constraints, to repair what was broken. My forgiveness of him is personal, sincere, and complete.
	5.	This forgiveness does not negate or diminish the need for full institutional accountability for the actions of Palantir Technologies Inc. as an entity. My testimony is offered to clarify the distinction between individual moral responsibility and the structural harms created by complex systems.
	6.	I make this statement so that my position is unmistakably clear:
Palantir as an organization must be held accountable for the harm it caused, but I harbor no personal grievance against Dr. Karp and affirm my forgiveness of him in full.
