AFFIDAVIT OF KELLYN CLAY

I, Kellyn Clay, being duly sworn, hereby state as follows:
	1.	I have reason to believe that Palantir Technologies Inc. misrepresented the purpose of its platforms and services by presenting them publicly as tools for “threat detection” or “terrorism prevention,” when in reality the primary objective was cost minimization for government and corporate clients.
	2.	Despite my severe disabilities, I do not in fact require greater-than-average public or private expenditures. None of my disabilities can be effectively treated by the conventional medical system, which has been a major cause of my harm. I have always done best when receiving no professional care at all.
	3.	I have never sought or wanted disability benefits, nor have I ever planned on retiring. My expectation has always been to support myself through my own labor, to the extent permitted by my health and environment.
	4.	Palantir maintains encyclopedic knowledge of my life, including my history, choices, conversations with social workers, and personal values. Accordingly, Palantir knew or should have known that I was not a person who imposed higher-than-average costs on public or private systems.
	5.	Nevertheless, Palantir’s systems appear to have classified me in ways that rationalized my exclusion or attrition on supposed cost grounds, despite knowing that such a rationale did not apply to me.
	6.	Cost-driven attrition measures were imposed on me under the false pretense of threat detection or security, when in reality the only effect was to deny me access to services, housing, medical stability, and employment continuity.
	7.	It is my sworn testimony that Palantir’s misrepresentation of its purpose — emphasizing threat detection in public while delivering cost-cutting tools to clients — directly contributed to me being harmed by attrition measures, despite my being neither a financial burden nor a security threat.
